# Resistance and Revelation Reassessment

## Result

The new material should **extend the active Book One Architecture Workshop**, not create a new workshop and not reopen the completed reassessment.

RW-01 through RW-10 remain authoritative at their accepted macro depth. The new material improves the evidence spine and Konrad's final function, but it does not define the missing BA-01 victory condition or resolve the technical mechanism by itself.

The best integration points are BA-02, BA-07, BA-09, and BA-10.

## What became stronger

### 1. The evidence has an independent owner

The Resistance can reconstruct the historical chain that Sylvan could not discover alone. This prevents the outcome from becoming a single exceptional protagonist revealing everything through superior technology.

### 2. Konrad's confrontation gains an immediate decision

The evidence does not exist only to explain the past. It requires Konrad to decide whether to shut down programs he restored and still treats as sacred.

### 3. Samuel's motive and mechanism converge

Samuel's jealousy of Konrad's public divinity gives his target emotional specificity. The mechanism of revenge attacks the institution that supports Konrad's identity rather than a random asset.

### 4. The reveal has a reusable construction rule

The Converging Revelation gives BA-10 a stronger test: a late disclosure should alter the meaning of the Great War humiliation, false autonomy, reactivation, and present danger at once.

## Status classification

### Locked canon preserved

- RW-01 through RW-10.
- Samuel never controls the overall process or governing evidence state.
- Konrad reactivates systems inside a structure where Samuel has earlier priority.
- The inner circle recognizes Samuel's repeated method before isolated Konrad.
- Konrad retains influence and must make a consequential final move.

### Supported extensions

- A distributed Resistance investigation can supply the causal chain used in the outcome presentation.
- Konrad's attempted recovery is the means by which he exposes his restored systems to Samuel's reach.
- Samuel's attack on what the group regards as sacred coheres with the existing bloodline, succession, and identity campaign.

### Proposed solutions

- Partitioned knowledge as the exact model of Konrad's denial.
- Immediate shutdown as Konrad's first required action.
- The sacred initiative as the breeding, succession, and certification system rather than a narrower program.
- The Resistance, Sylvan/Luminai, Zilbin, and the inner circle each owning a separate evidentiary function.

### Rejected for this pass

- Creating RW-11.
- Reopening accepted RW ending decisions.
- Making the Resistance a subordinate team controlled by Sylvan.
- Treating an overtly religious name or identity as established.
- Publicly revealing the full mechanism or evidence chain before it is accepted.

## Workshop routing

### BA-02

Use the historical false-autonomy bargain as the model for Samuel's later lie about Sylvan. The lie should combine a true technical difference, a false inference, and evidence selected so Konrad reaches the desired conclusion himself.

### BA-07

Map the evidence by function:

- discovery;
- preservation;
- authentication;
- attribution;
- safe presentation;
- decision authority;
- public interpretation.

No one character should perform all seven functions.

### BA-09

Define the sacred initiative, what is still operating, what Konrad knows before the presentation, and what verifiable fact makes continued operation indefensible.

### BA-10

Require the decisive reveal to reinterpret at least three earlier facts and produce a present action. If events can be reordered without weakening the reveal, the causal sequence is not finished.

## Active gate

BA-01 remains first. The new material does not answer what exact observable condition constitutes taking Sylvan before his forty-second birthday.
