# Counterfeit Sacred Authority Reassessment

## Result

The religious layer strengthens the endgame and resolves the broad motivation behind Konrad and Aiden's prophecy without requiring a borrowed real-world religion. Their attack on Sylvan now emerges from a sincere sacred identity whose trusted interpretive channel Samuel has captured.

The distinction is essential: the story can prove that Samuel counterfeited authority, altered provenance, selected signs, revised instructions, and designated rivals. It cannot prove or disprove metaphysical reality, and it does not treat belief itself as evidence of irrationality or evil.

## Causal addition

`Great War defeat -> sacred recovery mission -> Samuel captures interpretation -> selected signs confirm divine placement -> Sylvan designated as final threat -> Konrad and Aiden attack -> Sylvan refuses the role -> Samuel revises the sacred explanation -> Resistance traces the revisions -> counterfeit authority exposed -> Konrad breaks the captured channel`

## Public boundary

The real-world comparison that inspired development remains outside compiled story presentation. Public material may describe an original holy order, divine purpose, promised reward, captured interpretation, and counterfeit authority. It should not advertise a direct analogy to a named modern or historical faith.

## Remaining author gates

- The order's name and constructive origin.
- The promised reward and observable culmination.
- The trusted channel Samuel captures.
- The original prophecy that designates Sylvan.
- The believer who separates faith from obedience.
- The exact provenance evidence that proves Samuel's authorship.
