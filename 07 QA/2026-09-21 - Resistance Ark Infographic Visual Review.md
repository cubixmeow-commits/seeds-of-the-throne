# Resistance Ark Infographic Visual Review

## Verdict

Approved as the canonical explanatory infographic for the Resistance's established black-and-orange ark identity. It may be used as a visual reference for palette, ark symbolism, information hierarchy, and preservation tone. It does not establish literal architecture or a complete membership taxonomy.
