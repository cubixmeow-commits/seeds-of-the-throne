# Altered Reality Endgame Reassessment

## Result

The new layer strengthens the endgame because Samuel's current attempt against Sylvan now performs three functions at once: immediate attack, reconstruction of the postwar capture, and proof that other leaders were recruited through the same false autonomy.

It does not reopen the accepted ending. It supplies the causal route from Konrad and Aiden targeting Sylvan to Konrad's established decision to join Sylvan's exposure plan.

## Established additions

- Aiden Fitzgerald is Konrad's biological son and a younger public leader.
- Konrad and Aiden have remained inside a postwar altered reality maintained through story functionality, reactivated systems, controlled interpretation, and their own ideological commitments.
- Samuel tries to repeat the same trap against Sylvan.
- Konrad and Aiden target Sylvan before the repetition is exposed.
- The live trap becomes evidence of Samuel's old access and his use of the captured group to recruit other leaders.

## Structural recommendation

Use the endgame as a nested reveal:

`Konrad and Aiden attack Sylvan -> Sylvan refuses the assigned role -> Samuel revises the story -> the Resistance connects the live pattern to postwar reactivation -> false autonomy becomes visible -> other leaders recognize their versions of the trap -> Konrad changes sides -> Samuel's repetition fails`

The story remains a puzzle because the reader receives correct fragments in the wrong grouping. The reveal succeeds when one present action explains the Great War aftermath, the sacred-program capture, the hidden hierarchy, the recruitment of other leaders, and Samuel's offer to Sylvan.

## Remaining author gates

- The prophecy and rival identity assigned to Sylvan.
- The precise consent or activation Samuel needs.
- Aiden's first attack and later independent decision.
- The live contradiction that allows the inner circle to recognize the repetition.
- The Resistance fragment that authenticates the historical connection.
- The first additional leader or group whose altered reality becomes visible.
