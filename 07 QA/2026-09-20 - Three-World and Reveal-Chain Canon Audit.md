# Three-World and Reveal-Chain Canon Audit

## Verdict

**Compatible with targeted supersession and unresolved mechanical dependencies.** The developments strengthen the existing colonization, containment, evidence, and endgame architecture. No accepted terminal outcome needs to be reopened.

## Reinforced canon

- Samuel never controls the governing colonization process; he converts bounded local power into a false sovereignty claim.
- The same environment cultivates future leaders and exposes what dangerous people do when given consequential power.
- Planet Three's mature institutions, archives, synthetics, off-world command structure, and adaptive criminals make it a credible human–Luminai proving ground.
- Samuel captures structures through access, interpretation, dependency, succession, and compartmentalization rather than straightforward conquest.
- Sylvan, the Luminai, and the Resistance win through connected evidence and accountable action rather than domination.
- Konrad's commitment to expose Samuel remains locked and does not become redemption or subordination to Sylvan.
- Protected evidence, public proof, individual accountability, and private descendant records must remain distinct.

## Redundancies to consolidate

- The repeated statements that Samuel mistakes local access for control should be centralized in the Planet Three/process boundary and referenced elsewhere.
- “George is the attack; Samuel Jr. is the fallback” is now too coarse. The cleaner distinction is: George supplies direct operational action on Planet Three; Samuel Jr. supplies testimony, institutional evidence, and the attempted authorship transfer.
- The outcome presentation, public online story, protected evidence presentation, and mandatory participant presentations currently overlap without a hierarchy. They should become layers of one presentation system rather than four separate climaxes.
- Konrad's disclosure choice appears in Resistance, Book One architecture, and endgame notes. The choice should remain locked once, while those notes develop different mechanics: evidence, first action, audience, and consequence.

## Unresolved dependencies

1. **World identity:** confirm whether Samuel and Konrad's “previous colonization planet” is Planet Two and whether anyone else acts across both locations.
2. **Empire taxonomy:** distinguish parent-civilization empires, Planet Three surface empires, Houses, cities, and process jurisdictions.
3. **Authority:** identify who requires presentations and what enforcement, appeal, privacy, and evidentiary rules govern them.
4. **Chronology:** place Samuel Jr.'s birth, education, company rise, preparation, doubt, defection, and presentation against the Great War, containment century, and Sylvan's final years.
5. **Evidence:** define what each actor uniquely supplies and what turns allegation into authenticated attribution.
6. **Causality:** establish why Samuel's plan still needs Samuel Jr.'s testimony if the process possesses a governing record.
7. **Disclosure:** decide the order in which Samuel Jr., Konrad, affected families, other empires, and formal evaluators learn and act.
8. **Safety:** explain why the real leaders allow genuine local harm and empire capture without making the proving ground negligent or predetermined.
9. **Motivation:** preserve self-interest, culpability, and ideology in Samuel Jr. and Konrad's turns; neither should become suddenly heroic.
10. **Aftermath:** decide what happens to empire structures freed from Samuel and how Sylvan and Orzai's placement follows without making them automatic rulers.

## Canon risk if left unresolved

The new ideas fail only if the story relies on labels instead of mechanics: “empire” without jurisdiction, “mandatory” without authority, “evidence” without custody, “disclosure” without audience or cost, or “defection” without a prior belief state. The Reveal Chain Mechanics Workshop now holds those author gates.
