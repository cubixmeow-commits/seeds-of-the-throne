# Shared Leadership and Synthetic Embodiment Reveal Audit

## Controlling revision

The author establishes Samuel and Konrad as leaders of one criminal project; their apparent rivalry is an implemented cover that Samuel intends to turn into a frame against Konrad. Both pursue synthetic embodiment through transfer of their brains. Their reproductive experiments follow a false racist analogy about human ancestry and synthetic union. The entire pre-chapter development now centers progressive disclosure of this hidden project. Source: [[01 Sessions/Daily/2026-09-27 - Shared Leadership Synthetic Immortality and Layered Reveals]].

## Continuity findings
