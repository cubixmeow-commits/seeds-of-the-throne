# Existing Prophecy Endgame Reassessment

## Result

The new layer is compatible with the accepted endgame once one wording conflict is corrected: Samuel does not create, author, or own the prophecy. It already belongs to a wider original religion. His operational fraud is to identify Sylvan as the enemy it describes and manufacture confirmation inside the altered reality.

## Preserved facts

- Konrad and Aiden sincerely believe they lead a divinely placed holy order.
- Samuel uses story functionality and bounded access rather than mind control or authority over the governing process.
- Konrad, Aiden, and other leaders retain responsibility for their choices.
- Sylvan remains separate from Konrad's hierarchy.
- The Resistance owns the historical evidence chain.
- Exposure proves Samuel's manipulation without proving or disproving faith.

## Corrected drift

- “Samuel authored the sacred instructions” becomes “Samuel authored the target assignment and manufactured its apparent confirmation.”
- “Samuel counterfeits the authority behind the prophecy” becomes “Samuel counterfeits authority to apply the existing prophecy.”
- The exact prophecy is now an explicit development gate rather than an implied Samuel creation.

## Remaining risks

- The prophecy must not reproduce a real religion's text, enemy, order, or history.
- Samuel's influence must be mechanically traceable rather than supernatural.
- Each attacking leader needs knowledge, an off-ramp, a choice, and a consequence.
- The break sequence must distinguish doctrinal uncertainty from provable operational fraud.
- Approved diagrams must remain explanatory interpretations rather than literal interfaces or architecture.

## Routing

Develop the remaining questions in EG-09 through EG-12 before writing the attack and reveal as a final scene sequence.
