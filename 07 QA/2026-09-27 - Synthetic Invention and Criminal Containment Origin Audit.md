# Synthetic Invention and Criminal Containment Origin Audit

## Controlling author direction

Human-indistinguishable biological synthetics create a powerful new target. A generator most people mistake for a moon powers billions through hidden satellites. Resource-rich criminals try to merge with or appropriate synthetic capability and imagine themselves gods; laws respond; offenders are lured into colonization believing they can prove their claims. Fascist ideology plus technological sophistication provides the story's origin pressure. See [[01 Sessions/Daily/2026-09-27 - Synthetic Invention and Origin of Criminal Containment]].

## Narrative advantage

At first, the audience can see criminals who claim they built the colony, were constrained by a new law, or were challenged to prove themselves. Later contracts, recruitment materials, concealed failures, actual harm and access-revocation records can show what prompted the law, what they attempted against synthetic people, how the bounded offer differed from the sovereignty they imagined, and which administrators were responsible for the risks they permitted. That layered history gives the synthetic reveal a political and moral consequence rather than making it a surprise technical fact. The corporate-builder variant is a working option, not an accepted founder roster.
