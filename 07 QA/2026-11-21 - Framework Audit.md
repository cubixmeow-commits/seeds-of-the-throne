## 1. What the framework fixes

Each element below is established in the cited note. Where a compiled note carries a weaker status than "established" for part of an element, that part is named.

1. **Purpose of the colonization.** The controlled multi-planet environment exists to trap, contain, understand and expose the biggest problems in humanity, while building the new Luminai and training leaders. Source: `03 Context/CURRENT.md`; `07 QA/Decisions.md` (2026-09-28, primary purpose).
2. **The process succeeds overall.** Planet Two's criminal groups lose a real Great War; that loss is not a failure of the Planet Two process or of the larger program, and Planet Three is not an emergency repair of a failed colony. Source: `07 QA/Decisions.md` (2026-09-28, overall process success).

Source: `07 QA/Decisions.md` (2026-09-28, two planets comprise the first stage); `02 Story/Systems/Two-Planet Colonization Process - Working Plan.md` (status: working).

The defeated empire does not transfer into Planet Three as an institution. Source: `07 QA/Decisions.md` (2026-09-24 and 2026-09-28 entries); `03 Context/CURRENT.md`.

7. **Participant unawareness.** Participants must not know they are in a controlled environment, or their behaviour stops being evidence. A controller who quietly rigs opportunities or interpretations (Samuel's method) contaminates the experiment itself, not only its victims. Source: same notes as item 6.
