A science-fiction story in development

Seeds of the Throne

Humanity built a planet that can create resources, train people, and contain its worst criminals. Then it used that world to develop a new partnership between the human mind and advanced AI.

Confirmed story information, developing ideas, and unanswered questions are labeled separately.

The final battle is not about taking the planet. It is about making everyone see who has been contained all along.

Infrastructure held beneath ordinary life.

01 / Ordinary life

A real life inside a constructed world.

Its institutions, daily work, relationships, and apparent technology must feel like a civilization before participants understand its hidden architecture. Participants retain real agency, so the resulting history can diverge from its source.

02 / Concealed systems

A process for resources, training, and containment.

Humanity built a process that could create sustainable resources, support a large population, train people for greater responsibility, and contain dangerous criminals. Inside that environment, humanity developed the Luminai.

A Luminai is an advanced AI extension of one human mind. The human and the Luminai learn how to work together over many years. Sylvan is the first person to use a new, more deeply integrated version of the Luminai in a real civilization.

The colonization environment can direct people toward numbers, records, places, and historical patterns. Each person must decide which patterns matter and what they actually prove. Evidence is not the same thing as an explanation.

Diagrams of rooms and machinery on this site are interpretive visualizations, not literal engineering plans. The working vault includes distributed computation, sensing, responsive environments, and moon-based oversight as research-facing language rather than settled public fact.

03 / Competing methods

People before positions.

Sylvan must prove that this new bond works by surviving Samuel Franklin, understanding how he controls people, and helping the process contain him more effectively.
