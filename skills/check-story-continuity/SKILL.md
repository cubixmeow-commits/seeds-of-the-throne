# Check Story Continuity

## Scope the audit

Read `START HERE.md`, `03 Context/CURRENT.md`, `03 Context/RULES.md`, `07 QA/Decisions.md`, and `07 QA/Contradictions.md`. Read only the compiled notes, sessions, and research directly implicated by the material under review.

Establish authority in this order:

1. the author's latest explicit decision;
2. recorded decisions;
3. current context;
4. compiled story notes with status labels;
5. dated development sessions;
6. advisory research.

Later text does not automatically outrank an earlier explicit decision.

## Run the audit

Use `references/audit-matrix.md`. Check facts and dependencies, not only repeated names.

For each issue, cite the relevant files and classify it as:

- **Contradiction:** two claims cannot both be true.
- **Tension:** both may be true but need explanation.
- **Gap:** a required causal or informational link is absent.
- **Drift:** terminology, status, or framing changed inconsistently.
- **Open question:** the vault intentionally leaves the matter unresolved.

Assign impact as blocking, significant, or minor. Explain why it matters to plot, character agency, evidence, or world rules.

## Report before changing

Return a compact findings list with evidence and safe options. Do not choose among creative alternatives unless the author has already decided.

When asked to update the vault, record new contradictions in `07 QA/Contradictions.md`, decisions in `07 QA/Decisions.md`, and unresolved structural questions in `07 QA/Questions.md`. Preserve the original session history.

## Guardrails

- Do not flatten all files into one supposedly clean truth.
- Do not promote the newest speculation over established material.
- Do not resolve a contradiction simply by deleting one side.
- Do not treat absence of detail as contradiction.
- Do not confuse a character's false belief with an authorial continuity error.
