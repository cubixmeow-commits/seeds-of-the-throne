# Samuel two-paths choice diagram

## Purpose

Wide, left-to-right endgame diagram showing the mutually exclusive choices available to Samuel Franklin at the brink.

## Final content

- Samuel begins at the left and must let go of one group because he cannot pursue both.
- Path 1 continues terrorizing the contained leaders and lets go of Sylvan.
- Path 2 continues terrorizing Sylvan and lets go of the contained leaders.
- Both paths move horizontally through their consequences toward defeat.
- Closing statement: “Same obsession. Different path. One outcome.”

## Canon boundary

The diagram explains Samuel's choice and the consequences of his obsession. Icons, arrows, architecture, interface treatment, and exact environmental details are interpretive.
