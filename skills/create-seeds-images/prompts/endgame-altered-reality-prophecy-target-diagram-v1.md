# Altered reality prophecy-target diagram

## Purpose

Standalone icon-based endgame diagram showing Konrad Fitzgerald, Aiden Fitzgerald, and other leaders trapped inside Samuel's altered reality while Sylvan remains outside their hierarchy.

## Final content

- Samuel outside and above the altered-reality enclosure.
- Konrad, Aiden, other group leaders, and their groups inside.
- Samuel secretly tells them who to target.
- Sylvan is identified as the prophecied villain and targeted from inside the enclosure.
- Bottom lines: “Samuel names the target. The leaders attack Sylvan.” and “He uses the existing religious prophecy to control them.”

## Canon boundary

The prophecy predates Samuel and was not created by Konrad's group. Samuel's fraud is the identification and application of Sylvan as its enemy. Icons, enclosure, arrows, architecture, interface treatment, and exact staging are interpretive.
