# Orbital Generator and Synthetic Society Diagram v1

## Generation and revision

Created with OpenAI GPT Image through ChatGPT as a 16:9 speculative-science infographic. Initial prompt requested an Earth-like colonization planet, one apparently natural moon with a cutaway showing its orbital solar generator, hidden satellite relays, a human-looking biological synthetic society, and an enlarged nanocell with biological wrapper and hidden technological core. The requested reading order was generator → relays → planetary distribution → wrapped nanocells. Labels in the illustration identify the generator, relays, synthetics, and nanocell.

The author then specifically requested **lightning between components to represent wireless energy**. The image editor used the initial generated image as reference and was instructed to preserve the composition and labels while replacing smooth energy beams with branching gold and blue lightning between the moon and satellites, between relays, and down to planetary distribution points; a small symbolic continuation runs through the nanocell inset. The result is the version stored here. The unmodified initial image remains outside the Git repository in the generation history and is not the approved story asset.

## Interpretation boundary

Lightning is diagram language for **wireless energy**, not literal luminous electrical discharge visible to inhabitants. Moon cutaway, precise satellite shapes, exposed cores, planet geography and city layout are not fixed canon. No participant-accessible device can reveal the nanocell cores. The true coupling into each synthetic, energy budget, synthetic demographics by planet, and the specific civic role of synthetics remain open. The image's human-looking characters are generic inhabitants, not approved references for named story characters.
