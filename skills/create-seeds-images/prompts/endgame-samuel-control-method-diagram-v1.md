# Samuel control method diagram

## Purpose

Standalone icon-based endgame diagram explaining how Samuel successfully took control of Konrad Fitzgerald, Aiden Fitzgerald, other leaders, and their groups.

## Final content

- Samuel Franklin above the captured hierarchy in red.
- A counterfeit sacred-authority layer between Samuel and the leaders.
- Control operations: control the story, promise divine reward, designate the enemy.
- Konrad, Aiden, other group leaders, and their groups below.
- Closing statement: “They believe they lead. Samuel chooses the mission.”

## Canon boundary

The diagram explains the accepted control relationship. Icons, arrows, architecture, interface treatment, and exact spatial hierarchy are interpretive. “Counterfeit sacred authority” means Samuel fraudulently captures interpretation and target assignment; it does not mean he wrote the existing prophecy.
