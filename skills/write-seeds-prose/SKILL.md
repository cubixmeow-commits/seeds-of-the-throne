# Write Seeds Prose

## Identify the register

Determine whether the request is for manuscript prose, an exploratory scene, development prose, or public copy. Do not blend registers.

Read `03 Context/RULES.md`, the relevant character and world notes, and any author-written sample identified for the task. If no sample exists, use the current defaults cautiously:

- conversation rather than lecture;
- explanations remain partial, motivated, and limited by the speaker's knowledge;
- first-person present tense is a working mode, not a permanent rule;
- the author's final fiction remains author-controlled;
- do not use em dashes.

Use `references/prose-checklist.md` for drafting and revision.

## Draft

1. Establish whose experience controls the passage and what that person wants now.
2. Limit perception and language to what the viewpoint can know, notice, or misunderstand.
3. Put world information under pressure from action, desire, conflict, or consequence.
4. Let dialogue pursue goals, conceal information, and reveal relationships indirectly.
5. Favor concrete sensory and procedural detail over abstract explanation.
6. Preserve ambiguity when the story depends on manufactured reality or incomplete evidence.

When alternatives are useful, produce no more than three and explain the meaningful difference briefly.

## Revise

Preserve the author's distinctive choices unless asked to transform them. Diagnose before rewriting. Remove generic grandeur, repeated conclusions, false profundity, exposition disguised as dialogue, and phrases that explain an image after it already works.

Mark invented connective material as proposed when drafting from incomplete story notes. Do not smuggle new canon into polished language.

## Guardrails

- Do not imitate a living author's style.
- Do not convert faction prejudice into neutral narration.
- Do not make manipulated premises erase a character's remaining agency or responsibility.
- Do not let Luminai or Daemon become omniscient without established support.
- Do not use finished prose to settle an unresolved story decision.
