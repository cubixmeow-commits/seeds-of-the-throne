# Controlled Human Variance

## Governing principle

Keep voice coherent while allowing expression to change with cognition, attention, and pressure. Useful irregularity is attributable to a mind or documentary register doing something. It is not noise added to evade an AI detector.

Two layers operate together:

- **Voice invariants:** recurring shape of attention, knowledge limits, assumptions, motives, status behavior, and relationship to language.
- **Expression variables:** sentence length and syntax, paragraph shape, diction, descriptive density, compression, repetition, and register at this moment.

Do not hold expression uniform merely to protect voice. Do not change voice merely to create variety.

## Build a variance map

Before a sustained passage, identify only what materially helps:

1. **Local baseline:** the cadence and register appropriate before pressure changes.
2. **Attention weights:** what the controlling mind notices disproportionately, overlooks, or cannot stop returning to.
3. **Expansion points:** details, procedures, memories, or arguments that deserve more space because they matter to the viewpoint.
4. **Compression points:** events or implications delivered briefly because of speed, shock, avoidance, certainty, ignorance, or documentary distance.
5. **Breakpoints:** recognition, fear, absurdity, violence, decision, contradiction, or revelation that can disrupt the established pattern.
6. **Register transitions:** where documentary, technical, intimate, political, vulgar, frightened, or simple language enters, and whose mind or source authorizes it.
7. **Mutating recurrence:** a word, image, or idea that returns changed rather than repeating as a polished motif on schedule.

This map is causal guidance, not output metadata and not a requirement to use every form of variance.

## Drafting behavior

- Establish a pattern long enough for a break to register. Constant difference is another kind of monotony.
- Follow the movement of thought rather than alternating short and long sentences mechanically.
- Let neighboring sentences sometimes share syntax or cadence when the mind is proceeding steadily.
- Break that local order when attention, interpretation, or pressure actually changes.
- Distribute emphasis unevenly. Space on the page should reflect what matters to the controlling consciousness or record, not objective event size.
- Let paragraph boundaries mark changes in action, tactic, attention, evidence, or interpretation rather than a preferred visual size.
- Prefer a precise context-bound word because it is right, not because it is rare.
- Permit imperfect repetition when a mind is circling, insisting, rehearsing, or revising. Mutation matters more than thesaurus replacement.
- Preserve readability. Controlled variance can include calm runs of plain sentences.

## Register-specific use

### Archive reconstruction

Procedural or documentary regularity may be the baseline. A surviving contradiction can change density, syntax, source language, or paragraph shape without turning every record into a dramatic reveal.

### Close psychological prose

Variance follows selective attention, bodily response, memory intrusion, rationalization, and decision. Do not use fragments as a generic proxy for fear.

### Political grotesque and dialogue

Syntax can shift with status tactics: prepared language, irritation, euphemism, evasion, interruption, and loss of control. Preserve distinct speakers rather than giving everyone the same witty irregularity.

### Technical or institutional explanation

Use stable language while a procedure is working. Compress known steps. Expand the failed, disputed, costly, or misunderstood part.

## Diagnostic: statistical equilibrium

Inspect when a passage repeatedly settles into:

- similar middle-length sentences;
- paragraphs with matching size and neat concluding lines;
- evenly spaced emphasis;
- equal explanation for unequal events;
- one register regardless of source, pressure, or viewpoint;
- polished parallel structures that resolve every thought cleanly.

The correction is not automatic shortening or fragmentation. Find the missing change in attention, pressure, or information, then let form respond to it.

## Diagnostic: synthetic variance

Inspect when a passage uses:

- short sentences or fragments at predictable intervals;
- conspicuously rare words without viewpoint justification;
- constant oscillation between long and short sentences;
- random paragraph breaks;
- register changes no character, source, or circumstance authorizes;
- eccentric punctuation or error as evidence of humanity.

Remove the device or give the disruption a real cause. If no cause exists, steadier prose is better.

## Revision questions

1. What is the passage's local cadence before anything changes?
2. Where does attention, pressure, knowledge, or tactic change?
3. Does the expression change there, or remain statistically comfortable?
4. Is any visible irregularity unattached to cognition or circumstance?
5. Does the passage give equal space to events the viewpoint values unequally?
6. Can the prose become less regular without becoming less recognizable as this voice?

## Mechanical boundary

Sentence and paragraph statistics are descriptive evidence only. Do not target a standard deviation, sentence-length ratio, fragment count, or paragraph distribution. A calm procedural passage may be deliberately regular; a high-variance passage may still be mechanically artificial. Human judgment must connect geometry to function.
