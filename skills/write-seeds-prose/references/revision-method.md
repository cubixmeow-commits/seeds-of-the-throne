# Revision Method

## Principle

Revision should solve a named problem without erasing the choices that already make the passage specific. Diagnose before rewriting.

## Revision order

Work from largest to smallest:

1. scene purpose and pressure;
2. causality and character motive;
3. POV and information control;
4. scene order / paragraph order;
5. dialogue and exposition;
6. physical specificity;
7. sentence rhythm and diction;
8. mechanical cleanup.

Do not line-polish a scene whose dramatic engine is wrong.

## Passes

### Structural pass
Ask what changes because this passage exists. Cut or redesign beats that merely repeat setup, lore, emotion, or conclusion.

### Agency pass
Trace each important action to a want, belief, fear, loyalty, or tactical response. Remove actions performed only because the plot needs them.

### Knowledge pass
Mark what the POV can know, infer, misread, and not know. Check that narration does not quietly inherit vault omniscience.

### Exposition pass
For each explanatory sentence, identify why the reader needs it now and why this is the best delivery channel. Compress, delay, dramatize, or cut when necessary.

### Dialogue pass
Identify what each speaker is trying to make happen. Remove lines that only paraphrase narration or teach lore.

### Specificity pass
Replace vague abstractions with a small number of consequential physical, procedural, or institutional details. Do not add decorative detail everywhere.

### Rhythm pass
Vary sentence and paragraph shape according to thought and pressure. Remove accidental rhetorical templates and overused one-line emphasis.

### Anti-AI pass
Use `anti-ai-prose.md` and `scripts/prose_lint.py` if available.

## Preservation rule

Before changing a strong or unusual sentence, state what problem the change solves. If there is no concrete answer, leave it alone.

## Revision intensity

- **Surgical:** localized wording, clarity, rhythm, or continuity repair.
- **Paragraph:** reorganize or rewrite one affected unit.
- **Scene:** rebuild beats while preserving intended event and character outcome.
- **Structural:** change scene purpose/order; normally coordinate with story development first if events would change.

Match the intensity to the author's request. Do not escalate a surgical request into a stylistic rewrite.

## Comparison

When presenting alternatives, vary a consequential craft choice: distance, information timing, tactic, exposition channel, or ending type. Do not offer three synonym swaps as three versions.

## Stop rule

Stop revising when the named problem is solved and further edits become lateral preference changes. Polish is not automatically progress.
