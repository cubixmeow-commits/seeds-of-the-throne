# Evidence standard

## Claim labels

- **Established evidence:** supported by strong, converging sources.
- **Contested:** credible sources disagree or interpretation remains active.
- **Plausible extrapolation:** a reasoned extension beyond direct evidence.
- **Speculation:** useful possibility with weak or indirect support.

## Source order

1. Primary research, official records, standards, and direct institutional documentation
2. Scholarly reviews and reputable technical syntheses
3. High-quality journalism and expert analysis
4. Informal commentary, used only for perspective or leads

For every consequential story implication, include the supporting claim, confidence label, source, limitation, and the fictional mechanism it may inform.
