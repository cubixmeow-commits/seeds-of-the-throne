# 10 Facebook Page: the fan-base project

Created 2026-11-18 at the author's direction. This is a priority project.

## What this is
The working home, inside the vault, for building the Seeds of the Throne Facebook Page into a fan base: an audience gathered around the story and its compatible topics (ancient Egypt, remote viewing, astral projection, and whatever the analysis and the engagement data add), growing toward Page monetization.

## Who does what
- The author owns canon and final voice, as everywhere in this vault. Nothing in this folder is canon unless the author promotes it through the usual gates; everything here is analysis, research, and proposals, labeled the way the vault labels things.
- Muse runs the page day to day under the author's 2026-11-18 direction: plans the post strategy, creates and publishes, reads the engagement, adapts, and brings the author story suggestions drawn from what works, as proposals only.

## Where things live
- Analysis lives here: the component map, the compatible topics atlas, the audience and content system, the research agenda.
- Operations live outside the vault, in the engine's goal workspace (strategy, weekly plan, engine log), because they change daily. The strategy there points back here for the deep material.
- Post text that has run is archived under 05 Public/Published as usual. Generated images never enter the vault.

## The loop
Analyze the story. Map each component to the real-world topics it overlaps. Turn the overlaps into recurring, honest content. Measure what the audience actually does. Feed the results back into the analysis, and bring the author the story suggestions the data earns.
