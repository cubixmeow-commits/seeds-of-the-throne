# Daily Research and Analysis: Operating Structure

Set by Noa, 2026-11-19: structure the Cubix Meow page and the Seeds of the Throne page around a daily research report and analysis in this vault folder. Watch the interactions, compare against similar pages that are working, and adapt the content as effectively as possible.

## The daily loop

1. **Measure.** Every day, pull both pages' numbers: views, unique viewers, watch behavior, engagement, saves, comments, follower movement, per piece and per page.
2. **Compare.** Every day, look at what comparable pages and creators are doing that is working: for Seeds, the remote viewing, astral projection, Egypt, and unexplained documentary space; for Cubix, cat, sacred cat, mythology, and storybook pages. Named pieces and visible numbers, never vague trends.
3. **Analyze.** Put our formats, hooks, lengths, and timings next to theirs. Say plainly what is working for us, what is not, and what the peers prove is possible.
4. **Adapt.** Issue a dated adaptation brief to each page's engine, ranked and production-ready. The daily slate runs read the newest brief before producing, so research changes tomorrow's posts, not just a report.
5. **Produce and measure again.** The slate runs publish under each page's standing authorization and strategy. The next day's report measures what the adaptations did.

## Where things live

- **Daily reports:** this folder, `Daily Research/Daily Research Report - YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Numbers first, peer findings with sources, the comparison, and the adaptations issued that day.
- **Adaptation briefs:** each engine's own research folder in its goal workspace, `Facebook Effectiveness Adaptations - YYYY-MM-DD.md`. The newest brief governs craft and format; each page's strategy.md governs content, canon or characters, and cadence.
- **Strategy:** each engine's `hidden_files/strategy.md`, rewritten by the Sunday weekly reviews, which now read the whole week's daily reports before rewriting.
- **Production tools:** `~/workspace/agent-mow-toolkit/fb-tools/` (reelcheck, bank-status).

## The layers

- **Daily (about 12:40 PT):** the research and analysis run. Feeds both evening slates (Cubix about 17:13, Seeds about 19:38) and sends Noa a short summary.
- **Daily slates:** production, under the briefs.
- **Sunday weekly reviews:** one per page. They score the week, fold the daily reports into strategy rewrites, and bring Noa new compatible topics and, for Seeds, labeled story suggestions.

## Standing rules

- Canon and characters never move on engagement data alone. Adaptations govern craft and format; Noa governs content.
- Originality is a revenue rule on both pages, and doubly on monetized Cubix Meow: no templated, low-variation output, and a skipped slot beats a filler piece.
- Claims in public copy stay verified or they do not run. Peer research informs format, never facts.
