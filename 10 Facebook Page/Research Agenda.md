# Research Agenda: Seeds of the Throne Facebook Page Project

Created 2026-11-18 for the Facebook Page fan-base project.
Status of this document: analysis and plan, not canon. Research is advisory, never canon, everywhere in this project.
Threads are ordered by expected value: what the research unlocks, how soon the page needs it, and how much of the bench it deepens.
Story questions that only the author can decide are listed separately at the end. They are not answered here, and research should not be steered to pre-answer them.

---

## Thread 1: Verify the four web anchors to project standard

What to find: primary or standard scholarly sourcing for the four needs-verification anchors in the Atlas.
- The Dream Stele: a published translation of the stele text (Lichtheim's Ancient Egyptian Literature is the standard shelf copy) and a museum or university page fixing its date and placement.
- The LaBerge verification: the 1981 paper itself (Perceptual and Motor Skills 52, 727 to 732) and a reliable account of Hearne and Worsley, April 1975.
- Artemidorus: the Harris-McCoy Oxford edition (2012) or the Hammond translation (2020) for the five-book structure, the two kinds of dreams, and the ninety-five practice cases in Book 5.
- The PEAR lab: the lab's own closure statement (February 2007) and one mainstream account of the three-lab replication result.
Where to look: publisher and university pages, the Psi Encyclopedia for orientation, then the books themselves. The two Vinted Egypt books due around 2026-11-20 to 2026-12-01 may cover part of the Egypt side; the Book of the Dead edition needs its translator identified from its copyright page when it arrives (the author has already been asked for that one fact).
What it unlocks: Atlas pieces ranked 2 and 4 in the first ten, plus the Artemidorus question-post bank and the PEAR entry in Two Verdicts. Until this thread lands, those anchors stay labeled needs-verification and off the page.

## Thread 2: The Report No. 006 research pack (Tibetan dream yoga)

What to find: a deep file on milam to the standard of the earlier Report packs: the Six Dharmas of Naropa in sourced translation, the Bardo Thodol's actual date and treasure-text status stated cleanly, phowa's one-way nature, and delok cases with named documenters and dates.
Where to look: the RV AP Origins brief as the spine; published translations of the Six Dharmas literature; ethnographic sources for delok accounts into the twentieth century.
What it unlocks: Report No. 006 (already announced in No. 005's Tomorrow), a Written in Stone and From the Record crossover set for the Page, and the natural pairing with the LaBerge material from Thread 1. Three lanes fed by one pack is the highest leverage ratio available right now.

What to find: documented RV cases with checkable records beyond the founding four topics, each written up with the project's split: what checked, what did not, who graded it.

## Thread 6: The Two Books reading file

What to find: as the author and Brut read Third Eye Spies and Journeys Out of the Body, build a page-level citation file: each usable concept, its exact source, and the story question it pairs with. The loosh correction is the model entry: famous claim, checked against the actual book, found fourteen years off.

What it unlocks: Two Books, One Question as a Page series with receipts, and protection against the most common failure in this content space, repeating what the internet says the books say.

## Thread 7: Swedenborg and Kant to primary standard

What to find: Kant's 1766 Dreams of a Spirit-Seer in a citable translation, the Gothenburg fire accounts in their earliest forms, and a clean statement of the 19 vs 29 July date dispute and why it cannot currently be settled.
Where to look: Kant scholarship and the Swedenborg literature, skeptical and sympathetic both, per the two-verdicts habit.
What it unlocks: first-ten piece 10, and the template for every investigated-vision piece after it. Ranked below the case bench only because one piece, however good, unlocks less than a series supply line.

## Thread 8: The honest absences, made airtight

What to find: before the absences run as content, confirm them once more at source level: the Zaqiqu dream omen series and the Mesopotamian record for any soul-flight system; the ethnographic grounding for the Dreaming and hanbleceya contrasts, stated respectfully and without borrowing either tradition's material as decoration.
Where to look: Assyriology references for the omen series; Stanner for the Dreaming; the brief's own sourcing as the checklist.
What it unlocks: the credibility pieces. A page that publishes what is not there, correctly, earns belief for everything else it posts. These are also natural question posts: what would count as finding it?

## Thread 9: Community listening, logged

What to find: a weekly note of what the RV, AP, Egypt, and unexplained-phenomena rooms are actually asking and arguing about, gathered from public posts and the Report's Grok scans, with trend separated from fact at capture time.
Where to look: the communities the author already follows and participates in; the scan material the Report produces anyway.
What it unlocks: the Hot File's supply, question posts that meet real curiosity instead of guessed curiosity, and the earliest possible signal for new compatible topics, which the strategy's learning loop explicitly wants fed back into the Atlas.

## Thread 10: Artemidorus as a casebook

What to find: work through Book 5's ninety-five dreams and outcomes (in the Harris-McCoy or Hammond translation, once Thread 1 lands) and select the twelve strongest as question-post material, each with its recorded outcome held back for the comments reveal.
Where to look: the translation; Artemidorus scholarship for the enhypnion and oneiros distinction in his own terms.
What it unlocks: a season of The Open File with a two-thousand-year-old casebook, and a recurring audience game that teaches interpretation discipline by playing it: here is the dream, here is what the interpreter said, here is what happened.
