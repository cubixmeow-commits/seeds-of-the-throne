## The common thread (Noa, 2026-11-26)

Noa's requirement: the categories must share one thread that links them to Seeds of the Throne. The thread, stated once and used as a selection test:

**Every category is a documented way the mind reaches past the limits of the body and the senses. That reach is the ability the story's world is built on.**

In the story, the universe has built-in functionality and people work it through extended minds; the public record is humanity bumping into that same reach, in laboratories, in temples, in dreams, and at the edge of death, without ever agreeing on what it is. Each lane is one shape of the reach:

- REMOTE VIEWING: perception past distance.
- ASTRAL PROJECTION: perception past the body itself.
- DREAMS: perception while the senses are shut down.
- LUCID DREAMING: waking up inside that shutdown and steering it.
- TELEPATHY: perception past the wall between two minds.
- PRECOGNITION: perception past the present moment.
- THE AFTERLIFE: perception past death, and the reports of those who came back.
- ALTERED STATES: the deliberate loosening of the limits, by method rather than accident.
- ANCIENT EGYPT: the oldest surviving civilization-scale record of the reach, its dream books, soul journeys, and weighing of the heart.
- MYSTICISM: the reach pursued as a discipline and a life, across traditions.

Selection test that follows from the thread: a candidate belongs in the factory only if its record shows the reach. A piece that is merely ancient, merely strange, or merely religious, with no perception past the limits in it, fails the thread test no matter how interesting it is. This is the same overlap rule the engine has run since 2026-11-22, now stated as one sentence the whole category set hangs from.
