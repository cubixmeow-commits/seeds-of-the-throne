# Content Factory - Working Draft 01 (2026-11-25)

Status: PLANNING. Brainstorm draft opened by Noa's direction on 2026-11-25: design the whole factory first, build it step by step in the vault, then Muse runs it daily. Nothing in this folder is a build order yet. Posting on the Seeds Page is paused while this is designed.
Goal, stated plainly: interesting, unique content on the Seeds of the Throne Page every day, produced inside Noa's 1 to 2 hour windows, with Noa as the final gate rather than the labor.
Design stance: a factory, not a tip. Every layer has a defined input, output, and quality bar. Most ideas die inside the factory by design; that is what makes the survivors interesting. Uniqueness is enforced by a registry, not by memory.

## Design principles
1. Sources before formats. The old failure was picking a fact and formatting it. The factory starts from many independent source engines so the well cannot run dry, and no single source can dominate the feed.
2. Research creates the angle. A fact is not content. The content is the angle the research uncovers: the paradox, the near miss, the object that still exists, the question nobody answered.
3. Kill rate is a feature. Target: at least 7 of every 10 researched ideas die at the gate. A day with no surviving idea is a research day, not a posting failure.
4. Every stage leaves a file. The factory's memory is its registries: sources, ideas, angles, scores, published pieces, results. If it is not in a file, the factory does not know it.
5. The loop learns. Sources, templates, and rubric weights that produce winners get more work; the ones that produce the flat 250-view band get starved.

## The layers

### Layer 0: Source engines (where raw material comes from)
Each engine is a standing mining route with its own queue and yield tracking.
- S1 Peer outliers: weekly harvest of peer pages; only 3x-median outliers enter, as angle evidence (what shape worked), never as topics to copy. (v1 harvest exists; Facebook-native peers still blocked, scraper route via the Neo is an open build item.)
- S2 Primary texts shelf: a queue of primary sources mined one at a time for cases: declassified Star Gate files, the DIA CRV manual tradition, SPR Proceedings, and the old books (Muldoon 1929, Dunne 1927, Van Eeden 1913 are the proven vein). Each text yields case notes, not posts.
- S3 The record, on this day: anniversaries and dates in RV/AP history (sessions, publications, deaths, declassifications), checked against a standing calendar built from the record. Time hooks make old cases feel like news.
- S4 Fresh signals: new declassification drops, new archaeology and Egyptology announcements that touch the overlap (the gold tongues piece came from a 6-day-old ministry announcement), new dream and consciousness research papers.
- S5 Community questions: what the RV/AP communities are actually asking (Reddit r/remoteviewing and r/AstralProjection top threads, YouTube comments on peer outliers, recurring beginner questions). Questions are idea seeds with built-in demand.
- S6 The reading pipeline: what Noa and Brut are reading now (Targ, Monroe) and the niche accounts Noa follows (Marie Cachet and the research-spine accounts). Reading-driven ideas carry his real interest, which shows in the copy.
- S7 Cross-domain collisions: one record case crossed with one live modern question (AI, sleep science, simulation talk). The collision is the angle; neither half alone is.
- S8 The vault itself: the story's idea-space (evidence, provenance, control, the layered reality) as context and framing, within the story pause rules: no story content publishes until Noa lifts the pause; the idea-space can still inform record pieces.
- S9 Audience residue: our own comments, saves, and shares read as requests. What viewers saved is what they want more of, in their words where possible.

### Layer 1: Idea capture
Every engine deposits raw ideas into one Idea Bank file, one line each: the seed, its source engine, the date found, and the case or text it points at. No judging at this layer. Dedupe against the Registry (Layer 8) at entry: an idea already published, banked, or killed is marked, not re-entered.

### Layer 2: Research
An idea becomes a Research Card: the verified facts with sources, the honesty note (what skeptics say, what is anecdote-grade), the surviving anchor (object, text, place, and where it is now), and the open question the record cannot answer. A card that cannot produce an anchor or an open question is marked thin and usually dies here.

### Layer 3: Angle development
Each Research Card gets at least three angle attempts, hook-first: the intended first line written out in full for each angle, using the hook library templates. Angles are cheap; production is not. The angle set must include at least one question-form angle (the harvest shows question shapes winning in this niche) and one paradox-form angle (our proven shape).

### Layer 4: The gate
Angles are scored on the v1 rubric (hook 25, one-line paradox 20, checkable record 15, surviving anchor 15, lesser-known factor 15, save/send triggers 10). Produce at 70+, rework once at 55 to 69, kill below 55. Scores and verdicts are logged in the scoring log with the source template named. Noa sees the day's survivors as a short list, not the whole funnel.

### Layer 5: Format assignment
A surviving angle is assigned the format that fits it: breakout-shape Reel (narration budget spent only here and only on gate-passers), question card (zero narration cost), Practice Room episode (technique angles only), or a text/image post. Format follows the angle, never the other way round.

### Layer 6: Production
Scripts in Plain Account, production to the standing rules (New Viewer Standard, karaoke, watermark, likeness and gender gates). Every produced piece logs its predicted score, source template, and cost (credits, minutes) in its bank file.

### Layer 7: Scheduling and publishing
Cadence and timing set by the current experiment (the morning trial is unfinished and resumes when posting resumes). Pieces publish from the bank in score order, best first, so a thin day never ships a weak piece to fill a slot.

### Layer 8: The Registry (uniqueness control)
One file listing everything the Page has ever published or banked: topic, angle, template used, date, result. Checked at Layer 1 (no repeats in) and Layer 4 (no near-duplicate angles). A topic may return only with a genuinely new angle, marked as a return.

### Layer 9: Measurement and learning
48-hour and 14-day reads per piece. Monthly calibration: rubric weights re-fit to our data; source engines ranked by the average result of the pieces they fed (source yield), and mining time is reallocated toward the best engines; hook templates promoted to PROVEN or retired on evidence. The weekly review becomes this layer's report.

## The daily run (when built)
Morning: engines deposit, new Research Cards drafted, angles written, gate scored. Noa gets the survivor list on a break, one tap to approve or kill. Production runs on approved pieces while he works. Evening: pieces banked or published per the schedule, registry updated, costs logged. Weekly: harvest refresh, source yield report, one design improvement proposed to the factory itself.

## Noa's gates (to confirm)
- Survivor list approval: daily, one tap per piece or per batch.
- Canon and story: unchanged, always Noa's, and the story pause binds the factory.
- Factory design changes: proposed in this folder, approved by Noa, never silently edited.
