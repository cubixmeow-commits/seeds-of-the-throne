# Audience and Content System: Seeds of the Throne Facebook Page

Created 2026-11-18 for the Facebook Page fan-base project.
Status of this document: analysis and plan, not canon. It turns the Component Map and the Compatible Topics Atlas into a working content system. The engine's strategy file remains the operational document; this is the reasoning underneath it.
No em dashes. Plain Account spirit throughout.

## The baseline, stated plainly

Twenty-eight days to 2026-11-17, from Facebook insights:
- Followers: 1. Views: 1,423. Unique viewers: 1,054. Engagement including clicks: 10. New followers: 1.
- The four videos: Sanjaya 451 views, Pat Price 329, the Egyptian ba 207, Aristeas 200.
- The text posts: several at 0 views; the best reached 87 views with 2 unique viewers.

Three conclusions the system is built on:
1. Format is the proven variable, topic is not yet proven. Every strong performer is a video. The two strongest are text-led stories carried by video. Nothing in the baseline shows that a topic wins or loses on its own; it shows that a Reel gets distributed and a bare post does not.
2. The proven topics are all record stories. A man reporting a battle he could not see. A viewer whose drawing was checked against a real site. A soul-part that flies only after death, correcting the popular version. A body left lying while the soul traveled. Each one is a documented claim plus a check. That is the story's own epistemology wearing real clothes, and it is not an accident that it performs.
3. The audience does not exist yet in any meaningful size. One follower means the system's first job is discovery, its second is giving the discovered a reason to stay, and conversation is the cheapest signal that either worked. The weekly plan's shape (Reels nightly, story posts four evenings, question posts two evenings, one bridge) already reflects this. What follows names the series inside that shape so the page reads as a place with habits, not a feed of singles.

## The three jobs

- Discovery: a stranger stops, watches, and follows. Reels do this. A discovery piece must stand alone, need no story knowledge, and end on one plain payoff line.
- Loyalty: a follower comes back and starts to care about the story itself. Story posts and the reading series do this. A loyalty piece may assume the pinned post and nothing else.
- Conversation: a viewer answers. Question posts do this. A conversation piece must contain a genuine question the comments can actually answer from the documented case, not a rhetorical one.

Every series below is assigned one primary job. A piece that tries to do all three usually does none.

## The series

### 1. From the Record (discovery, F1)
One documented RV or AP case per Reel, told in the proven shape: time and place first, the claim, then what checked and what did not.
Working title: From the Record.
Source bench: the Atlas entries for the SRI protocol, Price, Hammid, the AIR verdict, PEAR.
Why it exists: it is the format of the 329-view Price video, generalized. The record is the brand.

### 2. Written in Stone (discovery, F2)
One ancient record per Reel: a stele, a spell, a set of carved cures, a dream book. The object first, then what it says, then what it cannot prove.
Working title: Written in Stone.
Source bench: the Dream Stele, the Weighing, the Heart Scarab, the gates, the Name, Artemidorus, the iamata lesson (used once, as the founding example).
Why it exists: Egypt and the ancient record are the page's second proven ground (the ba at 207) and the author's active study. Stone is also the natural image language: records that outlasted their makers.

### 3. Two Verdicts (discovery and conversation, F1/F2 crossover)
One document, two honest conclusions, both printed. The reader sits in the judge's chair.
Working title: Two Verdicts.
Source bench: the 1995 AIR evaluation (Utts and Hyman), Swedenborg and Kant, the PEAR closure, Neferti read forward and backward.
Why it exists: it is the story's epistemology as a format, it invites comments without begging for them, and it is nearly impossible for competitors in this space to copy, because it requires printing the verdict you did not want.

### 4. The Hot File (discovery, F3, opportunistic)
A topic moving in the RV, AP, Egypt, or unexplained communities that day, checked against the record before it is touched. If the claim cannot be verified, the piece is about the checking, or it does not run.
Working title: The Hot File.
Source bench: the Report's Grok scans and the day's podcast topic file, per the strategy.
Why it exists: it borrows other rooms' momentum. The strategy already allows an F3 topic to displace any slot; this names the slot it displaces into.

### 5. A Century Apart (loyalty, S1/S3)
The Process Mirror as a serial: Sylvan, Aiden, George, Konrad, Samuel, one figure or one turn of the mirror per post, each readable alone, building the story's central shape over weeks.
Working title: A Century Apart.
Source bench: the Component Map entries 4 to 11, bounded by the public-safe lines drawn there.
Why it exists: the launch-night Mirror post is the first data point; the mirror is the story's most distinctive asset and the one no other page can offer. Serial form turns a single post into a reason to follow.

### 6. The World File (loyalty, S2)
One story element per post, explained plainly: the Luminai, recurrence, the sealed record, the two groups by method, the layers.
Working title: The World File.
Source bench: the Component Map, Atlas-bounded.
Why it exists: followers acquired on Egypt and RV need a graded path into the world. One element at a time, no glossary dumps, each post ending at the element's human stake.

### 7. Two Books, One Question (loyalty, bridge-adjacent)
The existing reading series, brought to the Page in adapted form: one concept from Targ or Monroe, stated accurately with its source, the story's version set beside it as a question, judgment handed to the reader.
Working title: kept from the X series, Two Books, One Question.
Source bench: the Overlap Map, including the loosh correction and the Locale I and II material.
Why it exists: the authors are reading both books now; the series documents a real reading life, which is the kind of loyalty content that cannot be faked. Rules from the overlap map hold: books are real and cited, story material is labeled fiction, no claim that the story explains the books. The Viewing Room stays delayed; this series teaches and explores, it runs no experiments on the audience.

### 8. The Question Post (conversation, Tue/Thu slot)
One documented case in three or four plain sentences, ending in a genuine question.
Working title: none needed; the format is the identity. If a label helps the log, use The Open File.
Source bench: Etta Smith first, then the Artemidorus casebook (ninety-five dreams with outcomes), the delok, the honest absences.
Why it exists: comments are the cheapest follower signal there is, per the strategy, and a case with a real human stake earns them honestly.

### 9. From the Workshop (loyalty, S4)
An honest build note: a problem the book is working on, stated as a problem, with what is settled and what is open clearly separated.
Working title: From the Workshop.
Why it exists: the pinned post already frames the page as a development journal. Workshop posts are where the audience graduates from topic followers to story stakeholders, and where the Sunday story suggestions get their context.

### The Saturday bridge (loyalty, one per week, no more)
One post pointing to the week's Origins Unknown Report or a Seeds site page. The strategy caps this at one; the cap is the point. The page is a fan base, not a billboard.

## The role of the story itself

The story is the loyalty core and the frame, not the door.
- The door is the record: Egypt, RV, AP, dream records, prophecy records. Strangers arrive for a stele or a case file.
- The frame is the discipline: every discovery piece is told the story's way, claim first, check second, verdict left open where the record leaves it open. A stranger learns the story's values before they learn its plot.
- The core is the mirror: once a follower has the discipline, A Century Apart and The World File give them the actual fiction, in the order the Component Map's public-safe lines allow.
- Ratio, as a planning guide rather than a quota: in a fourteen-piece week, roughly seven discovery, four loyalty, two conversation, one bridge. The strategy's weekly plan already lands close to this. F4 story-through-overlap Reels count as discovery pieces that smuggle the core through the door, and they are the highest-leverage format the page owns: Price's beacon question, the Dream Stele's throne promise, the heart scarab's plea.
- Hard boundaries, from the strategy and the vault: no proposed religious layer specifics, no chips or implants (rejected), no real-world crime specifics, Samuel's methods at mechanism level only, nothing repeated or repackaged from what has run.

## Experiment discipline

The author has given creative freedom on style (2026-11-18): the engraving look is a starting point, not a house style. The system uses that freedom under the strategy's own rule: vary one thing at a time where possible, log the style of every piece in the engine log, and let the Sunday numbers keep or kill each direction. Candidate first experiments, in order:
1. Reel length: the proven pieces are short; test one 20 to 30 second record piece against the 10 second pattern.
2. Opening frame: object-first (the stone, the file, the drawing) against person-first.
3. The payoff line spoken and captioned against caption-only.
Profile picture and banner refresh waits for a few weeks of data, per the author's condition.

## What success looks like, in order

1. Views per Reel holding at or above the 200 to 450 baseline band on new topics, which would show the format travels without the founding four topics.
2. First follows attributable to a named series, which would show the page, not a single video, is becoming the attraction.
3. Comments on question posts from people who are not the author, which is the first loyalty signal money cannot buy.
4. A story post (A Century Apart or The World File) outperforming a discovery Reel once. That is the day the core starts pulling its own audience, and the ratio above should be revisited when it happens.
