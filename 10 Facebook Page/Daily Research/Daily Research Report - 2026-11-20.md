Fresh supplement, 2026-11-20 search:
- Meta's hook guidance (Dec 2025, summarized at vaybestudio.com/en/blog/reels-hook-first-3-seconds): good Reels establish the hook in the first few seconds, across text, image, and sound at once; average Reels watch time 8.5 seconds (Metricool, Jan to Feb 2026, 24M posts), so most viewers never reach the end of a 20 to 25 second Reel. Implication for both Pages: the payoff fact cannot sit in the final beat only; the hook must carry a real piece of the story.
- Emplifi 2026 study (10,110 Facebook Reels from 704 brands, summarized by Social Media Today 2026-03-22, via exponenta.marketing): a voice speaking in the first 3 seconds raised retention at 10 seconds by roughly 25% versus music only; a person on screen in the first 3 seconds raised it by roughly 10%. Third-party study, treated as directional, not law. It directly supports moving our narration to second zero.
- Practitioner synthesis (lilachbullock.com, updated Nov 2026): Facebook can only recommend a Reel widely if it recognizes the audio; the advice is to layer voiceover under an identifiable library track rather than relying on an unrecognizable uploaded bed. Flagged for the Sunday review: our Seeds NVS Reels use a quiet synthesized drone under narration. Test one Reel with a Sound Collection bed under the same narration style and compare distribution.
