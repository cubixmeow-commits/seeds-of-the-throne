What is working for us:
- Seeds Reels, one documented fact each, are a repeatable 200 to 450 view format on a 2-follower page. Every Reel in the window sits between 200 and 451 views; every non-Reel sits between 0 and 18. The Egypt dream-record strand is two for two at speed: Epidaurus 295, Dream Stele 265 and still in its first hours, and Dream Stele produced the page's second follower.
- The Thursday 09:07 PT slot outperformed the Wednesday evening slot on velocity for both pages on the same morning: Dream Stele at roughly 62 views per hour against Epidaurus at roughly 20, and The Offering Is Late earning the first reactions Cubix has recorded while the previous evening's launch Reel earned none on more views. One morning is not a pattern, but it lines up with Buffer's Thursday 9 a.m. finding, so the slot earns a formal test.
- Cubix's specific-predicament format works where the general introduction did not: "the offering is late" (one beat, one joke) got 3 engagements on 142 views; "meet the cats" (a premise, no predicament) got 0 on 271.

What is not working:
- Photo and text posts do not distribute on either page, at any hour tried: Seeds 1, 6, 18, and 0 views; Cubix's Nov 6 photo essays 23 to 26 views with zero reactions. The Seeds question post, the format meant to start conversations, reached 1 viewer. A question nobody is shown cannot be answered.
- Engagement depth is zero everywhere it matters most: no saves, no shares, and no comments on any piece on either page. Views are arriving; nothing yet gives a viewer a reason to stay, return, or bring someone.

What the peers prove is possible:
- Numbered, serial structure is the common shape of the working peers: Gaia's numbered parts, the cat story genre's Part 1 and Part 2 arcs, Ted the Ragdoll's numbered facts. Our strands are only partly numbered today.
- Participatory formats exist in the Seeds space (Astral Realms posts a target for its audience), but the Viewing Room delay means Seeds watches that lane instead of entering it.
- Scale in these niches is real but community-held: the largest comparable groups run 100,000 to 299,000 members, while Pages like ours grow one Reel at a time.

Platform changes that affect either page:
- Generative retrieval now labels and surfaces new posts before engagement exists (about half of Reels views), so a clear subject stated early in the caption and on screen is a distribution input.
- Originality enforcement remains the revenue rule: unoriginal or low-variation content loses recommendation and, on monetized Cubix, income. Cubix publishing three Reels a day in one house style is the exposure.
- Facebook Content Monetization pays on Reels of 10 seconds or more; views under 5 seconds do not qualify. Length floors in the briefs come from this.
