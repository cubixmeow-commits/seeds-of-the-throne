# Compatible Topics Atlas: Seeds of the Throne

Created 2026-11-18 for the Facebook Page fan-base project.
Status of this document: analysis, not canon. It maps each story component to the real-world topics it honestly overlaps, with the documented anchor material for each topic.
Every anchor is marked one of two ways:
- verified-from-vault: the anchor and its sourcing already live in project material (the vault's research notes, the goal-file research briefs, or the published Report archive). The location is named.
- needs-verification: found on the web during this analysis (2026-11-18) and consistent across the sources seen, but not yet checked to the project's standard. It does not go on the page until it is verified.
Used means the topic has already run on the Seeds Facebook Page or in a published Report. Unused means it has not. Unused is where the new value is.
The project's avoid list binds everything here: no Book of the Dead as an astral projection manual, no claim that the CIA proved remote viewing, no Gateway Process as proof that consciousness leaves the body, no Dreamtime as astral travel, no Swedenborg fire as verified fact, no Hermetica as primeval Egyptian wisdom. The full avoid list lives in the RV AP Origins research brief.

## Component: the uneditable record and provenance

This is the story's center, and it has the deepest topic bench.

### The SRI protocol itself
- Anchor: at SRI in the 1970s the viewer described and never interpreted; a blind judge matched transcripts to targets afterward; the target stayed hidden so the description could not be steered. Ingo Swann built coordinate remote viewing on exactly this discipline, and Report No. 001 retells it: every claim in the transcript could be traced to the moment it arrived. verified-from-vault (Report No. 001, published 2026-11-10; RV AP Origins brief; research report 52).
- Status: Report used; not yet a Page Reel. The protocol as a standalone Reel is unused on the page.
- Overlap: the Luminai are this protocol built into a mind; Samuel's Daemons are what happens when the judge starts whispering first.

### One document, two verdicts: the 1995 AIR evaluation
- Anchor: in 1995 the American Institutes for Research evaluated the whole Star Gate record. Statistician Jessica Utts concluded the laboratory statistics held up; psychologist Ray Hyman concluded anomalous cognition was still unproven; both agreed the operational record showed no case where viewing guided an intelligence operation. The program was terminated and declassified the same year, after about twenty years and roughly $20M. verified-from-vault (RV AP Origins brief; Report No. 005 Lead; research report 52 gives Utts's figures: about 34 percent hits where chance predicted 25 percent on a four-choice task).
- Status: used inside Report No. 005; unused as a Page piece in its own right.
- Overlap: evidence farming's mirror image. A record kept honestly, judged in the open, with both verdicts printed. The format is reusable: present the record, keep both verdicts visible, let the reader sit in the judge's chair.

### The Epidaurus iamata: a record that kept only the wins
- Anchor: at the sanctuary of Asclepius at Epidaurus, around 320 BCE, the sanctuary compiled visitors' cure tablets and carved about seventy accounts into stone slabs (IG IV2 1, 121 to 124). Successes only; no slab records the night the god did not come. Scholar Lynn LiDonnici showed the accounts were rewritten into one house style when gathered. Sources: Edelstein and Edelstein (1945); LiDonnici (1995); Pausanias 2.27. verified-from-vault (Report No. 004, published 2026-11-15).
- Status: used (Report No. 004 and the launch-night Reel, 2026-11-18). Do not rerun; its lesson is now the page's own standard.
- Overlap: the anti-record. It teaches the audience, in one image, why the story's sealed record matters: a record that keeps only the wins is an advertisement.

### A message sent out of a dream, on the record
- Anchor: in 1981 Stephen LaBerge and colleagues at Stanford published the verification of lucid dreaming by prearranged eye signals: trained dreamers, confirmed in REM sleep by polysomnography, moved their eyes in an agreed pattern (left, right, left, right) from inside the dream, and the signal was recorded (Perceptual and Motor Skills 52, 727 to 732). Keith Hearne recorded the same kind of signal from dreamer Alan Worsley at Hull in April 1975, published only in his thesis. needs-verification (web, 2026-11-18; multiple consistent sources including the paper's own abstract; not yet in project material).
- Status: unused everywhere in the project.
- Overlap: a signal agreed in advance, sent from inside an altered state, and checked against instruments. It is provenance for dreams, and it pairs directly with the dream yoga material queued for Report No. 006.

### The Princeton PEAR lab: twenty-eight years, no conclusion
- Anchor: Princeton's Engineering Anomalies Research lab, founded 1979 by engineering dean Robert Jahn with Brenda Dunne, ran random event generator and remote perception studies for twenty-eight years and closed in February 2007. Its benchmark result was tiny; a three-lab replication attempt with Giessen and Freiburg failed on its primary measure; Princeton published no final conclusion. needs-verification (web, 2026-11-18; consistent across the lab's own closure statement, the Psi Encyclopedia, and press coverage).
- Status: unused everywhere in the project.
- Overlap: an institution that kept the record for three decades and then declined to say what it meant. The honest-vacuum ending is very much this page's territory: the record exists, the verdict is yours.

### Swedenborg and Kant: the investigated vision
- Anchor: in 1759 Emanuel Swedenborg, in Gothenburg, reportedly described a fire then burning in Stockholm, a city he was not in. Immanuel Kant investigated the story and wrote about it in Dreams of a Spirit-Seer (1766). The date of the fire is disputed (19 vs 29 July), so the honest telling is: a claim, an investigation by a serious philosopher, an unresolved file. verified-from-vault (RV AP Origins brief, which flags the date dispute itself).
- Status: unused. It was in the brief's first content candidates in 2026-11-08 and has still never run.
- Overlap: provenance before the word existed. A claim about seeing at a distance, checked by the best critical mind of the age, and left honestly open.

## Component: the Process and the Process Mirror

### The Dream Stele: a dream used as a title deed
- Anchor: between the paws of the Great Sphinx at Giza stands a granite stele of Thutmose IV, dated to about 1401 BCE. It records that the prince, not the designated heir, slept in the Sphinx's shadow, dreamed that the god Horemakhet promised him the kingship if he cleared the sand burying the statue, cleared it, and became king. Historians generally read the stele as a legitimating document: a divine dream conferring a throne, carved where every later visitor would read it. needs-verification (web, 2026-11-18; consistent across encyclopedic and specialist sources; the stele's text is published in standard translations but the project has not yet logged one).
- Status: unused everywhere in the project. One of the highest-value finds of this analysis.
- Overlap: a throne promised in a dream, then carved into the record. It sits exactly on the story's seam between prophecy, legitimacy, and a record someone arranged. It serves the Process Mirror, the duplicated throne promise, and the prophecy material at once, and it is pure Egypt, the page's proven ground.

### Plato's Myth of Er: the man who came back with a report
- Anchor: in the Republic (about 375 BCE), Plato tells of Er, a soldier who died, traveled, and returned to life with a report of judgment and the choosing of lives. A moral myth and a return narrative, not a practice manual. verified-from-vault (RV AP Origins brief).
- Status: unused.
- Overlap: the delok pattern in Greek dress (see dream yoga below): the one who goes, sees how lives are weighed and chosen, and comes back to tell the living. Pairs with recurrence and with the Weighing.

### The delok: those who return from death
- Anchor: Tibetan delok, people said to have died, traveled, and returned with reports, are documented into the twentieth century. verified-from-vault (RV AP Origins brief).
- Status: unused; belongs with the Report No. 006 research pack.
- Overlap: returnees as witnesses. Their reports functioned as evidence inside their communities, which is the story's question in folk form: what makes a returned witness believable?

## Component: Samuel (supplied interpretation, counterfeit authority)

### The processional oracle and the written question
- Anchor: Egyptian processional oracles answered questions carried before the god's barque, and the surviving procedure includes an anti-manipulation device: questions submitted in writing, in paired positive and negative forms, so the answer could not be steered by the asker in the moment. verified-from-vault (Egypt and Prophecy Mechanics research brief, 2026-11-16, vault 04 Research).
- Status: unused.
- Overlap: an ancient institution that knew interpreters could be gamed, and built a paper check against it. It is Samuel's method seen from the defending side. Note the brief was assembled for the proposed religious layer; the Egyptian procedure itself is ordinary Egypt content and does not depend on that layer.

### Delphi, bribed twice
- Anchor: the Delphic oracle was suborned on the record at least twice in the classical sources: Cleomenes of Sparta bribed the Pythia to declare his rival unfit, and the Alcmaeonidae family was accused of buying favorable oracles against the Peisistratids. verified-from-vault (Egypt and Prophecy Mechanics research brief, 2026-11-16).
- Status: unused.
- Overlap: the counterfeit interpreter is not a modern invention. The record of antiquity already contains bought prophecy, and the exposure of it. Samuel's whole method in two documented cases.

### Hananiah and Jeremiah: the two tests
- Anchor: the Hebrew Bible's own criteria for prophecy (Deuteronomy 13 and 18) test a prophet by loyalty and by outcome, and the Jeremiah and Hananiah confrontation (Jeremiah 28) shows the tests applied in a live dispute between two claimants. verified-from-vault (Egypt and Prophecy Mechanics research brief, 2026-11-16).
- Status: unused.
- Overlap: a tradition auditing its own interpreters. Handle as comparative record only; the story contains no actual biblical religion, per the author's boundary, and page copy must keep that line.

### Artemidorus: the professional interpreter
- Anchor: Artemidorus of Daldis wrote the Oneirocritica in five books, roughly 180 to 210 CE, the only dream manual to survive complete from Greco-Roman antiquity. He distinguished dreams that merely reflect the dreamer's state from dreams that point beyond themselves, and his fifth book collects about ninety-five dreams with their outcomes as practice cases for his son, who was learning the trade. needs-verification (web, 2026-11-18; consistent across the Oxford translation's scholarship and reference sources).
- Status: unused everywhere in the project.
- Overlap: interpretation as a profession, with a training manual and a casebook. He is the ancient world's supplied interpreter, respectable division, and his enhypnion and oneiros distinction is the ancestor of the story's rule: impressions are not interpretations.

### Etta Smith: the woman who knew where the body was
- Anchor: Los Angeles, December 1980. ## Component: the layers of humanity

### The Person in Parts
- Anchor: the Egyptian person is a set of named parts: ba (the mobile, human-headed bird), ka, akh, ren (the name), shut (the shadow), ib (the heart). The ba moves after death; Spell 89 reunites it with the body. ### China: the hun that wanders and must be recalled
- Anchor: in Han dynasty medicine (the Ling Shu), the hun soul wanders in dreams while the po stays with the body; a lost hun is illness, and it is ritually recalled. Medieval Daoist inner alchemy (yangshen, the exit of the spirit) is the one trainable-exit doctrine in the Chinese record, and it is medieval, not five thousand years old. verified-from-vault (RV AP Origins brief).
- Status: unused.
- Overlap: a part of the person that leaves during sleep and a community procedure for calling it back and checking it returned. Layers, travel, and record in one medical tradition.

### Odin: the body left lying as if dead
- Anchor: the Ynglinga Saga (chapter 7, written about the 1220s) says Odin would lie as if asleep or dead while he traveled in the shape of bird, beast, fish, or snake to distant lands on his own or others' business. Scholars debate Sami seance influence on the passage. verified-from-vault (RV AP Origins brief).
- Status: unused.
- Overlap: the northern member of the Aristeas family of stories (below), and proof the pattern is not one culture's property. The saga is a layered record itself: a Christian-era text preserving an older world's claim.

## Component: RV and AP as built-in functionality

### Sanjaya: the first remote viewer in the record
- Anchor: in the Mahabharata, the charioteer Sanjaya is granted divine sight and reports a distant battlefield, including the thoughts of the combatants, to the blind king Dhritarashtra. The closest remote-viewing analogue in any ancient text; a one-off boon, not a technique. verified-from-vault (RV AP Origins brief; published as Seeing at a Distance No. 1).
- Status: used, and the Page's best performer (451 views). It is the proof of format, not a piece to rerun.

### Aristeas and Hermotimus: the body left behind
- Anchor: Greek tradition (Herodotus, Pliny, Plutarch) tells of Aristeas of Proconnesus and Hermotimus of Clazomenae, whose souls were said to leave bodies that lay as if dead, and to travel and return with reports. Late hearsay about legends, and labeled that way in the project's telling. verified-from-vault (RV AP Origins brief; published as Seeing at a Distance No. 2).
- Status: used (200 views on the Page).

### Pat Price and Semipalatinsk: the checkable case
- Anchor: in 1974, viewer Pat Price, working from coordinates in California, described a secret Soviet facility at Semipalatinsk: the large crane and derricks in his account did not all check, and the steel sphere under construction was confirmed when the site was documented in 1977. Report No. 003 built the whole issue on the split between what checked and what did not. verified-from-vault (Report No. 003, published 2026-11-13).
- Status: used (329 views on the Page). The follow-through format, what checked and what did not, remains the page's standard move.

### Tibetan dream yoga: the trainable practice
- Anchor: milam, dream yoga, one of the Six Dharmas of Naropa (1016 to 1100), is the best-attested pre-modern trainable dream practice in the brief. The Bardo Thodol is a fourteenth-century treasure text, not an eighth-century manual; phowa is death transference, one way. verified-from-vault (RV AP Origins brief).
- Status: unused on the Page; it is the announced subject of Report No. 006, which makes it the next cross-lane asset.
- Overlap: the strongest real-world answer to the story's open question, latent and trainable or produced. A discipline a thousand years old that treats the dream state as a place to practice being conscious, with the LaBerge eye-signal work (above) as its modern laboratory echo.

### Hella Hammid: the accidental viewer
- Anchor: SRI's statistically strongest viewer, per Targ's account, was Hella Hammid, a photographer and family friend, not a professional psychic. Monroe likewise was a radio executive running sleep-learning experiments on himself when the vibrations arrived in 1958. verified-from-vault (Luminai x Monroe x Targ Overlap Map; research report 52).
- Status: unused on the Page.
- Overlap: the two accidental people pose the story's open question in biographical form: capacities latent in ordinary people, or produced by a setup? Content keeps asking it, never answers it, per the overlap map's rule.

### Price's beacon: secrecy as a lighthouse
- Anchor: Pat Price's maxim, quoted by Targ: the more attention you place on hiding something, the more it shines like a beacon in psychic space. verified-from-vault (Overlap Map, from Targ; a participant's maxim, to be framed as one).
- Status: unused on the Page.
- Overlap: the story runs the largest hiding operation imaginable, a concealed infrastructure and replayed histories under containment. Framed as a question, the maxim is a perfect F4 bridge: if Price is even partly right, secrecy at that scale is not darkness, it is a lighthouse.

### The Inuit angakkuq: the journey as combat
- Anchor: among the Inuit of the Canadian Arctic, ethnographers Franz Boas (1907) and Knud Rasmussen (1929, recording the shaman Aua) documented the angakkuq's spirit journeys to the sea bottom, the moon, and the country of the dead, journeys that were also combat against hostile powers. Illness could mean a soul stolen or a person attacked from the other side. verified-from-vault (RV AP Origins brief; Report No. 005 Lead).
- Status: used in Report No. 005; unused on the Page.
- Overlap: the traveling self as something that can be aimed, and defended against. Told as reported belief, with the ethnographers named, it is the ancient overlap for the story's darker mechanics without a single invented claim.
