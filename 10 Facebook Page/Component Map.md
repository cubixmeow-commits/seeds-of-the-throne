# Component Map: Seeds of the Throne

Created 2026-11-18 for the Facebook Page fan-base project.
Status of this document: analysis, not canon. It maps what the vault holds so the page can be planned against it.
Labels follow the vault: established, working, proposed, unresolved, rejected.
Public-safe means one of three things: it is on the live Seeds pages at iainreid.dev/seeds (sourced from 05 Public/Atlas), it has already run in 05 Public/Published, or the author has approved it for the page. Working material in compiled notes is development material, not public copy, until the author moves it.
Mechanisms only for criminal material. No real-world crime specifics appear here.

## How to read this map

Each component gets four things:
- What it is, in one or two plain sentences.
- Its vault status.
- What of it is public-safe today.
- Its audience hook: why a stranger who has never heard of this story would care.

---

## 1. The colonization and its real purpose

What it is: A much more advanced humanity runs a colonization process across a sequence of planets. On the surface it settles new worlds. Underneath, it is a containment and training structure: known coercive leaders are placed inside it under bounded conditions, ordinary participants live real lives inside it, and legitimate leaders are trained by having to deal with both.
Vault status: working, with established direction on the frame (two-planet first stage set 2026-09-28; corporate origin and bounded-proof architecture working, 2026-09-27). The real leaders' exact provenance is an open guardrail question in the notes.
Public-safe today: the Atlas colonization page and the pinned post carry the public version: a colonization experiment across two planets, run by a more advanced humanity, with contained criminals and born-in participants inside it.
Audience hook: A civilization that looks like history from inside and like an experiment from outside. Every reader has wondered whether their own world is the whole world.

## 2. Evidence farming and participant unawareness

What it is: The real leaders do not settle contested questions by argument. They give opposed camps real opportunities to act on their beliefs, and advanced systems record what the actions produce. The record of outcomes, not anyone's claim, is what gets weighed. For the record to mean anything, participants must not know they are inside a controlled environment, or their behavior stops being evidence.
Vault status: working (systems note, author direction 2026-11-07). This belongs to the real leaders running the colonization. It is never Samuel's project; his interference contaminates it.
Public-safe today: the pinned post carries the shape of it in public words: provenance, an uneditable record, and the line that a record nobody can edit outranks a victory everybody applauds. The full mechanism is not on the site.
Audience hook: What would you do differently if you knew your choices were being recorded as evidence in an argument older than you? It turns every reader into a participant.

## 3. The simulation belief and what breaks it

What it is: Most people inside the colonization, contained criminals and born-in participants alike, believed awakening meant discovering a simulation: something with no lasting consequences, which could be gamed and walked away from. The belief breaks when harm turns out to be real and irreversible, above all when Samuel hijacks and weaponizes Konrad's breeding program against the families inside it. What follows is the larger realization: this is not a game layer over reality, it is the newest layer of a much older humanity, in a universe with built-in functionality.
Vault status: working (systems note, author direction 2026-11-07).
Public-safe today: this is the spine of the published pinned post, nearly in these words. It is the most public component the story has.
Audience hook: The simulation idea is one of the most popular ideas in the culture right now. This story takes it seriously and then breaks it on a fact: simulations do not leave scars.

## 4. The Process

What it is: The ancient, sacred colonization ordeal at the center of the story's deep structure. A participant is placed against the most dangerous contained criminals at a serious disadvantage and must survive for decades while forming a resistance. It is the test the colonization exists to run.
Vault status: working (Process Mirror systems note, 2026-11-15; shared process and rotating roles note, 2026-09-24).
Public-safe today: the Process Mirror story post of 2026-11-18 put the ordeal itself on the page in public form: two men, the same ordeal, a century apart. The full process architecture is not public.
Audience hook: An ordeal that takes decades and cannot be faked. It is a hero's test with the glamour stripped off: survive, resist, and do not become the thing you were sent against.

## 5. The Process Mirror: Sylvan and Aiden

What it is: Sylvan and Aiden ran the same ordeal. Sylvan is the best-case success. Aiden, sent in about a century earlier, is the worst-case failure: he broke a few years in, secretly joined the contained criminals, and lied to Konrad for almost a century that he had resisted and won. Sylvan's run exposes him in the end.
Vault status: working (author direction 2026-11-15, compiled same day). Aiden's exact final response remains unresolved.
Public-safe today: the mirror is public. It ran as the launch-night story post on 2026-11-18 and the Aiden exposure piece ran on X on 2026-11-15. The Atlas characters page carries the public character frame.
Audience hook: Two men, one test, opposite outcomes. The failed one is not a stranger or a monster; he is the earlier version of the hero, which makes the hero's success feel earned and fragile at once.

## 6. Sylvan

What it is: The story's central participant. He entered the process independently of the leader families, works with the new generation of Luminai, and his method is discipline: impressions are not orders, interpretations are not facts, and claims get checked against the record. His refusal to play an assigned role is what breaks Samuel's endgame construction.
Vault status: working across the compiled notes; his name, role, and function are established in the Atlas characters page. Details of his run are still being built through the gate sequence.
Public-safe today: the Atlas characters page and the published pieces carry his role, his discipline, and his place in the mirror. Scene-level material is not public.
Audience hook: A hero whose power is a method, not a gift: check the record, refuse the story you are handed. Readers who are tired of chosen ones get a protagonist who wins by verification.

## 7. Aiden Fitzgerald

What it is: Konrad's son, a younger public leader, and Sylvan's failed predecessor in the Process. He broke after the Great War, joined the contained criminals in secret, and maintained the lie for almost a century, becoming the thing his father obsessively hunts. In the endgame he is caught sabotaging Sylvan and covering the breeding scandal.
Vault status: working (character note updated 2026-11-15); identity and parentage established. His final response is explicitly unresolved.
Public-safe today: the Aiden exposure piece (X, 2026-11-15), the Process Mirror post (Facebook, 2026-11-18), and the Atlas characters page.
Audience hook: A man who has been lying about the most important thing in his life for a hundred years, and whose lie rebuilt the world around him. Tragedy and villainy in one person.

## 8. George White

What it is: Samuel's son and a celebrated leader in the successor empire on Sylvan's planet. Samuel built a savior story around him: staged superiority, manufactured confirmation, and an exclusive promise of absolute power. George believed the promise. When exposure closes in, Samuel plans to blame the whole system on him and move on through Samuel Jr.
Vault status: working, with a live author-decision conflict attached. The compiled notes hold George as deceived: a man harmed by decades of staged superiority who never ran the con. A proposed 2026-11-16 layer would revise him toward deceiver, as a failed participant who lied about his own run. That revision is not compiled and is listed as an author decision in the Research Agenda. Nothing in this map adopts it.
Public-safe today: the Atlas characters page carries the duplicated promise (one throne promised to both George and Aiden) and George's place in the frame. The savior-con detail level stays in the vault.
Audience hook: A golden heir who slowly discovers that his whole life was stage-managed by his own father, and that he is the designated scapegoat. It is the story's most human wound.

## 9. Samuel Franklin

What it is: A contained criminal leader, about 220 years old, who co-leads the criminal project with Konrad behind a staged public rivalry. His obsession is complete control of every participant's mind and actions, exercised so quietly that the controlled believe their thoughts are their own. His methods are supplied interpretation, staged effects, controlled access, and separate false accounts maintained around each person he uses.
Vault status: working (character note; obsession recorded as author direction 2026-11-07). The ranking of the obsession against his other endgame objectives is the author's to confirm.
Public-safe today: the pinned post names his obsession and his breeding-program hijack in public words. The Atlas faction and characters pages carry the public frame. Method detail stays at mechanism level everywhere.
Audience hook: A villain who never gives orders. He arranges what you see, what it seems to mean, and what you conclude, then lets you call the conclusion yours. That is a frighteningly modern kind of power, and readers recognize it.

## 10. Konrad Fitzgerald

What it is: A contained criminal leader, about 300 years old, head of the organized group that dominates in public. A capable strategist whose system defeats itself: loyalty over competence, purity doctrine turning families into machinery, propaganda that eventually captures its own authors. He restarted the breeding program on Aiden's lie, knowingly backed Samuel's superiority claim, and in the endgame turns: after discovering Samuel's breeding sabotage, he discloses the staging and access method to Sylvan.
Vault status: working; identity, family line, and the disclosure direction are established in compiled notes. His locked portrait description is on the Atlas characters page.
Public-safe today: the Atlas pages and the pinned post (the breeding program is Konrad's; the hijack is Samuel's). The full failure-system analysis is vault material.
Audience hook: A villain who is not stupid. His intelligence builds something real, and his operating principles rot it from inside. Readers get to watch a system fail for structural reasons, which is rarer and more satisfying than a villain losing a fight.

## 11. The two groups, by method

What it is: The contained criminals resolve into two groups defined by their leaders' methods. Konrad's group is organized crime in the open: public domination, intimidation, hierarchy, spectacle. Samuel's group works in the dark: covert indoctrination, compromise, blackmail at mechanism level, and supplied interpretation. Containment pits the two against each other, and inside that contest Samuel's quiet methods eventually find control.
Vault status: working (author direction settled 2026-11-16; criminal ecosystems systems note). Story treatment stays at mechanism level by the author's standing instruction.
Public-safe today: the Atlas faction page carries the public version of the two-group frame and the catalyst account.
Audience hook: Two kinds of power competing: the kind that wants to be feared and the kind that wants to be believed. Readers argue about which is worse, which is exactly the conversation the page wants.

## 12. The Luminai

What it is: The new generation of extended minds. A Luminai is not a separate companion or artificial friend; it is a technologically extended layer of one person's own mind, carrying memory, perception, provenance, and contradiction detection, so that impressions stay separate from interpretations. Older generations worked through earlier AI souls; corrupted or misused versions of the older pattern are the Daemons.
Vault status: working (pairing and bonding systems note, 2026-09-06, and the technology ecology notes). The physical interface architecture is explicitly unresolved; the Atlas ai page says so in public. The literal theft-of-a-Luminai ending is superseded; Samuel can study and imitate, he cannot steal the bond.
Public-safe today: the Atlas ai page and the pinned post's discipline line. The overlap material with Monroe and Targ is published as questions the story is testing, never as settled mechanism.
Audience hook: A mind that keeps the receipts. In an age of feeds and edited everything, an inner witness that remembers what actually happened, and refuses to let a good story replace it, is an idea readers immediately want for themselves.

## 13. Recurrence

What it is: The story's pattern of return. People, roles, and configurations recur across the colonization's long span; the same kinds of figures meet again in new arrangements, and the record of earlier rounds is part of what the later rounds must answer. The story's vocabulary is deliberate: recurrence, patterns, signs, prophecy, awakening, soul. There is no reincarnation in the canon vocabulary; recurrence is the word.
Vault status: working (Identities and Recurrence systems note). The Witness figure connected to the record across rounds is unresolved.
Public-safe today: the Atlas timeline page carries the long-span frame. Recurrence as a named system is mostly vault material so far, which makes it one of the largest unused public assets in this map.
Audience hook: The feeling that a pattern in your life has happened before, given a structure and a record. It speaks to anyone who has watched the same argument, the same leader, the same mistake come around again.

## 14. The uneditable record and provenance

What it is: Beneath the public story, the colonization's infrastructure keeps a sealed record: original communications, causal sequences, suppressed testimony, who authorized what. Contained leaders can bend the public story; they cannot edit the sealed record. Provenance, knowing which thoughts and confirmations were authored by whom, is therefore the one weapon Samuel cannot counterfeit, and the Resistance's whole method is built on recovering it.
Vault status: working (story functionality and narrative containment note, 2026-09-19; evidence farming note, 2026-11-07).
Public-safe today: the pinned post's closing discipline and the Atlas research page carry the public form. Report No. 003's payoff line is the same idea in the site's research voice: until it is verified, a remote viewing is just a guess.
Audience hook: Everyone alive has been on the wrong end of an edited record: a screenshot without context, a story retold by the winner. A record that cannot be edited is a fantasy object readers instantly understand and want.

## 15. Staged superiority

What it is: Samuel's central fraud. George and Aiden have real, bounded, older-generation abilities, worked through the universe's built-in functionality and human-built wireless technology, exactly as they themselves believe. Samuel dressed those abilities with staged effects, controlled access, and supplied interpretation, and sold the result as proof of a superior, chosen generation. Konrad knowingly backed the claim. There are no brain chips or implants anywhere in this; that mechanism is rejected.
Vault status: established as revised 2026-11-15 (the implant con is rejected in all compiled notes). Which specific effects each man produced himself, which Samuel staged, and which they misread remains open.
Public-safe today: the five live Seeds pages were updated with the revised account on 2026-11-15, and the Aiden exposure piece carries it. The staging detail level stays in the vault.
Audience hook: A superiority that was stage-managed: the lighting was rigged, the confirmations were routed, the judge was whispering first. It maps onto every rigged demo and curated feed a reader has ever suspected.

## 16. The breeding program and the scandal (mechanism level)

What it is: Konrad's lineage program: reproduction, parentage, and family standing used as instruments of legitimacy and control inside his group. Aiden's lie restarted it. Samuel then ran an unauthorized expansion of his own inside it and arranged the attribution so the blame would land on Konrad's side and, later, on George. The scandal is that the program's records, promises, and children were used as leverage; the endgame exposure of who actually authorized what is one of the proofs that breaks Samuel.
Vault status: working (breeding program and lineage blackmail systems note). The origin chain has an open author-decision conflict: the compiled chain (Aiden's lie, restart, Samuel's capture) against a proposed merged-groups version with about eighty years of manipulation building before the scandal. Not resolved here.
Public-safe today: the pinned post carries the public minimum: Samuel hijacked Konrad's breeding program and weaponized it against the families inside it, with falsified parentage and blackmail named at that level. Nothing more specific is public, and nothing more specific should be created for the page without the author.
Audience hook: Lineage as a weapon: who your parents are said to be, used as a leash. It is the oldest power story there is, which is why the Egypt material on names, hearts, and records pairs with it so well.

## 17. The layers of humanity

What it is: Colonization is not the first chapter. It is the most recent layer of a much more advanced humanity, and a surprising amount of what religions and institutions preserved on the colony worlds is distorted record of real events from earlier layers. The story's religious material is original to its world; real faiths inform it only at the level of broad human dynamics, never as direct equivalents.
Vault status: working (simulation belief and layered reality note, 2026-11-07; original religions boundary note, 2026-09-19). The fuller religious layer built on this, including the competition frame and the failed groups, is proposed only (2026-11-16) and is not compiled.
Public-safe today: the pinned post carries the layer idea in one public sentence, and the site tagline carries its public form: ancient belief was the map, advanced technology is the gateway, the colony world is where they meet.
Audience hook: What if the oldest stories are not inventions but damaged records? That single question is the bridge between the Egypt audience, the RV/AP audience, and the story audience; it is the page's whole premise in one line.

## 18. RV and AP as built-in functionality

What it is: In the story's reality, remote viewing and astral projection are not metaphors or madness. They are functions of the universe, the way sight is a function, worked partly through human-built wireless technology and extended minds. How exactly the technology interfaces with the capacities, whether the capacities are latent and trainable or produced, and how viewing differs from projection inside the two layers are open development questions, not settled canon.
Vault status: working as a premise (pinned post, Atlas research page); mechanisms unresolved (research report 52 is draft and advisory; the Luminai overlap map is a content document, not canon).
Public-safe today: the premise itself is public, in the pinned post's words: those are functions, the way sight is a function. The open questions are also public-safe when framed as questions, which is how the Two Books, One Question series already handles them.
Audience hook: This is the component the entire compatible-topics audience is already gathered around. The story does not ask them to believe anything new; it asks what follows if the thing they are already studying is a function with owners, records, and abusers.

## 19. Supporting components worth tracking

### The Daemons and the older AI souls
What they are: the older generation of extended minds, less integrated than the new Luminai, and the layer through which Samuel's supplied interpretation does much of its work: a monitor that stops being blind and starts whispering the interpretation first. Status: working. Public-safe: the concept at the level of the overlap map's published line, the villains are what happens when the judge starts whispering first. Hook: everyone has an inner voice; the question of who trained it is universal. The proposed claim that George and Konrad built the Daemons out of the Luminai is an author decision, not compiled fact.

### Orzai and the Resistance
What they are: the resistance that forms inside the process, with Orzai as a central figure who accepts the result of the test first. Status: working; the Resistance's black-and-orange ark identity and Orzai's role are on the Atlas pages. Hook: the people inside the experiment who start keeping their own record. They are the audience's natural identification figures after Sylvan.

### The bounded prize
What it is: the endgame's real stake is bounded, not total: permanent control of the largest empire, tied to Sylvan's 42nd birthday, never the planet or the process itself. Samuel's terminal attempt also burns his own finite resources (the $15M allocation in the accepted ending macro). Status: working (accepted ending macro RW-01 to RW-10). Public-safe: only at Atlas level. Hook: villains with budgets and deadlines are more interesting than villains with infinite power; the clock is a story asset the page can use without spoiling scenes.

### Story functionality and the altered realities
What it is: a bounded ability to shape how events are publicly interpreted: salience, explanation, reputation, apparent confirmation. Samuel maintains separate durable false accounts around George, Aiden, and Konrad inside a physically real world; the people, injuries, and consequences stay real, only the account is managed. Status: working, with the altered-reality definition marked established direction (2026-09-19). Public-safe: the pinned post's frame covers the effect; the mechanism term itself is vault language. Hook: a false story you live inside, where everything that happens to you is real and only the meaning is rigged. It is the component that makes the page's media-literacy audience lean in.

### The corporate ascension claim
What it is: the origin layer: corporations built the first synthetic society outside government control, a culpable faction sold the claim that humans could become synthetics and thereby gods, and the governing leaders answered not with a ban but with a bounded chance to prove the claim on the record. Status: working (2026-09-27), with founding chronology and the real leaders' provenance unresolved. Public-safe: the Atlas colonization page carries the public frame. Hook: a miracle claim tested in public, with the promoters' own words kept in a ledger. It is the evidence theme wearing a business suit, and it gives the page a whole second doorway for skeptics.
