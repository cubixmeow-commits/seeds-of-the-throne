# Current Story State

## Development phase

The project is currently in a vault stress-test phase. We are building a small number of story components to test persistent memory, retrieval, continuity, supersession, and compilation before expanding the story broadly.

## Current focal elements

- Sylvan — working name for the principal younger leader / hero.
- Logan — working name for the throwaway prince, the Throne figure's son.
- The Throne figure — contained authoritarian whose faction believes it won when it actually fell into containment.
- False Victory — the faction's foundational deception.
- Manufactured Reality — the technologically mediated reality in which Logan has been kept.
- Sylvan's recordings — extensive evidence capable of exposing the Throne figure.
- Sylvan–Throne confrontation — constrained final struggle over truth, legitimacy, evidence, loyalty, and control.

## Core mechanism currently under development

A technologically sophisticated environment can mediate a participant's experienced reality through information control and a lifelong AI companion / AI soul. This is fictional story functionality. The technical mechanism remains a research question, not a real-world claim.

## Major unresolved questions

- What are Sylvan's full identity, history, authority, and motivations?
- What exactly does Sylvan's evidence prove?
- How did Sylvan obtain and preserve the recordings?
- What is the Throne figure's exact role and name?
- How did the faction enter containment while believing it had won?
- What exactly does Logan believe about his father and his own life?
- How does Logan's AI soul participate in the manufactured reality?
- What causes Logan to awaken, if he does?
- What are the exact constraints preventing the Throne figure from simply eliminating Sylvan?
- What is the fictional breeding-program mechanism and what does it reveal about the Throne figure's ideology?

## Canon boundary

All real people, historical figures, real nations, real organizations, and real-world groups remain development analogies only. Final story data uses invented entities and fictional history.
