# Dynamic Story Workshop

This is the single canonical ongoing workshop for *Seeds of the Throne*. Update it after every desktop assessment. Dated sessions preserve development history; compiled notes preserve accepted story state; this file alone determines the next workshop questions.

Older BA, EG, RW, Reveal Chain, and broad Workshop files remain historical records and evidence of how decisions developed. They are not parallel active question lists. When an older unresolved question still matters, fold it into this file and link its source rather than reopening the old workshop.

## Current Canon Baseline

### Process boundary

- Planet Three is the mature containment-and-cultivation world. Samuel, Konrad, George, Aiden, and the contained criminal groups may hold consequential local power, but none controls the governing colonization process.
- Their apparent authority does not transfer into the larger civilization. When the contained structure fails, there is no external throne or empire waiting for them.
- The legitimate process preserves evidence and enforces terminal consequences, but it does not replace dangerous character choices with automatic resolution.

### Endgame board

- Sylvan and Orzai have developed separate successor human–Luminai bonds and a voluntary partnership capable of maintaining contact with reality under interference.
- The independent Resistance connects evidence kept separate across people, records, systems, bloodlines, institutions, and generations. Sylvan may act on or present its evidence without owning the movement or its discoveries.
- Konrad has committed his groups to Sylvan's separate exposure plan. This does not erase his responsibility or place either party under the other's hierarchy.
- The original prophecy predates Samuel. Samuel counterfeits authority to apply it and manufactures confirmation around the targets he assigns.
- The final sequence must preserve public proof, affected-group disclosure, protected evidence, and private descendant records as distinct layers.

### Resistance identity

- **Established colors:** black and orange.
- **Established central image:** an ark carrying belief, culture, memory, testimony, family continuity, and people through persecution by contained criminal groups.
- The ark signifies preservation, sanctuary, survival, and emergence—not divine chosenness, supremacy, or a mandate to rule.
- The movement remains independent from Sylvan and retains its own risks, discoveries, choices, and victories.

### George, Aiden, and Samuel

- Samuel runs one compartmentalized con on two marks. He secretly promises George White and Aiden Fitzgerald the same singular destination: absolute, godlike power over everyone else.
- George's long savior-prophecy is an altered reality. Selected signs, manipulated information, corrupted interpretation, and apparent weapon performance make his promised transformation seem real.
- George and Aiden each believe he alone is chosen. Neither is meant to learn that Samuel gave the same throne to the other.
- The supposed superweapon appears real only while Sylvan is severely disadvantaged. It fails once Sylvan begins publicly exposing Samuel and the structure supporting the illusion.
- Failure brings George and Aiden's separate accounts into the same evidentiary space. The duplicated promise reveals one con, two marks, and one throne promised twice.
- Samuel continues trying to aim them at each other. He tells each that the other sabotaged the weapon, corrupted the prophecy, or is stealing the exclusive position.
- Samuel's repeated tactic under public pressure becomes new evidence of his method and continuing command.

### Bloodline betrayal and escape

- George and Aiden belong to a younger generation shaped through the older contained leaders' breeding program, initially intended to extend their control into the second colonization planet.
- Samuel captured access to that program and targeted the bloodlines on which the older leaders based legitimacy, succession, and power. He produced unauthorized heirs, extended the attack through younger generations, and used lineage secrecy for leverage and control.
- George and Aiden's prestigious roles were always disposable. Even apparent conquest success would not have converted their contained roles into real power in the larger civilization.
- The endgame exposes Samuel as the deceiver the trapped leaders were warned about. Recognition alone is insufficient: they must choose to fight their way out against Samuel while the legitimate governing process remains outside his control.

## Conflicts/Compatibility Checks

### Resolved by explicit author decision

- **Aiden's reward versus George's divine destiny:** earlier notes treated these as distinct. The later decision supersedes that split. Both are secretly promised the same absolute godlike position; their subjective presentation may differ, but the destination is identical.
- **Leaders targeting Sylvan versus Samuel:** the attack on Sylvan remains an earlier phase of Samuel's con. After the weapon fails and the duplicated control structure is exposed, the trapped leaders recognize Samuel as the deceiver and turn their effort toward escaping and opposing him.
- **Resistance symbols unresolved versus ark locked:** the general name and internal culture remain open, but the author has now established the palette and ark metaphor. Older statements that all symbols were unresolved are superseded only at that scope.

### Compatible distinctions to preserve

- The prophecy may be genuine within the invented religion while Samuel's target assignment and promised godhood remain fraudulent.
- Manipulation explains the information environment; it does not erase George's, Aiden's, Konrad's, or other leaders' agency and accountability.
- The superweapon may have observable effects under asymmetric conditions without being the absolute weapon Samuel claimed.
- “Fight their way out” means overcoming Samuel's maintained story, proxies, dependencies, and local retaliation—not escaping governing rules that Samuel controls.
- Bloodline evidence proves Samuel's campaign without making descendants guilty, public property, or mere plot evidence.

### Still requiring compatibility proof

- The weapon mechanism must obey current Luminai, Daemon, access, and containment limits.
- The public exposure sequence must not reveal protected parentage or collapse the Resistance into Sylvan's support staff.
- George and Aiden's duplicated promise must fit the existing original prophecy without rewriting that prophecy as Samuel's invention.
- The leaders' reversal must follow from evidence and chosen action, not instant moral conversion.
