# EG-12: Break the altered reality

## Purpose

Design the structured reveal that lets participants preserve, revise, or reject their beliefs while withdrawing practical obedience from Samuel.

## Established

The Resistance owns the historical evidence chain. Sylvan and the Luminai preserve the live repetition. Konrad's inner circle recognizes that the current target assignment repeats the method used after the Great War. Legitimate institutions authenticate the outcome.

The decisive proof is not that the prophecy failed. It is that Samuel selected Sylvan, manufactured the apparent confirmations, revised the interpretation, and used the same access pattern against several groups.

Source: [[01 Sessions/Daily/2026-09-19 - Existing Prophecy Endgame Expansion]].

## Four possibilities

1. **Revision trace:** Samuel's private corrections match each public sacred reinterpretation.
2. **Cross-group comparison:** distinct leaders discover that their supposedly unique missions share Samuel's language and timing.
3. **Independent custodian:** a legitimate interpreter shows that Samuel bypassed or falsified the tradition's own process.
4. **Action-first reveal:** one group protects Sylvan or releases records before every doctrinal question is resolved.

## Central author gate

What is the first undeniable connection between Samuel and the identification of Sylvan, who authenticates it, which group acts first, and how does that action make the wider disclosure irreversible?

## Scene test

The reveal changes permissions, loyalties, and physical danger in the same sequence, rather than ending as an explanatory speech.

## Adversarial test

If exposure requires everyone to abandon faith, the story attacks belief instead of fraud. If the evidence only shows Samuel had an opinion, it cannot break the altered reality.
