# EG-08: Processing, presentation, and aftermath

## Purpose

Join the accepted terminal mechanics to the human consequences, protected evidence, and constructive choices that close the story.

## Established

Genuine failure of Samuel's independent plan triggers immediate processing. His Daemon is deleted because Daemon existence requires active participation. Samuel enters a stasis-like coma and biological synthetics transport him to the older contained criminal leaders while his conduct is presented. Sylvan and Orzai then accept an exploratory placement, travel to other Houses, and choose where to build their own House. Orzai leads their acceptance of the completed result.

Sources: [[07 Coordination/Story Completion Workflow/Reassessment Workshop/03 - Processing and exposure]], [[07 Coordination/Story Completion Workflow/Reassessment Workshop/04 - Sylvan and Orzai placement]], [[07 Coordination/Story Completion Workflow/Reassessment Workshop/05 - Orzai indispensable action]].

## Four possibilities

1. **Procedural climax:** the visible balance failure triggers processing while the evidence presentation continues across distinct audiences.
2. **Group-choice climax:** Samuel's processing occurs after the last affected group rejects his interpretation and preserves the record.
3. **Konrad-action climax:** Konrad's irreversible disclosure action removes Samuel's final shield immediately before the allocation reaches zero.
4. **Orzai-transition climax:** processing closes the adversarial process, then Orzai's independent decision begins the constructive future without an extended victory speech.

## Central author gate

What are the indispensable ordered beats from separation through Samuel's processing, the protected presentation, Konrad's consequence, George's choice, and Orzai and Sylvan's departure?

## Scene test

Every terminal mechanism is established before it pays off, every major character makes or faces a choice, and the last forward action belongs to the constructive future.

## Adversarial test

If the presentation exposes every private fact, accountability becomes indiscriminate. If the story ends on Samuel's removal, he remains the center of the future he failed to control.
