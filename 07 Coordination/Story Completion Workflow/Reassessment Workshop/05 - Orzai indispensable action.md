# RW-05: Orzai's indispensable action

## Purpose

Ensure the shared ending depends on Orzai's own judgment and bond rather than using her as Sylvan's support system.

## Established

Orzai shares decisive control, completes a separate bond, detects excluded premises, protects dignity, and can act after long private observation. Samuel tries to make Sylvan and Orzai appear irrational to each other.

## Four possibilities

1. **Independent authentication:** Orzai preserves or validates the evidence channel Samuel expects Sylvan to control alone. Strong system relevance, but should include a human choice.
2. **Protective refusal:** she refuses a more complete public exposure because it would instrumentalize victims, forcing a fairer proof. Strong ethics, but needs to preserve decisive attribution.
3. **Placement choice:** she selects the modest constructive path before Sylvan does, preventing the ending from becoming his inherited future. Strong character autonomy, but may arrive too late to affect the climax.
4. **Separation concurrence:** the system requires both independently bonded humans to authorize the final separation. Clear importance, but must not reduce her to a second signature.

## Central author gate

What independent decision by Orzai is necessary for separation, exposure, or placement acceptance to succeed?

Choose one action only. Her professional and emotional arc can be developed around it afterward.

## Scene test

Sylvan favors the most narratively complete outcome. Orzai notices the person, uncertainty, or concealed premise that plan would sacrifice. Her intervention changes the result.

## Adversarial test

If Sylvan could make the same choice alone with no material change, Orzai is not yet indispensable.
