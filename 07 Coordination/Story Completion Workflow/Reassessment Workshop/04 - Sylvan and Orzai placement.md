# RW-04: Sylvan and Orzai's accepted placement

## Purpose

Turn thematic acceptance into a tangible future without building an oversized reward structure.

## Established

Sylvan and Orzai successfully deal with Samuel, formally separate from him, and accept the placement they earned in the colonization process. Their plan is cheap and easy and uses only resources they preserved.

## Four possibilities

1. **Existing House placement:** they join or secure a qualified House already operating in colonization. Immediate and practical, but exact authority must stay bounded.
2. **Small founding placement:** they receive permission to begin a modest new group using preserved resources. Strong fresh start, but could resemble an unearned kingdom.
3. **Project placement:** they accept responsibility for one concrete colonization project before any larger House role. Human-scale and cheap, but may undersell the accumulated qualification.
4. **Phased placement:** they accept a modest project inside an established House with future expansion based on results. Balanced, but slightly more complex.

## Central author gate

What placement do Sylvan and Orzai accept, and what is the first cheap and easy action they take within it?

Name the practical beginning, not the final scale of their future influence.

## Scene test

After Samuel's exposure, Sylvan and Orzai face a choice among qualified placements. What do they choose, and what can they begin immediately without asking for more power?

## Adversarial test

The placement cannot function as inherited sovereignty, moral coronation, or compensation that makes their earlier losses irrelevant.
