# BA-05: The indispensable middle

## Purpose

Replace a long sequence of similar attacks with a cumulative ladder in which each event changes capability, relationship, evidence, or available choices.

## Established

The reserve and employment stages teach resource discipline, independent cooperation, and disciplined reality-and-ethical anchoring. Samuel escalates as the deadline approaches. Sylvan and Orzai must preserve their voluntary partnership and demonstrate placement-worthy conduct.

Sources: [[02 Story/Systems/The Schedule and Stages]], [[02 Story/Systems/Competitive Environments - Control Inversion and Sylvan Endgame]], [[07 Coordination/Story Completion Workflow/Reassessment Workshop/08 - Employment stage preparation]].

## Four possibilities

1. **Capability ladder:** each environment teaches one necessary survival or verification skill used later.
2. **Cost ladder:** each success protects one thing but causes a different material, relational, or reputational loss.
3. **Evidence ladder:** each episode converts a suspicion into increasingly authenticated proof.
4. **Braided ladder:** four to six episodes each advance at least two of capability, cost, evidence, relationship, and deadline pressure.

## Central author gate

Which four to six indispensable episodes connect the opening to certification, and what irreversible change does each episode produce?

## Scene test

Removing any selected episode creates a visible hole in the ending's causality or emotional credibility.

## Adversarial test

If several episodes teach the same lesson or restore the same status quo, compress them. If an ending capability appears without a prior failed or costly use, it has not been earned.
