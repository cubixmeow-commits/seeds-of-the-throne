# BA-01: Define “take Sylvan”

## Purpose

Turn the largest-empire offer into a rule that characters can pursue, resist, misunderstand, and finally fail.

## Established

The primary leaders genuinely offered Konrad's group permanent control of the largest empire if they teamed with Samuel and successfully took Sylvan before his forty-second birthday. The offer never includes the planet or overall process. Failure closes the imperial path permanently and leaves cooperation for a city as the only constructive option.

Sources: [[01 Sessions/Daily/2026-09-11 - RW-10 Permanent Empire Bait]], [[02 Story/Systems/Competitive Environments - Control Inversion and Sylvan Endgame]].

## Four possibilities

1. **Voluntary allegiance:** Sylvan knowingly chooses Samuel and Konrad's command structure over independent placement.
2. **Sustained governed dependence:** Sylvan voluntarily enters and maintains a defined dependent relationship through a review period.
3. **Transferable institutional commitment:** Sylvan knowingly commits his earned placement, bonded capability, or future House to their imperial project under valid rules.
4. **Combined consent-and-conduct threshold:** Sylvan must voluntarily join, demonstrate sustained allegiance through specified choices, and remain free to refuse until certification.

## Central author gate

What exact, observable condition would have counted as successfully taking Sylvan before his forty-second birthday?

Define what Sylvan must knowingly choose or do, how long it must remain true, who authenticates it, and why coercion, counterfeit evidence, or temporary access cannot satisfy the rule.

## Scene test

Samuel claims he is one step from victory. Another character can identify the specific missing requirement and the reader can understand why Samuel cannot fake it.

## Adversarial test

If “take” means capture, domination, bond theft, loyalty, institutional transfer, and symbolic victory whenever the plot needs it, the contest is not fair. If the condition can be forced, the leaders' claimed moral proof collapses.
