# Book One Architecture Workshop

This is the active story workshop. It preserves the completed RW ending decisions and develops the missing path from Book One's opening to that ending.

The modules are dependency ordered, not a promise that every question must remain separate. Ask one author gate at a time, record the answer in a dated session, propagate accepted canon, and then reassess later modules.

# Priority 1 — Make the contest legible

- [ ] **BA-01: Define “take Sylvan.”** Establish the exact observable victory condition, permitted route, failure state, and institutional safeguard.
- [ ] **BA-02: Specify Samuel's lie to Konrad.** Define the false account, its credible evidence, the concealed truth, and why that truth would have changed Konrad's choice.
- [ ] **BA-03: Activate the forty-second-birthday clock.** Establish when the story begins, who knows the deadline, and how the pressure becomes visible.

# Priority 2 — Build the human story

- [ ] **BA-04: Choose the Book One opening.** Select viewpoint, ordinary objective, first disruption, and first discrepancy.
- [ ] **BA-05: Build the indispensable middle.** Choose four to six escalating episodes that teach the capacities required by the ending.
- [ ] **BA-06: Earn Sylvan and Orzai's final partnership.** Map their disagreement, independent decisions, costs, repair, and final leadership transition.

# Priority 3 — Make the ending dramatic

- [ ] **BA-07: Order the evidence and exposure.** Separate public, affected-group, protected, and private revelations into a dramatic sequence.
- [ ] **BA-08: Give George his final choice.** Decide what he does when Samuel makes him the scapegoat and what that choice costs.
- [ ] **BA-09: Give Konrad his final move.** Decide how he uses his retained influence when Samuel's century-long lie collapses.

# Priority 4 — Make Book One buildable

- [ ] **BA-10: Lock the Book One sequence contract.** Assemble the accepted objective, opening, reversals, relationship turns, revelations, deadline, collapse, and placement into a scene-ready order.

# Completion gate

- [ ] A reader can understand what the antagonists are trying to accomplish and how success is measured.
- [ ] The forty-second-birthday deadline creates visible pressure from the opening onward.
- [ ] Every indispensable middle episode changes capability, relationship, evidence, or available choices.
- [ ] Sylvan, Orzai, George, and Konrad each make consequential choices in the final movement.
- [ ] The evidence reaches each audience in an intentional order.
- [ ] A scene writer can create packets without inventing missing macro causality.

# Preserved foundation

The completed [[07 Coordination/Story Completion Workflow/Reassessment Workshop/README|reassessment workshop]] remains authoritative at its accepted depth. This workshop implements its consequences; it does not reopen them.
