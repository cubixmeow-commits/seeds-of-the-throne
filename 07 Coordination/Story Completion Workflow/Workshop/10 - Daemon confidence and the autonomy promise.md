## Central author gate

> What apparently verified success makes Konrad trust the bargain while misunderstanding its boundary?

Ask this question alone during actual use. Do not ask every follow-up at once.

## Possibilities and tradeoffs

### 1. Real local command with hidden cross-domain visibility

**PROPOSED:** Konrad can issue genuine orders while Samuel observes dependencies. Strong alpha/beta fit; surveillance must have a later causal route into control.

### 2. Staged submission supported by selective genuine concessions

**PROPOSED:** Samuel sacrifices something real to appear captured. Preserves both men’s intelligence; the cost must be meaningful and later recoverable only through the coup.

### 3. A successful narrow daemon penetration

**PROPOSED:** The daemon genuinely controls one subsystem and falsely generalizes to Samuel. Strong technical temptation; avoid letting a single hack grant all authority.

### 4. A politically credible restoration package

**PROPOSED:** Followers, work and infrastructure return under Konrad’s formal role. Emotional and institutional appeal; signed terms must show the difference between appearance and actual limits.
