## Historical use

The active SC-010 gate and the remaining 27-task sequence were retired by author direction on 2026-09-09. The accepted results in these twenty packets remain valid where later decisions have not superseded them.

## First five high-impact sessions

1. **01 · Purpose:** determines what counts as success beyond defeating Samuel.
2. **02 · Entry and knowledge:** determines consent, refusal, and the fairness of evaluation.
3. **03 · Placement:** turns success into a concrete future while separating competence from authority.
4. **04 · Reconstruction:** determines which costs are real, what can change, and whom safeguards protect.
5. **05 · Luminai generations:** resolves what the comparative test can show and prevents a silent implant-model substitution.

The full causal pass is complete. The accepted answers define the governing structure, Konrad's bargain, George's cross-planet role, Sylvan and Orzai's partnership, declining endgame influence, the inner circle's recognition, Samuel's final attempted deal, procedural finality, and flexible later-book order. Open mechanics should now be developed from those constraints rather than reopening the accepted gates.

## Persistence and authority

Browser answers are local drafts on that browser and origin. They are not synchronized between sites or devices, and clearing browser storage can remove them. Export produces a Markdown file; importing a previous export restores the text for further work. No browser control writes to the vault, creates a canon decision, or sends data to an AI provider.

To integrate: place the exported answer in a dated session, restate it exactly, obtain author acceptance, update the current task and QA decisions, then update the affected canonical notes. Rebuild the websites with `python3 scripts/build_story_sites.py`.

Keep private answers out of a public repository unless the author has reviewed them for publication. The existing Explorer is public and read-only; spoiler disclosure is not access control.
