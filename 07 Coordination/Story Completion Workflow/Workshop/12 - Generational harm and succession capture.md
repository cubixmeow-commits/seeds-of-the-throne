## Central author gate

> Which individual path will make the generational takeover emotionally and causally visible?

Ask this question alone during actual use. Do not ask every follow-up at once.

## Possibilities and tradeoffs

### 1. Coerced facilitator who later exercises real power

**PROPOSED:** Shows movement from victimization to responsibility. Rich ambiguity; must identify concrete choices rather than treat coercion as permanent innocence.

### 2. Descendant who refuses both factions

**PROPOSED:** Makes survival outside doctrine and blackmail imaginable. Prevents the family from existing only as evidence; refusal needs resources and real costs.

### 3. Loyal operator whose family record becomes leverage

**PROPOSED:** Shows shame converting ideological loyalty into dependence. Strong insider view; do not validate purity by making ancestry itself the injury.

### 4. Institutional heir whose tools uncover their own origin

**PROPOSED:** Links Samuel Jr.’s company to independent identity evidence. Strong structural irony; avoid reducing the heir to a surprise parentage twist.
