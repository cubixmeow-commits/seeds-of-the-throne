## Central author gate

> What specific repeated act makes insiders compare their histories, and what do they do with the comparison?

Ask this question alone during actual use. Do not ask every follow-up at once.

## Possibilities and tradeoffs

### 1. Identical autonomy language attached to a new access request

**PROPOSED:** Easy reader recognition; phrases alone prove method resemblance, not historical guilt, so pair with permission records.

### 2. A repeated administrative exception with matching consequences

**PROPOSED:** Connects current behavior to a concrete earlier lock. Strong causal proof; needs a human who remembers why the exception mattered.

### 3. Incompatible private assurances become mutually visible

**PROPOSED:** Breaks compartmentalization through people comparing accounts. Strong agency; safe communication must precede disclosure.

### 4. A sacrificial intermediary recognizes their former role

**PROPOSED:** Emotional recognition precedes formal proof. Powerful but potentially unreliable; require independent corroboration and a real choice to act.
