## Central author gate

> What placement decision can this process legitimately make about a bonded person?

Ask this question alone during actual use. Do not ask every follow-up at once.

## Possibilities and tradeoffs

### 1. Role-specific competence portfolio

**PROPOSED:** Separate evidence for judgment, craft, collaboration, restraint and recovery. Fairer and dramatically rich, but less instantly legible than rank; anchor in one desired role.

### 2. Provisional stewardship with review

**PROPOSED:** Success grants a bounded responsibility rather than status for life. Supports accountable leadership; final triumph becomes a beginning, and monitoring must apply to leaders too.

### 3. Participant preference matched to demonstrated fit

**PROPOSED:** Sylvan may discover that a coveted placement conflicts with his values. Strong agency and personal arc; the civilization must offer real options rather than disguise assignment as choice.

### 4. Independent evaluation of process and participant

**PROPOSED:** The presentation judges both Sylvan and the institution that exposed him to pressure. Strongest accountability; adds adjudicative complexity and requires more than family authority.

## Scene test

**Non-canon situation:** Sylvan can maximize his visible performance by retaining a witness in the process; letting that person leave costs him evidence or an opportunity.

Record what each person wants, can know, can refuse, and can actually change. Name the observable difference between the selected alternatives.
