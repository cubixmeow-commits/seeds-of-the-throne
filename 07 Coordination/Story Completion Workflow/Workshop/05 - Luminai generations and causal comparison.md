## Central author gate

> What new capacity does deeper integration supply that must still be governed by human choices and institutions?

Ask this question alone during actual use. Do not ask every follow-up at once.

## Possibilities and tradeoffs

### 1. Richer reciprocal error correction

**PROPOSED:** The pair detects mismatch between prediction, experience and consequence sooner. Useful against performed obedience; it can still entrench shared mistakes without outside evidence.

### 2. Finer provenance and intention differentiation

**PROPOSED:** The pair can distinguish source, inference and conscious authorization. Supports evidence thriller; not truth detection and not immunity to persuasive false context.

### 3. Broader embodied environmental coordination

**PROPOSED:** The pair learns to act across physical systems with less translation. Offers vivid competence and costs; power alone says nothing about ethical use.

### 4. More stable revision across long experience

**PROPOSED:** The pair can update goals and self-models without identity collapse. Strong foil to Konrad; requires costly episodes where Sylvan revises a cherished premise instead of software doing it for him.
