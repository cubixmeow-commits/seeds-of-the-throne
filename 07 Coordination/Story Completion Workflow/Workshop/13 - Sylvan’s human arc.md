## Central author gate

> What does Sylvan have to relinquish or revise even after he can contain Samuel?

Ask this question alone during actual use. Do not ask every follow-up at once.

## Possibilities and tradeoffs

### 1. From proving himself to accepting a bounded result

**PROPOSED:** He stops treating perfect completion as the condition of worth. Directly opposes Konrad’s one-victory redemption; risk of making ambition inherently suspect.

### 2. From organizing everyone to respecting independent refusal

**PROPOSED:** His social talent becomes leadership when people can decline his plan. Strong Orzai and witness conflict; refusal must materially change events.

### 3. From solving the system to choosing a life beyond it

**PROPOSED:** He regains craft, partnership or ordinary commitments the test consumed. Humanizes long chronology; must not trivialize unresolved victims.

### 4. From filial trust to accountable loyalty

**PROPOSED:** He can love his parents while requiring review of their process. Strong institutional stakes; avoid replacing one total certainty with blanket cynicism.

## Targeted follow-up questions

- What ordinary thing does he want before the presentation?
- Where does his best trait create pressure on someone else?
- What would he choose if his placement were guaranteed?

## Scene test

**Non-canon situation:** Orzai declines a useful synchronization or disclosure request; Sylvan’s first plan becomes impossible, and he must devise another without punishing her refusal.

Record what each person wants, can know, can refuse, and can actually change. Name the observable difference between the selected alternatives.

## Adversarial questions

- If he already understands Samuel and controls the conclusion, what can he still be wrong about that matters to someone he loves?
- Which established constraint would this answer break, and what evidence would reveal that break before the payoff?
