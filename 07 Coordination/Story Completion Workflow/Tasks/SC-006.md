## Objective

Plant the first small observable inconsistency that gives George a plausible chance to notice the false reality around him is being actively maintained.

## Accepted SC-006 macro result

George's first anomaly is not a technical failure. It is a **narrative-scale mismatch** between the story he is told and the behavior he can directly observe.

George is told that Sylvan is essentially a nobody and that George's own side occupies a position of major importance and influence. Yet the environment repeatedly contradicts that framing:

- Samuel's obsession with destroying Sylvan is wildly disproportionate to Sylvan's supposed insignificance.
- Large amounts of attention, planning, manipulation, and environmental effort are spent on defeating this one person.
- George and his side appear to possess **less influence over the environment than their claimed position should give them**.
- Samuel's fixation is too persistent and strategically important to fit the explanation George has been given about who Sylvan is.

George can dismiss each contradiction individually, but together they create the first seed that the official explanation is incomplete or false. The later realization is not merely that events were manipulated, but that the false reality required continuing effort precisely because the real power relationships were different from what George had been told.

## Current boundary

At Macro Shape, preserve the anomaly as an observable contradiction in importance, control, and behavior. Do not yet define George's later proof chain, refusal points, or final interpretation.
