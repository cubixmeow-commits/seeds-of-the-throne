# SC-004 — Choose the Exact Target of the Attack on Sylvan's Startup

## Objective

Define the concrete thing that is attacked in the modern inciting event so the startup sabotage can later be built as a specific scene rather than a generic collapse.

## Source

- [[07 Coordination/Weekly Synthesis/Runs/2026-08-23/12 Weekly Story Completion Todo#Priority 2 — Make the modern story actually begin]]

## Accepted SC-004 macro findings

### Question 1 — accepted

The sabotage initially targets the **physical hosting environment for Sylvan's own servers** rather than directly attacking Sylvan or announcing itself as hostile action.

The failure is timed around a high-stakes **convention presentation** where Sylvan needs the system working in order to attract attention, establish credibility, and secure investment or future backing.

The event must initially look plausibly coincidental: a hosting-provider failure, infrastructure problem, outage, equipment issue, or other ordinary operational disaster that could reasonably happen without sabotage.

This gives the attack two functions at once:

- it hits a concrete operational dependency that Sylvan cannot instantly replace during the presentation window;
- it preserves ambiguity, because outsiders can reasonably interpret the failure as bad luck, vendor incompetence, or ordinary technical collapse rather than an attack.

### Question 2 — accepted

At the crucial presentation window, the **network connections to the server-hosting center go down for several hours**. The physical servers themselves do not need to be destroyed or corrupted; they simply become unreachable from the outside when Sylvan most needs them.

This kind of outage is possible but **very rare** for the hosting provider. The provider gives an explanation that is plausible enough to close the incident operationally but contains something slightly suspicious or unsatisfying.

At this point in the story, the event remains only a coincidence from Sylvan's perspective. There is no proof of sabotage, no overt antagonist signature, and no reason for the audience to conclude with certainty that the outage was deliberate.

## Current boundary

At Macro Shape, keep the target fixed as Sylvan's physical server-hosting environment during the convention/investment presentation. The visible failure is a rare multi-hour loss of external connectivity to the hosting center. Do not yet lock the exact hidden sabotage mechanism, access path, culprit, or surviving anomaly unless established in a later gate.

## Current author gate

**Question 3:** What is the immediate human/business consequence of the outage at the convention?

Choose the main visible consequence only: for example, Sylvan loses the live demonstration and looks unprepared, an investor meeting collapses, a partner backs away, the audience sees a degraded fallback instead of the real system, or another concrete consequence that materially damages the startup.
