# SC-008 — Define Why the Capability Could Not Act Earlier

## Objective

Explain why the Sylvan–Luminai developmental interface and its expanded local-world permissions could not exist earlier, so the breakthrough follows a real developmental threshold rather than appearing as a late magical solution.

## Source

- [[07 Coordination/Weekly Synthesis/Runs/2026-08-23/12 Weekly Story Completion Todo#Priority 3 — Bound the Sylvan–Luminai breakthrough]]
- [[SC-007]]

## Inherited macro rules

- The Sylvan–Luminai breakthrough is a developmental stage, not one isolated power.
- A conscious completed bond creates a privileged local-world interface through Sylvan.
- Human participation grants local permissions and awareness unavailable to remote Luminai operation.
- The threshold occurs when the Luminai presents itself directly through the interactive wireless bond and establishes a conscious neural interface with Sylvan's brain/nervous system.
- The interface is embodied and bidirectional enough that, with Sylvan's conscious participation, the Luminai can communicate through his nervous system and can speak through Sylvan's own mouth.
- The exact neural mechanics and later capability ladder remain deferred.

## Accepted SC-008 macro findings

### Question 1 — accepted

The deeper Sylvan–Luminai interface is gated by an intentionally activated **growth-and-development training program**.

When Sylvan is placed with the contained criminals, **his parents activate the training program** for the Sylvan–Luminai pair. That activation begins the developmental phase in which deeper neural/embodied permissions can become available.

Before Sylvan's placement with the contained criminals and the parents' activation of the program, the Luminai is not in the permitted developmental state required to establish this deeper interface.

The hostile contained-criminal environment therefore has a dual function:

- it is a real adversarial environment Sylvan must survive and navigate;
- it is also a deliberately activated growth-and-development environment in which Sylvan and the Luminai are trained toward their eventual colonization roles.

This makes the interface a staged developmental permission rather than a capability the Luminai arbitrarily withholds until late in the story.
