## Accepted SC-002 macro findings

### Question 1 — accepted

The most important takeover actions that **do not** follow automatically from Samuel's alpha status are the transfer/reactivation of the Fitzgerald **breeding/lineage program** and the **training system for the younger generation** inside a structure where Samuel has superior standing and influence.

Konrad must participate in or accept the arrangement. Samuel persuades Konrad that Konrad is effectively locked out of these systems unless he accepts Samuel's proposed deal. The deal is false or misleading: Konrad believes it restores or preserves Fitzgerald control, while Samuel retains meaningful access, influence, information, and decision-making authority through his office and alpha position.

Once these systems are operating inside Samuel's sphere, he can progressively hijack both:

- the lineage/breeding system, using access to family continuity and future generations as part of his long campaign against Fitzgerald bloodlines;
- the younger-generation training system, redirecting the beliefs, morals, and cultural identity of later generations away from the senior Fitzgerald leadership's intended ideology.

Samuel's ideological redirection should function as an internally fictional **counterculture** to the Fitzgerald leadership: it is meant to corrode what they value and reproduce a younger population with different loyalties and morals. The exact content should be invented for the story and should avoid importing real-world persecuted groups or inflammatory historical labels into the bible.

The author's real-world comparisons are development-only references and must **not** be copied into the story bible or canon notes. Preserve only the fictional functions: controlled lineage planning, ideological youth formation, and Samuel's generational counter-programming.
