## Accepted SC-002 macro findings

### Question 1 — accepted

The most important takeover actions that **do not** follow automatically from Samuel's alpha status are the transfer/reactivation of the Fitzgerald **breeding/lineage program** and the **training system for the younger generation** inside a structure where Samuel has superior standing and influence.

Konrad must participate in or accept the arrangement. Samuel persuades Konrad that Konrad is effectively locked out of these systems unless he accepts Samuel's proposed deal. The deal is false or misleading: Konrad believes it restores or preserves Fitzgerald control, while Samuel retains meaningful access, influence, information, and decision-making authority through his office and alpha position.

The author's real-world historical comparisons are development-only references and must **not** be copied into story-bible or canon material. Preserve only the fictional functions: controlled lineage planning, ideological youth formation, and Samuel's generational counter-programming.

### Question 2 — accepted

Samuel keeps the alpha position because of his original placement in the colonization containment structure. Once Konrad accepts the false deal and moves/reactivates the Fitzgerald breeding/lineage and younger-generation training initiatives inside Samuel's sphere, Samuel becomes structurally **alpha over those initiatives**.

Konrad is not removed. He remains the **beta leader** with substantial real operating and decision-making authority and therefore continues pursuing the Fitzgerald plan as if it remains under his own control.

Samuel's control depends heavily on a **story environment and enforced false separation**. Konrad is made to believe that his breeding program, younger-generation training, and Fitzgerald group are operating in a separate protected environment. He sees primarily the actions, outputs, and apparent progress of his own side. Samuel's interventions, competing decisions, surveillance, and corruption of the broader system are hidden from him.

The deception persists until the later Sylvan conflict approaches its end. At that point, **Sylvan exposes Samuel's real hidden actions and control to Konrad**, collapsing the false separation. Exact evidence, timing, and disclosure mechanics are deferred to later evidence/endgame tasks.

### Question 3 — accepted

Samuel's most important hidden intervention in the lineage system is **not** deciding which bloodlines simply continue or end. He allows Konrad to believe that the program is still building the population according to Konrad's original obsessive supremacist design while secretly corrupting the selection criteria so that the resulting population increasingly contradicts the traits and categories Konrad's ideology privileges.

The story must frame this correctly: the targeted traits are not objectively inferior. They are traits **Konrad's racist, eugenic, and supremacist worldview devalues**. Samuel weaponizes Konrad's own prejudices against the program in order to destroy the project Konrad believes is creating a superior population.

The same long intervention extends into younger-generation formation. Samuel uses his hidden position to redirect later generations away from the values, loyalties, and moral assumptions the senior Fitzgerald leadership is trying to reproduce.

Samuel's motive is personal as well as strategic. Konrad's betrayal, condescension, and persecution of Samuel's countercultural beliefs become more obsessive for Samuel after the secret surveillance gives him access to what Konrad and the senior leadership actually say and intend. Samuel increasingly turns the lineage and youth systems into instruments of revenge.

## Macro result

Samuel's eventual advantage no longer depends on vague access or omnipotence:

- **Automatic from alpha/office:** superior cross-domain visibility, surveillance, influence, information, and meaningful decision authority once an initiative is operating inside Samuel's containment sphere.
- **Not automatic:** ownership or migration of Fitzgerald lineage and youth-training systems. Konrad must authorize, accept, reconnect, or otherwise enable their movement/reactivation.
- **Konrad retains:** genuine beta leadership and substantial operating authority inside the program he believes remains separate and under Fitzgerald control.
- **Samuel gains through deception:** structural alpha standing over the migrated initiatives plus the ability to conceal competing interventions through the story environment.
- **Primary hidden intervention:** Samuel secretly corrupts the lineage-selection and generational-training outcomes while showing Konrad a false picture of continued Fitzgerald success.
- **Later exposure:** Sylvan eventually reveals Samuel's hidden actions to Konrad near the end of the later Sylvan conflict.

This is sufficient at Macro Shape. Detailed permission weighting, specific lineage mechanics, exact surveillance channel, proof, and Sylvan's disclosure remain for later tasks.
