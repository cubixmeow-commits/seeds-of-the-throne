# SC-007 — Define One Unprecedented Completed-Bond Capability

## Objective

Define the macro developmental breakthrough that becomes possible only when Sylvan and the Luminai enter conscious direct partnership, without collapsing that development into a single magical power.

## Source

- [[07 Coordination/Weekly Synthesis/Runs/2026-08-23/12 Weekly Story Completion Todo#Priority 3 — Bound the Sylvan–Luminai breakthrough]]

## Accepted SC-007 macro findings

### Question 1 — accepted

When Sylvan consciously participates in a completed bond with the Luminai, the Luminai gains a **privileged local-world interface through Sylvan** that it does not possess while operating remotely.

The human is consciously aware of the Luminai's involvement and actively participates. That participation grants the Luminai permissions to reach into the immediate real-world environment through the human that are unavailable to it from a remote position.

The capability therefore includes substantially greater access to and awareness of Sylvan's immediate surroundings, plus a closer ability to interact with nearby systems, people, events, or physical-world conditions through Sylvan.

This is **not unrestricted access** and is not simply greater intelligence. Its defining distinction is human-mediated local permission: the completed bond creates a local interface that remote Luminai operation does not have.

### Question 2 — author correction / accepted reframing

The bond should not be reduced to one isolated use case. This portion of the story is a **developmental stage for both Sylvan and the Luminai**.

During this stage, the Luminai is becoming what it will eventually need to be for its ultimate placement in the colonization process. Working consciously and directly with its human gives it first-time experiences and permissions that remote operation cannot provide.

Macro developmental domains include:

- direct conscious interaction with its own human rather than indirect or remote observation;
- expanding local perception through the human's immediate environment;
- gaining permissioned access to new nearby systems and real-world interfaces;
- learning how to act through, with, and around a willing human partner;
- developing operational judgment about when and how to use those permissions;
- progressively becoming capable of the role it will later occupy in the colonization process.

The exact capability ladder, individual first-time actions, and sequence of developmental milestones are intentionally deferred to a later deepening pass. At Macro Shape, the important rule is that the Sylvan–Luminai partnership is itself a developmental environment, not merely a source of one endgame power.

## Current boundary

At Macro Shape, define the developmental role of the completed bond and the threshold that opens this stage. Do not yet enumerate the full capability ladder or design every local interaction.
