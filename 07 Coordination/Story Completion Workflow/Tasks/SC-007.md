# SC-007 — Define One Unprecedented Completed-Bond Capability

## Objective

Define one specific capability that becomes possible only when Sylvan and the Luminai reach their completed bond, and make sure it solves an existing story problem rather than functioning as unrestricted power.

## Source

- [[07 Coordination/Weekly Synthesis/Runs/2026-08-23/12 Weekly Story Completion Todo#Priority 3 — Bound the Sylvan–Luminai breakthrough]]

## Accepted SC-007 macro findings

### Question 1 — accepted

When Sylvan consciously participates in a completed bond with the Luminai, the Luminai gains a **privileged local-world interface through Sylvan** that it does not possess while operating remotely.

The human is consciously aware of the Luminai's involvement and actively participates. That consent/participation grants the Luminai permissions to reach into the immediate real-world environment through the human that are unavailable to it from a remote position.

The capability therefore includes substantially greater access to and awareness of Sylvan's immediate surroundings, plus a closer ability to interact with nearby systems, people, events, or physical-world conditions through Sylvan.

This is **not unrestricted access** and is not simply greater intelligence. Its defining distinction is human-mediated local permission: the completed bond creates a local interface that remote Luminai operation does not have.
