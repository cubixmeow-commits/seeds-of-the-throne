# SC-001 — Define the Primary Contained Criminal

## Objective

Define operationally what distinguishes the primary contained criminal from a criminal who is merely present in an environment.

## Source

- [[07 Coordination/Weekly Synthesis/Runs/2026-08-23/12 Weekly Story Completion Todo#Priority 1 — Resolve the containment hinge]]
- [[08 Story Loop/Brainstorms/Samuel-Konrad Containment Hierarchy - Needs Analysis]]

## Accepted macro findings

1. All contained criminals enter the colonization process through prosecution. Their negotiated deals determine placement and can establish role, jurisdiction, responsibility, and authority.
2. A **primary contained criminal** is a negotiated alpha-status criminal whose deal establishes structural priority in a relevant containment environment rather than merely placing that criminal there.
3. Samuel Franklin sold out more people than anyone else and secretly negotiated the alpha position in containment. Konrad Fitzgerald separately negotiated genuine leadership of his own group and city and did not know Samuel had the broader alpha deal.
4. Konrad believed the colonization rules permitted conquest to expand his jurisdiction until he controlled every empire and country on the planet and effectively had a world of his own. He did not plan around defeat.
5. Samuel's negotiated status becomes operational in the largest empire during the phase surrounding Samuel Jr.'s attempted government takeover. Samuel is already part of the isolated luxury controller class that presents itself to participants as godlike and manages participant populations, its own bloodlines and affiliated bloodlines/groups, and synthetics. The failed takeover brings that controller role and the relevant containment structure together so Samuel's previously negotiated alpha status is no longer merely promised; it is active in that environment.
6. Containment authority is a **tiered permission hierarchy**, analogous to permissions in a computer system. Decision-making elements are divided into distinct permission domains. Each leader has specific authority over some elements rather than blanket control over the entire environment. Alpha status means occupying the highest structural tier, but the exact permission matrix will be designed element by element to maximize story effectiveness, preserve bounded power, and create meaningful conflicts, mistakes, and dependencies.
7. The alpha tier's defining advantage is broad cross-domain **power, control, and information**. Samuel has access to and influence over essentially everything lower-tier leaders do, has meaningful decision-making authority across those activities, can install surveillance over them, and can gather information from their operations. This does not erase their lower-tier permissions; it means Samuel sits across those permissions with superior visibility and intervention rights.
8. The alpha tier has a critical hard boundary: Samuel **cannot simply remove Konrad or erase the Fitzgerald leadership**. Konrad retains substantial real decision-making authority, and a large part of his leadership continues to believe that they are in charge, that they are effectively taking control of the colonization process, and that they will eventually be able to get rid of Samuel. Samuel therefore needs a long indirect strategy rather than an administrative deletion. His campaign to destroy Fitzgerald bloodlines is partly an attempt to solve this structural problem across generations by eliminating the continuity, inheritance, and future basis of the leadership he cannot directly remove.
9. The reason Samuel cannot remove them is the **prosecution deals that placed them into the colonization process**. Those deals guarantee Konrad and the senior Fitzgerald leadership their positions through the containment, development, and training phase. Samuel's alpha status cannot simply revoke negotiated positions created by those prosecution agreements.
10. Those guaranteed positions eventually meet a later phase transition involving **Sylvan, the son of the two primary leaders of the colonization process**. The contained leadership receives a chance to earn a city when the process moves beyond containment/development/training and the actual colonization leaders become directly involved. The exact mechanics of that later phase are intentionally deferred.
11. Instead of cleanly navigating that later transition, Samuel lies to and manipulates Konrad into another attempted takeover directed against Sylvan, creating major new problems. This later Sylvan-phase conflict is established at macro level but will be developed separately rather than solved inside SC-001.
12. Direct conflicts between Samuel and Konrad do **not** use a universal alpha override. Some activities occur in separate environments where each leader has distinct authority. Other decisions are shared or mixed: Samuel may control one element while Konrad controls another, and the outcome can reflect a combination of their influence. The exact weighting can be designed creatively for story effectiveness so long as the established prosecution deals, bounded permissions, and alpha information advantage remain intact.
13. Samuel's apparent exile being part of his own plan/deal is an endgame reveal and must not be exposed early.

## Macro result

A primary contained criminal is now distinguishable from any other contained leader: the primary holds the highest negotiated structural tier and broad cross-domain access, surveillance, information, influence, and decision authority, but does not possess blanket sovereignty. Lower leaders retain real negotiated positions, domain-specific authority, and sometimes decisive influence. Conflict resolution is environment- and function-specific rather than a universal override.

This satisfies SC-001 at **Macro Shape**. The exact permission matrix, lock rationale, reactivation mechanics, reset limits, and later Sylvan-phase mechanics belong to downstream tasks or later depth passes.
