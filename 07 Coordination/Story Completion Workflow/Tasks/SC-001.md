# SC-001 — Define the Primary Contained Criminal

## Objective

Define operationally what distinguishes the primary contained criminal from a criminal who is merely present in an environment.

## Source

- [[07 Coordination/Weekly Synthesis/Runs/2026-08-23/12 Weekly Story Completion Todo#Priority 1 — Resolve the containment hinge]]
- [[08 Story Loop/Brainstorms/Samuel-Konrad Containment Hierarchy - Needs Analysis]]

## Accepted macro findings

1. All contained criminals enter the colonization process through prosecution. Their negotiated deals determine placement and can establish role, jurisdiction, responsibility, and authority.
2. A **primary contained criminal** is a negotiated alpha-status criminal whose deal establishes structural priority in a relevant containment environment rather than merely placing that criminal there.
3. Samuel Franklin sold out more people than anyone else and secretly negotiated the alpha position in containment. Konrad Fitzgerald separately negotiated genuine leadership of his own group and city and did not know Samuel had the broader alpha deal.
4. Konrad believed the colonization rules permitted conquest to expand his jurisdiction until he controlled every empire and country on the planet and effectively had a world of his own. He did not plan around defeat.
5. Samuel's negotiated status becomes operational in the largest empire during the phase surrounding Samuel Jr.'s attempted government takeover. Samuel is already part of the isolated luxury controller class that presents itself to participants as godlike and manages participant populations, its own bloodlines and affiliated bloodlines/groups, and synthetics. The failed takeover brings that controller role and the relevant containment structure together so Samuel's previously negotiated alpha status is no longer merely promised; it is active in that environment.
6. Containment authority is a **tiered permission hierarchy**, analogous to permissions in a computer system. Decision-making elements are divided into distinct permission domains. Each leader has specific authority over some elements rather than blanket control over the entire environment. Alpha status means occupying the highest structural tier, but the exact permission matrix will be designed element by element to maximize story effectiveness, preserve bounded power, and create meaningful conflicts, mistakes, and dependencies.
7. The alpha tier's defining advantage is broad cross-domain **power, control, and information**. Samuel has access to and influence over essentially everything lower-tier leaders do, has meaningful decision-making authority across those activities, can install surveillance over them, and can gather information from their operations. This does not erase their lower-tier permissions; it means Samuel sits across those permissions with superior visibility and intervention rights.
8. Samuel's apparent exile being part of his own plan/deal is an endgame reveal and must not be exposed early.
