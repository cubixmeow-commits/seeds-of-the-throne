# SC-005 — Define Sylvan's Irreversible Choice and Loss

## Objective

Define the bounded choice Sylvan makes as the startup attacks escalate and the concrete loss that closes off an easier path back, so the modern inciting sequence changes his life because of both antagonist pressure and his own decision.

## Inherited macro rules

- The first modern incident is intentionally small and plausibly coincidental.
- Sylvan's physical server-hosting center loses external connectivity for several hours during a convention presentation.
- He can still present, but cannot perform the live demo.
- The hosting provider gives a plausible but slightly suspicious explanation.
- The event causes no major immediate business loss; its importance is that it is the first incident in a long sequence of attacks that later escalates substantially.

## Current boundary

At Macro Shape, do not design the entire escalation sequence. Identify the kind of decision Sylvan eventually makes in response to repeated attacks and what real thing that decision costs him. The choice should be understandable, bounded, and consequential rather than a generic refusal to give up.

## Current author gate

**Question 1:** As the attacks escalate beyond the harmless convention outage, what does Sylvan eventually choose to do that he could have avoided by walking away from the startup or accepting defeat?

Define only the major choice. The exact attack that forces it and the full consequences can be developed in later gates.
