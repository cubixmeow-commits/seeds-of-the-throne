# SC-005 — Define Sylvan's Irreversible Choice and Loss

## Objective

Define how the escalating startup attacks irreversibly change Sylvan's life and force the modern story into its next environment.

## Source

- [[07 Coordination/Weekly Synthesis/Runs/2026-08-23/12 Weekly Story Completion Todo#Priority 2 — Make the modern story actually begin]]
- [[SC-004]]

## Inherited macro rules

- The first modern incident is intentionally small and plausibly coincidental.
- Sylvan's physical server-hosting center loses external connectivity for several hours during a convention presentation.
- He can still present, but cannot perform the live demo.
- The hosting provider gives a plausible but slightly suspicious explanation.
- The event causes no major immediate business loss; its importance is that it is the first incident in a long sequence of attacks that later escalates substantially.

## Accepted SC-005 macro result

The startup collapse is **not** caused by a single optional heroic gamble Sylvan could avoid by walking away. The attacks descend into increasing chaos until the startup's social structure is deliberately broken around him.

Samuel and George manipulate Sylvan's partners inside a **separate wireless/story environment**, making promises and shaping their interpretation of events outside Sylvan's awareness. Over time the partners turn against Sylvan.

The irreversible loss is therefore the collapse of the startup team and the trust/working relationships that made the venture viable. By the time the structure fails, there is no clean path where Sylvan simply chooses to stop and preserves what existed.

The consequence is a **forced transition**: Sylvan leaves the failed startup environment and moves into the next stage of the story in another city.

At Macro Shape, this replaces the earlier assumption that Sylvan must make one avoidable decision that causes the collapse. His agency should instead be developed in how he responds to the forced breakdown and what he does in the next environment.

## Boundary

Do not yet design the full escalating attack sequence, the exact promises made to each partner, or the detailed mechanics of the separate wireless environment. Those can be developed in later sweeps/tasks.
