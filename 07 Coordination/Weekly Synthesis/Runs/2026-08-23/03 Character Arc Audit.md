## Relationship and pressure changes

- Samuel and Konrad now form the story's strongest antagonist relationship: each uses the other's vanity while contempt and dependency coexist.
- Samuel and George require clearer two-way causality. George is manipulated, but Samuel should also depend on George for something George can withhold.
- Sylvan and Orzai have complementary cognition but no dramatized conflict yet. Their first disagreement should concern a specific evidence/disclosure threshold with real risk on both sides.
- The Witness and partner relationship can give the historical thread emotional immediacy before the modern archive exists.
- Samuel Jr. and Orzai's brother risk becoming symmetrical plot devices unless their institutions and personal obligations differ sharply.

## Characters acting only because plot requires it

- **Highest risk: Sylvan's parents.** Without defined intervention limits, their non-intervention can look like plot convenience.
- **High risk: George.** Decades of belief require repeatable evidence categories, genuine rewards, and moments when he actively rejects corrective information.
- **High risk: Konrad after defeat.** The restoration lie needs an operational offer and verification failure, not psychology alone.
- **Moderate risk: the Witness.** The role currently exists to carry evidence forward; it needs a personal objective and cost inside the Great War.
- **Moderate risk: Orzai.** She must enter through her own work or decision rather than being assigned as evidence courier and partner.

## Voice/artifact opportunities

- Samuel's prediction/observation pages can prove brilliance before obsession bends the model.
- Konrad's ledger can place accurate shrinking totals beneath undefeated headings.
- Sylvan's notebook can preserve alternatives, permission, and the moment George stops explaining timing.
- Orzai's overlays can separate observed fact from persuasive representation and establish a consent boundary.
- George's journal can reveal copied formatting becoming individual only when the false identity loosens.
- The Witness needs an artifact designed around authorization risk and the need for one trusted reader.

## Promotion or definition needs

No promotion is recommended from this audit. The next definitions should be: primary contained criminal; modern sabotage/cost; Luminai capability/limit; Witness identity and artifact path; and the terminal evidence mechanism. Journal designs remain working and candidate, not approved.
