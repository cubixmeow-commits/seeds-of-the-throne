## Journals and handwriting

- Samuel's prediction/observation pages can establish genuine social intelligence and later objective drift.
- Konrad's accurate totals under victorious headings can foreshadow defeat denial without private confession.
- Sylvan's provenance brackets and retained alternatives can show honest inference formation.
- Orzai's overlays can distinguish observed data from constructed meaning and consent.
- George requires a separate design pass before journal evidence use.
- No handwriting similarity is proof without access, custody, comparison clusters, and alternatives.

## Genealogy and lineage certification

The payoff requires at least four distinguishable categories: genuine biological descendant, unauthorized biological descendant, falsified documentary branch, and synthetic substitution. Public proof should establish systematic manipulation without publishing private parentage unnecessarily.

## Surveillance and permissions

Every surveillance-derived fact needs actor, source channel, permission/abuse, time window, blind spot, preserved log, and independent corroboration. Samuel must never receive information solely because the plot needs him to know it.

## Behavioral fingerprints and recurrence

Use repeated timing, correction order, phrasing, permission routes, and response patterns rather than one signature flourish. The most powerful endgame fingerprint may be that the system receives exactly the intervention needed to preserve George's belief whenever contradiction appears.

## False histories and correction sequence

The archive should reveal revisions in order: original event -> immediate official record -> first Samuel reinterpretation -> later expansion -> corrective interventions -> modern comparison. The history collapses because changes are attributable and dated, not because one journal announces the truth.
