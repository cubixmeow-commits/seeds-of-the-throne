## Ask this week

Ask Rank 1 only within the active containment session. Once accepted, ask whether the answer is specific enough to distinguish responsibility, jurisdiction, and mere presence without deciding the later lock event.

## Research before asking

No new research is required for Rank 1. Research may later help test governance safeguards, long-duration evidence custody, and bounded AI decision support, but it cannot answer story-authority questions.

## Preserve as useful ambiguity

- George's final choice and degree of redemption.
- Whether Konrad can make one genuine concession.
- Exact calendar dates.
- Final narrator structure until two short prototypes exist.
- Whether the Disclosure Wars are entirely historical or partly present action.
- The parent civilization's ultimate judgment of its own experiment.

## Repeated unresolved signals

The same five missing elements recur across context, Story Units, QA, and character notes: operational permissions; evidence custody; the modern initiating loss; the bounded Luminai capability; and the human cost of victory. These are structural gaps, not requests for more lore.
