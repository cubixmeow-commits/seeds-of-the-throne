## Confrontations and betrayals

The highest-value confrontations are Konrad's flattering disposal, Samuel's restoration mirror, the first doubter's plain defeat statement, Sylvan's refusal, and George's confrontation with Samuel's true use for him.

## Discoveries and evidence reveals

Prioritize discoveries that change what a character can responsibly do: the exile record, recurrence, the prewar/postwar George contradiction, the timing anomaly, and the continued-maintenance proof.

## Missing transitions and aftermaths

The story needs aftermath scenes for George's bust, the Great War processing transition, startup collapse, the end of humiliation, entry into employment, first public warning, and post-exposure privacy/accountability decisions.

## Ordinary-life scenes carrying hidden pressure

Sylvan's errands, outdoor movement, bicycle routes, work orders, service calls, and coworker interactions can establish baselines. Orzai's client reviews, meal-system observation, video edits, 3D revisions, and community feedback can carry evidence ethics without visible science-fiction spectacle.

## Best representative prototype candidate

**Orzai refuses a misleading health visualization.** It is bounded, immediately understandable, exercises her independent agency, tests the surface-era setting, creates a durable artifact trail, and does not require solving the containment hierarchy or endgame technology. Keep it 700–1,000 words and non-canon until the author selects the project and cost.
