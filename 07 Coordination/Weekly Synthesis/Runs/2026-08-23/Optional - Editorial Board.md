## Agreements

- The project has a strong macro premise, antagonist mirror, and endgame moral choice.
- The next gains will come from causal bridges and dramatized decisions, not additional broad lore.
- S-008 is the correct active author-led boundary.
- Sylvan's capability must be bounded and foreshadowed.
- Orzai's independent professional refusal is the best low-dependency prototype candidate.
- The eighty-year middle should be curated, not exhaustively narrated.

## Productive disagreements

- The structural lens favors resolving the Witness early to anchor the middle; the first-time-reader lens favors modern Sylvan material first to establish immediate attachment. Test both opening strategies before deciding viewpoint architecture.
- The adversarial lens wants operational technology rules early; the character lens warns that rules without a choice scene will not create emotional meaning. Develop mechanism and representative human decision together.
- The continuity lens recommends terminology cleanup, while the development lens warns against naming institutions prematurely. A neutral functional phrase can bridge the two until names are approved.

## False consensus risks

The board's agreement that S-008 is highest leverage may reflect current-work visibility. S-002 and the Luminai capability are equally important to the reader-facing novel. Do not let historical mechanism work indefinitely delay the protagonist's initiating event.
