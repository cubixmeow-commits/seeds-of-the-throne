## Beginning-to-ending causal spine

`Konrad disguises disposal as opportunity -> Samuel makes a parallel containment bet -> Konrad loses the war he never planned to lose -> Samuel sells subordination as restoration -> Konrad migrates his machinery into the shared hierarchy -> Samuel turns access into an eighty-year revenge system -> the faction attacks Sylvan -> the ordeal develops Sylvan and his Luminai -> their tests expose contradictions -> Samuel cannot stop intervening -> continued command becomes evidence -> the false realities collapse.`
