## Journals and handwriting

Use journals as viewpoint-limited records with known creators, dates, corrections, and physical custody. Handwriting can authenticate authorship but cannot prove the truth of the writer's interpretation.

## Genealogy and lineage certification

Keep private recognition of unauthorized children distinct from later public proof. Research supports a possible sealed/public two-layer model, but this is not selected.

## Surveillance and permissions

Separate what Samuel can observe automatically, what Konrad exposes by reactivation, what requires active manipulation, and what remains outside Samuel's reach.

## Behavioral fingerprints and recurrence
