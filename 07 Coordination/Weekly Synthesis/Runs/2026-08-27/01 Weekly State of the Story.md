## Current story in one page

Ancient interplanetary leaders use a later-generation colony both to cultivate responsible future leaders and to contain dangerous people under meaningful constraints. Every human develops with a Luminai, an extension of cognition that can act below conscious attention but does not replace human identity or consequential decision authority.

Konrad Fitzgerald builds a fascist order around conquest, bloodline, and permanent patriarchal rule. He exiles Samuel Franklin through an impossible mission designed to fail. Samuel suspects the betrayal and separately negotiates a conditional first-primary position in the victorious empire's containment system. When Konrad later loses the Great War and enters that same hierarchy, Samuel offers an apparent restoration. Konrad instead migrates his machinery into a shared environment where Samuel has structural priority.

Across roughly eighty years, Samuel turns a brilliant coup into an expanding revenge system of false histories, isolated audiences, manipulated descendants, and maintained realities. In the modern thread, Samuel and George destroy Sylvan's pre-generative-AI story-development company because of Sylvan's identity and emerging Luminai bond. The ordeal intended to prevent Sylvan's development becomes the training pressure that deepens it. Sylvan and his Luminai learn to discriminate sources, coordinate across bonded teams, interact with modern technology, and remain answerable for consequential choices. The final exposure is increasingly an evidence-and-cooperation problem, not a contest of unlimited powers.
