## Chronology

Samuel's age anchor, thirty-year prewar exile, roughly eighty-year coup, George's role sequence, and wider integration-to-disclosure span still need common anchors. No contradiction was resolved by inventing dates.

## Character knowledge and agency

The strongest current design gives Samuel asymmetrical knowledge, not omniscience. Konrad must possess enough genuine evidence of autonomy to act rationally within his ideology. George must retain meaningful choices even inside a maintained reality.

## Causality and convenience

Every Samuel lie should be mapped to the person who believes it, the action it produces, the benefit it creates, the contradiction it generates, and the later consequence. This is the cleanest safeguard against villain exposition replacing causality.

## Systems, permissions, and limits

Human decision authority, shared-environment alpha priority, story functionality, modern-device integration, and cross-bond coordination must use one coherent permission vocabulary. The research offers vocabulary, not answers.

## Evidence, custody, and authentication

The project has many strong evidence families but not yet a complete chain from creation to custody, authentication, protected comparison, public consequence, and descendant privacy.
