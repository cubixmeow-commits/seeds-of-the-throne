## Relationship and pressure changes

Samuel and Konrad now form the central causal relationship: each man's manipulation of the other reverses an earlier hierarchy. Sylvan and Orzai form the positive counterstructure through consent, independent judgment, and shared verification. George shows how the criminal system consumes its descendants.

## Characters acting only because plot requires it

Konrad will feel plot-driven unless the evidence supporting his false autonomy is concrete. George will feel passive unless at least one earlier choice could have interrupted Samuel's script. Orzai risks becoming functional support unless she owns an independent professional and moral consequence.

## Voice/artifact opportunities

Konrad and Samuel's journals can preserve incompatible readings of the same transfer. Sylvan's records can show hypothesis revision rather than perfect intuition. Orzai's visual work can reveal a refusal to simplify evidence. Candidate journal art remains unapproved.
