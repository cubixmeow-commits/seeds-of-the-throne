## Dispositions

| Intake signal/source | Existing task relationship | Disposition | Reason | TODO or registry change proposed |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Use one disposition:

- `ALREADY COVERED` — link the source to the existing task; do not duplicate it.
- `REVISE EXISTING` — clarify or expand an existing task after author approval.
- `PROMOTE NEW` — add a genuinely load-bearing task after author approval.
- `LEAVE OPEN` — preserve it with a concrete revisit trigger.
- `NO TODO CHANGE` — integrate useful context or maintenance without creating story work.
