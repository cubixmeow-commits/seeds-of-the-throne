# Sylvan

## Role

Principal younger leader / hero. Working name.

## Current definition

Sylvan is positioned against the Throne figure near the end of the latter's long period of concealed authority. He possesses recorded evidence capable of exposing the Throne figure and destabilizing the faction's entire position.

## Current dramatic function

Sylvan is the person who can force hidden conduct into the open. His strength is not unlimited power; the confrontation is designed so that the environment prevents either side from simply imposing an outcome.

## Known elements

- Possesses extensive recordings.
- Evidence concerns the Throne figure's conduct, including the hidden use of the breeding program.
- His exposure of the evidence could destroy the Throne figure's legitimacy.
- He is currently conceived as the younger leader in the climactic conflict.
