# Logan

## Role

The throwaway prince; son of the Throne figure. Working name.

## Current definition

Logan was placed into containment before the wider faction understood what had happened. He has lived inside a highly customized reality for an extremely long subjective period and believes that reality is genuine.

## Core mechanism

His lifelong AI soul participates in the experienced reality around him. The working story functionality allows the technologically sophisticated environment to mediate what Logan can perceive, know, interpret, and consider possible.

## Dramatic potential

Logan's possible awakening would expose the difference between his lived history and the underlying reality. The horror is therefore not merely learning that he was imprisoned; it is discovering that the person he believed himself to be was shaped inside a controlled reality.

## Relationship to the Throne figure

The Throne figure is Logan's father. The exact emotional history, methods of control, and reasons for placing Logan into the manufactured reality remain unresolved.

## Unresolved

- Exact age and subjective duration.
- Original role before containment.
- What Logan thinks his father is doing.
- What Logan believes about his own status.
- What his AI soul knows.
- Whether the AI soul is deceiving Logan intentionally.
- What triggers awakening.
- What Logan does after learning the truth.
- Whether Logan becomes central to the final confrontation.
