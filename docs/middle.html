The missing middle becomes a story engine

Eighty years.
Many smaller stories. One accumulating history.

The decades between the Great War and Sylvan do not need to become exposition. They can generate semi-independent episodes involving future leaders, contained criminals, public roles, AI development, institutions, relationships, atrocities, discoveries, failures, and resistance.

01

Constrained roles

Future leaders can experience political, religious, institutional, or other public roles with real authority but hard limits. They must decide what can actually be changed from inside the role.

02

Recurring consequences

A decision in one episode can alter institutions, relationships, evidence, AI development, or faction behavior decades later.

03

Convergence

The episodes gradually explain the civilization Sylvan enters and create the evidence, people, grudges, capabilities, and unresolved problems that reach the endgame.

Episode question

What can a person do when they understand the system they are inside but still have to act through the limits of their role?
