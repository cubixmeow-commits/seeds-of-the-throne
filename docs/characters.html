Character atlas

Everyone inherits a world. Not everyone accepts its terms.

The central cast is divided less by blood or technology than by what they do when reality contradicts the future they expected.

Formation through shared reality.
Manufacture through isolation.

Established name and central role

Sylvan Elaria · the Inheritor

He builds connections before quieter evidence is ready.

Outgoing, socially curious, and verbally exploratory, Sylvan creates momentum across people and systems. That strength also exposes him to overextension, misplaced trust, and moving before uncertainty has matured.

After his company collapses, his real task is not to seize a stronger throne. It is to survive hostile systems without allowing them to make his choices for him.

Wants
Build useful systems and remain in contact with reality.
Risk
Mistaking openness for earned trust.
Trajectory
Builder → dispossessed learner → investigator → bounded leader.
02

Orzai · emerging leader

She notices what a persuasive picture leaves out.

Established name, generation, and temperament

Orzai is observant, deliberate, selective in disclosure, and resistant to crowd pressure. She builds fewer relationships at first, then builds them deeply.

Her independent working path joins health, nutrition, creative design, video, and 3D modeling, eventually pointing toward population-scale care and habitat systems. Her challenge is knowing when caution has become harmful delay.

Working
Health visualization → interactive training → colonization-scale human systems.
Unresolved
Education, first employer, defining project, first costly refusal, and first meeting with Sylvan.
Established antagonist

Samuel Franklin · the hidden maintainer

His best model of other people cannot survive his need to control them.

Samuel is a near-genius reader of motives, insecurities, status needs, and self-deception. His danger comes from real ability. His failure grows from what obsession, jealousy, grievance, and habitual lying do to that ability over time.

He builds dependencies one person at a time, controls interpretation, and keeps intervening after the strategic value is gone—because stopping would allow reality back in.

Wants
Status, revenge, and finally replacement.
Method
Isolation, tailored meaning, and controlled evidence.
Trajectory
Failed heir → manipulator → usurper → captive of his own maintenance system.

The wider pressure system

Three people carry three different forms of captivity.

Established

Konrad Fitzgerald

A brilliant ideological organizer and conquest leader whose greatest weakness is an inability to accept consequential defeat. He builds believers through belonging, certainty, and permanent hierarchy.

Established

George White

Samuel’s son, an active participant whose perception has also been managed. His story asks what responsibility remains when a person receives real choices inside a falsified horizon.

Working role

The Great War Witness

One of Orzai’s parents is the leading candidate: a constrained earlier-era heir with access to recurrence and evidence, but not the authority to solve what they see.
