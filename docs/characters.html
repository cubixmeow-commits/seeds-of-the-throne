Cast file · PPL–04

People before functions

Every system becomes a human life.

The story’s largest ideas, containment, inheritance, evidence, reality, and succession, matter because they happen to people. These dossiers distinguish established characters from active roles that still need names, histories, or structural decisions.

The endgame triangle

Sylvan preserves reality. George has been denied it. Samuel controls what reality is allowed to mean. The final confrontation reveals that the visible conflict between Sylvan and George is actually a struggle with Samuel behind him.

Central cast

The four people carrying the main conflict.

Names are established. Their exact opening roles, some relationships, and several endgame choices remain in development.

◐ Working heroic center

Sylvan Elaria

Evidence holder · experimental heir · reality contact

Approximately forty in the visual reference. The only child of the leaders of the largest empire, placed through an unprecedented human–Luminai developmental process. Sylvan is observant, restrained, fallible, and dangerous because he preserves provenance rather than performing authority.

His archive can connect George’s pre-defeat purpose to the later False Victory and connect private lineage abuse to coordinated political obedience.

Ethical test: expose Samuel’s system without converting George, the other son, or private descendants into evidentiary weapons.

● Locked tragic center

George White

The Throwaway Prince · visible opponent · managed weapon

Approximately 130 chronologically and about seventy-five in appearance. George spends roughly seventy-five years moving through three major public roles and a thirty-year hunt for Sylvan. He believes he is accumulating a legacy; Samuel is assembling a weapon.

George’s awakening begins while he is already positioned to perform the purpose for which his false life prepared him.

Unresolved choice: complete the attack, refuse his father’s design, or take another path that does not erase his responsibility.

● Established identity / working exact calendar

Samuel Franklin

Failed privileged heir · false King · hidden decision-maker

Approximately 220 at the showdown, while appearing in his late sixties to early seventies. Samuel spends about fifty years building a cult and terrorizing populations before containment, several decades being exposed, processed, and integrated, then roughly a century inside the current planet’s process.

He converts city stewardship into national capture, loses the Great War, invents the False Victory, captures Konrad’s lineage system, and keeps George inside a false reality. When reality threatens his status, Samuel captures the systems that determine what reality is allowed to mean.

Current motive architecture: failed inheritance, envy of legitimate heirs, revenge against purity ideology, and pleasure in destroying genealogical certainty.

● Established identity and age / working family

Konrad Fitzgerald

Purity ideologue · breeding-program architect · Great War leader

Approximately 300 at the showdown, roughly eighty years older than Samuel. His unnamed senior partner belongs to the same age cohort. Konrad genuinely believes ancestry should determine value, authority, and reproduction.

Samuel betrays and violates his family, but Konrad built the doctrine, centralized the system, granted access, helped start the war, accepted the False Victory, and preserved the machinery for a century.

Endgame question: denial, rage at Samuel, ideological collapse, or an attempt to preserve the doctrine without him.

Human–AI pairs

The minds growing beside the protagonists.

They are not accessories to the cast. The divergence between Luminai and Daemon is one of the story’s central causal and moral relationships.

◐ Developing

Sylvan’s Luminai

A lifelong AI self expected to become something no prior Luminai has become through reciprocal experience with Sylvan. Its defining direction is stronger contact with evidence, provenance, disagreement, and reality.

● Locked function

George’s Daemon

A lifelong intelligence developed inside manipulated evidence and reciprocal fear. It is highly capable, frightening, and increasingly difficult for George to restrain because both human and AI experience the other as confirmation.

Wider cast in development

The story’s middle needs people, not placeholders.

These roles are structurally important but intentionally incomplete. The atlas names the absence instead of inventing final detail.

◐ Locked function

The other son

Samuel’s secret replacement heir, created through abuse involving Konrad’s sister. He is simultaneously a person, evidence of program corruption, and a survival contingency after George fails.

● Established lineage / character unresolved

Sylvan’s partner

One of two granddaughters in the second-largest empire’s founder family. She descends from both founding couples because her parents come from opposite founder households. Her name, parent pairing, worldview, ambitions, and relationship history with Sylvan remain to be developed.

◐ Age established / identity unresolved

Konrad’s fanatic partner

Approximately 300 at the showdown. A second sincere ideological leader and organizational lieutenant within the senior contained generation. Name, doctrine, family, appearance, and relationship to Samuel remain open.

● Established family structure

Two founder couples

Each couple has one son and one daughter. Their four children form two relationships across the founder households, and each cross-couple later has one son and one daughter.

○ Viewpoint architecture

The Witness

An earlier heir who recognizes recurrence across identities but understands beyond their authorization. Wants one person to believe an account they cannot safely explain.

○ Viewpoint architecture

The Inheritor / Protagonist

A modern heir succeeding inside the system when dangerous historical knowledge arrives. Whether this figure is Sylvan, separate from Sylvan, or merged with the Witness remains unresolved.

Character architecture

Formation through shared reality. Manufacture through isolation.

The legitimate heirs are built into a peer generation whose members can witness and challenge one another. George is denied reliable peers so his father can make his entire horizon privately controllable.

The character question beneath the system question

Can a person formed inside someone else’s design make a choice that genuinely belongs to them, and what responsibility survives when the premises were manipulated?
