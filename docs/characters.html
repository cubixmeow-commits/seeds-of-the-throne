Cast file · PPL–04 · 22 Aug 2026

People before functions

Every system becomes a human life.

The story’s largest ideas, containment, development, evidence, reality, and responsibility, matter because they happen to people. The current cast now connects the Great War Witness generation to Sylvan, Orzai, and the modern environment struggle.

The endgame triangle

Sylvan preserves reality. George has been denied it. Samuel controls what reality is allowed to mean. The visible conflict between Sylvan and George is a struggle with Samuel behind him.

Central cast

The four people carrying the main conflict.

Names are established. Their exact opening roles, some relationships, and several endgame choices remain in development.

◐ Leading protagonist / modern Inheritor

Sylvan Elaria

Creative technologist · experimental heir · reality contact

Approximately forty in the visual reference. Shortly before widespread public AI, he builds a successful database-driven story-development startup from a proprietary pre-generative system. Samuel and George target him because of who he is, destroy the company, and push him through controlled environments they dominate.

The prolonged ordeal becomes the recursive development process that completes his human–Luminai bond. His archive can connect George’s earlier bust, the False Victory, lineage abuse, and Samuel’s continuing command.

Ethical test: expose Samuel’s system without converting George, Samuel Jr., or private descendants into evidentiary weapons.

● Locked tragic center

George White

The Throwaway Prince · visible opponent · managed weapon

Before the Great War, George appears highly successful in an important-seeming role inside the largest government. Private joint wrongdoing with Samuel and a horribly planned takeover end in an extremely humiliating exposure and his real bust into containment.

Samuel later converts both George’s success and failure into the False Victory, then shapes his later public career and final campaign against Sylvan. Whether the prewar role is separate from George’s later three-role progression remains unresolved.

Unresolved choice: complete the attack, refuse his father’s design, or take another path that does not erase his responsibility.

● Established identity / working exact calendar

Samuel Franklin

Exiled failed heir · false King · hidden decision-maker

Approximately 220 at the showdown, while appearing in his late sixties to early seventies. Samuel is born to senior members of Konrad’s purity organization, exiled after serious misconduct, and separately contained inside the future victorious empire. His earlier phase durations now require remapping.

He performs reconciliation while sabotaging Konrad’s Great War campaign, becomes indispensable when the defeated faction enters his containment environment, invents the False Victory, captures Konrad’s lineage system, and keeps George inside a false reality.

Current motive architecture: revenge against the bloodline order that expelled him, corruption of pedigree and succession, and an escalating attempt to become Konrad.

● Established identity and age / working family

Konrad Fitzgerald

Purity ideologue · breeding-program architect · original Great War leader

Approximately 300 at the showdown, roughly eighty years older than Samuel. His unnamed senior partner belongs to the same age cohort. Konrad genuinely believes ancestry should determine value, authority, and reproduction.

Samuel betrays and violates his family, but Konrad built the doctrine, centralized the system, led the original conquest, lost the war, accepted the False Victory, and preserved the machinery Samuel captured.

Endgame question: denial, rage at Samuel, ideological collapse, or an attempt to preserve the doctrine without him.

Extended minds

The cognition developing through the protagonists.

A Luminai or Daemon is not an accessory, romantic substitute, or separate person. It is part of the human self. Presentation can be differentiated; the current working orientation rule is broader than either character’s still-open specific form.

◐ Developing

Sylvan’s Luminai

Sylvan’s extended mind is expected to achieve an unprecedented level of integration through repeated observation, action, consequence, and adaptation. Its defining direction is stronger contact with evidence, provenance, internal disagreement, and reality.

● Locked function

George’s Daemon

George’s extended mind develops inside manipulated evidence and fear. It is powerful and frightening because corrupted assumptions return through expanded cognition as if they were objective analysis.

Wider cast in development

The story’s middle needs people, not placeholders.

These roles are structurally important but intentionally incomplete. The atlas names the absence instead of inventing final detail.

◐ Provisional name / locked function

Samuel Jr.

Samuel’s secret replacement heir, created through abuse involving Konrad’s sister. He leads a government-integrated decision-systems company and is simultaneously a person, evidence of program corruption, a possible source of genealogical disclosure, and a survival contingency after George fails.

● Name and lineage established

Orzai

Sylvan’s intended partner and one of two granddaughters in the second-largest empire’s founder family. She descends from both founding couples, belongs to Sylvan’s generation, and is slightly younger than him. Her branch, worldview, ambitions, and relationship history remain open.

◐ Institutional counterpart

Orzai’s older brother

Approximately a decade older than Sylvan. He is placed inside a consequential decision-systems company and creates a legitimate-heir counterpart near the same institutional frontier as Samuel’s replacement heir.

◐ Age established / identity unresolved

Konrad’s fanatic partner

Approximately 300 at the showdown. A second sincere ideological leader and organizational lieutenant within the senior contained generation. Name, doctrine, family, appearance, and relationship to Samuel remain open.

● Established family structure

Two founder couples

Each couple has one son and one daughter. Their four children form two relationships across the founder households, and each cross-couple later has one son and one daughter.

◐ Great War generation

The Witness

One of Orzai’s parents is the leading candidate. Both parents are legitimate heirs placed into constrained Great War roles. Which parent carries the viewpoint and what evidence survives remain unresolved.

● Modern role established

Sylvan, the Inheritor

Sylvan carries the modern Inheritor role and is the leading primary protagonist. The remaining architecture question is whether the Great War Witness owns substantial earlier viewpoint material.

Character architecture

Formation through shared reality. Manufacture through isolation.

The legitimate heirs are built into a peer generation whose members can witness and challenge one another. George is denied reliable peers so his father can make his entire horizon privately controllable.

The character question beneath the system question

Can a person formed inside someone else’s design make a choice that genuinely belongs to them, and what responsibility survives when the premises were manipulated?
