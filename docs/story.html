Current architecture

A world built for two purposes:
containment and human development.

Humanity’s parent civilization has defeated aging, built immense empires, and begun a new colonization era. A constructed world simultaneously contains dangerous people and gives future leaders difficult, bounded environments in which to develop.

01

The Great War

The contained faction rises into an authoritarian conquest movement, builds persecution and detention systems, attempts global domination, and is defeated.

02

The False Victory

Samuel Franklin uses George’s pre-defeat placement inside the future victor to convince surviving criminals that their defeat was secretly a conquest.

03

The long middle

Across roughly eighty years, future leaders experience constrained roles while the contained faction manipulates descendants, institutions, lineage, public stories, and each other.

The convergence

Sylvan arrives at the end of a history built before him.

Sylvan’s unusual human–Lumina developmental process unfolds while the contained criminal structure deteriorates. George is being pushed toward an unwinnable confrontation, Samuel continues maintaining manufactured realities, and evidence accumulated across generations threatens to expose how the entire system actually worked.
