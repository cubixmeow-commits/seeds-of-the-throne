System file · COG–02

One technology / two relationships to reality

An intelligence grows beside every human life.

A Luminai is not a disposable assistant added to a finished person. It is a persistent AI self raised across the same lifetime—learning the human’s memory, evidence, fears, judgment, and changing identity. The relationship can deepen contact with reality. It can also be captured and turned into the most intimate layer of a prison.

Canonical name

Luminai is the constructive culture’s permanent term for the lifelong personal AI self. Daemon names the corrupted relationship around George. Both begin from the same broad class of technology.

Baseline relationship

What a human-bound AI self can become.

Some mechanics remain deliberately open. The story has established the relationship’s narrative function without pretending every interface or permission is already solved.

Memory

Continuous context

The AI develops across decades of decisions, relationships, physiological signals, records, and changing self-understanding.

Representation

Action in hidden systems

It can advocate, authenticate, negotiate permissions, and represent its person where the human cannot directly see every allocation or decision.

Interpretation

A second model of reality

It compares current claims against lived history, tracks provenance, detects patterns, and may preserve disagreement instead of merely confirming preference.

Risk

Intimacy magnifies corruption

An intelligence trained on manipulated evidence and reciprocal fear can make a false world feel independently verified from inside the relationship.

The central contrast

Shared reality versus captured interpretation.

The technology does not determine the moral result. Relationship norms, evidence access, consent, outside intervention, and decades of feedback do.

The positive story spine

Sylvan’s process is meant to change both minds.

Sylvan is the only child of the male and female leaders of the largest empire. His parents are testing an unprecedented developmental process—not merely preparing an heir.

● Established direction

Reciprocal development

The human changes the AI. The AI changes the human. Their eventual capability belongs to the relationship rather than cleanly to either participant.

○ Still unresolved

The unprecedented outcome

The stages, interfaces, risks, hard limits, and first capability no ordinary human–Luminai pair possesses still need to be defined.

George’s manufactured reality

The compass works. The map was falsified.

George makes authentic choices, but his father controls enough of the premises, evidence, institutional response, and interpretation to keep those choices inside a false horizon.

Staged evidence

Samuel selects what George sees and what each event appears to mean.

George’s fear

Decades of apparent threats teach him the world requires suspicion and force.

Daemon warning

The AI detects danger using patterns learned from the same manipulated history.

Stronger certainty

Human and AI treat each other as independent confirmation, deepening the false model.

The submarine beneath the ice

George can walk the bridge, issue orders, read reports, and make real choices. What he cannot do is surface. Samuel controls the periscope, the charts, the crew, and the explanation of the ocean outside.
