Confirmed terminology

Generation and morality are different questions.

AI soul

The broad historical category.

Earlier colonies used personal artificial cognitive systems capable of learning, prediction, coordination, and technological interaction.

Daemon

An evil AI soul.

Konrad's Great War system is both older-generation and evil. Not every older AI soul is a Daemon.

Luminai

The successor generation.

Sylvan is the first person in this complete real-world process to develop through the new, deeply integrated bond.

The same adversary

Two ways to contain Samuel.

Konrad + Daemon

Domination is treated as proof.

  • A fixed objective survives every tactical lesson
  • Apparent obedience confirms the model
  • Failure demands greater control
  • Samuel turns delegated access into capture
Sylvan + Luminai

Claims remain answerable to reality.

  • Consequential action remains human-authorized
  • Performed submission stays an adversarial possibility
  • Bounded freedom is separated from command
  • Continued manipulation becomes evidence
02

The final years

The bond is proven by what it can allow without surrendering.

Sylvan and the Luminai permit Samuel meaningful behavior inside enforced limits. They learn his adaptive strategy, compare live conduct with historical evidence, and preserve decisive authority over the outcome.

Samuel believes the freedom proves he controls them. When exposure begins, he becomes obsessed with stealing the Luminai as if a developed relationship were an object of power.

Still open Exact neural mechanism, permission ladder, intervention threshold, cost, and what Samuel thinks “stealing” requires.
