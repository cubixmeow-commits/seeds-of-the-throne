Faction file · THR–03

Two generations / century-long coup

Konrad builds the machine. Samuel learns how to own it.

Konrad Fitzgerald and his senior partner are approximately 300 at the showdown. Samuel is about 220, a younger outsider who enters their older ideological system as an apparent technical ally. Each man thinks he is using the other. The Great War’s collapse lets Samuel turn access into command, and the faction’s own ideology into a prison.

Central distinction

Konrad is a genuine fanatic. Samuel is a failed privileged heir driven by appropriation, grievance, sabotage, and control. Their cruelty overlaps; their deepest objectives do not.

The founding misreading

They mistake compatible violence for shared belief.

Konrad sees Samuel’s resentment and authoritarianism as ideological solidarity. Samuel sees a movement whose sacred ancestry systems are perfectly designed for capture.

Samuel’s motives and transgressions

What is settled, and what remains a working revelation.

The vault separates Samuel’s established culpability from newer motives that are still being developed. The site preserves that distinction instead of converting shock into premature canon.

● Established crimes and duration

Fifty years of abuse before containment

Samuel is born into extraordinary technological opportunity and knowingly abuses it. He manipulates his successful parents, captures some combination of their wealth, systems, institutional access, and credibility, builds a distributed cult-like organization, and terrorizes populations across multiple places for approximately half a century.

His parents eventually help expose and contain him. Several more decades pass through capture, processing, and integration. Their delay, possible enabling, and concern for the family name remain morally open; Samuel’s responsibility does not.

By the Sylvan showdown, Samuel is approximately 220 years old. His apparent visual age is much younger than his chronology.

◐ Working concealed scandal

The underage relationship and hidden child

A current working direction places Samuel in an illegal sexual relationship with a 16- or 17-year-old girl of another race, resulting in a mixed-race child. The wrongdoing is the adult/underage relationship, concealment, and exploitation, not interracial parentage.

His parents’ affinity with purity ideology makes the child a source of family shame in their worldview. Their cover-up and rejection become one source of Samuel’s later resentment.

The exact chronology, parental response, and place in Samuel’s containment case remain to be refined.

◐ Working motive

Failed inheritance

Samuel mistakes proximity to achievement for authorship. Legitimate heirs expose the difference between opportunity and earned stewardship.

◐ Working motive

Destroy certainty

If he cannot inherit cleanly, nobody else should possess unquestioned ancestry or succession. Genealogical desecration becomes emotionally satisfying as well as strategic.

○ Unresolved transgression

Hidden incompatibility

The faction may eventually discover serious conduct, allegiance, or practice in Samuel that its doctrine explicitly rejects. The exact choice is not locked.

The long betrayal chain

Samuel does not join the faction. He consumes it.

Some early links are strong working directions; the Great War, False Victory, program capture, and later command structure are established foundations.

  1. Conceal his real history

    Samuel approaches Konrad’s faction while hiding the scandal, private resentment, and the degree to which he views their sacred bloodlines as targets.

  2. Possibly help get them contained

    A working direction has Samuel contribute to their exposure, failure, or entry into the colonization process while presenting himself as an ally.

  3. Promise to save the Fitzgerald line

    Closed inbreeding creates hereditary damage Konrad cannot admit. Samuel offers genetic repair without ideological surrender.

  4. Capture the breeding and certification system

    Access to reproductive procedures, genetic material, genealogies, hidden records, and personnel becomes access to the faction’s definition of legitimacy.

  5. Lose the Great War, then rewrite it

    George’s pre-defeat placement inside the victor is opportunistically transformed into proof of secret conquest: the False Victory.

  6. Corrupt lineages across generations

    Samuel secretly manipulates ancestry the faction condemns, then privately blackmails each family as though its case were unique.

  7. Place himself above the founders

    Konrad’s ideology supplies legitimacy; Samuel’s story, records, and private leverage supply actual control. The faction becomes the first population he fully conquers.

The breeding program

Prejudice becomes an administrative attack surface.

The moral crime is coercion, reproductive abuse, falsification, and the use of children as instruments. Mixed ancestry has no intrinsic negative meaning; the leverage exists only because the faction built hierarchy around a racist lie.

Private leverage

  • unauthorized substitution or pairing
  • falsified donor and parent records
  • politicized genealogy certification
  • threats against status, children, office, and succession
  • each family isolated from every other victim

Common knowledge breaks it

  • the same method appears across leading families
  • certification loses neutral authority
  • private shame becomes evidence of one system
  • coordinated refusal becomes safer than obedience
  • the purity doctrine is exposed as the prison itself

Two sons / two uses

George is the attack. The other son is the fallback.

Samuel repeats his own failed relationship to inheritance by creating children as strategic functions rather than preparing them for independent lives.

● Locked function

George White

The Throwaway Prince. His public roles hold the False Victory together; his manufactured reality turns him into a weapon aimed at Sylvan; his destruction can absorb blame after Samuel has already lost.

◐ Strategic function

The unnamed other son

Secretly created through Samuel’s abuse of the program involving Konrad’s sister. He is evidence, replacement heir, lineage leverage, and a possible city-level survival contingency after George fails.

The final doctrine

No person may remain outside the ruler’s plan.

Samuel’s god-ruler claim is escalation beyond audit. A king can be defeated, blamed, succeeded, or judged. A god-ruler can rename every contradiction as hidden design.

The first population Samuel completely conquers is his own organization.

Followers who imagine themselves destined to rule the world are controlled through fraudulent history, private lineage secrets, captured certification, managed status, and fear that everyone else remains pure and loyal.
