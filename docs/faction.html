Conspiracy file · moderate spoilers

Konrad builds the machine. Samuel teaches it to betray him.

A capable authoritarian founder loses a war he never planned to lose. An expelled insider turns that defeat into a private restoration story—and makes himself indispensable to everyone trapped inside it.

01

The antagonist mirror

One builds believers. One builds dependencies.

Konrad Fitzgerald

Collective certainty

He converts grievance, ritual, belonging, hierarchy, and selective warmth into obedience. His organization can rise because he is effective—not because his enemies are weak.

What ruins him is the system’s refusal to admit correction, limits, retreat, or defeat.

Samuel Franklin

Individual control

He reads each person’s needs and supplies a tailored explanation. After the war, he disguises subordination as restoration and turns access into an expanding private coup.

What ruins him is the compulsion to keep the controlled reality alive after it can no longer win.

The betrayal chain

Disposal becomes betrayal. Betrayal becomes a system.

  1. 01

    Exile disguised as trust

    Konrad gives Samuel an impossible mission designed to fail.

  2. 02

    Real success, real collapse

    Samuel and George gain enough standing to believe the mission, then choose wrongdoing and a takeover that ends in humiliating containment.

  3. 03

    A war sabotaged from inside

    Konrad’s hierarchy creates its own defeat while Samuel covertly makes the collapse worse.

  4. 04

    Defeat renamed restoration

    Konrad moves or reactivates Fitzgerald machinery inside Samuel’s earlier bounded jurisdiction, believing he is recovering command.

  5. 05

    The False Victory

    Public failure becomes hidden conquest. Restriction becomes secret assignment. Every contradiction becomes proof of a deeper plan.

  6. 06

    The lie needs maintenance

    Across roughly eighty years, Samuel must keep intervening in people, records, and interpretations. The coup becomes a revolution he cannot safely stop.

02

Why the system fails

Authoritarian order destroys the information it needs.

Hierarchy suppresses correction.Bad news becomes disloyalty before it becomes strategy.

Purity makes family administrative.Private identity becomes status, leverage, and a point of capture.

Propaganda captures its authors.A story that cannot admit failure eventually cannot recognize danger.

Permanent patriarchs make succession impossible.Every heir must inherit duty without receiving independent authority.

Open deeper development spoilersLineage capture, hidden command, and the unresolved endgame boundary

The conspiracy survives by keeping every contradiction private.

Samuel expands revenge into the capture of genealogy, certification, later generations, and controlled interpretation. The public resolution does not require every follower to accept the truth. It requires isolated people to recognize that their supposedly private exceptions are one repeated system.

Sylvan’s later evidence must separate original event, first record, later revision, beneficiary, and continuing intervention. The exact proof threshold, audience, privacy boundary, and final cost remain active development tasks.
