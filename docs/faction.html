Faction file · THR–03

Exile / defeat / century-long coup

Konrad builds the machine. Samuel returns to decide who belongs inside it.

Konrad Fitzgerald leads the older purity organization and its Great War conquest. Samuel is the failed privileged son of senior members: exiled after serious misconduct, contained inside the future victor, and positioned to sabotage the campaign before the defeated leadership falls into his environment.

Central distinction

Konrad is the sincere ideological fanatic and original conquest leader. Samuel is the exiled infiltrator whose revenge against bloodline certainty evolves into an attempt to replace him.

The founding misreading

They mistake compatible violence for shared belief.

Konrad sees Samuel’s resentment and authoritarianism as ideological solidarity. Samuel sees a movement whose sacred ancestry systems are perfectly designed for capture.

Samuel’s motives and transgressions

What is settled, and what remains a working revelation.

The vault separates Samuel’s established culpability from newer motives that are still being developed. The site preserves that distinction instead of converting shock into premature canon.

● Established origin and exile

A failed privileged heir is cast out.

Samuel is born to wealthy senior members of Konrad’s organization. His parents share its fascist obsession with pedigree, purity, succession, and hereditary belonging. They and the movement exile Samuel after serious misconduct about thirty years before the Great War.

Samuel does not interpret exile as accountability. He treats it as dispossession from an inheritance he assumed was his.

By the Sylvan showdown, Samuel is approximately 220 years old. His exact age at exile remains open.

◐ Established misconduct / unresolved full case

The underage relationship and additional abuses

Samuel’s misconduct includes a sexual relationship with an underage girl. Additional abuses, her exact age, the legal and cultural circumstances, and what Samuel’s family concealed or exposed remain unresolved.

No child from that relationship is established, and no single incident should mechanically explain Samuel’s later character.

The wrongdoing and exile are established; the remaining specifics must stay visibly open.

◐ Working motive

Failed inheritance

Samuel mistakes proximity to achievement for authorship. Legitimate heirs expose the difference between opportunity and earned stewardship.

◐ Working motive

Destroy certainty

If he cannot inherit cleanly, nobody else should possess unquestioned ancestry or succession. Genealogical desecration becomes emotionally satisfying as well as strategic.

◐ Working evolution

Revenge becomes replacement

Samuel first attacks what the organization worships—bloodline, genealogy, pedigree, purity, succession, and inheritance—then begins taking over Konrad’s functions.

The long betrayal chain

Samuel does not return to the faction. He consumes it.

The ownership is settled even where operations remain open: Konrad leads the conquest; Samuel performs reconciliation, sabotages it, interprets its defeat, and captures the systems that define belonging.

  1. Be exiled

    Samuel’s parents and Konrad’s organization cast him out about thirty years before the Great War.

  2. Manufacture apparent success

    Samuel and George enter an important-seeming situation inside the largest government. George looks highly successful.

  3. Fail publicly

    Private wrongdoing and a horribly planned takeover end in George’s humiliating exposure and real containment.

  4. Perform reconciliation

    During the Great War, Samuel keeps contact with his parents and appears to want forgiveness or readmission.

  5. Sabotage the campaign

    He worsens a collapse primarily caused by the faction’s own authoritarian failures.

  6. Interpret the defeat

    When Konrad’s leadership enters Samuel’s environment, he turns George’s success and bust into the False Victory.

  7. Capture the bloodlines

    Samuel controls reproductive records, pedigree, certification, descendants, and succession until the man once denied belonging determines who belongs to everyone else’s family.

The breeding program

Prejudice becomes an administrative attack surface.

The moral crime is coercion, reproductive abuse, falsification, and the use of children as instruments. Mixed ancestry has no intrinsic negative meaning; the leverage exists only because the faction built hierarchy around a racist lie.

Private leverage

  • unauthorized substitution or pairing
  • falsified donor and parent records
  • politicized genealogy certification
  • threats against status, children, office, and succession
  • each family isolated from every other victim

Common knowledge breaks it

  • the same method appears across leading families
  • certification loses neutral authority
  • private shame becomes evidence of one system
  • coordinated refusal becomes safer than obedience
  • the purity doctrine is exposed as the prison itself

Two sons / two uses

George is the attack. The other son is the fallback.

Samuel repeats his own failed relationship to inheritance by creating children as strategic functions rather than preparing them for independent lives.

● Locked function

George White

The Throwaway Prince. His public roles hold the False Victory together; his manufactured reality turns him into a weapon aimed at Sylvan; his destruction can absorb blame after Samuel has already lost.

◐ Strategic function

The unnamed other son

Secretly created through Samuel’s abuse of the program involving Konrad’s sister. He is evidence, replacement heir, lineage leverage, and a possible city-level survival contingency after George fails.

The final doctrine

No person may remain outside the ruler’s plan.

Samuel’s god-ruler claim is escalation beyond audit. A king can be defeated, blamed, succeeded, or judged. A god-ruler can rename every contradiction as hidden design.

The first population Samuel completely conquers is his own organization.

Followers who imagine themselves destined to rule the world are controlled through fraudulent history, private lineage secrets, captured certification, managed status, and fear that everyone else remains pure and loyal.
