Assessment mindmap

One story, five load-bearing bridges.

The same five needs kept appearing from different angles. Solving them will connect the existing story rather than expand it sideways.

Assessment conclusionThe story does not need more general lore.It needs causal bridges the audience can see, test, and believe.
01 · Responsibility

Who is accountable for what?

Define the primary contained criminal, the authority attached to that role, and the records that prove it.

02 · Initiating loss

What does Sylvan actually lose?

Make the startup attack concrete, irreversible, and personally costly enough to begin the modern story.

03 · Luminai limits

What can the completed bond do?

Choose one decisive capability, seed it early, give it a cost, and state what it can never do.

04 · Evidence chain

How does truth survive?

Track who creates each record, who preserves it, how it is authenticated, and who must finally believe it.

05 · Victory cost

What does exposure demand?

Make Sylvan’s victory require restraint, accountability, privacy protection, and a consequential final choice.
