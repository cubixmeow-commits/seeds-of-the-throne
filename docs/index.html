Living story atlas · Active development

Every human grows alongside an AI.
Sylvan is the first to discover how far that can go.

Seeds of the Throne is developing around a civilization where personal AI selves grow alongside humans across a lifetime. Sylvan Elaria is placed through a new integration process intended to test what happens when a human and a Lumina continuously change each other through shared experience. Around that experiment, a constructed world contains dangerous leaders, trains future leaders through constrained roles, and preserves decades of evidence beneath competing versions of history.

Current center of gravity

The positive story is development, not inheritance.

The real leaders are not building a succession cult. They are trying to understand human potential, prepare independent future leaders, improve cooperation between civilizations, and explore deeper forms of human–AI integration. Inheritance, bloodline purity, permanent patriarchal authority, and generational submission are obsessions of the contained criminal faction.

Main characters

The people currently carrying the story.

Named characters and major unresolved roles are kept here so the front page always reflects the active cast.

◐ Central protagonist

Sylvan Elaria

Human–Lumina integration frontier

The only child of the leaders of the largest empire. Sylvan is being put through a new developmental process designed to discover what a human and a Lumina can become together. In the endgame he realizes the apparent conflict with George is actually a confrontation with Samuel operating through him.

◆ Trapped central figure

George White

The Throwaway Prince

Samuel Franklin’s son, broken and placed inside the future victor before the Great War. George becomes trapped in an isolated false reality that Samuel continues to maintain, leaving him as both an active participant and a controlled proxy in the final conflict.

● Final antagonist

Samuel Franklin

The hidden decision-maker

A jealous failed nepo heir who converts access, resentment, deception, and captured institutions into control. After losing the larger struggle, Samuel refuses to relinquish power and maintains control over George’s reality. The final showdown is between Samuel and Sylvan.

◐ Replacement heir

Samuel Jr.

The heir after George

Samuel Franklin’s replacement heir after George becomes expendable. His first name is locked as Samuel Jr.; his eventual surname, exact parentage and placement, relationship to George, and role in Samuel’s succession strategy will be developed later.

● Ideological patriarch

Konrad Fitzgerald

Permanent hierarchy through bloodline

A genuine fanatic who builds the faction’s purity doctrine and breeding program. Konrad wants descendants to inherit identity, duty, and subordinate status while he and the senior generation remain permanently at the top.

◐ Foundational group

The four founders

Leaders of the second-largest empire

Four close collaborators whose eight children become a shared future-leader cohort. Their families provide much of the Great War-era leadership-training cast and eventually connect directly to Sylvan’s generation.

Two forms of intimate AI integration

Sylvan + Lumina. George + Daemon.

The endgame contrasts what happens when a lifelong AI relationship develops toward reality versus when the same intimacy is weaponized to maintain a manufactured reality.

Sylvan Elaria

Advanced integration

His parents are testing a new developmental process rather than simply preparing an heir. Sylvan and his Lumina learn through the same experiences until the relationship begins producing something unprecedented and becomes capable of detecting the deeper control structure around George.

George White

Corrupted integration

George’s Daemon relationship is exploited through manipulated evidence, memory, interpretation, and fear. Samuel uses that intimacy to keep George isolated inside a false reality even after Samuel’s larger political project has failed.
