01

The premise

This world closes one colonization era and initializes the next.

Humanity has already crossed the stars using earlier generations of AI souls. This mature colony carries their accumulated achievements, institutions, and failures.

Now the same real civilization becomes the first complete initialization environment for a new Luminai, deeply integrated with Sylvan's mind. It must learn inside history, uncertainty, relationships, and adaptive danger rather than inside a clean simulation.

The final test is not whether intelligence can dominate the worst person in the system. It is whether intelligence and human judgment can contain him without becoming him.

The central conflict

They attack the person they are afraid he may become.

  1. 01

    Konrad refuses the verdict

    After losing the Great War, he believes his experienced Daemon can control Samuel and turn every failure into preparation.

  2. 02

    Samuel accepts the invitation

    He performs submission, enters Konrad's reactivated systems, and captures the defeated group from within.

  3. 03

    Sylvan begins at the end

    During the final years, he allows Samuel bounded freedom while the new Luminai learns the complete strategy.

  4. 04

    The method repeats in public

    Samuel tries the same takeover against Sylvan, and Konrad's inner circle realizes what happened to them.

Established direction Book One begins near the outcome. Later books move backward into the Great War and the takeover. Exact scenes, evidence thresholds, and consequences remain unresolved.
