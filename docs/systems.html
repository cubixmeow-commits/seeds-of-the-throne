How the world works

Advanced enough to shape reality.
Constrained enough that choices still matter.

The setting uses civilization-scale AI, biological synthetics, personal AI selves, reconstructed environments, influence systems, and sealed records. The important design problem is defining hard limits so technology creates pressure and possibility without erasing agency.

01

Constructed colonization world

A biologically real civilization established by synthetics for settlement, development, containment, and eventual human placement.

02

Personal AI selves

Lumina and Daemons develop alongside humans and mediate information, memory, interpretation, learning, and increasingly deep forms of integration.

03

Story functionality

Operational reality, public interpretation, and sealed provenance can diverge. Advanced systems can stage environments without making the underlying record disappear.

04

Synthetic population

At some stages more than 95% of the population may be biological synthetics, allowing the environment to be shaped at enormous scale.

05

Influence allocation

Competing actors receive bounded influence over resources, opportunity, friction, timing, and institutional support rather than direct omnipotence.

06

Behavioral recurrence

People can be detected across changing identities through behavioral signatures, decisions, language, engineering choices, and other persistent patterns.
