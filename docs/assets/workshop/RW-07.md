# RW-07: George and Konrad's distinct endgame functions

## Purpose

Keep two important recognition arcs without allowing either to displace Samuel's rapid terminal collapse.

## Established

George can act directly on Sylvan's planet and has served as Samuel's bridge. Konrad remains on the older containment planet inside a false victory. Konrad's inner circle recognizes Samuel's repeated takeover method before Konrad does.

## Accepted result

Samuel drags George into the final apocalyptic attempt to establish himself as a living god and positions George as the scapegoat for its failure. George supplies the physical operational presence Samuel cannot have on Sylvan's planet, then becomes the visible person onto whom Samuel tries to transfer responsibility.

George is not given a convenient final refusal as automatic redemption. Exact agency, acts, knowledge, and recognition remain open.

Konrad is forced out of the story environment that has enclosed him for almost a century and into the reality of what Samuel did. The connected reproductive and genealogical attack against the faction's bloodlines becomes the most intense focus because it destroys the sacred future Konrad believed he was preserving. “Pollution” remains Konrad's ideological interpretation; objectively, descendants are people and the wrongdoing is coercion, abuse, falsification, and instrumentalization.

Konrad's function is confrontation, not immediate redemption. His forced return to reality proves the extent of Samuel's capture and collapses the False Victory. His exact response remains open.

Konrad retains substantial influence inside the containment environment, so his final moves after the confrontation will have material consequences. The module establishes the function and preserves those moves for later scene development rather than reducing him to a passive observer.

**Distinct functions:** `George = scapegoat dragged deeper into the final fantasy; Konrad = captive pulled out of the older fantasy into reality`

Source: [[01 Sessions/Daily/2026-09-11 - RW-07 George as Godhood Scapegoat]].

## Four paired models

1. **George corroborates; Konrad understands.** George preserves one live operational record, while Konrad finally understands the historical trap.
2. **George refuses; Konrad loses authority.** George's limited refusal breaks Samuel's current channel, while Konrad's circle withdraws performed obedience.
3. **George identifies command; Konrad identifies motive.** George proves who issued a present instruction, while Konrad recognizes why the old bargain worked.
4. **George protects a person; Konrad releases a record.** Their final acts repair different parts of the system without buying innocence.

## Central author gate

What single endgame function belongs to George, and what different function belongs to Konrad? **AUTHOR-ACCEPTED 2026-09-11.**

Give each one a distinct verb. Do not make either man the sole source of truth.

## Scene test

Samuel tries to push blame toward George and reinterpret Konrad's captivity as command. George and Konrad each do one thing Samuel's model did not allow.

## Adversarial test

A final refusal can change future consequence, but it cannot erase earlier responsibility. Recognition also cannot substitute for authenticated evidence.
