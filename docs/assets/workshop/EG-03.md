# EG-03: Bloodline operation and Samuel Jr.

## Purpose

Turn the bloodline sabotage from a shocking claim into a humane, evidence-based causal system with consequences for succession, trust, and control.

## Established

Samuel used Konrad's bloodline ideology against the group. Samuel Jr. is the unauthorized child of Samuel Franklin and Konrad's sister. Samuel also built a secret archive of concealed parentage and intended to use it to discredit, remove, and control people under the groups' own exclusion rules. The wrongdoing belongs to Samuel's coercion, concealment, falsification, and use of descendants as instruments. No child is inherently compromised by their origin.

Sources: [[02 Story/Systems/The Breeding Program and Lineage Blackmail]], [[02 Story/Characters/Samuel Franklin]], [[01 Sessions/Daily/2026-09-19 - Endgame Workshop Integration]].

## Four possibilities

1. **Certification trail:** altered parentage records reveal one administrator and one repeated authorization route.
2. **Protected family comparison:** several families compare sealed discrepancies without publicly identifying descendants.
3. **Samuel Jr. audits himself:** his institutional systems expose contradictions that force him to choose whether to protect or reveal Samuel.
4. **Blackmail book fracture:** a targeted person preserves a threat, allowing the Resistance to connect private leverage to the wider operation.

## Central author gate

What does Samuel Jr. know about his origin and intended role, what evidence can only he reach, and what choice makes him a person rather than a succession device?

## Scene test

The proof can establish Samuel's conduct and protect affected descendants at the same time.

## Adversarial test

If parentage alone proves guilt, the story repeats Konrad's ideology. If Samuel Jr. exists only to unlock evidence, he has no independent agency.
