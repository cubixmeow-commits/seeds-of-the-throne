## Central author gate

> What exactly would lawful exit have done to Konrad’s authority and to the people under him?

Ask this question alone during actual use. Do not ask every follow-up at once.

## Possibilities and tradeoffs

### 1. End command, continue individual review elsewhere

**PROPOSED:** Konrad leaves his governance role and faces ordinary adjudication; followers receive separate assessments. Clear distinction from freedom or absolution; requires another holding context.

### 2. Disband faction jurisdiction, retain bounded personal participation

**PROPOSED:** Konrad could remain as an individual but rejects loss of corporate authority. Concentrates motive on domination; explain how a later agreement lawfully restores any group functions.

### 3. Return eligible people to outside placements

**PROPOSED:** Those cleared can leave while responsible leaders remain restricted. Gives followers stakes; some may choose Konrad from genuine attachment or fear.

### 4. Offer supervised transition with revocable limited coordination

**PROPOSED:** The group can manage practical winding-down without political sovereignty. Creates the specific delegation Samuel exploits; must not legalize coercive reproductive institutions.

## Targeted follow-up questions

## Scene test

**Non-canon situation:** A senior follower has an approved independent placement; Konrad persuades them to abandon it for one final demonstration.

Record what each person wants, can know, can refuse, and can actually change. Name the observable difference between the selected alternatives.
