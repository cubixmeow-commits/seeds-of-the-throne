# 14 · George’s necessary role

## Purpose

Resolve overlap with Konrad by assigning George a distinct causal and emotional function.

## Central author gate

> What can George’s decisions reveal or change that neither Konrad nor a generic proxy can?

Ask this question alone during actual use. Do not ask every follow-up at once.

## Possibilities and tradeoffs

### 1. Active command witness who can refuse the final order

**PROPOSED:** His firsthand connection to Samuel exposes live maintenance. Strong present agency; corroboration is needed so one testimony does not solve everything.

### 2. Scapegoat whose protection tests Sylvan’s restraint

**PROPOSED:** Samuel tries to make Sylvan dispose of him. Gives Sylvan a costly human choice; avoid making George entirely Sylvan’s dependent or clearing his conduct.

### 3. Earlier-model recovery counterexample

**PROPOSED:** George and his older bond confront corrupted premises, showing old technology need not remain evil. Strengthens moral/technical distinction; exact recovery cannot be effortless.

### 4. Parallel partial awakening in a limited interlude

**PROPOSED:** George’s restricted viewpoint makes Samuel’s servicing of reality visible while Konrad stays isolated. Economical suspense; risks another exposition thread unless George acts on what he learns.

## Targeted follow-up questions

- What evidence can only George supply, or what decision can only he make?
- What does refusal cost him?
- Which crimes remain his after command responsibility is proven?

## Scene test

**Non-canon situation:** George receives an instruction justified by a fresh sign; instead of executing it, he preserves the original request and asks a question Samuel cannot answer consistently.

Record what each person wants, can know, can refuse, and can actually change. Name the observable difference between the selected alternatives.

## Adversarial questions

- If George were removed, would Book One’s plot or moral result change? If only exposition disappears, his role is not yet necessary.
- Which established constraint would this answer break, and what evidence would reveal that break before the payoff?
