## Central author gate

> What recurring cost forces Sylvan to learn how to use the bond rather than simply acquire it?

Ask this question alone during actual use. Do not ask every follow-up at once.

## Possibilities and tradeoffs

### 1. Attention and calibration burden

**PROPOSED:** Early signals compete with concentration; precision improves through practice. Easy to dramatize, but avoid making exhaustion a convenient switch that changes by scene.

### 2. Dependence and partial offline loss

**PROPOSED:** The bond persists while environmental reach narrows. Makes setting matter; distinguish retained human learning from unavailable tools.

### 3. Maladaptive shared habits

**PROPOSED:** Both layers learn an expedient shortcut that later fails ethically or perceptually. Gives a real internal arc; correction must be possible without pathologizing Sylvan.

### 4. Boundary and relationship cost

**PROPOSED:** Integration changes what Sylvan can keep private or responsibly share. Strong Orzai conflict; avoid treating intimacy as required data access.
