## Central author gate

> Which single action changes Samuel’s authority when Konrad reactivates the group?

**AUTHOR-ACCEPTED:** Konrad's authorization of reactivation is the legible hinge that attaches the revived system to Samuel's hidden hierarchy.

## Possibilities and tradeoffs

### 1. Parent-jurisdiction registration

**PROPOSED:** Konrad registers an apparently separate domain as a child of Samuel’s existing sphere. Clean hierarchy; danger is overbroad automatic inheritance unless protected domains stay explicit.

### 2. Delegated infrastructure administration

**PROPOSED:** Konrad delegates restoration services while retaining political authority; administrator visibility enables later compromise. Plausible gradual capture; the legal distinction must matter in scenes.

### 3. Shared emergency coordination mandate

**PROPOSED:** A crisis authorizes Samuel across several domains temporarily; dependencies survive the mandate. Strong political causality; avoid emergency-rule cliché and explain failed expiry.

### 4. Negotiated arbitration priority

**PROPOSED:** Conflicts between the two domains go through Samuel’s earlier Primary standing. Preserves genuine Konrad authority; slow bureaucratic leverage needs one decisive scene and protected appeal.
