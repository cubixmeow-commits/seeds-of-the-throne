## Central author gate

> What does reconstruction reproduce faithfully, and what is allowed or required to change?

Ask this question alone during actual use. Do not ask every follow-up at once.

## Possibilities and tradeoffs

### 1. Historical starting conditions, open subsequent history

**PROPOSED:** Reproduce institutions, material constraints and initial pressures, then let people generate history. Strong agency; exact familiar events cannot be guaranteed.

### 2. Selected regional reconstructions in a continuous world

**PROPOSED:** Different zones test different periods while sharing real logistics and consequences. Rich environment ladder; transitions and knowledge leakage need credible boundaries.

### 3. A single society with accelerated surface development

**PROPOSED:** Supports Great War through startup-era change and inherited records. Strong causal continuity; acceleration must not make families or technology arbitrary.

### 4. Historical constraints beneath an explicit safety substrate

**PROPOSED:** Advanced infrastructure preserves bodies and basic rights while limiting visible affordances. Helps legitimacy; the story must show costly permitted harm without claiming catastrophe was harmless.

## Targeted follow-up questions

- Who is native to the reconstructed society, and can they decline its continuation?
- Which scarcity is physical and which administratively imposed?
- Which historical cruelty must never be reenacted merely for fidelity?

## Scene test

**Non-canon situation:** A seemingly mundane failure exposes a hidden grid intervention, saving a person while contradicting the era’s official account.

Record what each person wants, can know, can refuse, and can actually change. Name the observable difference between the selected alternatives.

## Adversarial questions

- If leaders can manufacture abundance and prevent injury, why preserve this specific deprivation or danger?
- Which established constraint would this answer break, and what evidence would reveal that break before the payoff?
