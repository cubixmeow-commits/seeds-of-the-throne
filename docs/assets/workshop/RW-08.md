# RW-08: Employment-stage preparation

## Purpose

Make the long middle train the exact abilities that win the ending.

## Established

Sylvan survives for roughly ten years on an approximately $200,000 reserve, then enters a multi-environment employment stage. He must protect income, complete objectives, cooperate with a stable group, preserve agency, and develop reality-anchoring story tools while Samuel attacks his resources and credibility.

## Four candidate capacity sets

1. **Resource discipline, source discrimination, refusal.** Directly mirrors Samuel's failure, but may underuse the ensemble.
2. **Cooperation, distributed verification, repair.** Strong social payoff, but needs a clear financial survival line.
3. **Project completion, evidence provenance, bounded technology use.** Strong competence arc, but risks feeling procedural.
4. **Adaptive combination:** one financial capacity, one relational capacity, and one evidentiary capacity recur at increasing difficulty. Broadest payoff with the least repetition.

## Central author gate

Which three capacities learned during the reserve and employment stages directly enable terminal survival?

Choose exactly one financial, one relational, and one reality/evidence capacity.

## Scene test

An early employment problem should look small but contain the same structure as the ending: limited resources, a manipulated explanation, pressure to control others, and a cheaper cooperative solution.

## Adversarial test

The employment arc should not become unrelated workplace episodes. Each major stage must change what Samuel can attack or what Sylvan and Orzai can preserve.
