# BA-10: Book One sequence contract

## Purpose

Convert the workshop's accepted answers into a scene-ready architecture without prematurely locking chapters or prose.

## Established

The terminal macro is fixed. This workshop supplies the missing contest rule, deception, countdown, opening, middle ladder, relationship arc, evidence order, and final choices required to reach it.

Sources: [[07 QA/2026-09-11 - Book One Buildability Assessment]], [[02 Story/Storyline]], [[02 Story/Timeline/Timeline]], [[06 Draft/Scenes/README]].

## Four possibilities

1. **Three-movement contract:** opening pressure and false frame; escalating tests and evidence; deadline, separation, collapse, and placement.
2. **Five-sequence contract:** disturbance; commitment; midpoint truth; terminal escalation; processing and constructive exit.
3. **Countdown contract:** organize sequences around visible deadline thresholds and shrinking antagonist options.
4. **Evidence contract:** organize sequences around what can be known, authenticated, acted upon, and finally presented.

## Central author gate

What are Book One's indispensable ordered sequences, whose viewpoint owns each, what decision ends each sequence, what changes because of that decision, and what unresolved question pulls the reader forward?

## Scene test

Every sequence begins from the changed state produced by the previous one and ends with a consequential transition suitable for a scene packet.

## Adversarial test

If events can be freely reordered, causality is still missing. If the contract dictates every scene or sentence, it is too rigid to support discovery during drafting.
