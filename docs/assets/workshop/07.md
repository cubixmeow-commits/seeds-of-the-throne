## Central author gate

> Which boundary can even a highly capable bonded participant neither silently cross nor erase?

Ask this question alone during actual use. Do not ask every follow-up at once.

## Possibilities and tradeoffs

### 1. Purpose-limited device capabilities

**PROPOSED:** A granted action serves a specific task and expires. Gives clear scene puzzles; must handle combinations of permitted actions that create coercion.

### 2. Human quorum for consequential control

**PROPOSED:** Several independent people approve high-impact changes. Supports cooperative leadership; risks procedural scenes unless each approver has conflicting interests.

### 3. Regional authority with authenticated handoffs

**PROPOSED:** Location changes available actions and evidence access. Makes geography dramatic; require continuity of safety during outages or contested borders.

### 4. Independent audit plane and emergency refusal

**PROPOSED:** Actors can manipulate local appearance but cannot erase an external action record. Strong exposure logic; audit is not omniscience and rescue cannot wait for perfect attribution.
