Chronology file · HIS–05

Dependencies are locked / dates are not

A century-long lie begins with a real defeat.

The story’s macro sequence is clear even where exact dates, institutions, and middle episodes remain open. This chronology separates the locked chain of causes from the eighty-year story engine still being built.

How to read this page

“Deep time” belongs to the parent civilization. “Year zero” is the Great War era. The following century compresses changing roles, technologies, betrayals, and public stories that eventually converge on Sylvan.

Complete macro chronology

From preparation to public exposure.

Red markers show the criminal faction’s rise and betrayals. Blue markers show the evidence/disclosure endgame.

  1. Deep time

    Humanity defeats aging.

    Ancient empires endure for centuries or millennia. Their leaders postpone parenthood until they can build a process proportionate to the power their heirs may someday carry.

  2. Foundation

    The moon and planet are constructed.

    Synthetics establish biologically real cultures and institutions. The world advances through planned technological and political stages while remaining real to the lives inside it.

  3. Integration

    Heirs and contained offenders enter under different conditions.

    Hundreds of future leaders begin shared formative lives. Dangerous leaders receive identities, offices, families, and enough bounded influence to reveal whether they can cooperate or only capture.

  4. Before the war

    Samuel enters after abusing his first inheritance.

    He has already manipulated his technology-leader family, captured resources and credibility, built a distributed cult, and terrorized populations. His parents help expose and contain him.

  5. City era

    A genuine city opportunity becomes a foothold.

    Samuel receives stewardship, builds influence, portrays surrounding institutions as weak, converts temporary authority into personal rule, and captures a country.

  6. Before defeat

    George is broken, processed, and placed inside the future victor.

    George’s placement is a contingency, removal strategy, or possible foothold—not secret conquest. The precise institutional mechanism remains unresolved.

  7. Great War

    Samuel and Konrad attempt global conquest.

    Konrad supplies purity doctrine, dynastic legitimacy, and his family at the center of the regime. Samuel converts captured national resources into the war project.

  8. Total defeat

    The city, country, empire, and military project collapse.

    The victorious pluralistic republic and its allies destroy the conquest regime. Surviving leaders enter public containment roles inside the victorious order.

  9. Immediate aftermath

    Samuel invents the False Victory.

    He claims George secretly captured the victor and that visible defeat was a planned transition into covert authority. Confinement becomes prestigious assignment; failure becomes hidden design.

  10. Foundational century

    Story functionality preserves the lie while a sealed record grows.

    Failed operations become strategy, witnesses become traitors, and public biographies become evidence of secret rule. The deeper system preserves what actually happened and who benefited.

  11. Across generations

    Samuel captures Konrad’s breeding program.

    He promises to solve hereditary damage, gains access to reproductive procedures and certification, corrupts family lines, creates private blackmail, and appropriates two generations of the Fitzgerald lineage.

  12. Eighty-year middle

    Smaller lives accumulate the real story.

    Heirs occupy constrained Great War and postwar roles. George moves through administrator, statesman, and senior authority. Technologies mature; betrayals, institutions, discoveries, and relationships create the evidence Sylvan will later compare.

  13. Final thirty years

    George’s horizon narrows toward Sylvan.

    Samuel makes Sylvan appear increasingly dangerous. George and Daemon interpret each staged sign as confirmation that stopping Sylvan is the culmination of George’s life.

  14. Terminal environment

    Sylvan receives overwhelming influence—and refuses the assigned execution.

    The faction continues attacking after victory becomes impossible. Sylvan asks who keeps ordering, explaining, and benefiting. He realizes George is not the true decision-maker.

  15. Public phase

    Isolated secrets become one visible pattern.

    Original records, public revisions, lineage manipulation, command responsibility, and the maintenance of George’s reality can be compared by an audience capable of acting.

  16. Unresolved ending

    George makes his first informed choice.

    The war is already lost. His father sent him forward as a weapon and scapegoat. What George chooses, what Sylvan loses, and how the other-son contingency survives remain open.

The largest undeveloped region

The middle is a story engine, not an exposition block.

Roughly eighty years can hold semi-independent stories whose local choices become later evidence: constrained heir roles, faction schisms, public offices, technological shifts, betrayals, institutions, and lives built under incomplete truth.

Current development priority

Build the first complete Great War heir episode, then map a sequence of smaller stories that can stand alone while accumulating toward Sylvan’s endgame.
