Chronology file · HIS–05 · 22 Aug 2026

Macro sequence established / exact dates still open

A century-long lie begins with an exile and a stolen defeat.

The story now runs from an established interplanetary humanity through Samuel’s exile and earlier containment, Konrad’s conquest, Samuel’s covert sabotage, and the advanced planet’s century-scale False Victory. Samuel’s exact early durations still require remapping.

Age anchors at the showdown

Samuel: approximately 220. Konrad and his senior partner: approximately 300. The legitimate real leaders are thousands of years old. Physical appearance does not equal chronological age.

Complete macro chronology

From preparation to public exposure.

Red markers show the criminal faction’s rise and betrayals. Blue markers show the evidence/disclosure endgame.

  1. Deep time

    Humanity defeats aging and spreads across planets.

    Leaders live for multiple millennia. Their empires establish successful human civilizations on numerous worlds and accumulate generations of colonization knowledge.

  2. Earlier expansions

    Successful worlds make a more ambitious planet possible.

    Established colonies provide industry, institutions, expertise, and political support. The next project can be more technologically controlled because it does not begin alone.

  3. Thirty years before war

    Privilege ends in misconduct and exile.

    Samuel grows up inside Konrad’s purity organization. His parents and the movement exile him after serious misconduct, including a sexual relationship with an underage girl; additional abuses remain unresolved.

  4. Prewar interval

    George’s apparent success ends in humiliating containment.

    Samuel and George enter an important-seeming situation inside the largest government. Private joint wrongdoing and a horribly planned takeover collapse; George is publicly exposed and busted into real containment.

  5. Advanced foundation

    The moon and current planet are constructed.

    Synthetics establish biologically real cultures and institutions. The world uses denser technological management than earlier colonies while remaining real to the lives inside it.

  6. Integration

    Heirs and contained offenders enter under different conditions.

    Hundreds of future leaders begin shared formative lives. Dangerous leaders receive identities, offices, families, and enough bounded influence to reveal whether they can cooperate or only capture.

  7. Conquest era

    Konrad’s political base becomes a world-conquest project.

    The exact city, country, and imperial sequence remains unresolved, but its ownership is settled: Konrad’s faction builds the regime and starts the Great War.

  8. During the war

    Samuel performs reconciliation and sabotages the campaign.

    From inside the future victor, he maintains contact with his parents and appears to seek readmission while materially worsening the faction’s self-inflicted collapse.

  9. False evidence in waiting

    George’s success and failure become reusable facts.

    He never controls the victor, but his important-seeming role and real containment later give Samuel authentic events to reinterpret as hidden conquest.

  10. Total defeat

    Konrad’s state, empire, and military project collapse.

    The victorious pluralistic republic and its allies destroy the conquest regime. Konrad, Samuel’s parents, and the surviving leadership fall into containment inside Samuel’s environment.

  11. Immediate aftermath

    Konrad moves the defeated machinery into Samuel’s jurisdiction.

    Konrad never planned for failure. Samuel exaggerates what his earlier placement can restore and induces him to reactivate or migrate Fitzgerald systems inside a bounded hierarchy where Samuel has first-primary structural priority.

  12. Containment lock

    Access and dependency begin running backward.

    The migration hinge is established, but the practical hierarchy, lock trigger, exact lie, powers, evidence, observation limits, and reset conditions remain unresolved.

  13. False Victory

    Samuel becomes indispensable and rewrites defeat.

    He claims George secretly captured the victor. Confinement becomes prestigious assignment, failure becomes hidden design, and structural dependency becomes Samuel’s path toward the faction’s secrets and bloodlines.

  14. Foundational century

    Story functionality preserves the lie while a sealed record grows.

    Failed operations become strategy, witnesses become traitors, and public biographies become evidence of secret rule. The deeper system preserves what actually happened and who benefited.

  15. Across generations

    Samuel captures Konrad’s breeding program.

    He promises to solve hereditary damage, gains access to reproductive procedures and certification, corrupts family lines, creates private blackmail, and appropriates two generations of the Fitzgerald lineage.

  16. Eighty-year middle

    Smaller lives accumulate the real story.

    Heirs occupy constrained Great War and postwar roles. George moves through administrator, statesman, and senior authority. Technologies mature; betrayals, institutions, discoveries, and relationships create the evidence Sylvan will later compare.

  17. Modern opening

    Sylvan builds a pre-AI story-development company.

    In a surface world comparable to approximately 2015, Sylvan turns a proprietary database-driven creative system into a successful startup while his human–Luminai development begins.

  18. Modern inciting event

    Samuel and George destroy the startup because of who Sylvan is.

    They target the future of his bond and his parents’ colonization work, not the product itself. The company falls, but the attack begins the ordeal that will complete what they fear.

  19. Environment ladder

    Sylvan learns inside systems his opponents dominate.

    The working progression moves through unemployment and Luminai emergence, antagonist-controlled humiliation under a survival clock, and multi-zone employment leading toward bounded control if Sylvan survives.

  20. Final thirty years

    George’s horizon narrows toward Sylvan.

    Samuel makes Sylvan appear increasingly dangerous. George and Daemon interpret each staged sign as confirmation that stopping Sylvan is the culmination of George’s life.

  21. Terminal environment

    Sylvan receives overwhelming influence, and refuses the assigned execution.

    The faction continues attacking after victory becomes impossible. Sylvan asks who keeps ordering, explaining, and benefiting. He realizes George is not the true decision-maker.

  22. Public phase

    Isolated secrets become one visible pattern.

    Original records, public revisions, lineage manipulation, command responsibility, and the maintenance of George’s reality can be compared by an audience capable of acting.

  23. Unresolved ending

    George makes his first informed choice.

    The war is already lost. His father sent him forward as a weapon and scapegoat. What George chooses, what Sylvan loses, and how the Samuel Jr. contingency survives remain open.

The largest undeveloped region

The middle is a story engine, not an exposition block.

Roughly eighty years can hold semi-independent stories whose local choices become later evidence: constrained heir roles, faction schisms, public offices, technological shifts, betrayals, institutions, and lives built under incomplete truth.

Current development priority

Resolve the Samuel–Konrad containment hierarchy one author-led question at a time, then use the development gauntlet on one contained Great War gap. The accepted S-005 environment direction remains partial and cannot silently define its locations, permissions, evidence path, bond capability, or terminal mechanism.
