The history still moves forward.

  1. 01 · Deep time

    Humanity colonizes multiple worlds with older AI souls.

    The current planet becomes the bridge into a successor Luminai era.

    Established
  2. 02 · Great War

    Konrad's hierarchy attempts conquest and loses.

    His Daemon learns tactics without learning that its objective is the problem.

    Macro order
  3. 03 · Second experiment

    Konrad refuses to exit.

    He believes controlling Samuel will redeem every failure and restore his authority.

    Established
