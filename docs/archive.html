An author-controlled story engine

The archive remembers. The author decides.

Seeds of the Throne is built in a vault that preserves decisions, working structures, competing possibilities, research, contradictions, evidence paths, and the exact work still needed to finish the story.

Advance the story by one author-approved, validated state transition while preserving the whole-story graph.
01

Authority is part of the content

A polished idea is not automatically true.

Established

The author has decided it.

It can guide public description and downstream structure, though implementation may still be incomplete.

Working

It is the strongest active direction.

Useful enough to build against, still open to correction.

Proposed

It is a candidate under test.

Research, prototypes, diagrams, and generated images remain here until explicitly promoted.

Unresolved

A real decision is still owed.

The vault preserves the gap instead of letting plausible prose conceal it.

The development loop

Breadth first. Depth after the whole story holds.

  1. ScopeChoose one target and one scale.
  2. RetrieveLoad only relevant authority-classified context.
  3. Map gapsIdentify the missing causal or character bridge.
  4. DivergeGenerate several cheap alternatives.
  5. CritiqueTest causality, agency, systems, continuity, and interest.
  6. Author gateAccept, reject, combine, park, or deepen.
  7. IntegrateRecord the decision and trace downstream effects.
02

Horizontal completion

The story moves through eight depths together.

  1. Macro shape
  2. Causal architecture
  3. Character and agency
  4. Systems and evidence
  5. Sequence integration
  6. Scene mapping
  7. Scene development
  8. Draft

Every active task receives the current sweep before any one branch descends. A whole-story cross-check closes each layer. Invalidated work is reopened visibly rather than protected because it is polished.

Evidence inside the fiction

One event can leave three records.

Use the native disclosures to compare what occurred, what was believed, and what later investigators may be able to authenticate.

Operational reality

What physically occurred, who acted, what permissions existed, and what consequences followed.

Public story

The official or factional explanation that coordinates behavior, even when it turns restriction into promotion or defeat into strategy.

Sealed record

Provenance, configuration history, custody, dated revisions, and independent corroboration capable of testing both event and interpretation.

03

September 3 foundation revision

A new premise can change the whole graph without erasing its history.

The current revision establishes the planet as a bridge between the older AI-soul colonization era and Sylvan's successor Luminai initialization. It also moves Book One near the chronological end, where Sylvan's containment of Samuel exposes the buried failure of Konrad's older Daemon.

That decision now propagates through character motives, command rules, the Great War aftermath, criminal-group design, lineage conflict, the endgame matrix, the public atlas, and the open-question ledger. Earlier material remains traceable, but conflicting endgame assumptions must be reconciled before they can govern new work.

04

Authorship boundary

AI helps build the skeleton. The final fiction remains human.

AI can help construct and maintain world mechanics, character functions, dynasties, chronology, continuity, conflicts, evidence, alternatives, and disposable story prototypes. It does not silently decide canon or replace the author’s final voice.

The intended endpoint is deliberately physical: close the computer, take the developed material to pen and notebook, and write the fiction by hand.
