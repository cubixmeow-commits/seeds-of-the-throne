Evidence file · ARC–06

Story reality / development reality

Every event can leave three different records.

Seeds of the Throne is both a story about controlled interpretation and a public experiment in story development. This archive makes both layers legible: what happened inside the world, what people were told happened, what evidence survived, and how firmly the current story treats each idea.

Reader promise

Working directions are not presented as finished canon. Unresolved choices are not silently filled. Historical notes can conflict until the author deliberately decides between them.

In-world evidence model

One event. Three records.

Select a layer to see how Samuel’s False Victory survives ordinary verification, and what Sylvan’s archive must compare to defeat it.

● What actually occurs

George is genuinely processed into the victorious republic.

Samuel breaks and destabilizes George until the system genuinely contains him. George later occupies real public roles. The conquest regime loses the city, country, empire, and war.

The event
Real processing and public placement.
The meaning
George is a contingency inside the future victor, not its secret ruler.

● What the faction is told

George secretly captured the victor from within.

Samuel interprets the same visible facts as proof that defeat was planned. Public office becomes covert command; restriction becomes secrecy; failure becomes hidden strategy.

The event
George really holds prestigious roles.
The lie
Those roles prove the faction won.

◐ What Sylvan can compare

Before defeat, George was the fallback. After defeat, Samuel rewrote him as the victor.

Original orders, pre-defeat descriptions, later explanations, altered records, hidden beneficiaries, and continuing command decisions reveal when the meaning changed and whom the change protected.

Proof structure
Event → original record → later revision → beneficiary → next operation.
Ethical constraint
Prove the system without indiscriminately exposing descendants.

Build-in-public method

Develop with AI. Write the fiction by hand.

The vault is a deep story-development system, not an automated manuscript. AI assists structure, research, continuity, alternatives, world mechanics, and evidence. The author makes decisions and develops the final prose voice through human writing.

What the vault does

  • preserves chronological development
  • separates compiled story from exploratory sessions
  • tracks contradictions instead of erasing them
  • maintains status and continuity
  • turns explanations into evidence, choices, and situations

What the final fiction requires

  • a human authorial voice
  • scenes written beyond the development interface
  • choices about viewpoint, loss, and ending
  • a shorter handwritten fiction experiment first
  • the vault as reference rather than generated manuscript
