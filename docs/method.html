Build in public

Use AI to develop the universe.
Then close the computer and write.

Seeds of the Throne is an experiment in pushing AI-assisted story development as far as it remains useful without making generated prose the final work.

01

Persistent development memory

The Obsidian vault preserves sessions, compiled story information, compact AI context, research, continuity questions, decisions, and public development material.

02

AI builds the skeleton

AI assists with world mechanics, research, characters, chronology, continuity, conflicts, evidence, episode concepts, and exploratory scenes.

03

The human writes the fiction

Once the development is strong enough, the intended process is to close the computer and write the actual story by hand with pen and notebook, using the vault as reference.

Project experiment

How effective can AI-assisted development become before the actual act of storytelling returns entirely to the human writer?
