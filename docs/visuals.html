Visual grammar

Hidden technology should not turn ordinary life into generic cyberpunk.

The surface civilization and hidden colonization infrastructure are separate visual layers. Period-coherent streets, work, clothing, homes, and institutions come first. Advanced systems emerge through evidence, environment, cognition, and consequence.

Production path

A new image enters through four checks.

  1. Load identityAttach the approved references for every visible character.
  2. Load world truthRetrieve only relevant era, environment, wardrobe, and system constraints.
  3. Regenerate the sceneChange camera, action, light, space, and emotional state.
  4. Review authorityApprove identity and craft separately from story content.
