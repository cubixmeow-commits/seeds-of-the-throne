Visual grammar

Color carries the conflict.

The style is not a filter placed over unrelated art. Light, geometry, material, and empty space communicate what kind of power is operating in the scene.

Crimson conceals

Red marks coercion, corrupted certainty, false authority, inherited violence, and reality under control.

Gold reveals

Gold marks evidence, provenance, constructive power, Luminai integration, and paths back into reality.

Black preserves scale

Darkness creates negative space, isolates the human figure, and lets every luminous system feel consequential.

Images do not establish story canon.

The vault decides what is true. Artwork can interpret an approved scene, character, or system, but it cannot resolve an open question or turn a decorative choice into history.

Production method

How a new image joins the archive.

Every agent follows the same compact sequence, regardless of which image model creates the first draft.

  1. 01Load identity

    Attach the clean master for every visible character.

  2. 02Lock the style

    Apply the shared palette, material, lighting, and symbolic rules.

  3. 03Change the scene

    Describe action, emotion, setting, camera, and output crop.

  4. 04Score and promote

    Store the approved original, create a compressed WebP for the site, register both checksums, and discard ordinary rejected candidates.
