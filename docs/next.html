Current development queue

Define the integration.
Then populate the eighty-year world.

The immediate center of gravity is advanced human–Lumina integration, followed by the people and episodes needed to make the middle of the story concrete.

  1. 01

    Define baseline human–Lumina integration

    Establish what an ordinary lifelong AI relationship looks like before Sylvan’s experiment changes it.

  2. 02

    Define Sylvan’s advanced integration process

    Stages, interfaces, shared learning, adaptation, risks, constraints, intended discoveries, and failure modes.

  3. 03

    Define the unprecedented Lumina outcome

    Show how Sylvan changes the Lumina, how it changes him, and the first observable capability that previous pairs could not achieve.

  4. 04

    Build the Sylvan/Lumina and George/Daemon contrast

    One integration develops toward reality; the other is exploited to sustain manufactured reality.

  5. 05

    Define Sylvan’s parents

    Name them, establish their history, one-child decision, experimental purpose, and accepted risks.

  6. 06

    Define Konrad’s fanatic partner and Samuel’s hidden history

    Turn the senior contained leadership into a specific cast with conflicting motives.

  7. 07

    Name the four founders and eight-person cohort

    Build the second-largest civilization’s people, professional pairings, personalities, and Great War roles.

  8. 08

    Build the first Great War episode

    Choose one constrained future-leader role and turn it into a concrete moral crisis.

  9. 09

    Map the eighty-year middle

    Create an initial sequence of smaller stories whose consequences accumulate toward Sylvan.
