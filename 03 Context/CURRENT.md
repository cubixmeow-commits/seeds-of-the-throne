# Current

## Current phase

Foundation, dynastic architecture, and construction of the middle story. The beginning and end are increasingly defined; immediate work is to turn the contained-criminal leadership and legitimate leadership dynasties into specific people, generations, roles, episodes, and choices across roughly eighty years of colonization history.

## What currently appears strongest

- A colonization world that simultaneously contains dangerous people and cultivates future leaders.
- Senior contained criminals operating from positions of contained privilege and management access while descendants such as George are inserted directly into reconstructed environments.
- **Konrad Fitzgerald** as a genuine ideological patriarch and breeding-program architect who now needs a close fanatic partner / organizational lieutenant.
- **Samuel Franklin** as an outsider inside Konrad's alliance: a failed privileged heir driven by jealousy, resentment, sabotage, acquisition, and control rather than genuine membership in the ideological core. The faction believes he is more like them than he actually is.
- An unresolved future revelation that Samuel secretly engages in serious conduct even this criminal faction considers beyond its boundary, helping explain his incompatibility with them and later revenge. The specific conduct is not yet chosen.
- Sylvan as the sole child of the male and female leaders of the largest empire involved in colonization.
- Four closely allied founders of the second-largest empire, each with a son and daughter, creating an eight-person first heir cohort trained through constrained roles during the Great War era.
- A later descendant of that dynasty becoming Sylvan's partner and love interest, with both a genuine personal relationship and long-term civilizational significance for cooperation between the two great empires.
- Sylvan undergoing a new developmental process different from conventional heir training, intended to test what a human being can become.
- Sylvan's Lumina developing into something unprecedented because of that shared process. Mechanism, capabilities, and hard limits remain to be defined.
- Legitimate heirs learning leadership through bounded public roles where advanced technology enforces meaningful constraints. They must resist authoritarianism, detention, persecution, and mass abuse without simply overriding the environment.
- An overwhelmingly synthetic population and advanced story functionality that can shape roles and environments while still needing hard limits that preserve agency, evidence, responsibility, and resistance.
- An approximately eighty-year middle that can function as an episodic story engine: smaller stories involving different heirs, contained criminals, institutions, crises, discoveries, relationships, and moral tests that accumulate toward the Sylvan endgame.
- A beginning in which city stewardship becomes national capture, global conquest, the Great War, total defeat, and a century-long False Victory.
- George placed inside the future victor before the fall and later used as living proof that containment is covert conquest.
- Story functionality that preserves public cover-ups while a sealed record accumulates underneath.
- A racist purity doctrine weaponized through breeding-program abuse, lineage fraud, and individualized blackmail across the contained organization.
- A total-control doctrine that treats government, history, family, succession, identity, and interpretation as extensions of one ruler's will.

## Development and authorship boundary

The project is an experiment in pushing **AI-assisted story development** as far as it remains useful, not in having AI generate the finished novel. AI can help construct and maintain the skeleton: world mechanics, characters, dynasties, chronology, research, continuity, conflicts, evidence, episode concepts, and scene possibilities.

The intended final fiction remains human-authored. Once enough development exists, the author intends to close the computer and write from the developed material by hand with pen and notebook, likely beginning with a shorter story before attempting the full-length novel. The project therefore also documents a software developer / relatively new fiction writer learning the craft of writing while building an unusually deep story-development system.
