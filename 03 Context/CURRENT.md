# Current

## Current phase

Foundation and story architecture. The immediate work is to turn strong mechanisms into specific characters, events, scenes, places, and choices.

## What currently appears strongest

- A colonization world that simultaneously contains dangerous people and cultivates future leaders.
- Hundreds of legitimate heirs from ancient human empires sharing one consequential planetary history before inheriting civilization-scale power.
- Competing groups of good-faith real leaders testing reconciliation, boundaries, cultivation, and evidence through the same colonization process.
- Hidden influence allocation delivered through an overwhelmingly synthetic society.
- A contained criminal who converts seniority into false legitimacy and builds a throne inside the system.
- A beginning in which city stewardship becomes national capture, global conquest, the Great War, total defeat, and a century-long False Victory.
- George placed inside the future victor before the fall and later used as living proof that containment is covert conquest.
- Story functionality that preserves public cover-ups while a sealed record accumulates underneath.
- A racist purity doctrine weaponized through breeding-program abuse, lineage fraud, and individualized blackmail across the contained organization.
- A total-control doctrine that treats government, history, family, succession, identity, and interpretation as extensions of one god-ruler's will.
- The irony that the faction's supposed future ruling class is actually the first population the King fully conquers.
- Recurring identities and public biographies that conceal containment roles.
- A first-person narrator who can follow implications but never possesses the complete explanation.

## Immediate development priorities

1. Decide who experiences the opening movement from city opportunity into national capture and Great War.
2. Place the Witness, Inheritor, Sylvan, and George precisely relative to the war and first containment century.
3. Define George's age, public identity, and legal mechanism when placed inside the future victor.
4. Choose the first archive proof connecting the King's pre-defeat account to the post-defeat False Victory.
5. Define how Sylvan proves systemic lineage blackmail without exposing children individually.
6. Give the principal countries, movements, institutions, and leaders final invented names.
7. Decide which part of the total-control doctrine Sylvan proves first and how that proof becomes common knowledge.
