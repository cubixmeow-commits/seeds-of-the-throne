# World

A long-lived parent civilization builds a planet as both colonization infrastructure and a developmental environment. Biological synthetics populate and develop it before humans are integrated. A moon-scale command structure coordinates observation, communication, energy, and high-level control.

Humans inside usually experience the constructed civilization as ordinary reality. Some are born into it; some are heirs placed into developmental lives; some are contained criminals assigned roles rather than cells. Aging has been defeated outside, but mortal lifetimes are performed inside.

The environment does not simply issue visible commands. It allocates influence among competing people and plans, then makes cooperation, resistance, resources, and institutions follow those weights. Participants experience outcomes, not the hidden allocation.

The reconstruction models an invented era of the parent civilization's past. There is no Earth, real nation, real firm, or real historical person in final story data.
