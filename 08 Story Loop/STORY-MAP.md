# STORY-MAP

This is the master causal map for *Seeds of the Throne*. The order below is a **working causal order**, not an approved chapter order. Historical material may later appear as archive evidence, reconstruction, or earlier viewpoint material.

## Status key

- `missing` — load-bearing answer is absent.
- `partial` — useful material exists but the unit cannot yet hand off cleanly.
- `testable` — sufficient for a Gauntlet run.
- `integrated` — author-approved and reconciled into affected compiled notes.
- `blocked` — depends on an unresolved earlier unit or author choice.
