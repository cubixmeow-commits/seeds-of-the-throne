# Story Quality Bar

A Story Unit passes v0.1 only when the answer to every required item is yes, or the item is explicitly marked not applicable.

## Authority

- Every claim is labeled established, working, proposed, unresolved, or rejected.
- No research result or AI proposal has silently become canon.
- New load-bearing characters, factions, technologies, or powers have author approval.
- The author can see exactly which decisions are being requested.

## Function

- The unit has a clear reason to exist in the larger story.
- A character wants something concrete within the unit.
- Something meaningful resists that goal.
- The unit changes character state, information state, relationship state, or practical options.
- Its consequence creates or closes a later obligation.

## Causality

- The triggering event has a traceable cause.
- Major actions follow from what the character wants, fears, knows, and can do.
- No essential transition depends on “somehow,” coincidence alone, or unexplained competence.
- The next unit follows because of this one, not merely after it.

## Continuity

- Character knowledge arrives through a plausible path.
- Timeline and age claims do not conflict with current working anchors.
- Technology stays within established capability and permission limits.
- The ordinary surface world remains credible.
- The unit does not restore superseded canon.

## Pressure and necessity

- The unit closes a gap or strengthens a load-bearing path.
- Existing components are used before adding new ones.
- The same outcome cannot be achieved with a simpler existing component.
- The unit makes the full story closer to write-ready.

## Reader-facing potential

- The essential conflict can eventually be staged rather than explained only in notes.
- The reveal can be experienced through evidence, action, consequence, or changed interpretation.
- Surprise is retrospectively plausible.
- The unit preserves uncertainty the reader should feel without confusing the development team about canon.

## Pass result

- `PASS` — no blocking failure; ready for author integration gate.
- `REVISE` — fixable structural failures remain.
- `AUTHOR DECISION` — multiple viable creative answers remain and authority is required.
- `BLOCKED` — an earlier unit or missing canon source must be resolved first.

Scores may be added later for comparison, but a numeric score cannot override a blocking failure.
