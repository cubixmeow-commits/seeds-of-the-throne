## Real-run observations

### F-006 — Luminai personification drift

- **Run:** [[08 Story Loop/Runs/S-005/2026-08-21 - Unemployment Luminai Emergence Gauntlet 001]]
- **Observed behavior:** The first Architect pass naturally described Sylvan and his Luminai as "learning each other," which subtly converted the system into two separate agents forming a relationship.
- **Why it reduced quality:** It contradicted the established rule that a Luminai is differentiated, operationally independent extended cognition within one accountable human identity. It would also push later scenes toward artificial-companion tropes that the project explicitly rejects.
- **Role or stage responsible:** Architect produced the drift; Continuity Critic caught it.
- **Countermeasure tested:** Reframe development in terms of conscious access, differentiated presentation, testing, trust, correction, integration, and self-understanding while preserving operational independence.
- **Result:** Successful in the integration pass. Add an explicit recurring continuity check if the failure appears again.
