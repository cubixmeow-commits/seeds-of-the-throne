# Story Loop

The Story Loop is a human-readable, Obsidian-native development environment for moving from broad gaps to tested story structure while preserving author authority.

## Start a development run

1. Open [[08 Story Loop/DEVELOPMENT-ORCHESTRATOR]].
2. Scope one target and use [[08 Story Loop/GAP-ANALYZER]].
3. Choose Micro, Standard, Deep, or Cascade mode.
4. Route only to the modules needed for the uncertainty.
5. Stop at the author gate before promotion.

## Weekly intake

[[07 Coordination/Weekly Synthesis/README|Weekly Story Synthesis]] is the high-capacity diagnostic front end for the loop. Its dated reports may reconstruct the story, rank gaps, mine scenes, and recommend maintenance, but they do not establish canon. Route selected `BUILD NOW` and `BRAINSTORM NEXT` items through the Gap Analyzer and Development Orchestrator. Preserve `LEAVE OPEN` items as deliberate ambiguity until a revisit trigger occurs.

Character journals and other physical records are indexed under [[02 Story/Artifacts/Journals/00 Journal System|Character Journal Artifact System]]. Treat proposed artifacts as evidence/scene candidates until their story facts, knowledge state, provenance, and author gate are resolved.

## v0.1 target

A complete causal story map in which every load-bearing unit has:

- a known purpose;
- traceable source material;
- an entering state and resulting state change;
- causes, consequences, and dependencies;
- explicit missing decisions;
- a visible author-approval state.

## Folder roles

- [[08 Story Loop/STORY-MAP|STORY-MAP]] — the master causal backbone and work queue.
- [[08 Story Loop/DEVELOPMENT-ORCHESTRATOR|DEVELOPMENT-ORCHESTRATOR]] — the end-to-end routing contract.
- [[08 Story Loop/GAP-ANALYZER|GAP-ANALYZER]] — the front end for prioritizing missing structure.
- `Units/` — one durable specification per Story Unit.
- `Brainstorms/` — prepared context and author questions, followed by extracted decisions.
- `Runs/` — chronological records of proposals, critiques, gates, revisions, and outcomes.
- `Templates/` — reusable Markdown forms.
- `Evaluations/` — recurring failure modes and later quality calibration.

## Status vocabulary

Story completeness and authority are separate.

**Completeness:** `missing`, `partial`, `testable`, `integrated`, `blocked`.

**Authority:** `established`, `working`, `proposed`, `unresolved`, `rejected`.

An `established` idea can still be structurally `partial`. A polished proposal can still be `proposed` and therefore non-canon.

## Completion pressure

Every run must be classified as one of:

1. `close-gap`
2. `strengthen-structure`
3. `new-expansion`

Choose `close-gap` by default. New expansion needs a clear reason that it advances story completion.

## Phase boundary

Version 0.2 supports development from era through short prototype, but it does not make finished-novel drafting the default. Scene and 500–1,500 word prototype work may be used early when it cheaply tests dialogue, clarity, mechanism, or emotional effect. Final prose remains a separate downstream workflow after sufficient structure and explicit selection.
