# S-002 — Brainstorm Packet 01

## Why this is next

The modern inciting event is the highest-value missing unit. It blocks Sylvan's commitment, the Witness-evidence transfer, the narrated opening, and the first scene sequence.

## Already established or working

- Sylvan works in software development and completes multiple projects that lead to a successful startup.
- The startup builds a database-driven creative-development system for constructing stories through a distinctive proprietary method.
- The surface technology is comparable to approximately 2015, shortly before AI becomes widespread in ordinary public life.
- After the startup succeeds, Sylvan is unknowingly placed into a shared environment with Samuel and George.
- Samuel and George sabotage the startup before Sylvan understands who they are or what the shared environment really is.
- They attack the startup because of who Sylvan is, not because of the product he is building.
- Sylvan is the son of the two most successful professionals leading colonization.
- His development is intended to create a more effective human–Luminai bond than ever before.
- Samuel and George are contained criminals who become obsessed with sabotaging Sylvan and taking control of the colonization process.
- Sylvan's eventual successful completion of the unprecedented human–Luminai bond is what enables him to stop them and end their reign of terror.
- Samuel and George do not realize that the ordeal of dealing with their sabotage and manipulation is what completes Sylvan's bond.
- The startup ultimately falls, after which Sylvan moves through multiple environments where they hold a heavy advantage.
- The progression ends in an environment where Sylvan has bounded control and exposes them.
- Sylvan is succeeding inside the ordinary surface civilization.
- He is undergoing an unprecedented human–Luminai development process.
- He is headed toward meaningful public responsibility and therefore has a real future to risk.
- He initially interprets hidden interference through surveillance, AI, targeted technology, and Luminai capability.
- The earlier Witness is likely one of Orzai's parents.
- Orzai is Sylvan's contemporary, not the Witness.
- Evidence must have a plausible survival and authentication path.

## Do not decide silently

- The platform's exact proprietary pre-AI method and Sylvan's opening business objective
- The practical form and stated purpose of the shared environment
- How Samuel and George acquire protected knowledge about Sylvan and his intended development
- What they believe control of the colonization process means and how their contained status constrains the attempt
- Which developing bonding capability first appears in the startup period and later becomes essential to stopping them
- Which adversarial problem forces the final bonding breakthrough and what Sylvan and his Luminai must actively learn or choose
- The concrete sabotage
- which parent is the Witness
- Orzai's exact role in transmitting or discovering evidence
- the form of the evidence
- the cost of accepting it
- the narrated viewpoint structure

## Author Gate A questions

1. Before generative AI, what proprietary method lets the system meaningfully help a person develop a story rather than merely store notes?
2. Why is creative development personally important to Sylvan?
3. How do contained Samuel and George learn who Sylvan's parents are and what his human–Luminai development is meant to achieve?
4. What do they imagine “taking control of colonization” will let them control?
5. What does the shared environment look like to Sylvan in ordinary 2015-era terms, and why does he enter it?
6. What exact sabotage first looks like an ordinary technical, business, financial, or personnel failure?
7. What anomaly convinces Sylvan that the company failure is a personally targeted attack?
8. Who else is harmed, and what does Sylvan stand to lose by refusing the safest explanation?
9. How does his first investigation step connect the startup attack to Orzai's family or the Witness evidence?
10. What early sign of the developing human–Luminai bond lets Sylvan endure, detect, or learn from the sabotage in a way that foreshadows the endgame?
11. What do Samuel and George force Sylvan and his Luminai to become capable of doing together that peaceful development alone would not have produced?
