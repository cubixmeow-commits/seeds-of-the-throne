# Samuel–Konrad Containment Hierarchy — Needs Analysis

## Status

This mechanism remains **UNRESOLVED IN ITS DETAILS** and should be developed in a dedicated piece-by-piece brainstorming session before the exact implementation is canonized.

Do not patch the remaining mechanism by inference. Do not assume motivations or technical rules that have not been worked through with the author.

## Hard constraints

**Konrad Fitzgerald never considered failure.**

Any model in which Konrad planned for defeat, anticipated containment, prepared a fallback hierarchy for losing the Great War, or knowingly designed a post-defeat succession arrangement is incorrect unless the author later changes this.

**New established hierarchy rule:** If a contained criminal is exiled into a containment structure and becomes the first primary contained criminal integrated into that hierarchy, the hierarchy locks around that placement. Later promises, claims, bargains, or apparent restorations do not undo the structural priority created by that first integration.

**Established consequence:** Anything Konrad later reactivates inside that already-locked containment structure is operating under the jurisdiction/control structure established by Samuel's earlier integration, regardless of what Samuel promises Konrad about ownership or restoration.

Konrad Fitzgerald does not understand this rule when he falls into containment with Samuel.

The long-term consequence of that misunderstanding is catastrophic: by reactivating the Fitzgerald machinery inside a hierarchy already locked around Samuel, Konrad helps place the operational remains of his own organization under Samuel's control. This becomes a major causal mechanism in the group's permanent destruction.

## Established pieces that need to be reconciled

- Samuel was exiled from the Fitzgerald order and was told a false story about an important foreign-government takeover assignment.
- Samuel and George later attempted to take over a government and were caught; Konrad knew about that failure.
- Samuel remains an outsider in Konrad's eyes rather than being naturally restored to prestige.
- Samuel is the contained criminal who is exiled into containment before Konrad's later defeat.
- Samuel's earlier placement establishes the hierarchy before Konrad arrives.
- The hierarchy locks with the first primary contained criminal integrated into it.
- After the Great War, Konrad and his surviving faction fall into containment.
- Samuel has a separate part/domain of the containment environment in which he has real bounded control.
- Samuel lies to Konrad about what the hierarchy means and what Konrad can recover.
- Samuel's lie is driven in part by revenge for exile and by his longer-term intention to replace Konrad with himself.
- Samuel tricks Konrad into reactivating Fitzgerald programs inside Samuel's containment jurisdiction.
- Core hinge accepted by the author:
  **Konrad thinks Samuel is giving him back his empire. In reality, Konrad has just migrated the machinery of his organization into Samuel's jurisdiction.**
- Because the hierarchy was already locked by Samuel's earlier integration, the reactivated machinery was under Samuel's structural control the entire time, regardless of what Samuel told Konrad.
- Konrad does not understand this until the consequences are already in motion.
- Those consequences are a central reason the Fitzgerald group is ultimately destroyed permanently rather than merely defeated in the Great War.

## Important correction

Do **not** assume that Konrad made plans around the possibility of losing. He did not consider failure. Therefore Samuel's opportunity and Konrad's later decisions arise from conditions created by defeat and containment, not from a Fitzgerald contingency plan.

Do **not** assume Samuel must socially replace Konrad before gaining structural advantage. Samuel may remain contemptible or pathetic in Konrad's eyes while nevertheless occupying the structurally primary position created by the containment system.

Do **not** treat Konrad's later reactivation as restoration of neutral property. Once his programs are reactivated inside the locked hierarchy, they are already operating inside Samuel's jurisdictional structure.
