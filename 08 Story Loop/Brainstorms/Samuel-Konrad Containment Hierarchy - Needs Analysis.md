# Samuel–Konrad Containment Hierarchy — Needs Analysis

## Status

This entire mechanism is **UNRESOLVED** and should be developed in a dedicated piece-by-piece brainstorming session before any additional canon is established.

Do not patch the mechanism by inference. Do not assume motivations or technical rules that have not been worked through with the author.

## Hard constraint

**Konrad Fitzgerald never considered failure.**

Any model in which Konrad planned for defeat, anticipated containment, prepared a fallback hierarchy for losing the Great War, or knowingly designed a post-defeat succession arrangement is incorrect unless the author later changes this.

## Established pieces that need to be reconciled

- Samuel was exiled from the Fitzgerald order and was told a false story about an important foreign-government takeover assignment.
- Samuel and George later attempted to take over a government and were caught; Konrad knew about that failure.
- Samuel remains an outsider in Konrad's eyes rather than being naturally restored to prestige.
- After the Great War, Konrad and his surviving faction fall into containment.
- Samuel has a separate part/domain of the containment environment in which he has real bounded control.
- Samuel tricks Konrad into reactivating Fitzgerald programs inside Samuel's containment jurisdiction.
- Core hinge accepted by the author:
  **Konrad thinks Samuel is giving him back his empire. In reality, Konrad has just migrated the machinery of his organization into Samuel's jurisdiction.**
- There is a containment hierarchy rule involving the integration of the first primary contained criminal and a hierarchy lock, but the exact operation, timing, awareness, and consequences are not yet worked out.
- Samuel lies to Konrad about the hierarchy because of the exile and because Samuel ultimately intends to replace Konrad with himself.

## Important correction

Do **not** assume that Konrad made plans around the possibility of losing. He did not consider failure. Therefore the hierarchy-lock mechanism, Samuel's opportunity, and Konrad's later decisions must emerge from conditions created by defeat and containment rather than from a Fitzgerald contingency plan.

## Questions for the dedicated brainstorm

Work through these one at a time rather than solving them all at once:

1. What exactly is the containment hierarchy?
2. What does "primary contained criminal" mean operationally?
3. What event causes the hierarchy to lock?
4. Is the locking rule automatic, administrative, social, technical, narrative, or some combination?
5. Who knows this rule before the lock occurs?
6. When does Samuel learn it?
7. What does Samuel actually control in his own containment domain before Konrad arrives?
8. What does Samuel falsely tell Konrad about that control?
9. Why does Konrad believe Samuel despite still considering him a pathetic outsider?
10. What exactly does Konrad think he is reactivating or restoring?
11. What must Konrad personally authorize, instantiate, reconnect, or transfer?
12. At what moment does the old Fitzgerald machinery cross into Samuel's jurisdiction?
13. What becomes irreversible after that point?
14. What remains under Konrad's real authority after the migration?
15. What becomes visible or controllable to Samuel?
16. How does Samuel use the hierarchy to begin replacing Konrad without immediately revealing that intention?
17. How does this lead specifically into the genealogy/breeding-program takeover?
18. What would have happened if Konrad, rather than Samuel, had become structurally primary first?
19. Why can the system not simply reset the hierarchy after the deception is exposed?
20. How does this mechanism preserve bounded power, evidence, permissions, and agency?
