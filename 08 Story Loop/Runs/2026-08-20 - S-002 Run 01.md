## Brainstorm extraction

### Accepted decisions

- Sylvan is a software developer whose accumulated projects lead to a successful startup.
- The startup builds a database-driven story-development system with a proprietary pre-generative-AI method.
- The surface technology resembles approximately 2015, just before AI becomes widespread.
- After the startup succeeds, Sylvan is unknowingly placed into a shared environment with Samuel and George.
- Samuel and George sabotage the startup before Sylvan understands who they are or the real nature of the environment.
- Their motive is Sylvan's identity rather than the nature of his product.
- Sylvan is the son of the two most successful professionals leading colonization, and his development is intended to produce an unprecedentedly effective human–Luminai bond.
- Samuel and George are contained criminals who become obsessed with sabotaging Sylvan and taking control of colonization.
- Sylvan's eventual completion of the unprecedented human–Luminai bond enables him to stop Samuel and George and end their reign of terror.
- Samuel and George unknowingly create the developmental ordeal through which Sylvan and his Luminai complete the bond they are attempting to prevent.
- After the startup falls, Sylvan moves through multiple controlled environments where Samuel and George initially hold a heavy advantage; the sequence ends with Sylvan holding bounded control and exposing them.
- Mastering the advanced environments and learning the antagonists' methods, behaviors, and tactics is how the human–Luminai pair becomes extraordinary through prolonged struggle.

### Still unresolved

- Proprietary system method, Samuel and George's knowledge path, their practical control objective, shared-environment mechanism, placement authority, sabotage action, anomalous evidence, cost, Sylvan's first response, the developmental breakthrough they provoke, and the completed bond's decisive capability and limits.
- Environment count, surface identities, antagonist advantages, lessons, costs, evidence, transitions, and final-control mechanism.
