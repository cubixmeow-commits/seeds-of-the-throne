# X Post Loop Function Test

## Purpose

Test whether repeated specialized critique produces a materially better short public explanation of the Seeds Story Gauntlet and the Obsidian vault than a single-pass draft.

This is a deliberately small artifact test. The story-loop roles are adapted to the medium rather than mechanically applying story-only criteria.

## Source facts

- The vault stores established story material, unresolved questions, character/system notes, Story Units, the master STORY-MAP, brainstorm packets, critic rules, run logs, and handoff notes.
- The active Story Gauntlet is Markdown-first and author-led.
- Core story loop: retrieve relevant vault context -> prepare focused brainstorm -> architect proposal -> independent critique -> revision/integration -> author gate -> update vault -> advance to the next gap.
- Critics do not establish canon.
- The purpose is story completion, not autonomous lore expansion.
- Current real use: the environment ladder after Sylvan's startup collapse is being developed one story unit at a time.

## Iteration 1 — Architect / one-pass baseline

> I built an Obsidian vault for Seeds of the Throne that now does more than store notes. It feeds a story-development loop: pull the relevant canon, identify the next gap, brainstorm it, build a proposal, critique it, revise it, update the vault, and repeat until the story is complete.

### Diagnosis

Clear, accurate, but generic. It describes a workflow without explaining why the vault materially improves the loop or why multiple passes are better than a single AI response.

## Iteration 2 — Function critic

> The Seeds of the Throne vault is becoming the memory layer for a story-development loop. It finds the next unresolved story unit, retrieves only the relevant canon and constraints, prepares the brainstorm, then sends the result through separate builder and critic passes before anything is integrated back into the story.

### Diagnosis

Better explanation of the vault as persistent structured memory. Missing the author gate and the completion pressure that prevents endless worldbuilding.

## Iteration 3 — Credibility / authority critic

> The Seeds of the Throne vault is now the memory and control layer for an author-led story loop. It surfaces the highest-value gap, retrieves the relevant canon and open questions, structures my brainstorm, then runs independent builder and critic passes. Nothing becomes story fact until I approve it.

### Diagnosis

Stronger authority boundary and clearer reason the workflow is safer than a monolithic generation pass. Still does not explain that results are fed back into the vault so the next cycle starts from improved state.

## Iteration 4 — Continuity / recursive-loop critic

> The Seeds of the Throne vault is becoming the memory and control layer for an author-led story loop. It surfaces the next load-bearing gap, retrieves the exact canon and unresolved questions around it, structures my brainstorm, attacks the proposal with independent critics, then writes only approved decisions back into the vault so the next cycle starts smarter.

### Diagnosis

Now explains the recursive advantage. Slightly dense and procedural for X. Needs a stronger final idea and less process-list cadence.

## Iteration 5 — Compression / public-facing integrator

> I’m turning the Seeds of the Throne Obsidian vault into the memory layer for an author-led story loop. It finds the next load-bearing gap, pulls the relevant canon, focuses the brainstorm, then sends the result through independent builder and critic passes. Only approved decisions go back into the vault, so every cycle begins from a stronger story.

## Result

The fifth pass is materially better than the baseline in four ways:

1. It defines the vault's role rather than merely saying notes are stored there.
2. It explains the reason for retrieval: the next load-bearing gap and relevant canon.
3. It makes independent critique and author authority explicit.
4. It closes the loop by explaining that approved results become improved context for the next run.

The improvement came primarily from specialized criticism identifying missing function and authority, not from repeated stylistic paraphrasing.

## Failure / learning

A short-public-post run should not mechanically use every story critic. Create a lightweight public-artifact rubric that preserves role separation but substitutes audience clarity, credibility, compression, and project continuity where character or scene causality are irrelevant.
