# Surface Archive Consistency and Variation System

## Problem

The initial Surface Archive direction correctly made the photograph control the layout, but the production system did not yet state precisely which details must remain recognizable and which details must change. A successful image could therefore become an accidental fixed template.

The newest coastal-resistance image also retained the real place name `SUMMERLAND` from the source photograph until the author requested its replacement. This exposed the need for an explicit in-world place-name rule.

## Author decision

**Established:** Future Surface Archive records must preserve the recognizable series identity while creating a genuinely new composition for every photograph.

**Established:** The recognition anchors are the Seeds of the Throne and Surface Archive hierarchy, crown or throne mark, antique-gold archival linework, recurring palette family, restrained orbital or cartographic system geometry, sparse coral or ember registration nodes, photograph dominance, and phone-size readability.

**Established:** Canvas, crop, title placement, reading order, border behavior, geometric metaphor, palette emphasis, record category, Luminai-note treatment, and the image-specific signal remain responsive variables. At least three meaningful variables must change from the most recent record. Color swaps alone do not satisfy this requirement.

**Established:** A generation normally uses the source photograph plus no more than two Surface Archive references. One reference proves the closest identity and tone. The other deliberately demonstrates a different composition so the model does not copy a single layout.

**Established:** Real capture provenance remains in the private episode record. Images presented as in-world records remove readable real-world place names, coordinates, logos, and institutions. A fictional replacement is visual interpretation rather than automatic story canon.

**Established for Record 0063:** `MERIDIAN COAST` replaces `SUMMERLAND` in the visual artifact. This does not by itself establish Meridian Coast as a canonical location.

## Approved reference set

Four compressed WebP references now preserve complementary expressions of the same identity:

- Record 0043, Shoreline Observation: quiet natural observation and photograph dominance.
- Record 0061, Mountain Transit: daylight narrative record and strong archival frame.
- Record 0062, Dream Integration: dream-state contrast and connection geometry.
- Record 0063, Coastal Resistance: environmental story, fictionalized signage, and a subtle hidden signal.

The reference files, roles, dimensions, and checksums are recorded in `skills/create-seeds-images/assets/reference-images/surface-archive/README.md`.
