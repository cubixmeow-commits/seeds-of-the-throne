# RW-01 Process Completion Threshold

## Question explored

What exact completed condition makes Samuel's formal separation from Sylvan inevitable?

## Existing constraints

- Sylvan and Orzai already survive Samuel's high-influence stages and complete their separate human–Luminai bonds.
- Their relationship strengthens but does not replace their independent qualification.
- Samuel's final controlled-story bargain fails.
- Formal separation removes every process-provided system Samuel used against Sylvan and activates Samuel's independent $15 million allocation.
- The ending cannot depend on a rule invented in the final scene or on Samuel voluntarily accepting the result.

## Possibilities considered

The workshop considered a qualification threshold, a final-refusal trigger, an evidence threshold, and a combined threshold. The accepted direction combines completed bonds, independently demonstrated qualification, voluntary partnership under interference, and final refusal as confirmation.

## Author decisions

**Established:** Sylvan and Orzai complete the process when their individual human–Luminai bonds, preserved voluntary partnership, and demonstrated conduct satisfy the pre-established requirements for earned placement.

The process's assessment framework certifies that completed state and automatically executes the transition. Sylvan's final refusal of Samuel's bargain confirms that he will not renew controlled dependence, but the refusal does not create the qualification or function as a magical trigger.

The observable state change is immediate and irreversible:

1. Sylvan and Orzai's earned placement eligibility locks.
2. Samuel is formally separated from Sylvan.
3. Samuel loses every process-provided system he used to attack Sylvan.
4. Samuel's independent $15 million allocation activates.

Samuel cannot delay the transition by proposing another bargain or reinterpreting the outcome because certification has already completed.

The author accepted this direction with: **“That's good.”**

## Current working direction

The final refusal is the human character beat that makes the completed qualification visible. The assessment and separation machinery must be established earlier enough that the audience understands why the transition cannot be reversed. The final room does not need to understand every score; the locked placement status, revoked access, and activated independent allocation make the result observable.

## Unresolved questions

- Which exact assessment dimensions establish each placement path remains open.
- The material form of Sylvan and Orzai's accepted placement remains RW-04.
- Samuel's spending channels and secondary accelerant remain RW-02.
- The authentication that prevents forged qualification or corrupted records remains RW-06.
- Exact presentation choreography remains open.
