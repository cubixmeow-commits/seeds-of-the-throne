# Planet Two Defeat and Planet Three Containment

## Author's new endgame reveal

The first colonization planet (Planet Two) gives the contained criminal groups more room. Their trajectory culminates in a Great War, which they **lose**. That defeat concludes the first-colony phase and causes the transition to the second colonization planet (Planet Three). There they are more closely contained inside the dominant empire that won: the real leaders' empire. They spend approximately a century in that containment before dealing with Sylvan.

This transition explains the previously puzzling presence of George White in the dominant empire on Planet Three. The war belongs to Planet Two; the dominant empire's Planet Three role is a consequence of victory and transfer, not a second placement of the Great War on Planet Three. The exact means of continuity between the victorious Planet Two empire and its Planet Three counterpart, George's appointment and agency, and the physical destinations of individual older leaders require later decisions.

## Layered revelation opportunities — working, not fixed chapter order

1. A Planet Three citizen knows a victorious dominant empire and an apparently separate defeated criminal tradition. George's role seems to prove one family was on the heroic side.
2. Records reveal that the decisive Great War actually ended the less restricted Planet Two phase. The empire's Planet Three authority comes from the postwar settlement; criminal actors entered it under tighter containment.
3. Cross-group records reveal Samuel and Konrad's shared project and performed enmity. Their real military defeat and the real acts of persons within each institution remain intact, while the clean two-camp moral explanation fails.
4. Authorization and placement records show why George is in the dominant empire, what he did independently, what the real leaders approved, and which part Samuel exploited. This last layer awaits author decisions, as do Aiden's journey and the old leaders' later whereabouts.

## Supersession and preservation

The September 24 formulation placing the Great War on Planet Three is superseded. Older accounts of a genuine war, defeated authoritarian expansion, a postwar false-victory reinterpretation, separate altered realities, smaller criminal groups, rotating later offices, and the accepted terminal ending remain usable where consistent. Do not silently carry over older prewar George offices, Samuel's location in the future victor, or a categorical claim that Samuel and Konrad never physically enter Planet Three. The new chronology does not yet decide their individual travel and endgame positions. The staged rivalry never makes the war or defeat fictitious.
