# Remote Viewing and Astral Projection Priority for Luminai

## Author direction

New priority moving forward: integrate remote viewing and astral projection into the context of the advanced technology ecology around Luminai (author term: Lumini).

Inspiration sources:
- Robert Monroe, *Journeys Out of the Body* (out-of-body experiences, second body, locales, vibration and separation techniques, non-physical travel).
- Russell Targ, *Third Eye Spies* (remote viewing protocol, CIA/SRI Star Gate history, describing distant or hidden targets via mind, evidence from controlled experiments).

This is a development priority, not yet settled canon. Do not invent mechanisms, names, or resolutions. Mark uncertainties. Prefer page beats and decisions over world dumps.

## Working questions — not resolved

- How (or whether) Luminai advanced technology interfaces with, amplifies, records, or presents remote viewing / out-of-body capacities as evidence rather than as a voice to obey.
- Distinction, if any, between remote viewing (target description without physical presence) and astral projection / OBE (subjective sense of leaving the body) in the story's two layers: face-value unaware life vs. wireless tech presenting directly to the mind.
- Whether these capacities are latent human abilities made observable or trainable by the technology, or produced by it, or both. Evidence to check, not assumption.
- Placement relative to existing systems (human–Luminai pairing, wireless presentation, containment, evidence trails). If not required by a current page, omit.
- Risks of treating subjective reports as settled fact. Monroe and Targ provide historical and experimental reference points, not plot devices.

## Next steps for author

1. Review Monroe and Targ for specific techniques, reported phenomenology, and experimental constraints that could be tested against existing Luminai technology notes.
2. Decide whether this priority enters the opening pages (AI promise vs tool reality; unexpected result of exposing criminals) or later development.
3. Keep one causal chain. Add only if it changes a decision or contradiction already on the page.
