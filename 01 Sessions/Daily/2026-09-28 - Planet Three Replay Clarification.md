# Planet Three Replay Clarification

## Author correction

The **Great War, the criminals’ defeat, and the victorious real leaders’ empire all belong to Planet Two**, the first colonization planet. Its criminal groups are less contained. Their loss concludes that planet’s phase.

**Planet Three is a replay that places people and begins Planet Three’s own process.** The defeated criminal history informs the roles and tighter containment in this second colonization planet, roughly a century before the encounter with Sylvan. The replay does not establish that a Planet Two empire, its sovereign authority, or its entire population physically moves to Planet Three. It does not make Planet Three’s events imaginary: people live and act in a new real society with consequential roles and institutions. George White’s position in Planet Three’s dominant-empire role is an intentional placement within this replay, with his exact appointment, office, personal knowledge and agency still open.

This clarifies and supersedes the **institutional migration inference** in [[01 Sessions/Daily/2026-09-28 - Planet Two Defeat and Planet Three Containment]]. The Great War remains real and located on Planet Two. The Planet Three replay may surface a victor/loser narrative, but its exact historical fidelity, entry assignments, disclosure to participants, authorization and individual physical travel have not been settled. Older assertions that Samuel and Konrad remain on Planet Two in the Sylvan endgame are not disproved by replayed Planet Three roles. The accepted terminal sequence remains intact.

## Working audience layers

1. In Planet Three, George is seen in the dominant empire, while defeated-side roles and records suggest a familiar war history. The audience might assume that the war and victory happened there.
2. Independent records show the Great War and its actual victors belonged to Planet Two. Planet Three was initialized later as a new process that replays and places people into corresponding roles.
3. Placement and authorization evidence can reveal why George occupies his role, how the contained groups’ earlier defeat shaped tighter constraints, and who staged the apparently opposed camps. The latter mechanisms remain open.
4. Separate evidence exposes Samuel and Konrad’s shared project, Samuel’s staged blame and later betrayal, preserving the real agency and harm in both worlds.

## Next development decision

Define the **replay’s operational rules**: what is reconstructed from Planet Two (institutions, events, roles, records or some mixture), what is new on Planet Three, who assigns George’s and Aiden’s roles, and which participants know the historical source. The apparent empire in Planet Three does not need continuous sovereignty from Planet Two. Distinguish this world-building question from the older leaders’ physical locations at Sylvan’s endgame.
