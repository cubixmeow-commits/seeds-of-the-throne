# Four Character Journal Design Bibles

## Question explored

How can Samuel Franklin, Konrad Fitzgerald, Sylvan Elaria, and Orzai each have a journal whose object, handwriting, drawing language, page composition, and emotional evolution are immediately distinguishable?

## Existing constraints

- Journal images do not establish story canon. Physical designs, handwriting sheets, and drawing sheets retain their own approval status.
- Generated lettering is illustrative until checked against an exact transcription stored in the vault.
- Samuel is an impulsive, obsessive, greedy, jealous liar and near-genius individualized manipulator. His later failure comes from rage and replacement obsession corrupting brilliant analysis, not from stupidity.
- Konrad is a brilliant authoritarian organizer and cult persuader whose arrogance, contempt, hierarchy, and refusal to accept defeat make him exploitable.
- Sylvan is outgoing, socially exploratory, evidence-oriented, and trained for accountable leadership. He preserves competing explanations and develops through contact with reality.
- Orzai is more reserved and selective, with independent expertise across health, nutrition, creative design, video, and 3D modeling. She notices thresholds, omissions, and representational consequences.
- Orzai and Sylvan complement one another without sharing one voice or surrendering independent judgment.
- Exact eras, manufacturers, heraldry, signatures, and some object histories remain unresolved. No real-world insignia or accidental invented institution may be introduced through decoration.

## Author decisions

- Build four journals around Samuel, Konrad, Sylvan, and Orzai.
- Give each journal a unique physical design and material identity.
- Give each character unique handwriting, drawing style, and a unique way of combining words with images.
- Store both descriptions and many reusable writing/drawing samples in the vault.

## Current working direction

### Samuel — the instrument of occupation

Use a refined black-green calfskin intelligence portfolio with replaceable stitched packets, hidden document pockets, cream laid paper, black ink, graphite, and restrained red pencil. His narrow forward-driving hand turns sentences into nodes, routes underlined words into pressure maps, and increasingly writes through other people's names and systems. The page begins as analysis and ends as attempted possession.

### Konrad — the monument of order

Use a large rigid dynastic ledger in dark oxblood goatskin over boards, with blind tooling, protected corners, a clasp, thick warm rag paper, and formal ruling. His broad upright hand places declarations above vertical descent structures, ranked boxes, and administrative plans. The page does not explore; it announces, classifies, and retroactively renames defeat.

### Sylvan — the portable field of inquiry

Use a modular weather-resistant field-notebook family with charcoal woven-composite covers, exposed contrasting stitch, replaceable indexed volumes, mixed dot-grid/plain paper, blue-black waterproof ink, graphite, and removable provenance tabs. His open humanist print-cursive hybrid sends observations into question rings, alternatives, source brackets, timelines, and permission boundaries. The page stays conversational and revisable.

### Orzai — the layered model

Use a compact lay-flat journal in slate-blue linen and pale vegetable-tanned leather, with mixed-media paper and translucent vellum overlays. Graphite, indigo, muted terracotta, and occasional white pencil distinguish structure, uncertainty, and intervention thresholds. Her small precise upright hand sits beside anatomy, nutrition flows, storyboards, camera plans, and 3D form studies. Writing identifies what is known; overlays test what a representation changes.

These are `working` visual-development directions, not locked artifact canon. Generated reference boards remain `candidate` until the author approves them.

## Unresolved questions

- Which physical designs should become approved or locked after visual review?
- Does Konrad personally keep the ledger, dictate portions, or use clerks for some entries?
- Does Samuel's selective imitation of Konrad begin consciously, unconsciously, or in stages?
- What are each character's approved signatures or ownership marks?
- Which notebook version belongs to each story period, and what resolved surface era/material supply supports it?
- Which exact sample pages should become story evidence rather than style-only demonstrations?

## Vault impact

- Expand the four character journal profiles into design bibles.
- Add a writing-and-drawing sample library for each character.
- Add candidate visual reference boards and generation records without promoting them to approved status.
- Update journal and handwriting registries, context/coordination tracking, and QA decisions.
