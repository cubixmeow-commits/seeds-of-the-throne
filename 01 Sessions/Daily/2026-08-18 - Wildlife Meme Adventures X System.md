# Wildlife Meme Adventures X System

## Author direction

Create a dedicated vault section for Wildlife Meme Adventures posts on X. Preserve episode data, source photography, generated images, captions, publication history, and lessons so the series becomes repeatable without repeating jokes or visual concepts.

## Decision

The system belongs under `05 Public/Wildlife Meme Adventures/`. It is a separate public-photography project and has no story-canon authority for *Seeds of the Throne*.

Each episode begins with a real local wildlife photograph. Subject, location, and capture date must come from verified metadata or author confirmation. The generated image preserves the animal and authentic scene while adding the established Wildlife Meme Adventures poster treatment and an original episode title, joke, and visual direction.

## Image-responsive visual system

The author wants the series to behave like Vivaldi's adaptive theming: preserve the same feel and recognizable umbrella identity, but let each photograph determine a complementary design and color system. The series therefore does not have one mandatory palette or one mandatory layout.

Keep `WILDLIFE MEME ADVENTURES` and `LOCAL WILDLIFE PHOTOGRAPHY` as restrained recurring identity. Derive the episode palette from the real animal, habitat, weather, and light. Change composition, border language, typography accents, and graphic motifs to fit the image. When exploring options, make each concept structurally distinct rather than offering recolored copies.

This decision was tested on the Lake Los Carneros snake photograph through three directions: a banded field journal, a topographic trail survey, and a dusk naturalist poster.

The exact published caption, date, platform link, source photograph, generated image, approval state, and lessons belong in a per-episode record. Fictional joke premises remain clearly separate from factual wildlife metadata.

## Canonical X caption structure

The author supplied the recurring format:

1. `Wildlife Meme Adventures 🧭`
2. one short quoted episode joke;
3. a `📍` location line at the approved privacy level;
4. a `📅` line containing date, weekday, and local capture time;
5. a `Behind the meme:` paragraph that identifies the real photograph before adding one grounded comic observation.

Do not add hashtags by default. Preserve the structure while keeping every episode's wording original.

## Series emoji decision

The canonical recurring header is `Wildlife Meme Adventures 🧭`. The author selected the compass on August 18, 2026 because it emphasizes exploration and remains appropriate across different animals, habitats, and occasional non-animal adventure episodes. The earlier wave emoji is retired for unpublished drafts and templates.

## Current state

The vault now contains the Coleman outdoor-barbecue episode and a three-concept Lake Los Carneros snake design study. Both remain drafts until the author approves an image and caption.

## GitHub image decision

The vault stores only lightweight image previews, targeting roughly 50 KB each. Current poster previews are 512 × 768 JPEGs and source previews are 768 × 512 JPEGs. Full-resolution uploads and generated production masters remain outside the repository; episode records retain their original dimensions, checksums, and provenance. Retrieve the production master before publishing rather than posting the compressed vault preview.
