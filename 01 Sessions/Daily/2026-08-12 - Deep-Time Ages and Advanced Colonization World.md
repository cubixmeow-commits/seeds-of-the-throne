# Deep-Time Ages and Advanced Colonization World

## Question explored

How old Samuel, Konrad, their senior generation, and the legitimate real leaders are at the Sylvan endgame, and how those ages fit the history of the current colonization world.

## Existing constraints

- Aging has been defeated in the parent civilization.
- Samuel has a substantial criminal history before containment, then rises through the current planet's century-scale process.
- Konrad belongs to an older senior contained generation and predates Samuel.
- The legitimate empires and their leaders already operate on civilizational timescales.
- George's public-story period occupies approximately seventy-five years within the larger planetary century.

## Author decisions

- Samuel's pre-containment rampage lasts approximately fifty years.
- His exposure, capture, processing, and integration take several additional decades.
- Approximately one century of the current colonization process follows before the final showdown with Sylvan.
- Samuel is therefore a few hundred years old and substantially younger than Konrad.
- Konrad Fitzgerald and his still-unnamed senior partner are approximately three hundred years old at the showdown.
- The legitimate real leaders are thousands of years old.
- Humanity has already expanded to numerous other planets under those leaders.
- The current planet is unusual because it is more technologically managed and advanced than earlier colonization worlds.
- Its construction receives material, technical, and institutional support made possible by the success of the earlier planets.

## Current working direction

Samuel is approximately **220 years old** at the Sylvan showdown. This is a working anchor rather than a locked birth year. It allows roughly twenty years before the beginning of his rampage, about fifty years of escalating criminal activity, roughly twenty to thirty years for exposure and integration, and about one hundred twenty years from entry into the planet's process to the endgame. The broad proportions are established; exact durations can move as the calendar develops.

Samuel's chronological age is distinct from his approved visual appearance in his late sixties to early seventies. Konrad and his unnamed partner are each approximately **300 years old** at the showdown. Their visual ages remain unresolved.

The real leaders are not founding interplanetary expansion for the first time on this planet. They are multiple millennia old and already govern a mature network of human civilizations on numerous worlds. Earlier colonization successes provide the expertise, production capacity, population support, and political confidence needed to construct this more advanced world.

The current planet is a later-generation undertaking: unusually dense with technological control, synthetic preparation, hidden influence, authenticated records, containment architecture, and leadership cultivation. It is both a colony and the most ambitious use yet of accumulated colonization knowledge.

## Unresolved questions

- Samuel's exact age when the rampage begins and the exact interval between capture and planetary integration.
- Whether the current colonization process lasts exactly 100 years or closer to 120 years at the showdown.
- The exact ages, names, and physical appearances of Konrad's partner and the real leaders.
- How many earlier planets exist, how politically independent they are, and what each contributes to the current world.
- Whether the current planet is the first to combine colonization, containment, heir cultivation, and evidentiary adjudication at this scale.
