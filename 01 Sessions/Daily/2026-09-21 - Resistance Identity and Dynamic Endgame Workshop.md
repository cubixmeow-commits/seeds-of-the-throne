# Resistance Identity and Dynamic Endgame Workshop

## Question explored

How should the latest Resistance visual decisions and George–Aiden–Samuel endgame chain enter the vault, and how should desktop assessments maintain one canonical ongoing workshop rather than producing competing question sets?

## Existing constraints

- The Resistance remains independent from Sylvan and owns indispensable discoveries.
- Samuel never controls the governing colonization process; all apparent power held by the contained criminal groups remains bounded inside it.
- The original prophecy predates Samuel. Exposure proves his fraudulent application, not that faith or prophecy is false.
- George and Aiden retain agency and accountability even while Samuel manipulates their information and expectations.
- Descendants targeted through the breeding program remain people, not evidence objects, and private parentage must be protected.

## Author decisions

- The Resistance colors are **black and orange**.
- Its central visual metaphor is an **ark**: preservation of belief, culture, memory, testimony, family continuity, sanctuary, and safety from persecution by contained criminal groups. The ark does not claim divine chosenness or authority to rule.
- Samuel secretly and separately promises George White and Aiden Fitzgerald the same exclusive end state: absolute, godlike power over everyone else. This is one con run on two marks.
- George's long savior-prophecy is an altered reality built around signs that make the promised transformation appear real.
- The supposed superweapon appears effective only while Sylvan is severely disadvantaged. It fails once Sylvan begins publicly exposing Samuel and the underlying control structure.
- The failure forces George and Aiden's separate stories into comparison. They discover that the same singular throne was promised twice.
- Samuel continues trying to aim George and Aiden at one another, telling each that the other caused the failure or is stealing the promised position.
- George and Aiden belong to the younger generation produced through the contained older leaders' breeding program. Samuel turned that system against the older leaders' bloodline ideology, targeting their lines, producing unauthorized heirs, and extending the betrayal across later generations.
- The younger leaders' apparent roles were never intended to become transferable power outside containment. They were disposable even if the attempted conquest appeared to succeed.
- The trapped leaders eventually identify Samuel as the deceiver they were warned about and must fight their way out against him.
- One dynamic workshop becomes the canonical ongoing workshop and is updated after every desktop assessment.

## Current working direction

The false weapon's failure is the hinge between belief and comparison. Sylvan's public exposure does not by itself redeem or command the trapped leaders; it makes Samuel's duplicated promises and live revisions observable. The leaders must then choose and act. Samuel's attempt to preserve the con by redirecting both marks at one another becomes fresh evidence of continuing command.

The older BA, EG, RW, and Reveal Chain workshop files remain historical development records. Their unresolved material is folded into [[07 Coordination/Story Completion Workflow/DYNAMIC-WORKSHOP]], which alone governs the next assessment pass.

- Which trapped leader first acts against Samuel, and what makes that action irreversible?
- What does “fight their way out” require operationally without granting Samuel control over the governing process?
- Which Resistance cell preserves the decisive comparison and how is it authenticated without exposing descendants?
