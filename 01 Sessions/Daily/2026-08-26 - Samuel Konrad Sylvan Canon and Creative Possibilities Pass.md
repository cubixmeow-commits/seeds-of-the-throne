## Author decisions integrated

- Konrad always intended Samuel's impossible mission as removal; Samuel suspected betrayal and separately bet on failed revolution leading to favorable containment placement.
- Samuel's conditional alpha advantage activates because Konrad later enters the same empire's containment hierarchy.
- Samuel is the primary decision-making alpha and Konrad eventually the beta in a constant power struggle.
- Samuel's apparent separate half for Konrad is a story-functionality-supported lie over one shared environment.
- Samuel persuades Konrad that processing Sylvan will lawfully transfer control; the real path is to stop the rampage and cooperate with legitimate leaders.
- Konrad seeks willing public surrender as god-king coronation.
- Samuel's synthetic-army conquest theory has no sound mechanism and remains to be developed later.
- Samuel personally maximizes participating bloodlines across roughly eighty years, producing tens of thousands of unauthorized children, and intends the hidden pattern for blackmail.
- The superweapon promise is a desperate lie; attacks on Sylvan have almost no effect.
- Konrad's reality model collapses as Sylvan's information explains events better than Samuel's.
- Dozens of recognizable unauthorized children prove the bloodline targeting and false separation to Konrad.
- Sylvan neutralizes the attacks, exposes Samuel and Konrad to each other, and reveals the real cooperation requirement.
- Samuel immediately attempts bloodline-exposure blackmail; Konrad's first response centers blame and destruction on Samuel while preserving his broader culpability.
