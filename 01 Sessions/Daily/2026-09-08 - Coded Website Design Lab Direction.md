# Coded Website Design Lab Direction

After deploying the Hidden Planetary Infrastructure redesign and repairing its mobile overflow, the author rejected the resulting visual treatment. The pages function, but the black-dominant field, yellow or antique-gold accent, boxed layout, traditional archive typography, and detached poster-image composition do not provide a good or distinctive look.

The structural idea of a real surface civilization above concealed planetary systems remains useful. The deployed black-and-yellow archive treatment does not.

Future redesign work must begin as functioning responsive code rather than a generated image, palette table, or prose-only handoff. Two materially different prototypes will be built with real content and representative interaction. The author will review the working pages on mobile and desktop before any production template is changed.

The permanent workflow is defined by `skills/design-seeds-site/SKILL.md`. The immediate implementation assignment is [[07 Coordination/2026-09-08 - Cursor Coded Website Design Lab]].

No story canon or approved public copy changed.
