# Development System Integration Pass

## Question explored

How should the full August 21 mobile work become one coherent, author-led development environment rather than a collection of adjacent loops and handoff notes?

## Existing constraints

- The vault is currently for story development, not default finished-novel drafting.
- Readable 500–1,500 word development prototypes are disposable tests, not manuscript pages or canon.
- General high-level strengths associated with accessible long-form science fiction and *Expeditionary Force* may guide scene engineering; Craig Alanson's exact prose, voices, phrasing, jokes, and other distinctive expression must not be imitated.
- Exploration must distinguish established, working, proposed, unresolved, rejected, and non-canon material.
- Breadth is generated cheaply; depth is spent only on survivors.
- Research supplies mechanisms and constraints, not story authority.
- The author alone promotes candidates or resolves load-bearing choices.

Containment continuity that must survive integration:

- Konrad had genuine control in his own city and did not plan for defeat.
- The catastrophic loss of control happens only after defeat, inside the largest empire's containment environment.
- Samuel remains a socially disdained outsider in Konrad's eyes.
- Samuel's earlier placement, bounded jurisdiction, first-primary hierarchy lock, Konrad's later reactivation/migration, and the accepted migration hinge are established at the stated level.
- The practical hierarchy architecture, lock event, transferred powers, reset limits, exact lie, and full destruction chain remain unresolved and belong to the dedicated piece-by-piece brainstorm.

## Author decisions

- Finished-novel prose is downstream and is not the default development output.
- The development system must support gap analysis, research-to-story translation, multiscale construction, lightweight cast generation, Creative Interest and structural critics, token-aware exploration, short readable prototypes, comparison, and author promotion gates.

## Current working direction

Use one orchestration path:

`scope -> retrieve -> classify authority -> analyze gaps -> choose run mode -> diverge cheaply -> shortlist -> research only if useful -> expand one branch -> build only required characters -> create scale-appropriate structure -> prototype only when useful -> run relevant critics -> stop at author gate -> integrate only approved changes -> record next state`

The Gap Analyzer is the front end. The Development Orchestrator routes work among the Story Exploration Lab, Research–Creativity–Story Fit Loop, Character Factory, Multiscale Development Gauntlet, Problem-Solving Story Engine, Development Prototype Style, critics, and final author gate.
