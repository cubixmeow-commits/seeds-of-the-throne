# Orzai's Older Brother and the Company Counterpart

## Question explored

How should the other child of Orzai's parents participate in the modern story and contrast with Samuel's replacement heir?

## Existing constraints

- Each founder-child cross-couple has one son and one daughter.
- Orzai is the daughter in her branch, belongs to Sylvan's generation, and is slightly younger than him.
- Samuel's replacement heir leads a government-integrated intelligence and decision-systems company positioned at an information chokepoint.
- Legitimate heirs must develop through constrained roles rather than receive unlimited authority.

## Author decisions

- Orzai has an older brother.
- He is approximately a decade older than Sylvan.
- He is placed for development and training in a company similar in institutional importance and capability to the company associated with Samuel's replacement heir.

## Current working direction

Orzai's older brother provides a legitimate-heir counterpart to Samuel's replacement heir. Both operate near consequential government, identity, relationship, infrastructure, or decision systems, but their placements arise from different purposes. Samuel positions his replacement son as a contingency and potential instrument of control. Orzai's brother is placed under meaningful constraints so he must demonstrate judgment, service, accountability, and the ability to resist institutional capture.

The companies should not become simple duplicates. Their exact mandates, oversight structures, technical boundaries, relationship, and possible rivalry or cooperation remain unresolved. It is also unresolved whether Orzai's brother founded the company, leads part of it, enters through a bounded public role, or is deliberately denied ownership while being tested inside it.

## Unresolved questions

- What is Orzai's brother's name and exact age?
- What does the legitimate company do that overlaps with and differs from Samuel Jr.'s company?
- What authority does Orzai's brother possess, and what authority is deliberately withheld?
- Do the two heirs cooperate, compete, audit one another, or discover that their systems expose the same hidden genealogy?
- What does Orzai's brother believe about the Witness history carried by one of their parents?
- How does his relationship with Orzai differ from his relationship with Sylvan?
