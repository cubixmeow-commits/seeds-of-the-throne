## Proposed origin sequence — causal dependencies rather than settled timestamps

1. **Private initiative:** a small consortium starts the colonization enterprise outside the level of government control it rejects. It raises capital, recruits specialist firms and develops real infrastructure. Decide what public authority existed then and whether resisting it was legal or unlawful; the resistance itself is not the crime.
2. **Society-building breakthrough:** at some point the biological synthetic architecture and supporting orbital infrastructure make large colonization societies possible. The first venture's date relative to the breakthrough is not fixed; it may begin with other technologies and gain these later. The home world, Planet Two and Planet Three do not automatically share the same founding contracts.
3. **Power and captured story:** a subset of wealthy insiders or outside investors appropriates the venture's prestige. It conflates real regeneration or medicine with a promised leap to synthetic bodies, supremacy and godhood. Its ability to recruit large groups comes from real industrial achievements plus false interpretation, not from proof of the advertised transformation.
4. **Intervention trigger:** specify verifiable recruitment claims, false reporting, coercive treatment of human or synthetic persons, diversion of infrastructure or attempted monopoly. Laws and oversight respond to those acts; determine who holds lawful authority and how former builders' hidden access is independently removed.
5. **Contained test:** convicted offenders and/or participants under another lawful, specified entry path are drawn **into** a bounded colonization environment. The offer is a chance to demonstrate their **specific stated claim** under conditions protective of all participants, not a blank grant of control. Their followers may perceive the opportunity as validation.
6. **World-scale history:** on a real planet, synthetics and other residents build civic lives; heirs, criminals and descendants occupy consequential roles. Samuel and Konrad organize one project behind apparent war opposition. The failed enhancement of George and Aiden, the coercive lineage program and later Samuel-only sabotage are specific episodes, not interchangeable laboratory proofs.
7. **Public reckoning:** the governing leaders hope documented failure of promised ascension will be so unmistakable that mass recruiting collapses. A trusted result requires independent witnesses, comparable claims, protected subjects, known stop rules and evidence outsiders can authenticate despite no local tool capable of exposing nanocell cores. If the final Samuel collapse is used as its climax, the terminal sequence remains intact; no decision yet says the two events are identical. Followers may still dispute the interpretation.

## Causal and ethical limits for the spectacle

- A failure can be **spectacular in public meaning** through simultaneous disclosure of false promises, documented failures, survivor testimony and the offenders' inability to meet their own precise claim. It need not be a staged public humiliation or a dangerous show experiment.
- Those who run the process can be wrong, divided or culpable for allowing harm. Oversight must apply to them; the audience should have a way to evaluate whether they fairly defined success and protected others.
- Legitimate regeneration, long life and neural medicine may exist while a transfer of one's brain into a synthetic body, godlike command over others and a hereditary right to both remain unproved or false as advertised.
- Synthetic persons' independence matters even if an engineered body could technically be built; no corporate contract or test can turn a person into a spare vessel.
- No test permanently ends a doctrine. The leaders can aim to end **this demonstrably false mass promise and this group's access to power**, then face the social consequences of those who persist.
