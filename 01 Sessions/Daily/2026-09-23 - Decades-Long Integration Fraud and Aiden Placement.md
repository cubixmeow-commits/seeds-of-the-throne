# Decades-Long Integration Fraud and Aiden Placement

## Question explored

How do the failed George/Aiden experiments become a decades-long apparent contest with Sylvan, and where is Aiden during the endgame?

## Existing constraints

- The older contained leaders' experiments injured George and Aiden; Samuel removed the failed chips with Konrad's knowledge, without telling the two recipients.
- Story functionality can stage apparent success inside physically real environments but cannot actually confer the missing brain integration or command the governing colonization process.
- Sylvan and Orzai's successor bonds are genuine. Earlier Daemon systems may also be genuine; the absence of competition applies to this purported George/Aiden chip breakthrough.
- Konrad and Samuel are physically on the older containment world during the endgame. A prior location note placed only George directly on Sylvan's world and implied Aiden remained with Konrad; the author's new explicit placement supersedes that implication.

## Author decisions

- The George White and Aiden Fitzgerald implant attempts failed **decades before the endgame**. Samuel declared them successful instead of admitting failure; Konrad knowingly joined the continuing lie.
- Samuel sought to prove George and Aiden superior to Sylvan. They received roles in altered realities and mistook staged story-functionality effects for their own enhanced mental abilities, although the failed experiments had decreased their mental functioning. Their injuries are real, not the source of their moral choices.
- Samuel and Konrad attacked Sylvan throughout the supposed competition. The staged roles and attacks served the false proof, rather than demonstrating a genuine rival integration. Sylvan closes in on the con.
- **Aiden and George are both physically on Sylvan's planet, Planet Three, during this endgame** and can each act there directly. Konrad and Samuel remain on the older containment world. Konrad's participation in attacks on Sylvan need not imply he is physically present beside Aiden.
- At the end, Konrad's **only priorities** are shutting down the continuing breeding program and exposing Samuel. He discloses the chip-removal fraud after discovering the wider breeding sabotage as a means to those ends, not to vindicate his technology or redeem himself. His complicity in the decades-long lie remains exposed.

## Current working direction

The false breakthrough becomes a long proof theater: apparent roles and local effects are interpreted as George and Aiden's successful brain integration while Samuel and Konrad arrange attacks on Sylvan. When the controlled conditions and the removal records are compared with the actual capabilities, the claim collapses. This is a story-development structure, not a fixed list of scenes, specific powers, or public disclosure sequence.

## Unresolved questions

- When, how, and under whose authority does Aiden move from the earlier postwar arrangement with Konrad to Planet Three? Shared interpretive captivity does not require continued physical co-location.
- How long has each man been on Planet Three, what exact roles did each receive, and what did Konrad personally direct versus merely endorse?
- Which independent effects come from real older Daemons, and which specific claimed chip-enabled feats require Samuel's story machinery?
- What records prove injury, removal, staging, Konrad's knowing endorsement, and the actual attacks without collapsing George and Aiden's distinct accountability?
- Which breeding-program permissions can Konrad revoke or halt, and what remains outside his authority?
