# Five-Color Visual Grammar

## Author decision

Add green and blue to the public visual grammar with unique narrative qualities.

## Current working direction

- Crimson conceals: coercion, corrupted certainty, false authority, inherited violence, and controlled reality.
- Gold reveals: evidence, provenance, constructive power, Luminai integration, and paths back into reality.
- Green cultivates: living growth, repair, adaptation, ecological intelligence, and development that preserves identity.
- Blue connects: communication across distance, shared infrastructure, orbital coordination, interplanetary civilization, and cooperation between separate minds and worlds.
- Black preserves scale: negative space, isolation of the human figure, and the magnitude of luminous systems.

Green is not generic goodness. It belongs to living development and repair. Blue is not generic technology or cyberpunk light. It belongs to connection, coordination, and civilization-scale systems.
