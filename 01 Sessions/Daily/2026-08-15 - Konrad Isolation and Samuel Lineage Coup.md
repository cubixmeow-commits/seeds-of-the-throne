# Konrad Isolation and Samuel Lineage Coup

## Question explored

What does Konrad know about Samuel's targeting of the breeding-program bloodlines when Sylvan confronts Samuel with the evidence, and why is Konrad absent from the confrontation?

## Existing constraints

- Konrad created the purity doctrine and centralized breeding program, then granted Samuel access because he wanted Samuel's promises to be true.
- Samuel used the program to target leading families through reproductive abuse, false genealogy, synthetic substitutions, certification control, and individualized blackmail.
- Samuel's fixation on Konrad is an attempt to appropriate and replace Konrad's dynasty and authority.
- Sylvan's archive can expose the organization-wide pattern without treating descendants as culpable evidence.

## Author decisions

- By the Sylvan endgame, Konrad knows the breeding program and participating bloodlines have been targeted badly enough that he understands his own decisions made the abuse possible.
- Konrad is in hiding or severe isolation because he is ultimately responsible for creating and surrendering control of the system Samuel weaponized.
- Sylvan suspects Samuel is continuing to blackmail or control Konrad through one or more proxies.

## Current working direction

Konrad's knowledge remains materially incomplete. He may not know that Samuel targeted every participating bloodline or recognize that the system-wide campaign was a coup intended to appropriate Konrad's dynasty, followers, and authority. This partial knowledge explains both his withdrawal and Samuel's panic when Sylvan reveals that the full pattern is documented.

Konrad remains culpable as the architect and protector of the original program. His later targeting does not convert him into an innocent victim.

## Unresolved questions

- Which proxy or chain of proxies carries Samuel's threats or instructions to Konrad?
- What specific discovery drives Konrad into hiding?
- Does Konrad know the full scope before the results presentation, learn it during the presentation, or discover it afterward?
- Is Konrad hiding primarily from victims, his own faction, the legitimate system, Samuel, or all four?
- What does Samuel believe Konrad will do if he learns the targeting was organization-wide and part of a coup?
