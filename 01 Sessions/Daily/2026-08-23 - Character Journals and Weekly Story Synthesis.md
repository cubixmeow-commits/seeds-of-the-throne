# Character Journals and Weekly Story Synthesis

## Question explored

How can character journals become repeatable physical story artifacts and forensic evidence, and how can unused end-of-cycle model capacity be converted into a durable weekly development pass?

## Existing constraints

- Story claims retain the vault's `established`, `working`, `proposed`, `unresolved`, and `rejected` authority labels.
- A generated journal page cannot establish story canon, physical design, handwriting, or character knowledge.
- Samuel's revenge grows from exile into attempted replacement of Konrad.
- Konrad continues to regard Samuel as socially beneath him even after the containment hierarchy gives Samuel structural priority.
- Samuel's surveillance access is bounded. The exact channel, permissions, revealing record, and forensic chain remain unresolved.
- Weekly analysis may diagnose and recommend, but only the author may promote a story decision.

## Ideas considered

The journal system gives every major character a physical object profile, handwriting fingerprint, drawing and diagram grammar, recurring motifs, emotional evolution, reference set, entry index, and evidence metadata. Four contrasting initial languages were identified:

- Samuel: self-curated historical importance, analytic networks that become invasive replacement diagrams.
- Konrad: inherited ledger authority, declarations and lineage entries that assume reality already belongs to him.
- Sylvan: functional investigative notebooks that preserve competing hypotheses and circle questions rather than answers.
- George: issued structure that fractures as inherited identity and records conflict, with later disorder representing independent thought rather than moral decline.

A proposed Konrad page records contemptuous planning around Samuel's removal. A proposed Samuel page records the moment the exile story changes after Konrad enters the shared containment environment. These are artifact candidates, not transcripts of established documents.

The weekly system retains dated runs and produces ten core reports: story state, master outline, character arcs, adversarial continuity, ranked open questions, scene opportunities, foreshadowing/evidence, next-week queue, public-development ideas, and vault maintenance. Optional high-capacity modules simulate an editorial board, reconstruct causality forward and backward, and audit novelization readiness.

## Author decisions

- Build the journal artifact system in the vault and seed Samuel Franklin, Konrad Fitzgerald, Sylvan Elaria, and George.
- Store physical design, handwriting, drawing style, motifs, emotional evolution, and approved references as durable per-character records.
- Integrate journals with evidence, genealogy, surveillance, false-history, and behavioral-fingerprint work.
- Preserve the chat correction that Samuel learns the truth of his exile after Konrad falls into the same broader containment environment and the surveillance inversion begins. Exact access mechanics remain unresolved.
- Build the Weekly Story Synthesis as a dated, non-overwriting input to the Story Loop and Gauntlet.
- End each weekly run with `BUILD NOW`, `BRAINSTORM NEXT`, and `LEAVE OPEN` classifications.

## Current working direction

Samuel's journal evolves from analysis of Konrad into attempted authorship of Konrad's continuity. Konrad's journal remains disciplined and contemptuous; the contrast is strongest when Samuel begins copying small features of Konrad's written behavior. Sylvan's notes privilege unresolved evidence. George's handwriting becomes more individual as the identity imposed on him loses coherence.

The Weekly Story Synthesis feeds the existing development system through:

`vault delta -> synthesis -> ranked gaps -> Gap Analyzer -> brainstorm or exploration -> author decision -> Story Unit -> Gauntlet -> approved integration`

- Which handwriting similarities later become admissible evidence rather than visual coincidence?
- Who finds, authenticates, and dates each journal?
- What are Konrad's and George's approved visual identity references?
- Which journal materials and manufacturing methods fit each resolved era?
