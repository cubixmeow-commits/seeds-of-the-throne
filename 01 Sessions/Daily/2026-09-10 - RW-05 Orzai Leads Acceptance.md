# RW-05 Orzai Leads Acceptance

## Question explored

What independent decision by Orzai is necessary for separation, exposure, or placement acceptance to succeed?

## Existing constraints

- Orzai cannot function merely as Sylvan's supporter, verifier, or second signature.
- The legitimate process, not Sylvan personally, owns Samuel's terminal exposure and processing.
- Sylvan's evidence-anchored hyperfocus is necessary during Samuel's attack but needs a stopping point after the result is secured.
- Sylvan and Orzai must accept the outcome and move into their exploratory House placement together.

## Possibilities considered

The workshop considered independent authentication, a protective refusal, leadership in the placement choice, and required concurrence for separation. An initial recommendation proposed that Orzai limit a raw public evidence release. The author chose a more fundamental action: Orzai permits the process to expose Samuel and leads Sylvan into accepting the completed result and making their final decisions.

## Author decisions

**Established:** Orzai recognizes when the legitimate process has enough authority and evidence to complete Samuel's exposure. She allows the process to take over instead of continuing to center their lives on personally proving, pursuing, or narrating Samuel.

**Established:** Orzai accepts the result first and leads Sylvan forward into accepting it with her. She takes the leading role in their final decisions, including the transition into the exploratory period and the choices that will determine where they build their House.

Her indispensable action is not emotional reassurance. It is an independent leadership decision about when their adversarial responsibility is complete and constructive responsibility begins.

## Current working direction

Sylvan's evidence-anchored focus helps them survive Samuel, but it also creates a danger: Samuel can remain the organizing subject of Sylvan's attention even after Samuel has lost. Orzai identifies the legitimate stopping point. She does not suppress evidence or forgive Samuel; she entrusts the completed exposure to the process designed to carry it.

**Ending transition:** `process assumes exposure -> Orzai accepts the result -> Orzai leads the final decisions -> Sylvan moves forward with her -> exploratory placement begins`

Without Orzai's decision, the plot can defeat Samuel while leaving Sylvan psychologically and practically organized around him. With it, the constructive ending belongs to Orzai and Sylvan rather than to Samuel's punishment.

## Unresolved questions

- What final decision does Orzai make first in the scene?
- What does Sylvan still believe he must prove or complete before she intervenes?
- How does Orzai know the process is ready to assume exposure?
- What disagreement or hesitation makes Sylvan's acceptance emotionally earned?
- How does this decision connect to Orzai's earlier professional and leadership development?
