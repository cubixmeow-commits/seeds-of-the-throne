# Aiden the Gatekeeper — Seduction and the Letting In

## Author decision (2026-11-20)

**Aiden Fitzgerald did not go through the same process as Sylvan.** This supersedes the shared-ordeal half of the 2026-11-15 Process Mirror direction. Aiden's charge was narrower and plainer: he was posted to **shut Samuel Franklin and George White out**, and he let them in.

**Samuel Franklin controls George White.** They are not equals and not two of the same king. George was the face and the instrument: Samuel used George to manipulate Aiden, because George was the one Aiden could deal with, trust, and enjoy. Every dealing Aiden thought he was having with a man, he was having with Samuel's tool.

**The way in was seduction: drugs, perversions, and lies.** They did not force the gate. They made opening it feel good, pleasures with hooks in them. Once Aiden had taken what was offered, he belonged to the secret, and the secret belonged to Samuel. Compromise first, ownership after. The author chose this mechanism deliberately: a fall built from the dangers of ordinary real life, the way people are actually lost, rather than an abstract failure under an ordeal.

## What stands from the November 15 direction

- Aiden lied to Konrad that he had resisted and won them their own group, and held the lie for almost a century.
- Konrad restarted the breeding program on the strength of that lie, and Samuel took control through it.
- Aiden is exposed at the end sabotaging Sylvan to cover the breeding scandal and his own lies.
- Samuel then blames Aiden and George for everything and reaches for Samuel Jr.
- George was deceived. Aiden, after his letting in, deceived. The accountability contrast stands.

## What changes

- The mirror is now a contrast of outcomes, not of ordeals. Sylvan runs the process and refuses the bargain. Aiden held one gate and opened it.
- Aiden's break is re-dated in kind, not in time: it is a seduction and a compromise, worked through George, not a collapse inside a run of the process after the Great War. The Great War placement of his younger-leader role (September notes) is untouched by this decision.
- Samuel's control of Aiden runs through George and through the secret of what Aiden took. It is Samuel's standing covert method, indoctrination, compromise, and blackmail, applied to one gatekeeper.
