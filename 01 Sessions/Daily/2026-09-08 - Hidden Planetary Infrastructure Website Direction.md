# Hidden Planetary Infrastructure Website Direction

After the Project Explorer navigation repair was merged and deployed, the author reviewed three responsive homepage concepts and selected **Hidden Planetary Infrastructure** as the basis for the next website redesign.

The selected direction makes the story's literal premise visible: an authentic surface civilization exists above a concealed, planet-scale system used for cultivation, Luminai development, coordination, and containment. The design will borrow the fractured-record and recovered-evidence language from the second concept for conspiracy, timeline, archive, and evidence sections. It will preserve the first concept's readable editorial hierarchy.

This is a website design decision, not a new story fact. The generated planetary sections, city, moon connections, rooms, machinery, documents, and color placement are interpretive. They may guide composition and atmosphere but cannot establish literal architecture, mechanisms, institutions, geography, evidence, or events.

The approved implementation package is [[07 Coordination/2026-09-08 - Cursor Hidden Planetary Infrastructure Website Redesign]]. Its artwork and provenance are recorded in [[07 Coordination/Website Redesign/2026-09-08 - Hidden Planetary Infrastructure Asset Manifest]].

## Selected synthesis

- **Primary structure:** Hidden Planetary Infrastructure.
- **Secondary editorial language:** recovered history, contradictory records, provenance lines, and restrained redaction/fracture.
- **Legibility standard:** literal explanation and clear next actions remain visible on the first screen.
- **Surface-world rule:** ordinary civilization must appear real, inhabited, historically accumulated, and worth protecting.
- **Project Explorer role:** a calmer working instrument related to the story site, not a duplicate cinematic page.
- **Character rule:** use existing approved identity art. Generated mockup faces are not identity references.

Four new text-free candidate images were approved for website-design use: responsive planetary-cutaway heroes, a lived surface-civilization image, and a recovered-records evidence image. The selected mockup is retained as layout reference only.

No public copy, story canon, or unresolved story decision was changed.
