# Next Workshop Selection and Book One Buildability

## Question explored

Which parts of the story should the next workshop develop if the long-term goal is a finished story rather than continued accumulation of lore?

## Existing constraints

- The ten-module reassessment established the ending at macro depth and must not be reopened without new author direction.
- Book One ends near Sylvan's forty-second birthday with the imperial route closing, Samuel's separation and independent failure, processing and exposure, and Sylvan and Orzai accepting an exploratory placement.
- The exact meaning of “take Sylvan,” Samuel's lie to Konrad, the deadline's dramatic use, George's choice, Konrad's final move, and the middle sequence remain unresolved.
- The scene and manuscript directories contain process guidance but no drafted story sequence.
- The author expects many future workshops, so this pass should create a reusable progression toward scenes and prose rather than imply that ten questions finish the novel.

## Possibilities considered

1. Continue broad worldbuilding across many unrelated open questions.
2. Build the complete reverse-series chronology before returning to Book One.
3. Make Book One narratively buildable by resolving its objective, deception, clock, opening, middle, relationship arc, proof architecture, secondary-character choices, and sequence map.

## Current working direction

The third route has the highest leverage. The next workshop is the **Book One Architecture Workshop**, a dependency-ordered ten-module pass. It begins with the governing victory condition and ends with a usable sequence contract. It does not draft prose and it does not claim to complete the wider series.

This selection is a development recommendation made within the author's request to choose the next areas of work. Creative answers inside the modules remain open until the author accepts them.

## Unresolved questions

- How many sequences or chapters Book One ultimately requires.
- Whether the first opening prototype survives once the objective, lie, and countdown are established.
- Which later workshop should follow: scene packets, reverse-history architecture, or character-specific development.
