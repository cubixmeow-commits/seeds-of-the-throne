# Surface Archive Public Photography Direction

## Question explored

How should Wildlife Meme Adventures become part of *Seeds of the Throne* while preserving authentic wildlife photography, accessible humor, and the positive life of the colonization world?

## Existing constraints

- The source animals and environments must remain photographically real.
- Public copy cannot use em dashes.
- Generated overlays cannot establish new story canon.
- The surface civilization must feel inhabited and valuable rather than existing only as a criminal-containment mechanism.
- The older Wildlife Meme Adventures episodes and records must remain available as project history.

## Possibilities considered

1. **Seeds of the Throne: Photography Archive.** Clear but generic and primarily describes the medium.
2. **Seeds of the Throne: Field Records.** Strong procedural subcategory but too narrow for the entire collection.
3. **Seeds of the Throne: The Living Archive.** Positive and poetic but less directly connected to the surface-versus-hidden structure.
4. **Seeds of the Throne: Surface Archive.** Connects ordinary visible life to the story's hidden planetary architecture while supporting many photographic categories.

## Author decisions

**Established:** the new umbrella identity is **Seeds of the Throne: Surface Archive**.

**Established:** Surface Archive replaces Wildlife Meme Adventures as the active public direction. The existing folder and historical episodes remain in place for link stability and development history until a deliberate migration is performed.

**Established:** authentic wildlife and environmental photographs become in-world-inspired records of ordinary life on the colonization planet. They show that the constructed environment became a genuine living home filled with lives that continue for reasons of their own.

**Established:** the default emotional direction is positive, affectionate, observant, lightly funny, and life affirming. Criminal containment, prosecution, exposure, and evidentiary suspicion are not the default frame for ordinary wildlife posts. Those subjects may appear only when a particular story record genuinely requires them.

**Established:** each photograph must remain enjoyable without story knowledge. A restrained archive layer can add record numbers, observation categories, dry humor, and a short Luminai note that connects ordinary life to cooperation, growth, independence, adaptation, home, or shared reality.

**Established:** the visual system complements the current X banner through deep navy, parchment or cream, antique gold, fine system-blue cartography, and sparse coral or ember registration points. The photograph remains dominant. No wildlife is made futuristic, magical, or anatomically altered.

**Established:** the preferred X writing direction is a short positive paragraph about the story concept. Real capture provenance remains in the episode record and alt text but is not mandatory body copy in every public post.

## First direction-setting example

The sandpiper photograph establishes the initial Surface Archive balance:

- authentic color wildlife photography;
- a large field of ordinary shoreline life;
- restrained navy and gold story framing;
- fine blue orbital or cartographic lines;
- small coral registration points;
- a dry image caption;
- a hopeful public post about the colonization world becoming a living home.

Accepted public-post direction:

> In Seeds of the Throne, an ordinary shoreline is part of a much larger beginning.
>
> Every bird, tide and patch of life shows that the colonization world became more than an engineered environment. It became a living home, filled with small lives continuing for reasons entirely their own.

## Unresolved questions

- Whether the old folder should eventually be renamed after all internal links and tooling are migrated.
- Whether the first sandpiper candidate needs a typography-only refinement before final approval.
- Which Surface Archive categories should receive distinct but related layout systems after several photographs have been tested.
- Whether location and capture date appear publicly, in expandable metadata, or only in the private production record.
