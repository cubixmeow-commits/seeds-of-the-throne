# Sylvan Private Study Reference

## Question explored

How an author-supplied portrait should join Sylvan Elaria's existing visual identity system.

## Author decision

Integrate the portrait as a character reference for Sylvan Elaria.

## Current working direction

The portrait is an approved supporting reference rather than a replacement identity master. It strongly supports Sylvan's established apparent age, dark curly hair, short beard, analytical expression, black structured coat, circular pendant, warm natural lighting, and use in quieter lived-in environments.

The canonical front, strict profile, and clean three-quarter master remain the controlling references for facial geometry. The portrait's lamp, books, stone walls, chair, exact room, and exact tailoring do not become story canon through visual inclusion.
