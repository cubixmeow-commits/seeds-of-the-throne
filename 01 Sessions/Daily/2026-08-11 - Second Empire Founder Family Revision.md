# Second Empire Founder Family Revision

## Question explored

Correct the family structure of the four founders of the second-largest empire and establish the lineage leading to Sylvan's partner.

## Existing constraint

An earlier working session described four allied founders who each had a son and daughter, producing eight children. That version was never intended to remain the final genealogy.

## Author decision

The four founders are **two couples**.

- Founder Couple A has two children: one son and one daughter.
- Founder Couple B has two children: one son and one daughter.
- The son of Couple A forms a relationship with the daughter of Couple B.
- The daughter of Couple A forms a relationship with the son of Couple B.
- Each cross-couple has two children: one son and one daughter.
- The result is four founders, four children, two cross-couple relationships, and four grandchildren.
- Because each cross-couple joins one child from each founding couple, all four grandchildren descend from both founder couples.
- One of the two granddaughters becomes Sylvan Elaria's partner.

This decision supersedes the earlier eight-child working version.

## Current established structure

```text
Founder Couple A                         Founder Couple B
├── Son A ───────── partners ───────── Daughter B
│                           ├── Son
│                           └── Daughter
└── Daughter A ───── partners ───────── Son B
                            ├── Son

```

One of the two granddaughters is Sylvan's partner. Her exact parent pairing, name, personality, age, role, and relationship history with Sylvan remain unresolved.
