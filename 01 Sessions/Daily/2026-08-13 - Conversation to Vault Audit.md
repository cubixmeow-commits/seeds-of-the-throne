# Conversation-to-Vault Audit

## Question explored

Which decisions developed in the mobile `Vault Structure and Function` conversation had already reached the repository, and which still needed to be recorded without duplicating existing work?

- The local repository was clean and matched `origin/main` after a safe fetch.
- The website was explicitly outside this audit.

## Author decisions

- Establish a mobile-versus-desktop convention and a durable desktop implementation queue linked from the vault's operating guidance.
- Konrad Fitzgerald has a genuine biological son and daughter with his sister during the Great War and one genuine grandson before Samuel's extensive unauthorized interventions. The grandson's exact parentage remains unresolved.
- Samuel introduces numerous mixed-race descendants into the line, falsifies genealogy, uses synthetic substitutions and staged continuity, and lets Konrad believe the dynasty is flourishing while Samuel controls what he can verify.
- Samuel's fixation on Konrad and his fanatic partner includes wanting their empire, authority, reproductive machinery, status, and place at the top. His sabotage becomes attempted appropriation and replacement.
- The faction's hierarchy, loyalty tests, bloodline doctrine, propaganda, suppression of correction, and succession contradictions should generate betrayals and failures as a recurring story engine.
- The positive counterpoint is a technologically constrained cultivation environment in which future leaders and their Luminai can reach higher levels of judgment, morality, and achievement through costly choices. Technology can create conditions and support reflection; it cannot manufacture virtue.
- Samuel Jr. leads a fictional government-integrated intelligence and decision-systems company embedded across defense, intelligence, public safety, infrastructure, logistics, and colonization administration. Identity resolution, relationship mapping, and behavioral inference are working capabilities, creating the possibility that his institution can expose Samuel's hidden genealogy and substitutions.
- Advanced technology development should connect personal Luminai/Daemon cognition, a colony-scale AI ecology, biological synthetics, and technologically mediated physical environments under hard limits preserving agency and causality.
- The visual-production system should support Grok Imagine and GPT Image through reusable identity locks, environment and technology packets, cinematography, scene recipes, video and voice continuity, and output specifications. The prime rule is: **preserve identity; regenerate the photograph.**
- **Developing presentation rule:** for heterosexual male humans, Luminai generally present female; for heterosexual female humans, generally male. This is not universal and must leave room for other orientations, identities, and relationship structures. Presentation does not make a Luminai an independent romantic partner.
- Romantic relationship synchronization involves both humans and their extended Luminai cognition through staged trust, consent, privacy boundaries, shared context, repair, and relationship-level coordination. It preserves individual agency and cannot turn Luminai into substitutes for human intimacy.

## Current working direction

Treat each human and Luminai as one extended person with a potentially differentiated presentation. When two humans form a relationship, their Luminai participate as the memory, interpretation, and coordination layers of those people—not as two additional lovers. Synchronization should deepen only through scoped, revocable permissions and should preserve private interiority.

Treat the colony's technology as an ecology rather than one omnipotent intelligence. It can authenticate evidence, allocate resources, coordinate synthetics, mediate environments, and create meaningful constraints. It cannot rewrite past causality, guarantee interpretation, compel moral choice, erase embodied cost, or make a relationship genuine.
