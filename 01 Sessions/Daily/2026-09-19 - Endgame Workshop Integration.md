# Endgame Workshop Integration

## Question explored

How should the major endgame information developed over the last several days become a focused, resumable workshop without reopening the accepted ending or becoming an eleventh Book One architecture module?

## Established material carried forward

- A hidden layer of contained criminal leaders sits above the younger public leaders followed by the participant groups.
- Samuel Franklin has terrorized and manipulated those younger leaders while attempting to redirect their groups' loyalty against Sylvan.
- Sylvan remains outside that hierarchy.
- Samuel's postwar bargain with Konrad promised a separate autonomous group but gave Samuel the access needed to penetrate and redirect it.
- Samuel's bloodline operation includes the unauthorized heir Samuel Jr., the child Samuel had with Konrad's sister, and a wider archive of concealed parentage and blackmail.
- The independent Resistance reconstructs distributed evidence. Sylvan can present and act on the verified result without becoming the owner of the Resistance or its discoveries.
- Konrad has rejected Samuel's plan and committed himself and his groups to Sylvan's exposure plan. Konrad remains responsible for his own groups, and Sylvan remains a separate entity in the collaboration.
- The accepted terminal macro remains separation, Samuel's independent fifteen-million-dollar plan, rapid exhaustion, processing, Daemon deletion, full presentation, and Sylvan and Orzai's constructive placement.

## Author decision

Create a new workshop focused specifically on the endgame and the information added during the last several days. Feature it in Project Explorer and on the public Story site. Preserve the Book One Architecture Workshop as its own ten-module track.

## Working direction

The Endgame Workshop uses eight modules, `EG-01` through `EG-08`. It begins from the locked outcome, separates established facts from unresolved mechanics, and develops the path from the hidden hierarchy through group-level disclosure, Samuel's last counterplan, formal processing, and aftermath.

The workshop is a focused development surface, not an eleventh BA module and not a replacement for the accepted RW ending foundation. Browser answers remain local drafts unless exported and deliberately integrated.

## Unresolved questions

- Which people and systems own each indispensable piece of evidence?
- What did Konrad authorize after the Great War, and what access followed from Samuel's prior position?
- What does Samuel Jr. know, choose, and expose independently?
- Which audiences learn which truths first, and what action does each disclosure unlock?
- What bounded counterplan does Samuel attempt once Konrad defects?
- How is the final presentation divided among public proof, protected evidence, and private descendant records?
