# Orbital Synthetic Society Diagram and Macro Foundation

## Author's clarification

The orbital generator, hidden wireless relay satellites, and biological synthetics made from wrapped nanocells form the **macro foundation for synthetics used to create societies on a colonization planet**. The prior author decision establishes billions of powered synthetics at the system scale. Colonization involves real planetary civilizations with synthetics as participating inhabitants and practical contributors to their development, not simply an isolated synthetic-body experiment. The exact population on any one planet and the first steps of settlement remain open.

## Explanatory diagram

![[docs/assets/images/orbital-generator-synthetic-society-v1.webp]]

The author requested **lightning between the components to represent wireless energy**. The picture is an **audience-facing system cutaway**, not a scene showing what residents can observe or build. It depicts the established path: apparent moon / orbital solar generator → hidden satellite network → wireless energy on a colonization planet → biological synthetics composed of wrapped nanocells with concealed cores. Most people mistake the generator for a moon, the relays remain hidden, and no participant-accessible technology exposes the cores. The lightning is a **visual symbol of wireless energy**, not literal atmospheric discharges visible between orbiting components. The apparent map, moon cutaway, satellite forms, city, anatomical transparency, core shape, and receivers are visual interpretation. The glowing endpoints do **not** settle the final delivery mechanism, exact power budget, or whether an exposed person can identify a synthetic.

The colony may include synthetic and human residents, legitimate heirs, descendants, and ordinary social institutions. How synthetics reproduce or are manufactured, their legal standing, their economic and political roles, and the proportion of any society that is synthetic remain to be developed without denying their agency.
