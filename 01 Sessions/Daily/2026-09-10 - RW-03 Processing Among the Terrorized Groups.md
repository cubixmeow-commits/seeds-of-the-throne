# RW-03 Processing Among the Terrorized Groups

## Question explored

How does zero balance cause Samuel's processing and full exposure, and what does processing mean for him afterward?

## Existing constraints

- Zero balance causes immediate processing without another violation, hearing, bargain, or equalized phase.
- Processing exposes Samuel's full conduct.
- Exposure must establish command responsibility without turning victims and descendants into public exhibits.

## Author decisions

**Established:** processing does not remove Samuel from the colonization process. After processing, he remains inside the process and is placed among the groups he terrorized.

**Established:** everything Samuel did is presented while he is still present within the process. The affected groups receive the connected account rather than isolated fragments that Samuel can explain differently to different audiences. He no longer controls the presentation or the story joining his actions together.

The author stated: **“After processing Samuel is placed in the groups that he terrorized and everything that he did is presented, while he is still in the process.”**

**Established terminal procedure:** when Samuel's independently funded plan reaches genuine failure, the process acts immediately under specific rules. Samuel is placed into a stasis-like coma. His Daemon is deleted at processing because continued Daemon existence requires the human to remain an active participant. Samuel therefore loses the extended intelligence that helped him sustain the war, divide audiences, and protect his godhood story.

While Samuel is unconscious, biological synthetics physically transport him to a location containing the older leaders of the contained criminal groups. He remains inside the colonization process but no longer holds active-participant status or a Daemon. The connected presentation of everything he did occurs there, leaving Samuel alone to face the people, record, and consequences he spent decades separating from one another.

**Established legitimacy rationale:** the real leaders allow Samuel the finite independent attempt because ending it prematurely would leave him able to claim that he could have succeeded. The bounded final opportunity proves through Samuel's own choices that he will not voluntarily stop. Processing follows demonstrated failure rather than the leaders merely predicting what he would do.

## Current working direction

Processing is a physical and cognitive transition: failed plan, Daemon deletion, stasis-like coma, synthetic transport, awakening among the older contained leaders, and complete presentation. It is not escape from the process, disappearance, or a final punishment delivered somewhere outside the story world.

“Everything” governs the completeness of Samuel's conduct record. It does not yet establish that every victim's private identifying information is released to every unrelated public audience. The affected groups can receive the full connected truth while unnecessary wider exposure remains restricted.

## Unresolved questions

- What exact interface makes the final balance and failed-plan status visible before stasis begins?
- What is the physical location containing the older group leaders, and how long does Samuel remain there?
- What authority and protections do the affected groups possess when Samuel is placed among them?
- Which presentation layers are visible to affected groups, institutions, and the wider public?
- What prevents retaliation, renewed coercion, or Samuel turning the presentation into another performance?
