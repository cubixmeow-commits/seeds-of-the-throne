# George Prewar Success and Humiliating Bust

## Author decision

- Samuel's exile occurs approximately thirty years before the Great War begins.
- During that interval, Samuel and George are placed into a situation inside the largest government.
- George appears highly successful and his role appears unusually important.
- Samuel and George privately commit serious wrongdoing that remains to be developed.
- They participate in a horribly planned attempt to take over the largest government.
- The attempt collapses in an extremely humiliating exposure.
- George is busted into real containment for the private conduct and failed takeover.
- Samuel later uses George's authentic apparent success, important role, exposure, and containment as raw material for the False Victory.

## Continuity effect

This partially resolves George's prewar placement and processing chain without returning Great War ownership to Samuel. Konrad's faction still starts the later Great War. Samuel's own containment experience and his wartime reconciliation/sabotage remain intact.
