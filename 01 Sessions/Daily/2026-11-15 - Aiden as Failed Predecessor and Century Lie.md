# Aiden as Failed Predecessor and Century Lie

## Author's core idea (2026-11-15, brainstorm — proposed, not yet canon)

The colonization process is a very ancient, sacred process. Sylvan is going through it now, dealing with Samuel Franklin and George White, the most dangerous contained criminals. The process gives the contained criminals a very serious advantage; the participant has to struggle and survive for decades, basically forming a resistance to the contained criminals and surviving.

Almost a century earlier, Konrad Fitzgerald put his son Aiden into a similar process.

- Aiden broke down a few years into it, after the Great War, joined the contained criminals, and entered their group.
- Aiden lied to his father: he said he had resisted and won them their own group.
- Konrad restarted his breeding program based on Aiden's lies, and Samuel Franklin got control of it through that access.
- Aiden lied to his father for almost a century. He became everything Konrad is obsessively trying to destroy.
- Aiden is busted at the end trying to cover up the breeding scandal and his rampage of lies. He is exposed trying to sabotage Sylvan at the end, while Sylvan is exposing him.
- In the exposure, Sylvan / the record shows Samuel used that access to create an unauthorized heir with Aiden's own mother (Konrad's sister): Samuel Jr.
- Samuel Franklin blames Aiden and George White for everything, especially the breeding scandal, and tries to take control of the different groups using the unauthorized heir, Samuel Jr.

## What this fits in the existing vault

- **Samuel Jr.'s parentage is already established:** Samuel + Konrad's sister, via abuse of the breeding program. Konrad's sister is also Aiden's mother (Aiden is Konrad + his sister's son), so Samuel Jr. is Aiden's maternal half-brother. The new dynamic makes that fact personal to Aiden, not just to Konrad.
- **Samuel's scapegoating pattern is already established:** he prepares to blame Konrad for their shared project, scapegoats George operationally in the terminal attempt, and prepares Samuel Jr. as a constructed witness to transfer authorship of the bloodline campaign to Konrad's family. Blaming Aiden + George together extends that pattern to both sons.
- **Konrad's false autonomy is already established:** he believes he reactivated his group, breeding program and machinery in a separate, sovereign domain; in fact it sits inside Samuel's jurisdiction / shared environment. Aiden's lie ("I resisted and won us our own group") could be the *human origin story* of that false autonomy, underneath Samuel's story-functionality version.
- **The breeding program restart is already established as reactivation/migration** postwar, on Konrad's compulsion to win and Samuel's false promises. The new dynamic adds a new causal trigger: Aiden's false report.
- **Aiden and George are already paired marks:** same duplicated promise of singular godlike power, same failed chip experiment, same staged superiority over Sylvan, separate altered realities. The new dynamic breaks the symmetry in a useful way (see tensions).
- **The Resistance is already a working force,** separate from Sylvan, connecting evidence Samuel kept partitioned. Sylvan's process in the new dynamic — survive decades, form a resistance — aligns with that, and gives Aiden's fake "resistance / own group" a true counterpart.
- **The endgame reveal chain is already cascading:** weapon failure, duplicated promise, chip removal, breeding campaign, Samuel Jr.'s turn, Konrad's commitment to Sylvan's exposure plan. Aiden sabotaging Sylvan during the exposure, and being caught by it, fits as a late compensation move that exposes continuing command.

## Tensions / what this would change

1. **Aiden's agency flips.** Compiled Aiden is currently a manipulated mark: sincere believer in the holy order / prophecy, Samuel counterfeits the interpretation, chip injured him, his final response after exposure is explicitly *unresolved* and must not be inherited from Konrad. The new dynamic makes him an active, knowing collaborator for almost a century. That is a major character decision, not a detail.
2. **The chip story collides.** If Aiden joined knowingly, did he also know the chip was removed / the enhancement was fake? If yes, he is not Samuel's dupe on that layer, he is Samuel's partner in deceiving Konrad (and George?). If no, Samuel still duped his own collaborator on that layer, which keeps a piece of the old Aiden.
3. **Konrad gets more deceived, less originating.** Currently Konrad knowingly joins the chip lie and brings the breeding program back because he believes his Daemon can beat Samuel. If the restart rests on Aiden's lies, Konrad is still culpable (he built it, he wanted to believe), but the initiative shifts. Preserve: Konrad built the program and the doctrine; Aiden's lie gave him the excuse, not the desire.
4. **"Sacred process" is new language for the process itself.** The vault has a sacred layer (Konrad/Aiden's holy order, the existing prophecy Samuel counterfeits) and a governing process (trap / contain / understand / expose, evidence through actions, train leaders, build Luminai; process succeeds overall). Is the ordeal Sylvan and Aiden undergo the *same* rite the legitimate leaders run, a sacred office inside it, or the criminals' own name for it? Do not collapse the governing process and Konrad's religion into one thing without a decision.
5. **Timeline needs one pin.** Aiden is a Great War dictator figure, then postwar. "Broke a few years into it after the Great War" works cleanly if his process placement is postwar, running in parallel with the ~80-100 years of Samuel's containment coup, ending in Sylvan's endgame where Aiden is physically on Planet Three. It needs a decision on whether Sylvan's decades and Aiden's decades overlap or run in sequence.
6. **Samuel Jr. as heir-claim vs constructed witness.** Compiled Samuel Jr. is primarily a constructed witness / closing argument, not a conventional replacement heir, and his turn toward Sylvan is established direction. Samuel *trying* to use him as an heir to seize the groups fits, if Jr.'s refusal / turn is what defeats that attempt.

## Brainstorm development (proposed options, author's choice)

### The mirror that does the work

Aiden is Sylvan's failed predecessor: same ordeal, opposite outcome.

- Aiden was sent by his father. Sylvan chose / volunteered (established: Sylvan's participation is voluntary and he can leave). Aiden was an instrument from the start; Sylvan is a participant.
- Aiden claimed he formed a resistance and won autonomy. He formed neither; he joined the thing he was sent to resist. Sylvan, with the independent Resistance, actually does the slow work Aiden only reported.
- Aiden's "own group" lie becomes the template for everything Samuel later sells Konrad: false separation, false recovery, false victory. Samuel did not invent the lie that captured Konrad. Aiden handed it to him, or Samuel learned from Aiden's report what Konrad would believe.
- Both sons are offered / promised the singular throne. George believes the promise. Aiden, in this version, may have stopped believing it long ago and kept lying anyway, which is colder: George is the dupe, Aiden is the accomplice.

### Three versions of Aiden's break (pick / blend)

- **A. The collaborator:** he broke, joined, understood what he was doing, and chose survival + status for a century. Most damning, cleanest mirror to Sylvan, hardest break with current compiled Aiden.
- **B. The broken convert:** the process / chip injury / postwar altered reality broke him first; joining began as surrender, then self-deception, then active lying to avoid facing it. Keeps the vault's harm layer (injury does not excuse choices, but explains the doorway) and the story's rule that mental harm is not the same as moral evil.
- **C. The double-agent who stayed too long:** he joined intending to infiltrate / survive, sent Konrad true fragments early, and somewhere in the first decade the infiltration became membership. His final sabotage of Sylvan is him still telling himself he is protecting his father's group. Most tragic, most scene-rich, most continuity work.

### Endgame beat shape this enables

1. Sylvan's exposure is underway (weapon failure, duplicated promise, breeding pattern becoming common knowledge).
2. Aiden, not Samuel directly, moves to sabotage it: discredit a record, isolate a witness, protect the breeding archive. Because he is on Planet Three and Sylvan is not expecting the Fitzgerald son to be Samuel's inside man, it nearly works.
3. The sabotage uses the same concealed-access method as the postwar capture. Sylvan / the Resistance trace it, and the trace does not lead first to Samuel. It leads to Aiden. That is the shock: the resistance hero of Konrad's family story is the access point.
4. Aiden's century of reports to Konrad become evidence. Konrad can compare what Aiden told him, decade by decade, with the sealed record. Konrad's final commitment to Sylvan's exposure plan now has a personal trigger that is not only Samuel: his own son built the room Konrad has been living in.
5. Samuel immediately reframes: Aiden and George did it, the breeding scandal is the sons' rampage, Samuel was their instrument / victim. He produces Samuel Jr. as the "true" heir who can unite / take the groups. Jr.'s established turn is the refusal that breaks that move: the heir will not authenticate the story.
6. Aiden's exposure lands differently from George's awakening. George learns his life was a lie told to him. Aiden is confronted with a lie he told, and kept telling, until it became his life.

### What it gives Konrad

Konrad is obsessively trying to destroy the thing that secretly runs his order, supplies its interpretations, and corrupted its bloodline. In this dynamic, that thing is partly his own son. His final choice (commit his groups to Sylvan's exposure) costs him Aiden as well as Samuel, which may be the first irreversible action the workshop has been looking for.

## Open questions for the author

- Which Aiden: collaborator, broken convert, or double-agent who stayed (or a blend with a dated turning point)?
- Did Aiden know about the chip removal and the staged effects, or was Samuel still duping him on that layer?
- Is the "sacred process" the legitimate leaders' ordeal by design, and is Aiden's placement authorized by them, by Konrad alone, or is Konrad abusing the process by putting his son in?
- Do Aiden's decades and Sylvan's decades overlap, or is Aiden's ordeal finished (in failure) before Sylvan's begins?
- When Samuel blames Aiden + George, is any part of it true enough to be dangerous (Aiden's access really did enable the breeding capture), and how does Sylvan keep true blame on Aiden from becoming Samuel's acquittal?
- Does Samuel Jr. learn Aiden is his half-brother as part of his turn, and does Aiden try to use / threaten Jr. before Samuel does?
- Does Aiden get a final choice after exposure, like George and Konrad do, or is his refusal to choose the point?
