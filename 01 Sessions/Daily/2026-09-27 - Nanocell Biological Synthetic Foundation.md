# Nanocell Biological Synthetic Foundation

## Question explored

What is a synthetic at the macro level, before specifying tissue engineering, personhood, or Samuel and Konrad's attempt to occupy one?

## Author decisions — controlling foundation

- Nanotechnology constructs a biological wrapper around **each individual nanocell**. The cell-like building block is therefore nanotechnology enclosed in biology, not merely a biological cell fabricated by a nanomachine that then disappears.
- Many such wrapped nanocells together can form a **biological computer** and a larger synthetic organism. The exact topology of computation, bodily form, and relationship to the existing described biological processor remain to be developed.
- A moon-like orbital solar generator supplies wireless energy to this architecture. The existing integrated wireless grid and visible artificial moon provide the setting foundation; precise collection, transmission and local delivery are not chosen yet.
- **Later author clarification:** A network of hidden satellites relays the generator's wireless energy. The required macro path is orbital solar generator → hidden satellite network → distribution to synthetics. The satellites' positions and engineering, how their existence is hidden from participants, and the last step into each wrapped nanocell remain open.
- Participants on the colonization planet have **no technology capable of exposing the hidden nanotechnology** inside wrapped nanocells. This is a local detection limit, not a claim that synthetics can never be learned about. The author's preceding decision still stands: different people acquire information about synthetics through different channels and at different times, including after the Great War.

## Minimal operating model for discussion — interpretation, not additional canon

An individually wrapped nanocell is a powered core inside living-looking material. A population of these units could process signals and coordinate as tissue and as a computer. The orbital source supplies usable energy across the planet's infrastructure; a synthetic must receive that energy through some still-undecided pathway. Building a body still requires physical material. A participant might recognize or be told that a person is synthetic without having a microscope or scanner that can expose the core.

## Superseded assumptions in the prior proposal

The September 27 technical proposal in [[02 Story/Systems/Synthetic Embodiment and False Godhood Project]] described molecular tools building cell equivalents as though the tools could be separate from the finished cell. That is not the author-established architecture. Its proposed organs, vascularization, neural transfer ladder, and creator/creation motivations remain optional downstream ideas, not the definition of a synthetic. The biological-computer foundation must be established first.

## Open macro questions, held for the author's next idea

1. Is a nanocell one artificial core per biological wrapper, and does each core retain its own computation, or does only the network become computer-like?
2. Does the biological wrapper metabolize, grow, divide, and repair like a natural cell, or is it a maintained interface and camouflage around the core?
3. How do the hidden satellites pass energy onward to a synthetic or local infrastructure, and how does it couple into individual nanocells? What happens during shielding or failure?
4. Where do the materials for biological growth come from? Energy cannot supply the body's mass by itself.
5. Is the apparent brain one dense network of wrapped nanocells, a separate biological processor, or a coupled system? What distinguishes a synthetic's own mind from the colony's distributed intelligence?
6. What kinds of non-instrumental evidence can reveal that a person is synthetic without exposing the nanotechnology itself?
