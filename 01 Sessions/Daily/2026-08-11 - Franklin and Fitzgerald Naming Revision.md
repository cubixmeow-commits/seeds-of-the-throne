# Franklin and Fitzgerald Naming Revision

## Locked naming change

The permanent names of the two primary contained criminal leaders are now:

- **Samuel Franklin** — the failed privileged technology heir who captures family resources, builds a cult, manufactures royalty, and converts defeat into the False Victory.
- **Konrad Fitzgerald** — the genuine purity fanatic who creates the breeding program, supplies the Great War faction's ideological legitimacy, and enables Samuel's internal coup.

These surnames replace the previous development surnames everywhere in current vault memory and in the public build-in-progress page. Their established histories, motives, relationships, culpability, and story functions remain unchanged.
