# RW-09 Primary Leaders Vindicated

## Question explored

What must the legitimate leaders admit and change when Samuel is exposed?

## Existing constraints

- The real leaders deliberately permit Samuel a finite independent attempt so his own choices demonstrate whether he will stop.
- The process uses strict rules, meaningful alternatives, time, and a complete behavioral record.
- Villainy is grounded in conduct, doctrine, ambition, and choice rather than identity or mental illness.
- The containment planet is intended to produce civilizational evidence, not merely punish Samuel.

## Possibilities considered

RW-09 originally assumed some combination of institutional failure, knowledge limits, fragmented authority, negligent delay, remedy, and future safeguard. The author rejected that premise for the primary leaders.

## Author decisions

**Established:** the primary leaders were absolutely correct in their design and judgment. Samuel's exposure does not produce a finding that their containment process failed. The completed colonization planet validates their approach.

**Established:** the larger humanity has sustained major debates about behavior, evil, responsibility, mental illness, distinctive interests, and related causes or explanations. The colonization planet finally produces enough long-duration, rule-bound, real-world evidence to put those debates to rest.

The primary leaders do not need to confess an institutional error to make the ending legitimate. Their achievement is allowing participants and contained criminals enough real agency, opportunity, alternatives, resources, and time for behavior to answer questions that abstract argument could not settle.

The exact propositions and conclusions remain open. The story will not generalize Samuel's conduct into a claim that mental illness is evil, nor treat harmless identity or consensual interest as wrongdoing. The relevant distinctions must ultimately be expressed through invented larger-humanity debates and observable conduct.

## Current working direction

RW-09 is not an accountability reversal against the primary leaders. It is a civilizational validation:

`long moral dispute -> primary leaders build rule-bound living test -> participants exercise real agency over time -> complete behavioral record -> terminal comparison -> debates settled`

Other institutions, schools, or leaders may still have made mistakes, but this answer does not establish any. The primary design and its terminal handling of Samuel are vindicated.

## Unresolved questions

- What exact competing positions existed in the larger humanity?
- What did the primary leaders believe before constructing the planet?
- Which terminal evidence resolves each debate?
- What does “distinctive interests” mean in the invented civilization, and which interests are harmless versus connected to coercive conduct?
- How are the conclusions communicated without stigmatizing mental illness or converting behavioral evidence into a permanent caste system?
- What policies change across the larger humanity once the debates are settled?
