# Wildlife Meme Adventures Workflow

## Author direction

Make the Wildlife Meme Adventures workflow discoverable inside the active vault so future sessions do not mistake one retained example for a fixed template.

## Rules established

- Preserve the supplied wildlife photograph as the factual source and visual center.
- Keep the finished image close to the source photograph's orientation, aspect ratio, and practical dimensions. Landscape photographs remain landscape; portrait photographs remain portrait unless the author requests a different crop.
- Build the decorative palette dynamically from the supplied photograph. Do not carry a fixed palette from a prior post into every episode.
- Preserve the small **Wildlife Meme Adventures** and **Local Wildlife Photography** umbrella identity while giving each photograph an original episode title, joke, and visual direction.
- Use the established X caption layout exactly: `Wildlife Meme Adventures 🧭`; a blank line; the quoted episode joke; `📍` plus the verified location; `📅` plus the full capture-date/time label; a blank line; and one `Behind the meme:` paragraph that describes the real photograph before connecting it to the joke.
- Do not substitute a conventional hashtag caption, promotional opening, closing call to action, or different emoji system unless the author requests it.
- Make the **Wildlife Meme Adventures** series name clearly readable and recognizable, but scale it to the photograph. It must not dominate the animal or make the full text system feel oversized.
- Default to warm, friendly, wildlife-centered humor. Do not use military, tactical, enforcement, combat, territorial, patrol, perimeter, security, threat, or conflict jokes unless the author explicitly requests that direction.
- Do not invent a species identification. Use a broader factual subject label when the exact species is not verified.
- Inspect recent finished posts in `~/Documents/x memes/` for the current flexible visual language, but do not repeat their jokes, themes, layouts, or palettes automatically.
