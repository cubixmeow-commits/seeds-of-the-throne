# Corporate Origins of Synthetic Ascension Fraud

## Later author direction — private origin and visible reckoning

The colonization process **begins as the initiative of a few corporations that resist government control**. Their research, investment and ambition grow into an extensive colonization and synthetic-technology enterprise. Some investors and company figures become extraordinarily wealthy. A subset uses this wealth and access for a supremacy project and draws large groups into a cult-like promise that humans can become synthetics and wield godlike power. The real leaders have a responsibility to prevent those actors from converting private wealth into coercive control over people and the colonization infrastructure. The specific firms, leaders, constitutional order, chronology and individual offenses remain undecided; successful entrepreneurship, investment or resistance to a government's preferred terms alone are not grounds for containment.

The leaders let the offenders attempt their claim **in a controlled colonization setting** partly because many followers demand to know whether it can be done. A conspicuous, independently verifiable failure could break the appeal of the movement and deter repetition on a wider scale. This is the leaders' hoped-for result, not permission to fabricate evidence, engineer a failure, harm test subjects, or claim that one experiment disproves every possible future discovery. The target of the demonstration is the sellers' extraordinary *promised ascension to synthetic godhood*. Regeneration and other legitimate medical uses of advanced technology remain separate possibilities and should not be discredited by its failure.

This refinement makes the earlier **mixed builder-investor network** the lead direction rather than one of three equal possibilities. The exact roles of Samuel and Konrad, the first legal response, the offer or sentence terms, the participants' safety protections, and how the result is communicated to believers are still open. The real-world analogy motivating the author is translated here into invented corporate, institutional and ideological dynamics; no real person, state or firm is a story identity.

## Question explored

What if some contained criminals came from the companies that built or financed the colonization process, then used their wealth and insider position to promise an impossible form of human synthetic enhancement? Could the real leaders use bounded colonization to bring that conflict to a lasting resolution?

## Existing constraints

- Synthetic invention precedes a legal response to attempted appropriation; the offenders are lured **into** multi-planet colonization, while expansion, synthetic societies, heir development and Luminai cultivation remain legitimate purposes.
- Samuel and Konrad's shared ambition is to put their biological brains into synthetic bodies. Their racist lineage theory is false. Their *promised* godhood and supposed enhancements are not verified achievements.
- Criminals have bounded local roles, never ownership of the underlying process. Synthetic persons and other residents need protection and agency; investors do not acquire rights over them.
- The relative dates of the invention, company formation, original colony construction, laws, conviction, Planet Two and Planet Three remain open.

## Possibilities considered

1. **Founding contractors:** a small technical leadership circle actually helped construct orbital, synthetic or civic systems; its knowledge gives the fraud credibility but creates a severe insider-access risk. The leaders must demonstrate that privileged access was severed and independently audited.
2. **Capital syndicate:** the organizers financed development but did not design the substrate. Their contracts, publicity and political connections create a persuasive public claim of entitlement. This simplifies the security question but gives them weaker direct technical knowledge.
3. **Mixed builder-investor network (now the lead working structure):** some individuals built or maintained legitimate technology, others financed it, and another layer sold the impossible enhancement promise. Membership and culpability differ. Their shared organization converts a real contribution into a false claim that investment or invention confers ownership of synthetics and the right to remake humans. Samuel and Konrad may later lead or capture this network; their exact founding status is unchosen.

## Proposed causal history, not fixed chronology

1. **Later author clarification supersedes the earlier breakthrough-first wording:** a few private corporations initiate the colonization project while resisting government control. The timing of their first construction relative to biological synthetics and orbital support remains open; investment and legitimate company contributions continue as the technology matures. Their agreements and reserved rights remain unchosen.
2. A faction markets a supposed path from human embodiment to synthetic power. Investors and recruits receive promises of transformation and privileged rule that the faction cannot demonstrate. **"Impossible" can describe the grandiose product as sold without establishing that every imaginable human/synthetic interface is impossible.** Decide what the founders know about the gap.
3. The faction uses its money, professional prestige and possibly retained technical access to escalate from marketing to coercive experiments, attempted appropriation of synthetic persons or infrastructure, and falsified progress. These acts—not investment, dissent or unsuccessful research alone—create a criminal case.
4. Laws and independent oversight respond to documented conduct. Contracts and permissions are separated from sovereign process authority; dangerous insider access is revoked, replaced or independently verified. How this can be achieved without giving offenders a route back in is an unresolved engineering and governance gate.
5. The real leaders make an offer or enforce a lawful sentence that brings specific offenders **into a bounded colonization environment**. They believe it will let them prove their synthetic-integration claims; what is truthfully offered, what is withheld, and whether participants are convicted, consenting or deceived remain separate questions.
6. A controlled record of claims, attempts, harms and failures might allow final adjudication, restitution and protection against renewed control. The author's later direction adds an intended conspicuous, independent public failure of the godhood promise in response to large followings who want an answer. This is a possible meaning of the leaders' desire to put the *specific fraud and captured infrastructure* to rest permanently—not a guarantee that all ideology or criminal ambition can be eliminated.

## Unresolved questions

1. Which specific company roles—engineers, funders, managers, marketers—belong to the first contained leaders, and which, if any, to Samuel and Konrad?
2. Did the colony project exist before synthetic invention, or did the invention make the first society-building colony possible? What exactly predates the laws?
3. What was publicly promised and charged for, and what did the sellers privately know was unproven or impossible as advertised?
4. Which actual crime supports each person's containment, and which entity can review the sentence and protect bystanders and synthetic persons?
5. How is contractor access independently revoked if some of the offenders helped design the infrastructure and might hide dependencies or backdoors?
6. What would count as a final, credible adjudication rather than a forced demonstration conducted at the expense of ordinary residents?
7. What exactly do the corporations resist: ownership by one state, regulation, compulsory public access, limits on synthetic personhood, or some combination? When and how do the real leaders obtain lawful oversight?
8. Which public evidence makes the failure spectacular *and trustworthy* to people outside the project, including followers who distrust the leaders? Who observes and can contest the result?
9. Which types of regeneration or medical care are genuinely available, and where is the boundary between them and the promise of total synthetic embodiment and godlike status?
