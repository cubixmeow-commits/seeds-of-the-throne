# Free Flowing Two-Planet First Stage and Future Rules

## Author direction

The process is “a giant free flowing narrative that moves an entire process forward.” Its **first stage is two planets**, building around problems and setting rules based on what people do for all future citizens of both planets.

This changes the earlier working picture of a first stage ending on Planet Two and a second stage beginning on Planet Three. Both colonization planets participate in the first, continuing stage of a larger process. This does not undo the real Planet Two Great War and criminal defeat, Planet Three's replay through placed people, or the overall success of the colonization program. It does not establish how many later stages or worlds exist.

The primary purpose remains to trap, contain, understand and expose humanity's major problems, develop the new Luminai and train leaders. These activities accumulate into the governance of living societies: future citizens on both planets inherit rules shaped by what earlier people actually did. Their own actions can create further problems and revisions. The narrative need not be a prewritten sequence of experiments or a closed two-world trial.

## Working interpretation, subject to author selection

An event can reveal a problem, but the jump from conduct to a binding rule needs an accountable process. A possible cycle is: a person or institution acts; independent and affected witnesses preserve evidence; a problem is identified and disputed; legitimate authorities decide a proportionate rule and its planetary scope; the AI ecology applies the authorized rule; new citizens encounter, challenge and revise it through subsequent history. This is a **proposal**, not established legislation or automatic AI rulemaking. The source of authority, representation, appeal, privacy, remedy, local variation and prospective versus retrospective effect are open.

Planet Two's war can become a consequential source of evidence and precedent without being staged by overseers. Planet Three's replay can put inherited claims and rules under pressure through new choices while its own society develops. Neither planet is merely a laboratory: synthetics, ordinary settlers, heirs, descendants and affected people have lives and claims apart from the original offenders. Rules for future citizens cannot be punishment inherited through bloodline.

The audience may first see separate wars, rival families or a seemingly repeated history, then learn how the worlds' choices and institutions inform one shared process. Each reveal should change what an earlier event means and show whose evidence, interests and decision made a rule possible. This is a narrative design direction, not a fixed chapter order.
