# Workshop Module 04 — Reconstruction Draft

## Gate

What does reconstruction reproduce faithfully, and what is allowed or required to change?

## Author answer — verbatim

> Use Earth as an example for this. It takes major technological changes, creates a time of great war, and bases, you know, characters, roles in the public story on real people from the larger humanity, but can customize these events specifically for the effectiveness of the colonization process. So a lot of this stuff is based on information from the larger humanity, but customized.

Authority normalization: Earth remains a development analogy. The final story uses the invented larger humanity and does not import real Earth people, nations, or events into compiled canon.

## Exact restatement — author-accepted

The reconstruction creates a customized version of history drawn from the larger humanity. It faithfully uses major technological transitions, a Great War period, and public characters or roles based on people who actually existed in that civilization. The colonization process may alter, combine, sequence, or adapt those events and roles when doing so makes training, containment, Luminai development, or planetary sustainability more effective.

The reconstruction preserves historical information and recognizable pressures without requiring a literal replay. It creates the conditions and public roles through which history can be explored, while the people inside retain real agency and can cause the reconstructed history to diverge.

## Workshop interpretation

- **Selected direction:** historical source material with purposeful customization.
- **Faithful elements:** major technological changes, a Great War era, recognizable social pressures, and public roles grounded in people from the larger humanity.
- **Customizable elements:** identities, combinations of people, sequence, timing, circumstances, institutions, and event design when adaptation improves the colonization process.
- **Agency boundary:** the environment supplies conditions and roles; participants still make consequential choices rather than merely reciting a predetermined history.
- **Final-story boundary:** Earth is the explanatory example, not the named source inside the fictional world.

## Scene test — non-canon

A participant discovers that a famous public role combines evidence from several people in the larger humanity. The role's inherited biography points toward one expected decision, but the current participant refuses it. The environment preserves the refusal and its consequences instead of forcing history back onto the original track. The discovery proves that the world uses history as structured material rather than a script.

## Adversarial result

The leaders preserve past deprivation or danger only when it creates a necessary condition for training, containment, historical understanding, or system validation. Customization allows the environment to remove irrelevant cruelty and add hidden safeguards. It cannot make every harm legitimate merely by calling it historically useful. Exact stopping rules remain unresolved.

## Remaining uncertainty

- Which technological transitions and Great War conditions are reproduced most faithfully?
- Are public roles based on one person, composites, recurring positions, or all three?
- What may be changed for effectiveness, and who authorizes that change?
- Which historical harms are prohibited from reenactment?
- How much do participants know about the historical source of their roles?
- What happens when open participant choices move history away from the planned training environment?
- Which inhabitants originate outside the participant classes, and what rights do they have over continued reconstruction?

## Compound Writing adaptation test

### Tracks pass

The phrase “you know” is conversational filler rather than a story decision and does not need to survive the restatement. The pass finds no outline labels, critic instructions, or other process residue. Earth remains explicitly labeled as a development analogy so the explanation does not leak a forbidden real-world entity into final story canon.

### Pressure-turn placement

The decisive idea arrives near the end of the author's answer: the reconstruction is based on real information but customized for the colonization process. The restatement moves that rule forward, then identifies what remains faithful and what may change.

This improves the result because technological change, the Great War, and public roles are examples of one governing mechanism rather than three disconnected features.
