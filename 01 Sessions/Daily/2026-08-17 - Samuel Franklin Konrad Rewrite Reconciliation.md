# Samuel Franklin / Konrad Fitzgerald Rewrite Reconciliation

## Source decision

This desktop pass reconciles the author's August 17 Samuel/Konrad rewrite with the current local repository after fast-forwarding the recent GitHub updates.

## Integrated direction

- Samuel is the failed privileged son of wealthy senior members of Konrad Fitzgerald's fascist purity and bloodline organization.
- His parents and the organization exile him after serious misconduct, including a sexual relationship with an underage girl. Additional abuses remain deliberately undefined.
- Samuel separately falls into containment inside the future victorious American-equivalent empire.
- Konrad remains the sincere ideological fanatic, breeding-program architect, and original leader of the world-conquest project and Great War.
- Samuel maintains contact with his parents during the campaign, pretends to seek reconciliation, and covertly sabotages the faction from inside the future victor.
- Konrad's faction still loses primarily through its own authoritarian failures; Samuel's betrayal materially worsens the defeat.
- Konrad, his senior partner, Samuel's parents, and the surviving leadership then fall into the same containment environment Samuel already understands.
- Samuel turns greater familiarity into indispensability, interprets defeat, builds the False Victory around George, and captures the faction's breeding and genealogy systems.
- His revenge targets what the movement worships: bloodline, pedigree, purity, succession, inheritance, descendants, and replacement heirs.
- The goal evolves from revenge for exile into attempted replacement of Konrad.
- Archive Thriller / Dark Historical Reconstruction is the active project development style. Jurassic Park-style exposition is retired as current. Supporting registers remain cold historical reconstruction, political grotesque/leadership dialogue, close psychological prose, and the sparing turn.
