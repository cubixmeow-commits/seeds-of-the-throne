# Orzai and Great War Witness Integration

## Question explored

Do the inherited Witness and Inheritor roles still add value when integrated with the current Sylvan, George, and founder-family architecture?

## Existing constraints

- The Witness is an earlier-era heir who notices recurrence across identities and carries dangerous historical knowledge.
- One granddaughter descended from both founder couples of the second-largest empire becomes Sylvan's partner.
- Sylvan's partner must have an independent identity, ambitions, and personal reason for choosing him before the relationship carries civilizational significance.
- The relation among the Witness, Inheritor, Protagonist, and Sylvan remains structurally unresolved.

## Possibilities considered

- Preserve the Witness and Inheritor as two new protagonists.
- Merge the Witness with George and the Inheritor with Sylvan.
- Integrate the Witness with Sylvan's eventual partner and test Sylvan as the modern Inheritor.

## Author decisions

- Sylvan's intended partner is named **Orzai**.
- Orzai belongs to Sylvan's generation and is slightly younger than him.
- Orzai is **not** the Witness.
- **Sylvan Elaria carries the modern Inheritor role.**

## Current working direction

Orzai is a legitimate heir from the second-largest empire's founder family and one of the two granddaughters descended from both founder couples. She is Sylvan's contemporary and slightly younger than him. Her exact age remains unresolved; with Sylvan's current approximate age of forty, she likely belongs somewhere in the later thirties unless the chronology changes.

The earlier Witness role belongs in the Great War generation. Orzai's parents are one of the two cross-couples formed by the founders' children, and both can be placed into constrained Great War roles for development and training. The leading working possibility is that one parent carries the singular Witness role while the other becomes the first crucial believer, doubter, corroborator, or person placed at risk by the Witness's knowledge. Which parent carries which function remains unresolved.

This creates a generational chain: a parent witnesses recurrence and preserves dangerous knowledge; Orzai inherits a personal relationship to that history; Sylvan carries the modern Inheritor role and can authenticate and act on the evidence. Whether the Witness appears directly as a viewpoint character or survives primarily through records, testimony, and consequences remains unresolved.

Orzai's exact parent branch, age, worldview, capabilities, ambitions, relationship history, and role in carrying her family's evidence remain unresolved.

## Unresolved questions

- Which cross-couple are Orzai's parents?
- Which of Orzai's parents carries the Witness role?
- What constrained Great War roles are assigned to each parent?
- What does the Witness first mistake recurrence for, and what forces revision?
- Does the other parent believe the Witness, doubt them, or become the person whose danger makes disclosure costly?
- What survives from the Witness into Orzai's generation?
- Why does she choose Sylvan personally, apart from his usefulness to the investigation?
- Is Sylvan the sole primary narrator, or does the Great War Witness own substantial earlier viewpoint material?
