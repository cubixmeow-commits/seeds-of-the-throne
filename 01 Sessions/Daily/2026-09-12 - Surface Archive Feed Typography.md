# Surface Archive Feed Typography

## Author correction

The secondary text in the first Mountain Transit candidate was too small. Surface Archive record lines, observations, and Luminai notes must be readable in the ordinary X phone feed without opening or zooming the image. Decorative microtext does not receive an exception.

## Established production rule

- Treat the title, record line, observation, and optional Luminai note as the only normal text tiers.
- Make the record line, observation, and Luminai note substantially larger than the first Mountain Transit candidate. As a starting proportion, the smallest informational capitals should be at least one third of the height of the `SURFACE ARCHIVE` capitals in the same composition.
- Use adequate weight, contrast, spacing, and a restrained navy support field when the photograph is visually busy.
- Remove coordinates, catalogue labels, compass letters, and other decorative microtext when they cannot pass the feed-preview test.
- Inspect the complete image at a longest edge of 768 pixels. Any essential copy that requires zooming fails review and must be enlarged, shortened, moved onto stronger contrast, or removed.

## Candidate-specific direction

The revised Mountain Transit candidate uses the road photograph for a story-inspired observation about a defeated leader and the remaining contained criminals being offered a final choice to leave town before legal processing begins. It must not imply that they had already departed or escaped accountability. This image remains a candidate and does not establish a specific offer, departure, or processing event as story canon.
