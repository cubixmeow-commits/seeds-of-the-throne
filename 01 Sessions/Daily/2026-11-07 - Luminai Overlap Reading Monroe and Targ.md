# Luminai Overlap Reading: Monroe and Targ

Exploration note, not canon. Follows the 2026-11-04 author direction. The author and Brut are reading Third Eye Spies and Journeys Out of the Body; overlaps below are candidate development material and content source material, to be tested, not adopted.

## Overlaps identified

1. SRI protocol (viewer describes, blind judge decides, target hidden) structurally matches the Luminai design brief: perception in the human, provenance and contradiction detection in the Luminai, sealed record as final judge. A Daemon reads as a monitor who stops being blind and supplies interpretation first (George's cue, explanation, confirmation loop).
2. The Learned Wireless Cognitive Channel's pre-awakening phenomenology list (intuition, knowing where something is, sensing wrongness, environment anticipating intention) resembles viewer and OBE reports with the mechanism swapped for infrastructure. Open question it sharpens: how would a participant distinguish a genuine capacity from a supplied one? Provenance training is the in-development answer.
3. Monroe's Locale I (checkable physical) vs Locale II (thought-shaped) resonates with the story's two layers: face-value life vs wireless presentation to the mind. Resonance only, not a mapping.
4. Attention as steering (Monroe) and analytic overlay as failure (Targ) both rhyme with the channel's provenance discipline: raw impression first, interpretation after, or the signal is corrupted.
5. Price's beacon maxim (hiding draws attention) as a pressure on the containment premise: planet-scale concealment as planet-scale signal. Question, not mechanism.

## Author direction added 2026-11-07 (villain exposure arc)

The story's villains begin secretive and covert. The first group then becomes the loudest participant; the Great War exposes that group's true intentions (author analogy: the pattern of a covert movement that goes overt and is exposed by war, as with the Nazis in World War Two; analogy of pattern only, kept in session notes, not compiled story). ## Author direction added 2026-11-07 (Samuel's obsession)

Samuel Franklin's primary obsession is complete control of every participant's mind and actions. Not influence: control. His covert methods (supplied interpretation, manufactured confirmation, distributed intention through proxies and systems) are how that obsession operates while remaining deniable. This is why exposure by an uneditable record is his terminal threat: a participant who can compare records and recover provenance of their own thoughts is a participant leaving control. Status: author direction, session level.

## Author direction added 2026-11-07 (what the leaders are farming)

What the real leaders are farming is evidence through actions: data. Participants must not know they are in a carefully controlled environment, or their behavior stops being evidence. The environment takes contested questions that societies cannot settle by argument (the author worked through one current, sharply polarized social debate as the private example; per vault rules the example stays out of this note and only the mechanism is recorded) and gives opposed camps real opportunities to enact their beliefs in practice. Outcomes are produced by actions, not declarations, recorded by advanced AI, and weighed to determine which model of reality actually holds. Farming targets: enacted belief, lived consequence, adjudicable record. This is also the experiment-integrity stake: a controller who quietly rigs opportunities or interpretations (Samuel's method) is not just tyrannical, he is contaminating the data the whole environment exists to produce, which is why the sealed record and provenance are existential to the project. Open question preserved: who sets the criteria the AI weighs outcomes against?

## Author direction added 2026-11-07 (simulation belief and what breaks it)

The contained criminals and participants born into the colonization process mostly believed there would be no lasting consequences; when they awakened, they interpreted the hidden structure as a simulation. What breaks that belief, for everyone, is Samuel Franklin's hijacking and weaponization of Konrad Fitzgerald's fascist breeding program: real, irreversible harm inside real families proves the stakes are real. That event is the catalyst for the larger realization: the universe has built-in functionality; much religious and institutional material is based on real events; and the colonization process is not a simulation but the most recent layer of a much more advanced humanity. On that foundation, remote viewing and astral projection are a real functional part of their reality, not metaphors.
