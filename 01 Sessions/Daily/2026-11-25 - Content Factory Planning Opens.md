# 2026-11-25 - Content Factory planning opens

Noa directed a full stop on the old way of making Seeds Page content: no more basic search, pick a fact, format it. Posting on the Page is paused and the work goes into research and development mode.

His framing: build a content generation factory, planned properly and in layers before anything else is built. Ideas come in from many sources, get researched, get turned into angles, compete, and only the survivors become content, every day, unique and interesting. The factory will live in the vault at `10 Facebook Page/Content Factory/` and Muse will run it daily once it is built and proven.

First artifact: `10 Facebook Page/Content Factory/Factory Blueprint - Working Draft 01 - 2026-11-25.md` (status: working, planning only). It lays out nine source engines, the layer stack from capture to learning loop, the registry that enforces uniqueness, Noa's gates, five open decisions for him, a build order ending in a one-week dry run with posting still paused, and pre-mortem seeds.

What already exists and feeds the design as raw material, not as the design: the Content Selection System v1 doc, hook library, scoring log, and the first peer harvest (all in the Seeds engine workspace, 2026-11-25). The blueprint absorbs the rubric and gate as its Layer 4 and the harvest as source engine S1.

Open at the end of the day: Noa's answers to the five decisions in the blueprint, and Draft 02 developed with his reactions.
