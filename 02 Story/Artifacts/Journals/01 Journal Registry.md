## Status meanings

- `proposed`: design candidate only.
- `working`: accepted for development and testing, not a locked visual artifact.
- `approved`: author-approved reusable visual authority.
- `locked`: replacement requires an explicit supersession record.

Character identity approvals live in `skills/create-seeds-images/references/visual-registry.json`. Journal approvals do not alter that registry unless separately promoted.
