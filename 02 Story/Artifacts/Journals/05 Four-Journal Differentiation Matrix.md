# Four-Journal Differentiation Matrix

## At-a-glance identity

| Dimension | Samuel Franklin | Konrad Fitzgerald | Sylvan Elaria | Orzai |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Object logic | portable modular portfolio that can reorder reality | fixed monumental ledger that declares permanent order | replaceable field volumes that preserve inquiry | layered mixed-media journal that separates observation from representation |
| Size | 165 × 235 mm | 260 × 360 mm | 140 × 205 mm | 155 × 215 mm |
| Primary material | polished black-green calfskin | rigid oxblood goatskin and dark metal | charcoal weather-resistant woven composite | slate-blue linen and pale vegetable-tanned leather |
| Paper | cream laid packets and thin inserts | thick warm formal rag leaves | dot-grid, blank, and provenance slips | mixed-media leaves and registered translucent overlays |
| Instrument palette | near-black ink, graphite, restrained red | broad and narrow sepia-black nibs | blue-black ink, graphite, muted amber tabs | graphite, indigo, terracotta, occasional white |
| Hand | narrow, driven, compressed semi-cursive | broad, upright, monumental formal hand | open, legible print-cursive hybrid | small, upright, architectural hand |
| Drawing instinct | model a person's private vulnerabilities | classify a person into inherited order | preserve questions, sources, alternatives, and permission | compare accurate structure, human meaning, and intervention thresholds |
| Signature integration | sentence becomes mechanism | declaration becomes structure | conversation remains visible inside map | transparent overlay separates fact from constructed meaning |
| Stress response | margins invade; branches converge on him | order hardens; headings deny shrinking facts | density rises but provenance survives | writing compresses; withheld layers accumulate |
| Core forensic risk | deliberate imitation and packet substitution | dictation/clerk ambiguity and copied formality | later reconstruction mistaken for contemporaneous note | overlay separation, version order, and protected-source custody |

## Silhouette test

The closed objects must remain recognizable in grayscale:

- Samuel: flexible medium leather portfolio, recessed blank frame, modular spine.
- Konrad: largest and heaviest object, rigid corners, clasp, raised spine.
- Sylvan: smallest field volume family, rounded corners, exposed stitch, elastic.
- Orzai: compact rectangular linen field, pale leather vertical spine/edge, overlay pocket.

## Page-without-color test

- Samuel is identified by cross-page invasion, narrow prose, pressure maps, and branches that return to the writer.
- Konrad is identified by centered headings, vertical hierarchy, formal ruling, and subordinate numeric evidence.
- Sylvan is identified by open question rings, provenance brackets, retained alternatives, and review gates.
- Orzai is identified by registered layers, disciplined negative space, paired interpretations, and the model-to-action triangle.

## Color-without-text test

- Samuel: black-green / cream / graphite / restrained red.
- Konrad: oxblood / rag ivory / blackened metal / sepia.
- Sylvan: charcoal / warm white / blue-black / muted amber.
- Orzai: slate blue / pale leather / indigo / terracotta.

## Anti-convergence rules

- Samuel may imitate some of Konrad's capitals, but never inherits Konrad's genuine downward doctrinal logic; Samuel's structures remain relational and audience-specific.
- Konrad may contain accurate observations, but he places them inside rank and doctrine instead of exploring individualized vulnerabilities.
- Sylvan may use visual models, but his diagrams prioritize sources, alternatives, permissions, and conversation rather than Orzai's representational layers.
- Orzai may share evidence with Sylvan, but her base page, overlay, confidence, and action threshold remain independently authored.
- No character receives generic frantic scribbles as shorthand for evil, pressure, genius, introversion, or instability.

## Approval boundary

This matrix makes the four working designs internally distinct. It does not approve any object, handwriting, signature, diagram, image, entry, or story event. Approval remains component-by-component through the registries and character profiles.
