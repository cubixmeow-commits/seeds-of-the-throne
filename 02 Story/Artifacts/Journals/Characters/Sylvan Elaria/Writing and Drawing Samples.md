# Sylvan Elaria — Writing and Drawing Samples

## Use and authority

This library defines a reusable working style. The lines are exercises, not canonical entries or discoveries. Evidence language demonstrates method without establishing a culprit, date, source, or technology capability.

## Hand construction

- Open print-cursive hybrid with a slight 4–7° right slant, medium x-height, round counters, and generous word spacing.
- Moderate pressure and a stable baseline. Fast notes join common letter pairs, but names, dates, source codes, and negations are printed separately.
- Capitals are plain and open. `S` is rounded, `E` has a shortened middle bar, and `Q` is an open circle with a small outward tick.
- Lowercase `t` has a centered medium crossbar; `i` dot is round and directly above; `g` is single-storey with an open tail.
- Numeral `1` is unadorned; `4` is open; `7` is uncrossed; zeros in evidence codes receive a diagonal slash.
- Questions are frequent. Colons introduce tests or sources. Square brackets contain provenance and permissions. Confidence uses words, never unexplained percentages.
- Corrections remain readable: a light single strike, revised language, then a small reason such as `source conflict` or `too certain`.

## Emotional-state bands

### Social builder

Short observations alternate with names, paraphrases, and open arrows. The page has several entry points and often ends with a question for another person.

Sample: `Mara sees a training problem. I see a retrieval problem. Same failure, or two?`

`Mara` is a non-canon placeholder used only to demonstrate layout and must not appear in generated visual text.

### Disorientation

Lines shorten; facts are boxed separately from explanations. Repeated questions protect uncertainty from an imposed narrative.

Sample: `Known / claimed / felt / independently checked.`

### Adaptive investigator

Cross-references increase. Alternatives remain visible after one becomes strongest. Dates, permissions, and confidence labels become more prominent.

Sample: `Best fit: B. Keep C until the timing test.`

### Accountable action

The conclusion becomes concise because the supporting chain is indexed elsewhere. Writing grows larger at the action threshold, not more dramatic.

Sample: `Enough to warn. Not enough to accuse publicly.`

## Drawing sample library

1. **Open question ring:** an incomplete circle around a question, with space deliberately left for later evidence.
2. **Competing-path fork:** two to four hypotheses share the same starting observation and retain separate falsification tests.
3. **Provenance bracket:** a square bracket groups source, permission, date, and custody.
4. **Evidence chain:** each link records observation, corroboration, and remaining uncertainty.
5. **Behavior comparison:** predicted versus observed actions across multiple environments.
6. **Permission boundary:** a dotted or double line shows what may be shared, with whom, and for what purpose.
7. **Timeline braid:** independent source histories run in parallel before any justified intersection.
8. **System boundary map:** actors, tools, institutions, and unknown interfaces sit in separate regions.
9. **Stakeholder orbit:** people are positioned by consequence and responsibility, not importance to Sylvan.
10. **Contradiction box:** two incompatible claims are preserved side by side with tests below.
11. **Conversation path:** speaker contributions appear as differently shaped nodes without ranking one person as the sole source.
12. **Decision gate:** evidence threshold, risk of delay, reversibility, consent, and next review date.

## Unique writing–drawing combinations

### Question ring with an open seam

A prose observation ends in a question mark that expands into an incomplete circle. Alternative answers grow from the gap. No line closes until evidence warrants it.

### Provenance travels with the arrow

Every evidentiary arrow carries a small bracket containing source, date, permission, and confidence. A connection without provenance is dotted and cannot enter the final chain.

### Conversation changes the map

Paraphrased comments sit directly beside the diagram element they challenged. Old branches remain lightly visible, making intellectual change traceable and social rather than magically intuitive.

### Action threshold

Dense investigation resolves into one clean line at the bottom: act, wait, warn privately, or disclose. A next-review date prevents uncertainty from becoming indefinite delay.

## Forensic fingerprint cluster

Use at least five features: open-tail `g`; slashed zeros only in codes; plain open `4`; source brackets; light recoverable corrections; question rings with gaps; confidence written as words; dates printed rather than joined; arrows labeled with provenance; alternatives preserved after selection; next-review dates; permission boundaries attached to information.
