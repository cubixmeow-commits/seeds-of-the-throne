# George — Journal Profile

## Authority and scope

George's imposed false reality, apparent prewar success, humiliating bust, later use as Samuel's visible authority, and corrupted Daemon relationship are working story memory. Whether he keeps a journal, who issues or monitors it, his preferred name on the object, and the exact awakening path remain unresolved.

Physical design, handwriting, and diagrams: `proposed`. Approved identity or journal references: none. Return `NEEDS DEFINITION` if generation requires George's appearance, institutional seal, signature, or issuer.

## Physical identity

- **Proposed early object:** clean, issued, structured, possibly monitored or designed as part of instruction.
- Printed or supplied headings may encode the identity and terminology George is expected to repeat.
- Middle pages accumulate insertions, inconsistent records, scratched-out language, and drawings that do not fit the supplied narrative.
- Late use may abandon, reverse, or repurpose the imposed structure. Exact object family and monitoring mechanism remain open.

## Handwriting identity

Initially neat and imitative, with copied terminology, cautious corrections, and deference to supplied headings. Individual forms emerge gradually: freer spacing, self-chosen labels, confident corrections, and marks not traceable to the official model.

## Drawing and diagram grammar

Early copied diagrams and approved categories; middle contradictions, repeated places, faces or shapes he cannot source, mismatched timelines, and boundary tests; later maps organized around firsthand memory rather than imposed interpretation.

## Recurring motifs

Printed line versus handwritten deviation, missing name, doubled memory, sealed section, repeated unknown place, redacted heading, boundary crossed from inside, copied symbol becoming personal mark.
