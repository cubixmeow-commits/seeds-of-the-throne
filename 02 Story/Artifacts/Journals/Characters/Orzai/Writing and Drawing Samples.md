# Orzai — Writing and Drawing Samples

## Use and authority

This library tests a distinct interdisciplinary visual language for Orzai. It does not establish her exact job, project, signature, chronology, or evidence custody. The exercises must remain separate from canonical entry records.

## Hand construction

- Small upright architect's hand with almost no slant, low x-height, open counters, and deliberate air between phrases.
- Light-to-moderate graphite or fine-nib pressure. Important conclusions are not made darker; they gain space around them.
- Capitals are simple geometric forms. `O` is slightly oval, `R` has a short bowl and precise leg, and `A` has a low crossbar.
- Lowercase `t` has a short centered bar; `i` dot is a tiny square; `g` is double-storey; `r` is a minimal shoulder.
- Numeral `1` has a base but no flag; `4` is open; `7` is crossed lightly; `0` is unslashed. Decimal points are exact and units are never omitted in health or modeling notes.
- Punctuation is restrained. A centered dot separates parallel observations. Small triangles mark thresholds; paired vertical strokes mark consent or disclosure boundaries.
- Corrections use one fine strike and a dated replacement in the margin. Visual claims may be tagged `accurate`, `simplified`, `persuasive`, or `unsupported`.

## Emotional-state bands

### Spacious observation

One concise statement occupies a line beside a small diagram. Wide margins hold unresolved qualifications. Nothing touches the page edge.

Sample: `Observed: three pauses before agreement. Meaning: unresolved.`

### Selective trust

Paired columns hold her conclusion and another person's authorized perspective. The columns touch only through a dated consented cross-reference.

Sample: `My confidence: moderate · Shared confidence: low.`

### Evidence pressure

Writing becomes smaller and layers accumulate on vellum. The main paper stays ordered while overlays reveal how much she is withholding pending corroboration.

Sample: `Threshold met for private warning. Public disclosure still incomplete.`

### Public leadership

The final action line is short, isolated, and written slightly larger. Supporting detail is indexed behind it.

Sample: `Revise the system now. Publish the reason with the revision.`

## Unique writing–drawing combinations

### The vellum test

The base page records observation and a clean structural model. A translucent overlay adds consequence, uncertainty, consent boundaries, or an alternate audience interpretation. Removing the overlay reveals exactly which meaning was constructed rather than observed.

### Three-truth labeling

Every important visual can receive three small labels: factual accuracy, ease of comprehension, and persuasive force. These are never treated as the same measure.

### Negative space as unresolved duty

Blank margin beside a conclusion is reserved for the person not yet consulted or the measurement not yet obtained. Later additions enter that space with their own date instead of being folded invisibly into the first conclusion.

### Model-to-action seam

A drawing ends at a small triangle threshold. One sentence on the far side states the justified intervention. The text does not dominate the drawing; it records when observation becomes responsibility.
