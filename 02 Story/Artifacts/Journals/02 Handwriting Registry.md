## Required approval sheet

Each approved sheet must include lowercase and uppercase alphabets, numerals, signature behavior, dates, common names/phrases, punctuation, arrows, underlines, cross-outs, diagram samples, and at least three emotional states. Approval applies only to the named hand/version.

## Forensic caution

A shared generated style is not in-world proof of common authorship. A handwriting clue becomes story evidence only when an entry records the compared features, provenance, plausible access, expert/technical method, alternatives, and author-approved conclusion.
