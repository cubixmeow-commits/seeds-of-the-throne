# Other Power Structures

These labels are inherited working categories and require fresh development before preservation as groups.

- **High Command:** possible coordinating body or contested authority above major contained factions.
- **Sponsors:** people or houses who place heirs into the process and define starting conditions or intended destinies.
- **Secret orders:** cross-generational institutions that preserve doctrine, sponsorship, and obligation through ritual and degrees. The form does not indicate moral alignment.

Do not treat these labels as final names or their existence as fully established.
