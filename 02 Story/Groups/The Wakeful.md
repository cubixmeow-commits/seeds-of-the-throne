# The Wakeful

An ideological movement among the contained population. Its members believe they are the only people awake inside a manufactured world. Participants appear asleep, synthetics appear manufactured, and institutions appear staged; from those nearly accurate observations they conclude that everyone else is scenery and the world belongs to those who recognize it.

The movement has converts, rival claimants, schisms, and a political objective: control the colonization process and hold the planet. The Throne is one faction within it—the faction that achieved territorial dominance and captured the doctrine around one man's divinity.

The belief system must be invented from the setting. Real-world movements may inform private analysis of mechanisms but never supply final names, identities, vocabulary, or cast.
