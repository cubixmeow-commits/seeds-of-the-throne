# The Throne

Both the kingdom and its central figure currently use a placeholder label.

The central figure is a contained criminal, not an architect of the colonization system. Because he arrived early, he understands procedures and identities later entrants cannot interpret. He claims he was deliberately placed as legitimate authority, converting the length of his sentence into institutional seniority.

Groups seeking certification hand him genealogies and records. He alters, catalogs, and returns them, creating leverage with the victims' cooperation. His ideology is an instrument; he needs others to believe it strongly enough that his certification matters.

His century of promises is maturing. He cannot deliver the settlement or standing he claimed to guarantee. Godhood is an escalation beyond audit: a response to promises coming due, not proof of secure power.
