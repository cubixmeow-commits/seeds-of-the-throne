# The Second-Largest Empire: Founder Family

## Established family structure

The second-largest empire has four founders organized as **two established couples**, not four separate founder lines.

Each founding couple has two children, one son and one daughter. The four children form two relationships across the founding couples:

- the son of Founder Couple A partners with the daughter of Founder Couple B;
- the daughter of Founder Couple A partners with the son of Founder Couple B.

Each cross-couple has two children, one son and one daughter. The resulting family contains:

- four founders;
- four children of the founders;
- two cross-couple relationships;
- four grandchildren, two sons and two daughters.

Every grandchild descends from both founder couples. One of the two granddaughters becomes Sylvan Elaria's partner.

```text
Founder Couple A                         Founder Couple B
├── Son A ───────── partners ───────── Daughter B
│                           ├── Son
│                           └── Daughter
└── Daughter A ───── partners ───────── Son B
                            ├── Son
                            └── Daughter
```

## Story function

The structure turns cooperation between the founders into a lived family history across three generations. The founders build one empire together. Their children bind the two households through chosen relationships. Their grandchildren inherit both sides of the founding family rather than representing isolated dynastic branches.

Sylvan's partner therefore belongs to both founder couples by descent. Her relationship with Sylvan can carry long-term significance for cooperation between the largest and second-largest empires, but that significance must not replace her independent identity or personal choice.
