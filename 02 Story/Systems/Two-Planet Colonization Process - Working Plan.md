# Two-Planet Colonization Process — Working Plan

## Established frame

Planet Two and Planet Three are the **first and second colonization planets** after the origin world. Planet Two's criminals have comparatively more room; they lose a real Great War to the real leaders' empire. Planet Three begins a distinct, more tightly contained process that **replays history through placed people and roles** about a century before Sylvan. Its society and political decisions are real. Its dominant empire is a Planet Three institution/role, not the original Planet Two sovereign state physically moved. George and Aiden act physically on Planet Three during Sylvan's endgame; older Samuel and Konrad are placed on Planet Two in prior endgame decisions, with individual travel and access channels to be specified. Sources: [[07 QA/Decisions]], [[01 Sessions/Daily/2026-09-28 - Planet Three Replay Clarification]].

**Later author correction:** The **overall two-planet colonization process succeeds**. The criminals' Great War defeat is an outcome inside that successful process, not evidence that Planet Two's process failed. Planet Three continues the larger program at a more contained stage; do not describe it as an emergency repair. The precise success criteria and the relation between the two stages remain development questions. [[01 Sessions/Daily/2026-09-28 - Overall Colonization Process Success Clarification]]

**Later author direction — primary purpose:** This controlled environment is designed to **trap, contain, understand and expose humanity's biggest problems**, while **building the new Luminai and training leaders**. The Samuel–Konrad conspiracy, fascist domination and synthetic-ascension fraud are cases inside this broader purpose. “Trap” does not erase accepted entry, review, exit and resident protections; the exact mechanism remains to be designed. [[01 Sessions/Daily/2026-09-28 - Controlled Environment Primary Purpose]]

**Established commitment rule:** Participants who continue in the process cannot abandon, skip or rewrite their assigned environment. Voluntary heirs may leave the whole process and forgo its unfinished self-made result. Convicted offenders require separate release/review rules; Konrad chooses continuation after the war rather than losing his exceptional claim through lawful exit. Entry and rights of locally born descendants are unresolved. See [[02 Story/Systems/Participant Governance and Command Rules]] and workshop modules [[07 Coordination/Story Completion Workflow/Workshop/02 - Participant knowledge and entry|02]], [[07 Coordination/Story Completion Workflow/Workshop/07 - Grid permissions and failure|07]] and [[07 Coordination/Story Completion Workflow/Workshop/09 - Great War defeat and refusal to exit|09]].

## Working design, not author-approved mechanism

| Dimension | Planet Two | Planet Three |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Process role | Successful first colonization stage with comparatively looser criminal containment; large-scale problems emerge as real decisions and institutions, including the actual war and defeat of the criminal project. | Successful continuation in a new society using Planet Two history as a source for placements; tighter bounded roles allow exposure, leadership training and Sylvan–Orzai's successor Luminai proof. |
| Power | Criminal groups gain enough real opportunity to recruit, govern locally and attempt domination; the outer process and evidence system remain beyond them. | Criminal children and legitimate heirs can occupy consequential and rotating offices; criminals still cannot own process authority, participant identity or sealed records. |
| Continuity | Actual acts, victims, victory and defeat stay in the historical record; Konrad's postwar refusal has consequences. | Historical correspondences may shape roles and public beliefs, but individual acts create new consequences. Original Planet Two sovereignty does not transfer by default. |
| Endpoint | Great War defeat ends the first less-contained criminal bid **within the successful larger program**; exact sentencing, exit, postwar reactivation and continuing Planet Two operations need dating. | Roughly a century before Sylvan; the accepted Book One and terminal process provide outcome and exposure, with exact individual presentation geometry open. |

**Proposed outer framework:** One governing authority may maintain process rules, independently auditable records and protected cross-world channels while each planet has distinct assignments and local institutions. Its charter and enforcement are **unresolved**. The existing accepted causal claim that Konrad reactivates his Daemon and group inside Samuel's earlier priority sphere remains; its planetary location and reach need a separate decision.

**Proposed replay boundary:** Reproduce roles, institutional pressures and selected verifiable history, then allow Planet Three residents to change the trajectory. Whether archives, biographies, exact incidents or a whole civilizational arc are also reconstructed is open. The broader older source of reconstructed parent-civilization history must be reconciled with Planet Two as the immediate replay source. A replay is a curated starting structure, not proof of a second Great War or an unreal world.

## Proposed AI-managed architecture — not author-established

The program can succeed as **one governed colonization effort with two different planetary assignments**. Human governing leaders set lawful objectives and restrictions. The AI ecology implements permitted placements, infrastructure, communication, evidence preservation and stage transitions. No single AI needs sovereign discretion to redefine success or cause a war. The process records what participants actually do; it does not manufacture their moral achievement. See [[02 Story/Systems/Advanced Technology Ecology]].

| Layer | Proposed responsibility | Boundary needed for the story |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human charter and review | Define the colonization purposes, participation categories, protected rights, allowable risks and final outcome procedure. | The leaders can be scrutinized for what they allowed; they cannot call any harm a success merely because a verdict was recorded. |
| Planetary AI ecology | Maintain habitats, energy, synthetics, ordinary institutions and local permissions on each real planet. | Environmental capability does not grant a criminal authority over the process or make residents props. |
| Placement and replay administration | Use authenticated Planet Two history to place particular people into Planet Three's newly constituted roles and starting conditions. | The source war stays on Planet Two; Planet Three's outcomes can diverge as people act. Exact archives, offices and individual authorizations remain open. |
| Sealed evidence and cross-world interface | Preserve source events, role assignments, later changes and communications across worlds for independent challenge. | Samuel can influence what people believe and some local systems but cannot overwrite the sealed chain or terminal rules. Its actual custodian and channels remain open. |

**Working success measures under the established purpose, not a selected scorecard:** (1) both planets sustain real societies; (2) dangerous patterns are surfaced and bounded without ceding the governing process; (3) actual choices and harm remain attributable and independently challengeable; (4) protected disclosure reaches affected people and legitimate reviewers; (5) future leaders and successor Luminai gain demonstrated competence at recognizing and responding to adaptive problems; and (6) ordinary and synthetic residents retain rights. The author has established **overall success**, not these exact measures or the absence of wrongs.

## Proposed trap–contain–understand–expose cycle

This is the operational design question for the next workshop pass. “Problems” can include attempts to dominate, exploit technology, recruit through false promises, capture institutions, suppress evidence or instrumentalize families. The author has not selected an exhaustive list; harmful behavior by governing institutions may also need scrutiny.

| Function | Proposed use of the controlled environment | Non-negotiable design question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trap | Offer a consequential but bounded opportunity to pursue a participant's own project, so covert plans become chosen acts that can be observed. | Who enters voluntarily, who is sentenced, what offer is truthful, and who is protected from inducement? |
| Contain | Separate local power from process authority; limit resources, access, physical reach and ability to coerce or suppress records. | What is the intervention threshold before residents suffer avoidable harm? |
| Understand | Preserve independent records of action, knowledge, permission, beneficiaries and attempted concealment across both worlds. | What can an AI actually infer, and how can people challenge a mistaken attribution? |
| Expose | Compare public stories, sealed records and affected people's testimony at appropriate stages. | What is public, what remains protected, and who may demand review? |
| Train and build | Let legitimate heirs and new Luminai learn to identify, interrupt and resolve those patterns through accountable choices. | How is competence demonstrated without scripting an heir's morality or using residents as expendable tests? |

The AI ecology can set conditions, preserve provenance, enforce permissions and warn or intervene; human authority must define rights and review. Neither level can manufacture a participant's moral achievement. The exact sources of authority, enforcement and remedies remain **working**, not canon.

## Causal bridge to the accepted endgame — proposed mechanism

1. Planet Two provides true history: criminal ambition, shared Samuel–Konrad arrangements, the war, defeat, Konrad's lawful alternative and his actual decision to continue. None of these facts requires the process itself to fail.
2. Konrad's reactivation inside Samuel's earlier bounded priority sphere grants Samuel information and influence over the older organization. **Open:** where this permission sphere physically resides and who authorizes the postwar continuation.
3. Planet Three opens with selected replay placements. George and Aiden eventually occupy real, consequential positions there, with apparent opposing family legacies. Their exact times and assignments are not yet set.
4. **Working cross-world channel:** the governing process permits specific, logged communication or delegation between an older leader's Planet Two role and a Planet Three participant or institution. Samuel exploits a limited channel and the younger men's beliefs rather than directly commanding Planet Three's governing AI. The existence and exact permissions of this channel require author selection.
5. Samuel uses local story functionality, intermediaries and partial records to maintain separate George, Aiden and Konrad narratives while concealing the failed enhancement attempt and his independent abuses. The public replay lends his apparent anti-Konrad position plausibility, without deciding George's or Aiden's conduct for them.
6. Sylvan, Orzai and the independent Resistance bring source history, placement logs, present acts and protected testimony into comparison. Samuel's live attempt to repeat his old access bargain exposes the method. His accepted terminal failure and presentation conclude his participation without converting Planet Two's earlier war into a failed program.

The cross-world channel is the design bottleneck. If it gives Samuel unrestricted authority, the endgame violates containment. If it gives him no access at all, the sustained attack on Sylvan has no causal route. A bounded, observable and contestable channel is a **proposal** that could bridge those needs; its rules, delays, safeguards and victims' remedies are open.

## Development sequence

1. **Define the protected population and classes:** distinguish offenders, recruited followers, heirs, ordinary settlers, synthetic people and locally born children; specify entry, knowledge, environment commitment, exit and review separately.
2. **Define Planet Two conditions:** original colonization purpose, corporate/government relationship, synthetic society, criminal opportunity and limits before the actual Great War.
3. **Date the war and postwar choice:** record true victor, culpability, lawful alternatives, Konrad's voluntary continuation and Samuel's separate jurisdiction. Identify which stated goals of the overall process the Planet Two stage achieved without confusing criminal defeat with process failure or assuming the war was engineered.
4. **Specify Planet Three's replay:** select the source history, who places people, which offices/institutions are new, what is concealed, how the planet can diverge and which people physically move.
5. **Build the overlapping century:** map cross-world access, George/Aiden's roles, older leaders' locations, legitimate heirs, synthetics, rotating offices, story functionality and independent evidence custody.
6. **Connect the endgame:** preserve Book One's reverse discovery, the Resistance's independent proof, Samuel's attempted blame transfer, Konrad's disclosure and the accepted terminal sequence.

This is an order for **development decisions**, not necessarily the series' narrative order. See the fuller assessment and candidate replay scopes in [[01 Sessions/Daily/2026-09-28 - Two-Planet Colonization Architecture Assessment]].
