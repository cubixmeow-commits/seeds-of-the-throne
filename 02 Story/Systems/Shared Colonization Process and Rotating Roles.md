# Shared Colonization Process and Rotating Roles

## Established — one world, many simultaneous plans

Planet Three, the **second colonization planet**, is a developing civilization, not a backdrop reserved for one contained group or one heir. Contained criminals, their children, legitimate leaders' children, synthetics, and many other residents participate in overlapping histories. Institutions, war, government, work, family, and religion have real consequences as the whole planet advances. The real leaders manage the underlying process and its limits while allowing contained criminals bounded room to attempt their own plans and expose their methods through chosen action. No contained leader owns the process.

The criminal leaders seek domination and use children and roles to extend it. The legitimate leaders cultivate heirs who can learn cooperation, judgment, responsibility, and the right to build lives distinct from their parents' plans. Both kinds of children may hold the same kinds of offices in successive eras; they are not interchangeable as people or reducible to their parents. The planet's development, the containment outcome, and Sylvan and Orzai's successor human–Luminai breakthrough advance together. The breakthrough is an earned result within this larger process, never the only purpose of everyone on the planet.

## Established — public roles rotate

An office such as president can be held by one contained leader's child, then another, then an heir of a legitimate leader. Such succession is part of the historical continuity of the planet. A public title is a role with bounded, consequential local authority, not a permanent identity, a guarantee of the holder's parentage, or an indication of who governs the hidden process. Institutions continue across administrations; people compete, learn, and make consequential choices in each role.

**Unresolved mechanics:** how roles are requested, approved, earned, inherited, elected, or reassigned; the durations and limits of particular offices; whether the same participant returns under a later identity; and how ordinary residents exercise political agency. Rotating roles cannot make every election or public institution a stage trick.

## Established — the war's public lie

The Great War establishes the misleading image of two separate contained empires on Planet Three. George White, Samuel Franklin's son, becomes the celebrated leader of the dominant, supposedly heroic empire. Aiden Fitzgerald, Konrad's son, and the sons of other main contained leaders begin in leadership roles in the authoritarian empire that loses. The dominant empire appears to defeat the criminal supremacy project. The underlying criminal leaders have organized smaller, partly cooperating groups behind both public sides; Samuel seeks to direct and capture these groups. The supposed anti-domination hero story gives Samuel and George public distance from the breeding program publicly associated with the defeated empire.

These empires are real public political and institutional arrangements with real harms and consequences. They are also **roles and narratives within containment**, not the full map of who is allied with whom. A visible president, a national government, a contained faction, and the process authority are four different things. George's prominence never means he truly rules the planet or the parent civilization. Aiden's placement does not make him the sole author of the defeated empire's actions.

The criminals' con makes them appear to be fighting the program they are trying to advance. Earlier recruitment invokes supposed mental enhancement, implants, technology, breeding, and future godlike rule. Falsified progress presentations support that claim. Their claim of superiority is ideology and deception, not a truth about people or a consequence of lineage. Samuel and Konrad may have partnered in that recruitment project earlier than the legacy Great War account assumes; the precise partnership, betrayals, and transfer of control remain to be settled.

## Established — why the real leaders allow this

The governing leaders permit criminals to pursue bounded projects so that behavior, claims, power seeking, and attempted concealment become observable and attributable. The wars and role assignments do not grant unbounded permission to harm, automatic legitimacy to a criminal government, or the governing machinery of the colony. Containment exposes what a dominion project does when its architects believe they can win. The constructive side develops future leaders and the successor Luminai through lived choices, cooperation, and tested bonds in the same shared world.

**Open implementation:** the authorization chain for empire assignments; the real leaders' prior knowledge of the specific con; safeguards and intervention thresholds; protection of ordinary inhabitants; the division of authority among real leaders; and how public claims are eventually audited without exposing private descendants or attributing ancestral guilt.

## Established — relationship to the endgame

George and Aiden have separate manipulated interpretations and were separately promised the same exclusive godlike destination. Their failed chip trials were concealed. Public failures against Sylvan, independent evidence, and comparison of accounts can expose the gap between their projected roles and their actual capabilities. Konrad's visible family association with the fallen empire's breeding project, coupled with Samuel's apparently heroic public position, makes blame transfer credible. Konrad's knowledge of the shared recruitment con does not by itself give him knowledge of Samuel's later unauthorized breeding sabotage or of every staged effect. Sylvan's genuine successor bond and the independent Resistance's evidence help make the whole concealed topology legible; neither alone possesses every fact.

The endgame must still distinguish the earlier public Great War story, decades of rotating offices, the individual chip fraud, Samuel's later capture of other groups, and the formal endpoint. The accepted separation, independent $15 million attempt, processing, and later comprehensive older-leader confrontation are not changed by this process-level clarification.

## Working reveal architecture — earlier accounts become deeper accounts

The older war and False Victory material remains a useful source of **what inhabitants and readers can plausibly believe at different times**. The reveal should not say the war, political offices, defeat, exile, or individual decisions were imaginary. It changes their interpretation and reveals who coordinated the roles behind them.

| Reader's current account | Later evidence changes its meaning | Evidence and agency still to design |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two empires fought a recognizable war and one lost. | The opposing public identities did not exhaust the contained criminal network; smaller groups and parents' cross-camp plans were concealed. | Role proposals, private agreements, independently held group records, and witnesses on both sides. |
| George rose as the victor's great hero while Aiden led the defeated empire. | George's placement was useful to Samuel's con; the losing leaders' sons carried conspicuous association with the breeding program. | Who proposed each placement, what the governing leaders approved, and what George and Aiden each actually chose. |
| An exile and later postwar bargain show Samuel opposed Konrad's organization. | A personal rupture and sabotage could be real while earlier shared recruitment and later coordinated deception also occurred. Their exact dates and knowledge boundaries need rebuilding. | Earlier agreements separated from later betrayals; proof must show both complicity and individual authorship. |
| Rotating presidents or other public leaders prove continuity or takeover by one camp. | The offices are durable institutions occupied by different people, including legitimate heirs; office succession is not a reliable faction map. | Succession records, contested decisions, and independent actions by each holder. |
| George and Aiden possess extraordinary implant-enabled abilities. | Their chip trials failed; staged effects, genuine older systems, and a successful successor Luminai must be distinguished. | Physical records plus live tests and the younger men's own responses. |

**Proposed disclosure rhythm:** reveal a local contradiction, then the hidden placement plan, then individual parental knowledge and Samuel's changing command over the smaller groups. Each discovery should cause a decision in the present; the public should learn enough to audit leadership without turning children and descendants into collective suspects. Exact book and scene order remains unchosen.

## Working compatibility rule for older notes

The old account in which a single pure Konrad faction wages war while Samuel is simply an exiled outsider in the future victor is **superseded wherever it denies a shared criminal project, George's planned heroic placement, Aiden's and other sons' opposing placements, or the network of smaller groups behind both sides**. Its observable events and convincing false explanations can seed successive audience revelations. A prior personal rupture, exile, sabotage, actual military defeat, and a later postwar access bargain may still be used if developed to fit this new topology. Their old ordering and explanations are no longer controlling. The exact war dates, role clocks, planet-crossing chronology, and steps by which Samuel captures specific groups remain open.
