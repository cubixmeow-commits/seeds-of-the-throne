# Influence Allocation

## September 5 integration boundary

**Unresolved agency boundary:** weighted plans cannot silently manufacture human consent or cooperation. Clarify whether effects operate through resources, permitted infrastructure, incentives, or synthetic behavior. The status and rights of synthetic participants also remain relevant. Treat descriptions of making people receptive as a mechanic requiring review, not a universal override.

Source: [[01 Sessions/Daily/2026-09-05 - Integrated Foundation Audit and Workshop Build]].


The environment receives competing plans, weights them, and synthesizes an implemented outcome. Influence can shift gradually, causing institutions and people to become more receptive to one participant's plans and less receptive to another's without any visible command.

Participants experience the symptoms: cooperation, friction, funding, adoption, delay, or loss. Influence is the underlying currency; resources are the visible consequence. Because the system knows each person's hidden advantages, scoring can account for conditions rather than measuring raw outcomes alone.

Open: whether influence attaches to people, positions, plans, or all three; how it is earned; and whether experienced actors can perceive the allocation.
