# The Schedule and Stages

The constructed civilization advances through large planned phases: terraforming and foundation, growth, political development, world war, settlement, and later modernity. Technology and institutions evolve inside rather than remaining a static period simulation.

Different stages create different tests and defeated blocs. Scores and standing may carry from highly competitive eras into quieter later roles. The historical schedule is therefore both civilization-building and curriculum.
