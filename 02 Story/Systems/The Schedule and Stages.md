# The Schedule and Stages

The constructed civilization advances through large planned phases: terraforming and foundation, growth, political development, world war, settlement, and later modernity. Technology and institutions evolve inside rather than remaining a static period simulation.

Different stages create different tests and defeated blocs. Scores and standing may carry from highly competitive eras into quieter later roles. The historical schedule is therefore both civilization-building and curriculum.

The current planet's main human integration history reaches the Sylvan showdown after approximately a century, potentially closer to 120 years once its entry phases are fixed. The established scale is century-long rather than an exact numbered calendar. The approximately eighty-year middle and George's seventy-five-year public biography fit inside this larger period.

This scheduled history belongs to a later-generation colonization world. Earlier successful planets already exist and help provide the capacity to manage this world at a denser technological level than previous settlements.
