# AI Souls

## Established terminology

Luminai are AI extensions of human minds developed within the interactive colonization environment. Sylvan uses a new, more deeply integrated version; earlier participants use earlier versions. The leaders' new development program spans thousands of years; exact stages remain open. AI soul remains broad story vocabulary for human-linked artificial cognition.

**Daemon identifies an evil AI soul, not a generation.** Konrad's experienced Great War daemon is an earlier version. Earlier systems are not collectively evil, and a new architecture does not guarantee good values.

## Generation, identity, and conduct

Deeper integration is the established distinction for Sylvan. Exact differences in bandwidth, memory, embodiment, autonomy, portability, and learning remain unresolved. Konrad's destructive objective does not prove that every earlier system lacks correction.

The working identity model treats a human and Luminai as one extended person, with bounded operation outside conscious attention. Consequential human authorization is a separately accepted governance rule. Values, evidence, behavior, accountability, and safeguards determine what capability becomes in use.

## Research boundary

A personal neural interface could combine reciprocal learning with individualized computation and environmental resources. The existing implant-free channel and newer interface research require an explicit architecture decision. Neither complete cognitive union nor invulnerability has been scientifically demonstrated.
