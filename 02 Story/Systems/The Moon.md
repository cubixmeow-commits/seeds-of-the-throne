# The Moon

A moon-scale artificial structure visible in plain sight. It supplies high-level command, observation, communication, data infrastructure, and energy coordination for the planet and its satellites. Immediate local control may be distributed through synthetics, personal agents, infrastructure, and environmental technology rather than performed directly by the moon.

**September 27 established direction:** the moon-like orbital structure functions as a **solar generator** that supplies wireless energy used by **billions of synthetics** and their wrapped nanocells. **Most people mistake the generator for a natural moon.** A **network of hidden satellites** relays this power onward. This builds on the existing integrated wireless energy grid. How the satellite network delivers power to local infrastructure or individual units, and how participants are kept from identifying the satellites, remain open. Its energy role does not make the moon the single mind or direct controller of every synthetic. See [[02 Story/Systems/Synthetics]].

**Origin integration (working):** the orbital network supports an inhabited colonization society, not just a villain's proposed transformation. Whether the initial private corporations invented, financed, assembled or merely relied on any version of this structure is open; earlier ownership does not imply retained control over the mature network. The story diagram uses lightning to show wireless energy to readers; no such visible discharge or precise receiver topology is established. See [[02 Story/Systems/Corporate Ascension Claim and Public Proof]] and [[01 Sessions/Daily/2026-09-27 - Orbital Synthetic Society Diagram and Macro Foundation]].

Open: which people know its true nature, whether the Throne understands it, and how autonomous it is from the parent civilization.

See [[Advanced Technology Ecology]] for the working decision to treat planetary intelligence as a distributed ecology rather than one omnipotent mind located in the moon.
