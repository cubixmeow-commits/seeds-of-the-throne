# The Moon

A moon-scale artificial structure visible in plain sight. It supplies high-level command, observation, communication, data infrastructure, and energy coordination for the planet and its satellites. Immediate local control may be distributed through synthetics, personal agents, infrastructure, and environmental technology rather than performed directly by the moon.

**September 27 established direction:** the moon-like orbital structure functions as a **solar generator** that supplies wireless energy used by synthetics' wrapped nanocells. This builds on the existing integrated wireless energy grid. Whether energy travels directly from orbit to every unit, through regional relays and local infrastructure, or through a layered combination is still open. Its energy role does not make the moon the single mind or direct controller of every synthetic. See [[02 Story/Systems/Synthetics]].

Open: who knows what it is, whether the Throne understands it, and how autonomous it is from the parent civilization.

See [[Advanced Technology Ecology]] for the working decision to treat planetary intelligence as a distributed ecology rather than one omnipotent mind located in the moon.
