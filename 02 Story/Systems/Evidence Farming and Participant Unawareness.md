# Evidence Farming and Participant Unawareness

## November 7 core purpose — author direction (working)

The colonization environment's real leaders are farming **evidence through actions**: data. The environment exists to produce trustworthy outcomes on contested questions that the originating society cannot settle by argument, where opposed camps hold their models with complete certainty and talk alone changes nothing.

## How it works

- Each camp is given real opportunities to enact its beliefs in practice, not merely to declare them.
- Outcomes are produced by actions and lived consequences, then recorded by the advanced AI infrastructure.
- The records are weighed to determine which model of reality actually holds: what happened, to whom, at what cost, under which enacted assumptions.
- The product is an adjudicable record, not a victory announcement. See [[02 Story/Systems/Story Functionality and Narrative Containment]] for the sealed record this depends on, and [[03 Context/OPEN-QUESTIONS]] (FP-01) for the open governance question of who may convert an observation into a rule, which this system inherits: **who sets the criteria the weighing uses remains unresolved.**

## Why participants must not know

Participant unawareness is structural, not decorative. It functions as blinding at civilization scale:

- A participant who knows they are inside a controlled environment performs for the observers instead of living; performed behavior is not evidence.
- Awareness would change which opportunities people take, which risks they accept, and which beliefs they actually enact.
- The participants are therefore, by design, the last people who can be told. This is the environment's deepest ethical tension and connects directly to the central question of whether a system that uses people as material can cultivate leaders who refuse to do the same.

## Data integrity is the existential stake

Because the product is evidence, covert interference is not ordinary tyranny; it is contamination:

- A controller who quietly rigs opportunities, pre-loads interpretations, or edits which records become salient (Samuel Franklin's established method; see [[02 Story/Systems/Encoded Pattern Interpretation and Manufactured History]]) falsifies the dataset the entire environment exists to produce.
- This is why the sealed record sits outside contained leaders' reach, and why provenance is the Luminai's core discipline (see [[02 Story/Systems/Human–Luminai Pairing and Bonding]]).
- Samuel's obsession with complete control of participants' minds and actions (see [[02 Story/Characters/Samuel Franklin]]) is, in this frame, experimental fraud: he would rather own the results than know the truth.

## Boundaries

- The environment weighs outcomes of enacted beliefs; it does not read minds, guarantee verdicts, or settle what criteria justice requires. The criteria question stays open.
- No real-world contested issue is mapped onto this system in compiled notes. Development examples stay in session context and are reduced to this mechanism before entering the story.
- How much of this the governing AI can weigh directly, versus what requires human judgment, is unresolved.
