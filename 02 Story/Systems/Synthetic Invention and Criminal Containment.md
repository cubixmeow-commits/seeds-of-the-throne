# Synthetic Invention and Criminal Containment

## Established macro premise

Invention of sophisticated nanotechnology brings biological synthetics into existence. They are indistinguishable from humans in form and function, while their wrapped nanocells contain technology that colonization-planet participants cannot expose with available instruments. A solar generator in orbit, mistaken for a moon by most people, supplies wireless power to **billions of synthetics** through a hidden satellite network. Their existence changes the scale of possible power, raising new legal, social and security questions. Resource-rich criminals seek to merge with synthetics or absorb their capabilities and imagine becoming gods. Laws are enacted in response, and the criminals are lured **into** a multi-planet colonization process with an opportunity to attempt to prove their ambitions under bounded conditions. The collision of fascist entitlement and advanced technological capability is a causal engine for the story.

This does not establish that synthetics are inherently a threat, that every criminal has the same motive, that a human can actually become synthetic, or that the villains' doctrines are biologically valid. Their desired appropriation and the laws addressing it are distinct from synthetic people's identities and agency.

**Established ideological connection:** Samuel and Konrad take their false inherited-superiority doctrine and apply it to synthetic embodiment. They imagine that appropriating synthetic bodies and capabilities will transform them into enhanced human gods, placing themselves permanently above everyone else. This is a villain belief and political motive, not evidence of biological superiority, a successful transfer method, or a hierarchy among humans and synthetics. See [[01 Sessions/Daily/2026-09-27 - Supremacist Godhood Applied to Synthetic Embodiment]].

## Relationship to the three worlds — working

The home world's technological breakthrough and legal response offer a specific origin for why powerful criminal organizations become a colonization problem. Planet Two's earlier managed conflict can absorb factions wanting to show their systems work. Planet Three inherits lessons from that experience and becomes a more mature, shared civilization containing descendants, future leaders, synthetics, ordinary inhabitants and successor Luminai development. These worlds are physically real. Criminals can acquire real local positions but not the governing process or the civilization's unrestricted synthetic substrate.

**Open chronology:** which invention, statute, recruitment and containment steps occurred before Planet Two; whether colonization already existed for other purposes; which offenders entered voluntarily versus under conviction; and what exactly they were promised. A lawful invitation, a sentence and a deceptive inducement are not identical and cannot be used interchangeably without deciding the rules.

## Private corporate origin — leading direction, details working

The author's leading origin direction begins with **a few private corporations pursuing colonization while resisting government control**. They acquire investors, build legitimate infrastructure, develop or acquire advanced AI and synthetic capabilities, and generate immense private wealth. A subset of founders, investors, executives or recruiters later supplies the people who become contained criminals. Their real contributions explain capital, privileged insight and an aggrieved claim that the process belongs to them; no investment or contractor role grants ownership of synthetic persons, residents, the generator, or the governing process. The identities of firms, founders, public authorities and Samuel and Konrad's corporate positions remain open. Not everyone involved in the original private project becomes an offender.

The proposed offense is not private enterprise, legitimate medical improvement, developing useful synthetic technology or failing to solve a hard problem. An internal faction may **sell certainty of synthetic integration and godlike human enhancement that it cannot demonstrate**, build large cult-like followings, conceal contrary results, exploit recruits, and attempt coercive experimentation or appropriation. Whether the sellers know the advertised outcome is impossible, know only that it is unproven, or differ from each other is an evidence question. The broad boast may be impossible *as promised* without canonically denying regeneration, other legitimate medical uses, or every future form of safe interface.

This option yields a possible chronology: corporate colonization initiative resisting government control → technological breakthrough and expanded investor participation (relative dates open) → false ascension promises and attempts at appropriation → legal and independent institutional response → revocation of insider access → lawful, bounded inducement or sentence into colonization → public accounting. The corporate origin does **not** settle whether synthetic invention precedes the first colony construction. A founder who helped build the machinery poses a greater security risk than an outsider: the process cannot be assumed safe merely because their visible permissions are revoked. Independent verification of hidden dependencies and synthetic/person protections is required to make containment credible.

**Purpose of the bounded attempt — leading author direction:** the real leaders let the offenders attempt their specific claim in a controlled setting because large followings demand an answer. They hope a conspicuous and independently verifiable failure will break the promise's grip, discourage similar movements and settle the specific fraud and attempted capture. This is a *public epistemic and accountability goal*, not certainty that one failure proves universal impossibility, permission to predetermine the result, or a cure for all extremist ideology. A fair test must define in advance what the offenders claim to achieve, who may consent to take part, what harm stops the attempt, who verifies evidence independently, and how the result is made legible to skeptical followers. Legitimate regeneration and medicine are separate from the promised ascension.

The audience could first hear the offenders' plausible complaint that they built the colony, then find contracts and technical records that distinguish contribution from control, followed by recruitment ledgers and concealed failures showing the unproven sales pitch. Later joint agreements and Samuel's separate unauthorized acts reframe the apparent Samuel–Konrad disagreement. This is a proposed reveal chain, not fixed chapter order. ## The lure — proposed structure

Rather than promising an actual path to godhood, governing leaders could permit a **bounded test of the offenders' claims**: territory, resources, organizational roles and time in which they can try to demonstrate the political and technical system they insist will work. This can be attractive to leaders who interpret any real access as the first step toward unrestricted synthetic power. What was offered, who was truthful, and which limits were disclosed remain author gates. An offender's attempt can produce useful evidence without turning abuse against others into an ethically costless experiment.

Samuel and Konrad's shared ambition to place their brains in synthetic bodies is one historically specific use of this broader incentive. Their false racist breeding premise, failed enhancement of George and Aiden, staged enmity and later blame transfer remain separate events requiring their own chronology and proof.

## Laws — function established, content unresolved

The author establishes that laws followed synthetic invention. The exact statutes, sovereign authority, status of synthetic persons, prohibited conduct, protected rights, due process, enforcement and relationship to colonization remain open. Potential subjects for later workshop—not laws already passed in canon—include bodily autonomy, use of a synthetic processor, deceptive identity substitution, unauthorized cognitive merger, theft of energy/control access, and experiments on people or synthetics without consent.

## The causal distinction

Technological sophistication makes appropriation imaginable; wealth and organization make attempts feasible; fascist ideology turns entitlement into a justification; bounded colonization makes the attempted system visible and accountable. Each link matters. The real leaders' legal and containment systems must also face scrutiny for harms they allow and for the rights of synthetics and ordinary residents.
