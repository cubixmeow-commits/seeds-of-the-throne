# Integration and Scoring

Integration levels describe how much of the hidden structure a person can access, participate in, or discuss. A person may understand above their authorized level, creating danger rather than freedom.

Everyone is scored relative to the conditions and influence they were given. Standing may carry across environments, eras, identities, or generations. Difficulty tiers permit different kinds of pressure without pretending every participant faced identical conditions.

## Assessment and self-placement

The older leaders create an extensive assessment framework for newly trained human–Luminai pairs. It determines which roles and responsibilities a pair has demonstrated the qualifications to undertake. Actual path decisions and developed skills matter; raw output or integration depth alone cannot define qualification.

The framework opens credible placement paths. It does not arbitrarily assign the participant's future. The human and Luminai choose their own placement among the paths their record supports, with the human retaining responsibility for the consequential choice.

Assessment establishes qualification for a role rather than human worth, moral superiority, or a permanent right to govern. A pair may qualify for several paths, including meaningful nonleadership work. Exact dimensions, evaluator independence, accommodation, review, appeals, revocation, and what happens when no preferred path is available remain unresolved.

Open: exact levels, scoring authority, assessment dimensions, appeals, carried standing, how options are presented, and whether participants ever see a score.
