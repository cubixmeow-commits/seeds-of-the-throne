# Environments and Technology

## Environment packet

Each recurring place should define:

- era and social function;
- geography, scale, climate, and time cues;
- architecture, circulation, and signs of maintenance;
- dominant materials and how they age;
- population, labor, class, and ordinary use;
- visible history, repair, damage, adaptation, and local culture;
- palette roles and motivated sources of light;
- what the location permits, constrains, or conceals;
- unresolved details the image must not settle.

A convincing environment should show what happened before the frame and what can happen after it. Avoid empty concept-art plazas unless emptiness is the story beat.

## Technology packet

Describe technology by function and consequence before appearance:

1. Who uses it and for what decision?
2. What physical infrastructure makes it possible?
3. What information or energy enters and leaves?
4. What does it permit, refuse, record, or make visible?
5. What can fail, be misunderstood, or be abused?
6. How has ordinary life adapted around it?

Then define visual language: material, scale, interface behavior, integration with architecture or bodies, maintenance evidence, and culturally specific design. Avoid generic holograms, arbitrary neon, decorative circuitry, and interfaces displaying unreadable walls of text.

## Luminai and Daemons

Represent extended cognition through behavior, reflection, environmental response, selective overlays, sound or voice implication, or a chosen differentiated presentation. A Luminai should not automatically appear as a glowing humanoid assistant. A Daemon should not become a horned supernatural creature. Their visual distinction emerges from information integrity, feedback, attention, and the human's relationship to reality.

## Biological synthetics

Synthetics are biological and socially embedded. Do not default to metal joints, glowing eyes, or identical bodies. Show synthetic difference only when the story has established a detectable marker, provenance interface, production context, or behavioral constraint. Images must not settle unresolved personhood or self-knowledge questions through visual shorthand.

## Planetary AI ecology

Show colony-scale intelligence as distributed infrastructure: responsive buildings, logistics, energy, transit, evidence chains, permissions, local systems, biological labor, and moon-scale coordination. Avoid one giant face or central glowing orb unless a specific institution deliberately uses that representation.

## Continuity rule

An environment or technology image is interpretive until approved. Store useful material and design discoveries as candidate visual decisions; do not promote a generator's convenient architecture or mechanism into story canon automatically.
