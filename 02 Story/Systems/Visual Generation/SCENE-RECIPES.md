# Scene Recipes

## Core formula

`character identity + era + location + immediate change + objective + physical action + opposing pressure + environmental evidence + camera language + continuity contract + output contract`

## Discovery frame

- Subject finds one piece of evidence that changes the next decision, not the entire mystery.
- Show the evidence's physical or authenticated source.
- Give the subject a bodily response smaller than the significance of the discovery.
- Keep unresolved conclusions out of visible interfaces and decorative text.

## Relationship synchronization

- Establish both humans as primary romantic participants.
- Show Luminai involvement through chosen presentations, environmental response, or shared context—not parallel flirting.
- Define the consent action: permission granted, boundary negotiated, access paused, context shared, or repair attempted.
- Include one visible sign that privacy and individual agency remain intact.

## Contained authority failing

- Show a system or room ceasing to obey rather than relying on crowns or villain posing.
- Place evidence, personnel, or institutional change in depth.
- Give the contained leader an action aimed at preserving authority while the environment demonstrates its expiration.

## Cultivation under constraint

- Give the future leader a bounded office, incomplete information, and a decision with real cost.
- Show the people affected as people, not background proof of the leader's goodness.
- Let technology expose options and consequences without highlighting one answer as correct.
- Represent the Luminai as cognitive support, not an oracle.

## Technology-in-use frame

- Begin with the human task and institutional consequence.
- Show inputs, infrastructure, maintenance, and limits.
- Include ordinary users or downstream effects.
- Avoid product-advertisement composition and unexplained glowing interfaces.

## Environment establishment

- Include foreground ordinary life, middle-ground institutional function, and background geographic or infrastructural scale.
- Show at least one trace of history or repair.
- Let architecture direct movement rather than merely decorate the horizon.

## Three-frame micro-sequence

1. **Before:** objective and stable expectation.
2. **Change:** action or evidence breaks the expectation.
3. **After:** behavior reveals the new decision or unresolved consequence.

Maintain identity and spatial logic across frames while changing shot size or camera position. Do not render three nearly identical portraits with different expressions.

## Variant matrix

For exploration, vary one field from each group:

- story: discovery, refusal, repair, arrival, failure, decision;
- place: public institution, intimate room, infrastructure, transit, exterior, synthetic community;
- camera: wide observational, medium behavioral, close evidentiary, obstructed, moving follow;
- light: dawn, practical interior, emergency, weather-filtered, reflected night;
- action: crossing, turning away, reaching, withholding, releasing, listening.

Keep the canon anchor fixed while testing combinations. Promote only combinations that strengthen story logic.
