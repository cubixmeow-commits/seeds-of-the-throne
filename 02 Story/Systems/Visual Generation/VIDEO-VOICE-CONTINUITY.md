# Video and Voice Continuity

## Video packet

Define one short dramatic action per clip:

- start state and end state;
- subject action and speed;
- camera movement and reason;
- environmental movement;
- duration and aspect ratio;
- identity references and immutable traits;
- objects, hands, wardrobe, and background elements that must remain continuous;
- transitions or changes that are deliberately allowed;
- failure risks such as face drift, morphing clothing, duplicate limbs, sliding objects, or unmotivated camera motion.

Prefer a restrained action that can complete cleanly over a long chain of unrelated motion. Generate adjacent clips around clear matching states when a scene requires more time.

## Image-to-video rule

The source still establishes the first frame and identity, not permission to keep the character frozen. Specify weight shift, eye target, breath, hand action, environmental response, and camera relation. Preserve geometry while allowing performance.

## Sequence continuity

Maintain a shot ledger containing:

- scene and shot identifier;
- timeline position;
- character appearance and wardrobe state;
- location, time, weather, and light direction;
- props and hand continuity;
- emotional state before and after;
- camera side, movement, and matching eyeline;
- source image, prompt, tool, and approval state.

## Voice bible

Until persistent voice locking is demonstrated, define voice textually for each recurring character:

- apparent age and vocal health;
- pitch range and resonance;
- accent or invented regional influence;
- cadence, tempo, pause pattern, and breath;
- articulation and degree of formality;
- vocabulary, sentence length, and recurring rhetorical habits;
- emotional compression or expressiveness;
- how stress, intimacy, fatigue, authority, and deception alter delivery;
- explicit avoidances such as theatrical villainy, generic trailer voice, or another character's cadence.

Never identify a fictional voice only by naming a real performer. Describe audible qualities and dramatic function.

## Voice continuity test

Generate the same short neutral passage and the same emotionally demanding passage across tests. Compare pitch, cadence, accent, articulation, and emotional behavior separately. Save the prompt, model/tool, settings when exposed, date, and chosen sample. A written bible supports continuity but does not prove the platform can persist an exact voice.

## Luminai voice

A Luminai voice may differ in gender presentation, register, or processing rhythm while remaining recognizably connected to the human's extended identity. Avoid making it automatically calm, seductive, omniscient, or emotionally perfect. In synchronized relationships, Luminai voices must not create a parallel romance or speak consent that belongs to the humans.
