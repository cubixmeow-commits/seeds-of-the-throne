# Awakening and Processing

Participants become aware that ordinary reality has a hidden structure but are not given a complete revelation. Each explains the experience using the vocabulary their era provides. Processing supplies enough information to continue in a structured role while withholding the whole account.

The variety of incompatible but reasonable explanations helps the reader understand that no character's framework is fully reliable. Knowledge is gated by role, integration, timing, and possibly by the system's purpose for the individual.

## Samuel's established terminal processing — 2026-09-10

Samuel's terminal processing does not remove him from the colonization process. Genuine failure of his independently funded plan triggers specific pre-established rules. His Daemon is immediately deleted because continued Daemon existence requires active participation. Samuel enters a stasis-like coma, and biological synthetics physically transport him to a location containing the older leaders of the contained criminal groups.

Samuel remains inside the process but no longer holds active-participant status. Everything he did is presented while he is present without his Daemon, removing his ability to keep each group inside a separate explanation of his conduct. Exact location design, safeguards, and public-versus-protected audience layers remain open.
