# Awakening and Processing

Participants become aware that ordinary reality has a hidden structure but are not given a complete revelation. Each explains the experience using the vocabulary their era provides. Processing supplies enough information to continue in a structured role while withholding the whole account.

The variety of incompatible but reasonable explanations helps the reader understand that no character's framework is fully reliable. Knowledge is gated by role, integration, timing, and possibly by the system's purpose for the individual.
