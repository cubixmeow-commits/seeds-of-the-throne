# Synthetics

## September 27 nanocell foundation — author established

Nanotechnology builds a **biological wrapper around each individual nanocell**. The nanotechnology remains inside its wrapped unit. Organized populations of these units can form a **biological computer** and a larger biological synthetic. Their power ultimately comes wirelessly from a moon-like solar generator in orbit, through the planet's energy system. The exact pathway from orbital generation to each cell, the matter source for growth, the organization of computation, and any biological metabolism remain open.

**Detection and knowledge are different:** no technology available to participants on the colonization planet can expose the nanotechnology hidden within the wrapped nanocells. This does not undo the later author decision that different people can learn information about synthetics through different means at different times, including after the Great War. Learning about synthetics need not include a direct scan of their internal nanotechnology. The means and limits of non-instrumental, outside, or permitted disclosure remain open.

The prior proposal in [[Synthetic Embodiment and False Godhood Project]] that nanoscale tools merely build a body of cell equivalents is subordinate to this architecture: the core persists inside every biological wrapper. Do not infer humanlike organs, distributed consciousness, a vacant transplant host, or the success of brain transfer from the presence of a biological computer.

## September 27 embodiment research — established motive, proposed mechanism

Samuel and Konrad jointly pursue becoming synthetic through transfer of their own biological brains into synthetic bodies. This is their intended outcome, not proof that transfer has succeeded or that a transplanted brain preserves identity. Their theory that children of mixed human ancestry can reveal a bridge between a human and a synthetic is an invented racist error, not a feature of this world's biology. The program and its victims must be traced through [[The Breeding Program and Lineage Blackmail]].

**Earlier downstream proposal, not the foundation:** tissue organization, vascular support, repair and connection to a transplanted brain still need definition after the nanocell architecture is understood. The existing synthetics below have processors and can be independent people; this proposal does not turn them into vacant bodies. Whether the leaders seek an unoccupied engineered host or plan to violate an existing person's autonomy is unresolved. Their Frankenstein-like creator/creation relationship is thematic development, not a literal plot outcome.

Biological beings cheap enough to exist in planetary populations and indistinguishable from humans to ordinary participants. Their biological processors function like brains. Their apparent lifespans support the mortal reality expected inside the reconstruction.

They are not merely props. Synthetic generations built the civilization before human integration and may have ordinary developmental histories, reproduction, institutions, and cultural continuity. Their cooperation is also a principal delivery mechanism for invisible influence allocation.

Open: self-knowledge, reproduction, rights, susceptibility to compromise, and the relation between synthetic agency and system control.

See [[Advanced Technology Ecology]] for the colony-scale systems, responsive physical environment, and hard-limit framework in which synthetics operate.
