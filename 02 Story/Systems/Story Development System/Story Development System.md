# Story Development System

Status: working (built 2026-11-21, Noa's direction). This is our own system, assembled in the vault from the valuable parts of the open-source story tools surveyed the same night (Manuskript, novelWriter, bibisco, graphify-novel, oh-story), fitted to the machinery this vault already runs: the Dynamic Story Workshop gates, the prose engine, the POV Campaign Engine, the Component Map, and the QA pass. It exists to carry the structure-completion phase: integrate the surfaced material, complete the beginning, middle, and end architecture, and bring Book One to scene-ready.

## What was taken from each tool, and why

- **Manuskript: the outline tree.** The whole book lives as one hierarchy: Book, then Beginning / Middle / End, then sequences, then scenes. Every node carries a one-paragraph synopsis, its purpose, and its status label. Nothing is drafted before its node exists. This is the spine the sequence map grows from.
- **novelWriter: the scene synopsis card.** Every scene gets a card before it is written: synopsis, point of view, place, strand, and what changes. Our version adds the POV Campaign Engine's discipline: what the point-of-view character can know, and what the scene must do (collapse an illusion, increase uncertainty, narrow geography, or block information) or be cut as filler.
- **bibisco: the fabula, the strands, and the character interview.** Two orders are kept separate at all times: the fabula, the true chronological order of events (the century of hidden history), and the plot order, the order the reader is allowed to learn them. The gap between the two is this story's engine, and it is where the evidence order lives. Characters are built by interview, a fixed set of questions answered in the author's voice, not by trait lists. Narrative strands are tracked as separate lines that must each earn their crossings.
- **graphify-novel: the thread graph.** Characters, places, claims, lies, and objects are nodes; every connection is written down with its source scene. When a fact moves, the graph shows what else it touches, which is how continuity breaks get caught before the QA pass has to.
- **oh-story: the planted-thread ledger.** Every planted thread (a lie told, an object placed, a claim made, a name withheld) is logged when planted, with its intended payoff and the latest point the payoff can land. Nothing is planted without a ledger entry; nothing in the ledger is allowed to die silently.

## Our own parts, which none of them have

- **The gates stay the decision layer.** The system prepares; the author decides. FP-01, CE-01, PT-01, OR-01, OR-02, SPR-01 and the downstream gates consume the tree, the cards, and the ledger as their inputs. A gate output is not canon until the author rules it, and compiled material keeps its labels: established, working, proposed, unresolved, rejected.
- **The integration register.** Material surfaced by the project's other lanes (the documented RV and AP record, the Egypt overlap, the Origins Unknown Reports, the topics the Facebook audience responded to) enters through a register: what it is, where it could live in the world, what it would change, and its status. Proposed until ruled. Research is advisory, never canon.
- **The who-knows-what-when matrix.** Drawn from the thread graph, one row per major fact: who knows it, who believes a false version, who planted the false version, and the scene where each state changes. In a story built on supplied interpretation and impersonated orders, this matrix is the master continuity document.

## The working order

1. Framework audit against the tree: every settled decision placed, every gap named.
2. Integration register processed into the tree as proposed nodes.
3. Beginning, middle, and end architecture fixed at the top level of the tree.
4. Gates worked in order, each fed by the cards, the ledger, and the matrix.
5. Scene cards completed until the book meets the scene-ready definition: contest, deception, clock, opening, middle, character choices, evidence order, sequence map.
6. Drafting begins from the cards, development-prototype style first, the author's voice final.
