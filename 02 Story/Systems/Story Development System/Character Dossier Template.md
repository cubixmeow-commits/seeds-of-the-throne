# Character Dossier Template

Status: working template (Story Development System, 2026-11-21). Built by interview, not trait list: answer the questions in plain words, as the character's record, before writing scenes for them. Story-specific sections follow the interview.

## The interview (from bibisco, adapted)

1. What do they want, stated the way they would state it?
2. What do they want that they would never state?
3. What do they believe about how the world works, and where did that belief come from?
4. What are they wrong about?
5. What have they done that cannot be undone?
6. What do they do when they are afraid?
7. What do they do when they win?
8. Who do they protect, and at what cost?
9. Who do they use, and do they know they are using them?
10. What would make them change sides, and what would make that impossible?

## The record

- **Role in the structure:** (which strand, which function in the contest)
- **Knows:** (facts held, with the scene or era each was gained)
- **Believes falsely:** (supplied interpretations, planted claims, impersonated orders believed)
- **Conceals:** (what they hide, and from whom)
- **Choices:** (the decisions this character must make in Book One, in order)
- **Status:** (established / working / proposed / unresolved)
