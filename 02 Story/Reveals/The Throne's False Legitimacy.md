# The Throne's False Legitimacy

The Throne's central lie is not that the system is imaginary. His father really placed him, a real operation existed, and he genuinely arrived before later groups. The false part is his claim that he entered as a legitimate authority rather than as a contained offender.

That one inversion makes punishment appear to be tenure and lets him explain procedures to disoriented arrivals as their resident official. Because the supporting facts are true, the lie survives ordinary verification.
