# The Throne's False Legitimacy

The Throne's central lie is not that the system, war, public offices, or George's placement are imaginary. The supporting events are real.

He received a real city, captured a real country, initiated a real conquest, and lost the Great War. Before the original city fell, he placed George inside the future victorious republic. George later occupied genuine public roles there.

The false part is the King's claim that George secretly took control of the victor and that the empire's destruction deliberately converted territorial rule into hidden global authority.

That inversion makes defeat appear to be strategy, captured leaders appear to be embedded agents, public containment roles appear to be covert offices, and George's supervised career appear to prove successful infiltration.

The same structure supports his lineage authority. Real genealogies, births, certifications, and family relationships exist, but the King secretly manipulates the records and reproductive system. He then presents his private fraud as neutral knowledge only he can certify.

Because the supporting facts are true, both lies survive ordinary verification. Exposure requires comparing original records, later explanations, concealed interventions, and the political obedience those explanations produced.
