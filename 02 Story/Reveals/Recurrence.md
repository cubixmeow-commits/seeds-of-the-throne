# Recurrence

The protagonist discovers the same behavioral signature across identities separated by decades or centuries. The investigation moves through five reinterpretations:

1. Historical anomaly.
2. Behavioral recurrence.
3. Administrative anomaly.
4. Institutional discovery.
5. Moral inversion: history may record a sentence as an admirable public life.

Not every admired person is contained. Mixed cases preserve discernment and prevent the discovery from collapsing into universal cynicism.
