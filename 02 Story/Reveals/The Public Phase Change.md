# The Public Phase Change

After a participant survives beyond an unresolved threshold, a concealed contest becomes public. This does not create safety; it changes the governing conditions. Information asymmetry, remote manipulation, unverifiable authority, and quiet reassignment are less effective once the conflict is witnessed.

A public victory cannot be silently recast into another biography. This may explain why tribunal records, testimony, and archived evidence survive as the frame of the story.

Open: trigger, audience, whether disclosure is reward or test, and whether adversaries can refuse to surface.
