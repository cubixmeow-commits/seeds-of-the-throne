# The Inheritor

A modern-era heir and possible Thread B protagonist, entering roughly eighty years after the Witness. They explain awakening through surveillance, simulation, or targeted technology. Their interpretation is intelligent, era-shaped, and incomplete.

They are competent, resourced, and apparently headed toward a meaningful public placement. The Witness's intervention therefore threatens something real. The Wakeful would approach through a favor or opportunity rather than an obvious threat.

Open: inciting event, relationship to the Witness, founding house, what they know on entry, whether they accept the offer, and ultimate placement.
