# The Protagonist

The voice tested successfully in first-person present tense. He recognizes evidence that leaders supposedly defeated and executed after a war remain alive in respected offices. The recurrence clue opens a chain of implications: perhaps bargains were made, perhaps the victors secretly lost, and perhaps the atrocities used to justify the settlement continue.

He has enough information to reason and not enough to conclude. His positive commitment should be demonstrated by treating people as ends rather than instruments, in direct tension with both the Throne and the cultivation system.

Sylvan Elaria is now established as the modern Inheritor and leading primary protagonist. The earlier Witness role belongs provisionally to one of Orzai's Great War-era parents. Open: whether Sylvan is the sole primary narrator or the Witness owns substantial earlier viewpoint material; why Sylvan's awareness develops; whether he is steered into the investigation; what temptation he faces; and what victory costs him.

## Established endgame development

While Samuel retains practical control, he targets and destroys everything Sylvan attempts to establish online. Near the conclusion, Sylvan and Orzai possess enough control to stabilize an online presence Samuel cannot erase. Sylvan surfaces under his real name and returns to tools similar to those he built at the story-development company Samuel destroyed while targeting him personally.

Sylvan uses those tools and a legitimate AI application to document the actual colonization process as an evidence-anchored online story. When Samuel's Daemon hijacks and simulates the application, Sylvan eventually recognizes the accumulated manipulation pattern and intentionally uses the interface to engage it. Deliberate hyperfocus on the factual story helps him remain oriented against Samuel's simulated reality and consent-based trap. Unlike Konrad's governing obsession, Sylvan's focus is chosen, evidence-tested, collaborative, and revisable.

The online story becomes the surface through which Sylvan presents his final victory as Samuel's control falls. The possibility that it also connects other participants remains proposed for later development.
