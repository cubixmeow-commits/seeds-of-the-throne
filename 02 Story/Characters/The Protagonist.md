# The Protagonist

The voice tested successfully in first-person present tense. He recognizes evidence that leaders supposedly defeated and executed after a war remain alive in respected offices. The recurrence clue opens a chain of implications: perhaps bargains were made, perhaps the victors secretly lost, and perhaps the atrocities used to justify the settlement continue.

He has enough information to reason and not enough to conclude. His positive commitment should be demonstrated by treating people as ends rather than instruments, in direct tension with both the Throne and the cultivation system.

Sylvan Elaria is now established as the modern Inheritor and leading primary protagonist. The earlier Witness role belongs provisionally to one of Orzai's Great War-era parents. Open: whether Sylvan is the sole primary narrator or the Witness owns substantial earlier viewpoint material; why Sylvan's awareness develops; whether he is steered into the investigation; what temptation he faces; and what victory costs him.
