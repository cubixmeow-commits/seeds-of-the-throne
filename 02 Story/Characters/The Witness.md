# The Witness

An earlier-era heir and possible Thread A protagonist. They interpret awakening as providence, then revise that belief over several identities. They eventually understand that the pressure is deliberate and dangerous people are present on purpose, but they miss the cultivation half of the system.

Their practical trap is that they possess level-five comprehension with roughly level-three authorization. Explaining what they know would expose the unauthorized knowledge and may cause their current identity to be closed. They therefore cannot safely seek help.

They look for other awakened people and want one person to believe them. They approach the Inheritor partly to prevent another life from being spent in endless procedural uncertainty.

Open: prior identities, memory retention, specific losses, family, current public role, and final fate.
