# Erosion Environments

Participants begin with meaningful control and build something worth keeping. Influence is then shifted toward contained adversaries. Each concession or dependency is individually defensible, so the participant may not recognize that a contest has begun until their work, resources, or position has been taken.

The lesson is not simply to resist. Overcorrection into paranoia, failure to perceive predation, and correct perception followed by late action are distinct failures. The process deliberately produces victims because it considers experienced judgment more valuable than protected innocence.

## Solvency pressure

At defined thresholds, financial or institutional failure triggers processing. A participant near the threshold becomes vulnerable to offers of information, protection, influence, or standing from the people causing the erosion.
