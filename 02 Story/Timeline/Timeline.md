# Timeline

Dates are intentionally unset. This sequence preserves dependencies without pretending chronology is settled.

1. Parent civilization overcomes aging and develops planetary colonization systems.
2. The moon and planet are constructed; synthetics establish civilization.
3. First human populations and heirs are integrated.
4. Dangerous offenders are placed into containment roles.
5. The Throne's central figure arrives early and establishes false legitimacy.
6. Major political movements and contained lineages form.
7. An invented world war ends with defeated leaders expected to disappear.
8. Defeated figures recur in later public identities; the Throne calls the outcome a negotiated settlement.
9. The Witness lives through one or more earlier-era identities.
10. The Throne's promises and genealogical leverage destabilize; Disclosure Wars occur or begin.
11. Roughly eighty years after the Witness's war-era thread, the Inheritor enters the modern thread.
12. Recurrence is rediscovered and the concealed conflict moves toward public exposure.

Open: whether items 9–10 overlap, whether the Disclosure Wars are historical or present action, and where the final public victory belongs.
