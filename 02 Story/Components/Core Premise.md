# Core Premise

A technologically mature civilization uses a developing colonization planet for two purposes at once: containing dangerous criminals and cultivating younger generations into capable leaders. The future leaders enter at a deliberate disadvantage and must learn to recognize predation, retain judgment under uncertainty, resist corruption, and recover from genuine loss.

Containment is assignment to a role, identity, environment, and public life—not necessarily a cell. Cultivation is not safe instruction; the contained population supplies real pressure and sometimes real catastrophe.
