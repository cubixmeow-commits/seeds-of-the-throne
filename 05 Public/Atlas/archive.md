## How story information is labeled

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmed | The author has accepted this as part of the current story. |
| Working | A useful current direction still open to revision. |
| Proposed | A possible answer the author has not accepted. |
| Unresolved | A missing choice or conflict that must remain visible. |
| Historical | An earlier version kept so the development of the story is not lost. |

A well-written idea is not automatically part of the story. Research can make an idea believable, but the author still decides whether the story will use it.

## How an idea moves into the story

1. Find the notes connected to the idea.
2. Identify the missing cause, character choice, or world rule.
3. Compare several different ways the story could work.
4. Test each possibility in a specific scene or situation.
5. Ask the author one clear question.
6. Add the accepted answer to every part of the story it changes.

The story is developed in stages. It begins with the overall structure, then adds causes, character choices, evidence, exact events, scenes, prose, and revision.

## The story keeps changing, but nothing important disappears

The Luminai were developed inside the interactive colonization environment. Earlier participants use earlier versions. Sylvan's new bond is more deeply integrated. A Daemon is an evil AI soul, not the name of an older generation.

Book One now begins near the end, when Sylvan already controls the conclusion and Samuel is running out of moves. The unresolved work remains visible: safeguards, the physical interface, the postwar trap, and the difference between proving a pattern and exposing private people.

Astra's first assessment helped build a twenty-part workshop whose accepted results remain part of the project's history. The story then changed enough that the unfinished 27-task checklist began asking questions from an older version.

A new whole-project assessment retired that stale checklist and created a focused [current workshop](workshop.html). It starts from Samuel's terminal separation, rapid financial collapse, processing and exposure, and Sylvan and Orzai's accepted placement.

## Evidence inside the fiction

An event can leave an operational record, a public explanation, and a sealed account. Recognition, authentication, attribution, and safe action are different achievements. The story should not ask one dramatic resemblance or one impressive display to do all four jobs.

## The author remains in control

AI can help organize the world, find missing causes, run workshops, plan scenes, draft prose, test continuity, and revise weak sections. It cannot quietly decide what the story means or turn an unapproved suggestion into fact.

The author supplies the story, the important choices, the corrections, the taste, and the final approval. The system supplies the time-consuming structure and production work needed to reach a finished book.
