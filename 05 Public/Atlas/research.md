## What the evidence can support

Human brain–computer interfaces have decoded trained attempted actions. A 2023 speech study reported 62 words per minute in one participant, with substantial errors on a large vocabulary. It did not read unrestricted private thought. [Primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06377-x).

A brain–spine interface supported standing and walking in one person with chronic spinal cord injury. That is a functional neural bridge, not general cognitive union. [Primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06094-5).

Nine months of tactile Braille training in sighted adults was associated with anatomical changes. It supports learning-related plasticity, not direct reception of arbitrary wireless information. [Primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-017-17738-8).

## Keep demonstration types separate

| Evidence tier | Example | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human demonstration | Trained speech decoding; brain–spine movement support. | Specific tasks and limited participants. |
| Animal experiment | Ultrasonic neural dust recorded nerve and muscle signals in anesthetized rats. | Peripheral recording, not human cognitive bonding. |
| Mixed preclinical and human feasibility | A 1,024-channel cortical-array study reports porcine/cadaver delivery and a five-patient intraoperative pilot. | Does not establish a chronic, invisible, non-surgical everyday interface. |
| Engineering prototype | Room-scale wireless power in an engineered resonator. | Does not show that an entire city safely powers microscopic brain nodes. |
| Fictional synthesis | A personal bond integrated with environmental computation and sensing. | Components have not been demonstrated together as a Luminai. |

Primary sources: [neural dust](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27497221/), [cortical array](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41551-025-01501-w), [room-scale resonator](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41928-021-00636-3).

## Open architecture questions

An interface needs physical transduction before the brain can learn its signal. Power transfer, data transfer, sensing, and neural stimulation are different jobs. A single unspecified field cannot answer all four.

Candidate systems face range, alignment, coupling, heating, interference, signal drift, and maintenance constraints. Moon-based oversight also needs local safeguards for delays and disconnection. These are design questions, not approved failure modes.

## Claims deliberately left unproven

The brief is preliminary. Its complete source list has not become a verified catalogue merely by being saved. The evidence audit identifies verified claims and references still needing checks.

Neither a biological identity check nor individualized learning guarantees security. Neither intelligence nor deeper integration guarantees virtue. The story has to earn those outcomes through conduct and accountable institutions.
