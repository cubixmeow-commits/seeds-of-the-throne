That stays, but the center of gravity moves: **every original post this month is chosen as profile furniture.** When a reply, a quote post, or a follow brings a stranger to @realiainreid, the top of the profile must answer three questions in under 30 seconds: Who is this? What do they post? Why follow?

Consequences:
- **Evergreen over timely.** A post qualifies for this month only if it still looks right pinned to the top of the profile in January. No news-chasing, no fragments that need outside context, no replies-as-posts.

- **Replies now have one job:** bring people to a profile worth landing on. The reply engine, anchors, and rules from v1 are unchanged.
