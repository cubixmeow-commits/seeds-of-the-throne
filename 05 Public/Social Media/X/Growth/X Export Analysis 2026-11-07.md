# X Export Analysis — @realiainreid
**Source:** official X data export `Xdata-11-07-2026.zip` (downloaded from Noa's Google Drive 2026-11-07; size verified byte-for-byte, 157,174,684 bytes).
**Boundary honored:** only `tweets.js`, `follower.js`, `following.js`, and `account.js` were extracted and read. DMs, likes, email/IP/security files were not extracted or opened.
**Cross-check:** export totals match the live account exactly (630 posts, 44 followers, 308 following at export time; account created 2026-07-19; export already carries the new display name "Origins Unknown ᛊᛁᛚᚹᚨᚾ").

## The headline numbers
- **630 posts, 2026-07-19 to 2026-11-06.** 616 top-level, **only 14 replies**, 2 reposts of others.
- Volume by month: Jul 31 (from the 19th), **Aug 290 (~9 posts/day)**, Sep 208, Oct 74, Nov 27 (6 days). High early volume, declining since.
- **545 of 616 top-level posts (88%) have zero likes and zero reposts.** Average engagement per top-level post: 0.13 (fav + 2×RT). The single most-liked post has 2 likes.
- This confirms the live-analytics diagnosis from the other side: the constraint was never output. The account produced at scale, into a room with nobody in it.

## Topic mix (top-level posts, keyword-tagged, multi-tag possible)
- Story / Seeds of the Throne: **215 posts** (Samuel, Sylvan, Luminai, colonization, etc.) — the dominant pillar, engagement ~0.1/post.
- AI: 137 posts, ~0.2/post.
- Other/unclassified: 258 posts (software projects like VibeKB and Project Professor in Jul, general commentary) — the account's early identity was AI tooling before the story pivot.
- Politics/news: 50 posts, ~0.2/post.
- Wildlife/photography: **only 31 posts, but the highest average engagement (~0.4/post)** and the account's #2 post by live impressions (Wildlife Meme Adventures intro, 558). Underproduced relative to its performance.
- **RV / AP / consciousness: only 7 posts.** The pillar the whole new strategy is built on essentially does not exist in the archive yet. The Monroe/Targ overlap content is genuinely new territory for this account, not a continuation.
- UAP/UFO: 1 post.

## Format findings
- Media: 237 of 616 top-level posts carry media; no measurable engagement difference in the export counts (impressions live in Analytics, not the export).
- Long posts (>280 chars, Premium long-form): 168 posts, marginally higher engagement (~0.2 vs 0.13). Combined with Analytics (an Article is the #1 post at 655 impressions), depth beats frequency in this account's own data.
- Posting time clusters midday Pacific (10:00–15:00), consistent, no action needed.
- Longest silence: 4 days (twice). Cadence was never the issue either.

## What this changes in the plan
1. **The reply engine is the single biggest behavioral delta available.** 14 replies in 111 days vs the plan's 5/day. Discovery has simply never been tried.
2. **Volume should drop, not rise.** ~9 posts/day produced 204 verified Home impressions in 90 days. Fewer, stronger top-level posts plus Articles and replies is the correct inversion.
3. **Wildlife is underweighted.** 31 posts, best per-post engagement, #2 impression post. The plan's weekly Environment File is justified; consider it the #2 pillar in practice, not a garnish.
4. **The RV/AP pillar starts from ~zero (7 posts).** The Overlap Article is therefore a true launch, and expectations for its first weeks should be set accordingly: it is planting a flag, not harvesting an existing audience.
5. Nothing in the archive contradicts the audit; the vault's Aug 17 export (233 posts) is simply an older slice of the same account and can be retired as a reference.
