## The core move

You don't have a finished book to sell, and you don't need one. The story is built *out of* real, heavily-followed topics — declassified psychic-spy programs, simulation theory, synchronicity, AI consciousness, manufactured history. So the content formula is:

> **Real topic, told accurately → the story's twist on it → a question or exercise the audience joins → the follow.**

Your stance everywhere: *an author researching these fields while building a story that stress-tests them.* That stance is credible in communities that punish both gullibility and contempt — and the story's own epistemology (evidence, provenance, falsifiable prediction) is the differentiator. Nobody else in these spaces is building fiction about **how you'd verify any of it**.
