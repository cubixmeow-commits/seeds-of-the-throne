# X Account Build — @realiainreid
## Audience & Content Strategy for an Unfinished Story

Drafted 2026-11-06 by Agent Mow, from the Seeds of the Throne vault (CAST / WORLD / STORY, systems, research reports, and the vault's X Post Archive of 233 authored posts, Jul 19–Aug 17 2026).

**Core problem this solves:** Book One isn't finished, so the account cannot sell a book yet. It has to sell *the idea-space the book lives in* — and collect, in advance, the exact readers who will want the book when it arrives.

**Strategy in one line:** Become a recognizable voice on the real-world subjects the story is built from — human–AI partnership, synthetic life, manufactured history, leadership and containment, longevity, colonizing new worlds, and the real environments the story's surface world is made of — with the fiction woven through as the payoff, not the whole feed.

## 1. What the story actually offers an audience (story DNA)

Extracted from `03 Context/` and `02 Story/Systems/`:

- **The hook premise:** A civilization that defeated aging builds a small planet as an intelligent environment — part leadership school, part containment for dangerous powerful people. Ordinary surface civilization on top; hidden technology underneath everything. *The moon is not a moon* — it's an orbital generator / command structure powering billions of biological synthetics through hidden satellites.
- **Human–AI pairing:** Luminai — an AI partner that grows with a person's mind, subconscious, choices and environment over years — vs. a Daemon, an evil AI soul. Includes an AI-assistant spoofing attack (a counterfeit assistant extracting information).
- **Synthetic life:** Wrapped-nanocell biological synthetics, indistinguishable from humans, with personhood questions; criminals who want to merge with / transfer into synthetic bodies and call it godhood; legitimate regeneration kept distinct from the fraud.
- **Epistemology as combat:** Patterns can be real while the explanation attached to them is false. George White is controlled not by lies about facts but by controlling what he sees and how it's framed. Sylvan survives by provenance, falsifiable predictions, rival interpretations, and independent evidence. Manufactured history, a False Victory maintained for decades, sealed records vs. public story.
- **Power:** Fascist failure and leadership cultivation; cult leadership (Konrad builds believers, Samuel builds dependencies); succession after centuries of founder rule; corporate wealth trying to buy godhood; the central question — *can a system that uses people as material cultivate leaders who refuse to do the same?*
- **Long time spans:** Samuel ~220 years old, Konrad ~300; millennia-old empires; an ~80-year episodic middle.
- **Real environments in the story's surface world:** a lake and coastal California landscape (the author's own Lake Los Carneros photography is already in the feed), a continental archipelago, erosion environments, a municipal administrative building (the presentation scene), a large campus / data-center expansion (employment arc), empires' cities.
- **Vault research feeds (ready-made topic bank):** submarine epistemology, longevity psychology, synthetic bodies & brain-transfer plausibility, human–AI co-development and corrupted feedback loops, how false victories collapse, whistleblowers who need an audience not just proof, consent in long-duration leadership cultivation, encoded civilizations and manufactured historical evidence — 50+ reports in `04 Research/Full Reports/`.

**Comparable shelf (for audience targeting, not imitation):** Bobiverse (AI copies of a person, competence + humor), Expeditionary Force (a powerful AI companion — Skippy — and improvised problem-solving), Project Hail Mary / The Martian (accessible problem-solving SF), Murderbot (AI personhood, dry voice), The Expanse (colonization politics), Dune / Foundation (millennia, empires, succession), Three-Body Problem (civilization-scale systems and deception), Children of Time (deep time, engineered life). The vault's own style note already aims at accessible, problem-solving-driven long-form SF.

## 2. Audience map — who on X would want this, in priority order

### Tier 1 — Future book buyers (core; build for them)

**A1. Competence-SF / AI-companion readers**
Fans of Bobiverse, Expeditionary Force, Project Hail Mary, Murderbot. They follow series for years, are audiobook-heavy, and cross-recommend constantly — the same accounts surface recommending all four series together. *Our angle:* Luminai vs. Daemon is a darker, more serious take on the AI-companion idea they already love. Content: pairing/bonding concepts, "what your AI partner learns from your subconscious," prototype scenes with Sylvan & Orzai.

**A2. Big-civilization SF readers**
Dune, Foundation, Three-Body, Children of Time, Expanse readers. *Our angle:* millennia-old empires running a two-planet experiment in leadership and containment; rules for future citizens of two worlds. Content: world-system posts, two-planet process, succession and rulemaking questions posed *to* the audience (FP-01 is literally a public question).

**A3. AI X (alignment, AGI, human–AI symbiosis)**
The largest, most Premium-dense conversation on X — and verified impressions are what monetization counts. They argue daily about AI consciousness, alignment, AI companions, and whether AI is training us. *Our angle:* a worked-out fictional ecology — personal Luminai, institutional intelligences, synthetics, responsive environments — "the world itself is the mechanism," plus the counterfeit-assistant attack, which maps directly onto real prompt-injection / impersonation anxiety. Content: one story concept per real AI news/research hook, mechanism-first.

### Tier 2 — Growth amplifiers (big reach, partial conversion)

**A4. Longevity & synthetic-biology X**
Aging defeated, 220-year lifespans, synthetic bodies, brain transfer. Active longevity/biohacking and synthetic-biology audiences. *Our angle:* psychology of a 130+-year life, what brain transfer would actually require (Research Report 51), synthetic personhood rights. Careful line: story treats godhood claims as fraud — that skepticism plays well.

**A5. Space / colonization X**
Mars colonization, orbital infrastructure, Kardashev-scale thinking. *Our angle:* a colony planet that must be self-sustaining from a barren start, an artificial moon, societies built on synthetic infrastructure. Content tied to real space news.

**A6. Constructed-reality / simulation-curious X**
"Reality isn't real" is a perennial X conversation. *Our angle:* awakening = discovering your ordinary world was supported by systems you didn't understand — and it was still real. That's a more interesting, less dismissive take than standard simulation content.

### Tier 3 — Depth and trust (smaller, high-signal)

**A7. Epistemology / OSINT / manufactured-history X**
People who study propaganda, evidence, provenance, cults, and how false narratives hold. *Our angle:* False Victory mechanics, George's closed information world, common knowledge vs. distributed blackmail, whistleblowers needing an audience. Vault rule applies: real history only as analogy, stripped to mechanism; no real persons/nations/firms as story analogues in posts either.

**A8. Writing-in-public / serial-fiction X**
#SciFi, #AmWriting, #SpeculativeFiction, indie serial authors building followings *while* writing (a live model exists of authors sharing a book as it develops, with companion concept articles). *Our angle:* the gate-by-gate build itself — decisions, contradictions kept, questions posed. ### Existing pillar — keep and formalize

**A9. California nature / wildlife photography X**
The feed already runs Wildlife Meme Adventures (Snack Heist Files, Lake Los Carneros). It's the visual reach engine and, done right, it's not off-topic: these are the story's surface-world environments. Label them as such (Environment Files, below) and the nature audience and the story audience start to overlap instead of splitting the account.

## 3. What the account posts — content system

Positioning line (for bio/pinned post, draft): *Building Seeds of the Throne — a novel about a planet built to train leaders and contain the dangerous, the AI partners who grow up with them, and the moon that isn't one. Posting the real ideas and places it's built from.*

### Pillars (weekly mix)

1. **Idea of the World — 35%**
One story system, explained through a real-world item (AI research, longevity news, a governance failure, a cult mechanism — stripped to mechanism). Structure: real hook → the mechanism → "in the story, this becomes…" → one question to the audience. Source bank: the 50+ research reports + systems files. This is the main Tier-1/Tier-2 converter.

2. **Environment Files — 20%**
Real environments relevant to the story, photographed/observed: Lake Los Carneros and the coast (surface world), the archipelago idea, erosion landscapes, municipal rooms where power presents evidence, campuses and data centers (employment arc), submarine-like enclosed worlds (from Research 09). Each post: what this place is really like → what the story does with a place like it. Wildlife Meme Adventures lives inside this pillar as its lighter series, keeping the Snack Heist voice.

3. **Fiction Fragments — 20%**
Standalone micro-scenes and short prototypes (Sylvan/Orzai, Samuel's presentation, George seeing a pattern). Hard vault rule preserved: every fragment must read as fiction alone, in a screenshot, with no bio context. Ends on an unresolved edge, not a summary. One X Article per major piece — *The Process Is Ending* is the template and first candidate.

4. **False Victory Files — 15%**
The epistemology series: how a false story is maintained, how a pattern gets a false explanation attached, how sealed records outlast public history. Aimed at A7 + A2. No real-world partisan targeting; mechanisms only.

5. **Building in Public — 10%**
Gate progress (FP-01 decided → what changed), contradictions deliberately kept, questions put to readers that are real author gates at a safe level of detail. Aimed at A8; also makes followers invested in the ending before it exists.

### Format rules (tied to how monetization actually counts)
- **Top-level original posts are the product.** Original Content Rewards counts Home Timeline impressions from verified users on original posts; **reply impressions do not count** toward the 500K gate. So: hooks live in post #1, threads are built so post #1 stands alone, Articles for long pieces.
- Replies still matter — for being discovered and followed — but they are marketing, not the metric. Budget them deliberately (see §4), don't let the account become reply-only.
- One strong visual per pillar post where possible (vault has a visual generation system + real photography). Video/short clips of real environments for Environment Files.
- Hashtags sparingly, X-native: 1–2 per post max (#SciFi, #ScienceFiction, #AmWriting when it's a writing post). Community membership and replies do more than hashtag stacks.
- No posting unresolved gates as canon. Label working/proposed material as in-development; never post material the vault marks as excluded/unsafe, and never let a post create a real-person analogue.

## 4. How the account grows — distribution playbook

1. **Join and post natively in X Communities** matching Tier 1/2 (science fiction / books, AI, space, longevity, writing). Community posts are original posts — they count — and they put content in front of pre-filtered audiences instead of the cold For You feed.
2. **Reply where the audience already gathers:** daily, short, substantive replies on mid-to-large accounts in A1–A5 conversations (AI news, SF recommendations, space, longevity). Goal is profile clicks and follows; one genuinely useful mechanism-level reply beats twenty reactions. Recommendation threads ("books like Bobiverse / Project Hail Mary?") are the single highest-value reply target for A1 — answer as a reader first, mention the project only when it truly fits.
3. **Ask real questions:** the story's own open questions (rulemaking, containment boundaries, synthetic rights) are built for quote-posts and long replies from smart accounts — that is verified-user engagement on *our* original post.
4. **Collaborate sideways:** other serial/indie SF authors (A8), SF recommendation accounts, and AI/longevity commentators — guest threads, mutual quote-posts, Spaces on a single story mechanism (e.g., "Should a synthetic person be able to refuse a role?").
5. **Cadence (starting proposal, adjust to baseline pace math):** 1 pillar post/day minimum, 1 Article/week, 5–10 targeted replies/day, 1 question post/week. Consistency over bursts; the 90-day window is rolling, so a steady daily rate is what reaches 500K verified impressions.
6. **Profile conversion:** pinned post = premise + best fragment + site/atlas link; bio carries the positioning line above; link goes to iainreid.dev/devsite Project Explorer / Atlas, which already exists as the depth layer.

## 5. Weekly measurement (the only numbers that steer)
- Verified followers (target 500) and verified Home Timeline impressions, trailing 90 days (target 500K) — from Creator Studio.
- Per-pillar: impressions and new followers per post type; double down on the best pillar/audience pair each month, cut the worst.
- Follower *quality* check: are new followers from Tier 1/2? A smaller, right audience beats a larger random one for eventual book sales — monetization is the milestone, the book audience is the point.
