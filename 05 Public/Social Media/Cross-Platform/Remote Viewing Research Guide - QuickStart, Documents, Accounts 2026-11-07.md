# Remote Viewing Research Guide
**Compiled 2026-11-07 from two live research sweeps (all URLs verified in search results or fetched).**
**Tiers:** PRIMARY/DOCUMENTED = the declassified record or scholarly source. COMMUNITY/STANDARD = the practitioner community's canonical material, reliable for what is taught and practiced. CLAIMS-HEAVY = interesting, sometimes well produced, assertions beyond the checkable record.

## THE QUICKSTART PATH (do these in order)
1. **FAS program history** (30 min, skeptical-institutional): https://irp.fas.org/program/collect/stargate.htm
2. **Psi Encyclopedia "Remote Viewing" entry** (balanced academic overview): https://psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk/
3. **The 1986 DIA CRV Manual** (the actual method, free): https://www.remoteviewed.com/crv-manuals/ — clean annotated PDF: https://hrvg.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/crv-manual-smith.pdf
4. **Both sides of the 1995 verdict:** Utts' assessment (PDF): https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/NSAEBB/NSAEBB438/docs/doc_57.pdf — pair with Hyman via the FAS page.
5. **IRVA's curated 50 landmark Star Gate documents:** https://www.irva.org/stargate-guide
6. **Try one blind practice target** before reading anyone's success stories: https://www.aestheticimpact.com/practice.html

## PRIMARY SOURCES
- **CIA FOIA Reading Room, STARGATE collection:** https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/collection/stargate — ~12,473 documents / ~89,900 pages, released Jan 2017. Naming chronology for dating documents: SCANATE → GONDOLA WISH → GRILL FLAME → CENTER LANE → SUN STREAK → STAR GATE.
- **The 1995 AIR Evaluation** (*An Evaluation of Remote Viewing: Research and Applications*, Mumford/Rose/Goslin, 29 Sep 1995): Utts found the lab effect statistically established; Hyman called it premature; AIR recommended termination. Full text transcription: https://theclassifiedrecord.com/documents/stargate/primary-documents — also published as journal papers in *Journal of Scientific Exploration* 10(1), 1996.
- **The Gateway Process document** (Lt. Col. Wayne McDonnell, US Army INSCOM, 9 Jun 1983; CIA-RDP96-00788R001700210016-5): the Army's assessment of Monroe's Gateway/Hemi-Sync, Focus 10/12, RV among Focus 12 techniques. Transcription: https://theclassifiedrecord.com/documents/malech-patent/gateway-process — one officer's theoretical assessment, not an experiment or agency finding.
- **RemoteViewed Star Gate documents page** (incl. "33 Key SRI Projects/Papers/Reports"): https://www.remoteviewed.com/star-gate-documents/
- **Ingo Swann Papers, University of West Georgia** (~200 boxes, finding aid): https://aspace-uwg.galileo.usg.edu/repositories/2/resources/274
- **Searchable-archive guide** (Black Vault searchable PDFs, Internet Archive "STARGATEDataset"): https://www.psionicresearch.com/articles/stargate-archive-89901-pages.html

## ORGANIZATIONS & REFERENCE
- **IRVA:** https://www.irva.org/ — nonprofit umbrella founded 1999 by program principals (Puthoff, Targ, Buchanan, Paul H. Smith). Conference, *Aperture* magazine, research library. Applied Precognition Project (Debra Katz's ARV world): https://www.irva.org/applied-precognition-project
- **Psi Encyclopedia (SPR):** https://psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk/ — 750+ signed, referenced articles, includes a Sceptical Views section. Swann entry: https://psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk/articles/ingo-swann/
- **FAS STAR GATE page:** https://irp.fas.org/program/collect/stargate.htm — the institutional skeptic's program biography (~$20M over two decades; flatly notes RV data was never used to guide operations).
- **Center Lane glossary:** https://centerlane-rv.org/glossary/irva — names, viewer numbers, terms.
- **Loyd Auerbach's meta-list:** https://loydauerbach.com/pages/parapsychology-online-resources — links Ed May's Laboratories for Fundamental Research (lfr.org), the lab-science continuation.
- **Journals:** *Journal of Scientific Exploration*, *Journal of Parapsychology*, *Explore*.
- **Farsight Institute:** https://farsight.org/ — CLAIMS-HEAVY conclusions (ET/Mars material unverifiable); genuinely useful as a methods artifact: full session transcripts posted, free SRV manual: https://farsight.org/pdfs/SRV/SRVManualByCourtneyBrown.pdf

## KEY PEOPLE
- **Russell Targ:** https://espresearch.com (his books, demo videos); film site thirdeyespies.com. PRIMARY voice, claims at full strength — cross-check against the archive.
- **Ingo Swann:** https://ingoswann.com (estate); books *Natural ESP* (method), *Penetration* (claims go furthest). Note: Swann disowned authorship of the 1986 manual ("I did not write it"); it was compiled by Paul H. Smith from trainee notes.
- **Paul H. Smith:** https://rviewer.com — Star Gate viewer 1983–90, primary author of the CRV manual, the field's most sober explainer. Books: *Reading the Enemy's Mind* (2005), *The Essential Guide to Remote Viewing*.
- **Joe McMoneagle:** no central personal site; Monroe Institute is his home. *The Stargate Chronicles* (2002) is the memoir to read. His discipline: RV against unverifiable targets is "a waste of time."
- **Lyn Buchanan:** https://www.crviewer.com — viewer 018, CRV teacher since 1992; *The Seventh Sense* (2003), friendliest single-volume intro. Lineage: Swann → Buchanan → Lori Williams.
- **Courtney Brown:** see Farsight above. CLAIMS-HEAVY.
- **Hella Hammid:** no site (died 1992); she lives in the SRI papers as the "non-psychic" who became the strongest statistical performer.

## HONESTY FOOTER
The declassified record proves the program existed, ran ~23 years, and was seriously staffed and funded. It does not settle whether RV works: the 1995 closing verdict is split on the lab statistics and unanimous that no operational intelligence value was demonstrated. Method fidelity (blind protocol, AOL discipline, sealed judging) is checkable against the documents; efficacy claims are not thereby endorsed. Prices/dates current as listed late Oct–Nov 2026 and will drift.
