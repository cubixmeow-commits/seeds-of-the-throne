# @cubixmeow on Threads — Account Analysis and Transition Fit

*Analyzed 2026-11-06 via the Threads connector (account id on file). Companion to the Serious Transition Plan v2, which was written for Facebook. Verdict up front: **Threads is the easier half of the transition.** The serious voice the Facebook page has to grow into already exists here, proven, in the account's own history.*

## The account today

- **@cubixmeow, 158 followers**, public, not verified. Growing slowly and steadily: 140 on Oct 9, 149 on Nov 5, 158 now (net +9 in the last 28 days despite near silence).
- **Effectively dormant right now:** 23 views total in the last 28 days, 1 like, zero replies, reposts, or quotes. Most of what little traffic exists arrives via search and logged out views, not the feed. Like the Facebook page, this is a sleeping asset, not a dead one.
- **Audience shape:** about 68% male; largest age bands 35 to 44 and 45 to 54 (about 24% each), with 55+ another ~31%. Geography is global: US ~23%, then Tanzania ~8%, India ~7%, Brazil ~6%, Nigeria ~4%. An older, male, international audience. Very different from a typical cute cat following, and much closer to the audience for history, mystery, and consciousness content.

## The discovery: this account already went viral, as something else

The account's top posts are not cat posts. In a five day burst, June 25 to 30, 2026, @cubixmeow posted text commentary in a distinctive curiosity voice and Threads pushed it hard:

- A "Freedom 250 Great American State Fair" explainer: **~98,000 impressions, 852 likes, 173 replies.**
- US vs Turkey World Cup: **~94,000 impressions, 487 likes.**
- A House of the Dragon observation: **~85,000 impressions.**
- Norway benching Haaland, psychology angle: **~48,000 impressions.**
- Trump rally crowd claims vs reporter accounts: ~29,000 impressions, with the closing move "Same event, two completely different realities, who do you believe?"
- Paraguay's World Cup upset (the same story that appeared on the Facebook page): ~11,000 impressions, 636 likes.

The format is consistent and it is a real voice: a hook built on a buried detail ("there is a genuinely fascinating detail buried in this", "I cannot stop thinking about"), short single idea paragraphs, the facts laid out plainly, and an ending that hands the judgment to the reader. No cats anywhere in the hits. The account's name promises cats; its proven content is *curiosity about things hiding in plain sight*.

## What this means for the transition

1. **Threads does not need the cute to serious ramp. It needs a subject change, in a voice it already owns.** The v2 plan's Facebook phases (revival, deepening, watch posts) exist to retrain a cozy illustration audience. The Threads audience followed a commentator. Sacred cats, Star Gate documents, Monroe, and the story's epistemology are all "buried detail" subjects; they slot into the existing format almost mechanically.
2. **The pillars divide by platform.** Facebook (visual, community, ritual): the Meows' illustrated journeys, The Sleeping Watch, the Sealed Target reveal as an event. Threads (text, argument, serial): the same material as sourced text threads: document readings, Monroe and Star Gate explainers, Overlap questions as standalone posts, story world dispatches. One research nugget, two renditions. Threads carries the argument; Facebook carries the ceremony.
3. **The audience demographics favor the serious material.** Older, male, global, and already responsive to "two realities, who do you believe?" framing, which is, not coincidentally, the story's core epistemic move. The Trump crowd post is structurally a False Victory post wearing news clothes.
4. **The risk profile is inverted vs Facebook.** Facebook's risk is losing cozy followers by deepening. Threads' risk is the opposite: its followers never signed up for cats at all, so cat content is the unproven element here. The safe Threads mix leads with the curiosity voice and lets cats appear as the recurring emblem and occasional subject (a Sacred Cats thread is a history thread, which this audience has already rewarded), rather than leading with illustration posts.

## The Threads rendition of the v2 plan

- **Phase alignment:** Threads skips v2 Phase 0 and 1. It starts at the equivalent of Phase 2 content (perception, dreams, documented mysteries) within its proven explainer format, while Facebook is still deepening.
- **Cadence:** 4 to 5 text posts per week is sustainable in this format; threads of 3 to 6 posts for document stories (Star Gate session, Gateway report, Pelusium with sources). Every Ancient World Facebook installment gets a Threads text twin, posted same day, rewritten for the voice, not copied.
- **Signature Threads formats:**
  - *Buried Detail:* the proven hook, applied to RV/AP and cat history documents.
  - *Two Realities:* two sourced accounts of the same event or claim, reader judges. (Star Gate's 1995 evaluation is literally this: one evaluator said significant results, the other said flawed methods.)
  - *The Overlap, standalone:* the story questions as single posts, which on Threads can travel without the illustration.
  - *Sealed Target, text edition:* the ritual works natively here: target number post, impressions as replies, reveal as a follow up thread with honest scoring.
- **Growth mechanics:** Threads rewards replies and conversation; the plan's question endings fit. Spend engagement time inside spirituality, consciousness, and history Threads communities rather than cat Threads initially, because that is where this account's proven audience lives.
- **Naming:** same rule as Facebook. "The story world we are building" until the coordinated naming moment, so both platforms reveal together.
