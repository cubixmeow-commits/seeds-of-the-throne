## Strategic read
- Facebook: real group infrastructure exists for RV and AP. Entry points: Remote Viewing (public flagship), Remote Viewing Practice Targets (Sealed Target analogue; learn its norms before ever proposing anything), TMI OBE Group (Monroe thread), the two giant private AP groups. IRVA group bans AI content: text-only genuine participation there.
- Threads: all three niches under-occupied; spiritual cats is open territory. Reach runs through Gaia, Monroe Institute, Dana Kippel, Astral Projection Official. The reply engine targets: @themonroeinstitute, @dana.thealien, @astralprojection, @ancient_egypt_kemet, @amberadrian.
- The sacred-cat gap on both platforms supports the pivot premise: the niche has devotion but no flagship. Temple of Bastet (FB, 5K public) is the community to be a good citizen of first.
