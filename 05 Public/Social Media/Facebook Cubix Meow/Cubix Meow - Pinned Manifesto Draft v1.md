THE HOUSE OF THE WATCHER CATS

This page is changing. Here is plainly what it is becoming.

Cats have always been stationed at the doorway. They guarded temples in Egypt, were accused of walking between worlds, and sit awake at 3 a.m. staring at something we cannot see. We think they were watching something worth understanding.

This is now a house with four rooms:

SACRED CATS: cats in spiritual history, temples, goddesses, folklore.
THE SLEEPING WATCH: dreams, sleep, and what animals perceive that we miss.
THE VIEWING ROOM: remote viewing and astral projection, from declassified documents and honest practice. Once a month, a Sealed Target: a photograph nobody has seen, described blind, revealed and scored in public.
THE STORY WORLD: a story being built in the open, out of the other rooms.

Our rules, in public: documents are documents, legends are labeled legends, and when we miss, we publish the miss.

Mr. and Mrs. Meow are your guides. They have always been watchers. Now you know what they are watching.
