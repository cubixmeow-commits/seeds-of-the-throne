## Caption (copy-ready)

🐱🏺 CATS OF THE ANCIENT WORLD №1 — The City Where Cats Were Royalty

Mr. and Mrs. Meow have travelled a long way back — to Bubastis, the city of Bastet, the cat goddess of ancient Egypt. 🏛️

Here, cats weren't just pets. They were sacred. The Greek historian Herodotus wrote that when a household cat died, the whole family shaved their eyebrows in mourning. Harming a cat could be punished by death. And when archaeologists opened a cat cemetery at Beni Hassan in 1888, they found an estimated 80,000 mummified cats, carefully buried by people who believed these small travellers deserved a proper journey into the next life. 🐈‍⬛✨

Look at the temple steps in the picture — that's not artistic license. Temples really were full of cats, fed, protected, and treated like honored guests.

Nine lives, the Egyptians may have started: nine was a sacred number, tied to the gods themselves. Other cultures count differently — seven lives in Spain, six in Arabic and Turkish tradition. How many does YOUR cat seem to have? Tell us in the comments — we read every one. 💬
