🐱🏺 CATS OF THE ANCIENT WORLD №2: The Day Cats Decided a War

Mr. and Mrs. Meow have arrived at Pelusium, on the edge of the Nile Delta, in 525 BCE.

Legend says the Persian king Cambyses knew how sacred cats were to Egypt. His soldiers painted Bastet, the cat goddess, on their shields, and placed cats, dogs, sheep and ibises in front of their line. The Egyptian soldiers, unwilling to harm a sacred animal, held their fire.

It worked, says the story. Persia took Egypt.

One caution: this tale comes from Polyaenus, writing 700 years later. Herodotus, writing closer to the time, records a fierce battle and an Egyptian defeat, but no cats. Legend, not settled fact. The lesson still stands.

What would you protect so fiercely it could be used against you? Tell us below.
