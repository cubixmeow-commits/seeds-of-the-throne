# Cubix Meow × Seeds of the Throne — the Overlap Plan

*Drafted 2026-11-06. Author direction: do NOT add cats to the story. The integration happens at the content level — ancient cat history is real, established content the page's audience already wants, and the story's themes genuinely overlap with it. The story enters as a coda, never as canon and never by rebranding the page.*

## The two-layer post

Every bridge post has two layers:

1. **The history** — accurate, sourced ancient-cat content. This is the post; it must stand on its own for the existing audience.
2. **The Overlap** — a short, branded closing segment (same divider/format every time, so regulars learn to look for it): one story-world question that the history just raised. No story exposition, no characters dumped in — a question and a door (Atlas/story link in comments or About).

## Staging (protects the existing monetization)

- **Phase 1 — Revival (weeks 1–4).** Pure animal content returns: the page's proven formats plus the new "Cats of the Ancient World" history series (history only, no story). Goal: restore posting rhythm and reach before introducing anything new.
- **Phase 2 — The Overlap appears (weeks 5–8).** Roughly 1 in 4 posts carries the Overlap coda. Watch unfollows and per-post engagement; the coda is a question, not an ad, so it should read as enrichment.
- **Phase 3 — The door opens.** Occasional standalone bridge posts; About section gains one line pointing to the story world (Atlas); cross-posts begin. By now the audience has self-selected: the ones who lean into the Overlap are the story's first Facebook readers.

## Guardrails

- History stays accurate and sourced; where a tale is legend (Pelusium's cat shields), label it legend — credibility is the brand on both sides of the bridge.
- The story is never presented as hidden truth about real cats/Egypt (Report 49 itself forbids that move: real monuments/religions were *not* made by the story's forces).
- Page name and core identity stay cat-first. Cubix Meow's monetization is an asset; the bridge is built to not spend it.
