# Refined Plan — Finish Seeds of the Throne Book One + Monetize @realiainreid

Locked 2026-11-06. Author definition of "finish": **Book One scene-ready, then draft it.**

## The one idea
One work stream, two outputs. Every story gate resolved produces (a) progress toward a scene-ready Book One, and (b) an original X post/Article. Story development is the content engine; X is the audience and monetization layer. No separate filler content plan.

## Track A — Story: scene-ready, then draft

Source of truth: `07 Coordination/Story Completion Workflow/DYNAMIC-WORKSHOP.md` — one author gate at a time, updated after every accepted assessment. No parallel checklists.

### Phase A1 — Gate sequence (dependency order, from the workshop's Next Assessment Pass)
1. **FP-01 (current)** — Rulemaking authority: who can turn a problem observed on either planet into a rule binding future citizens of both, and how can citizens challenge/revise it? Acceptance: distinguish local vs shared rules; name who can propose, authenticate, decide, promulgate, enforce, contest, revise; define AI role without sovereign legislation; safeguards for affected people, synthetics, settlers, heirs, locally born.
2. **CE-01** — Containment mechanism: what opportunity lets dangerous people reveal plans, and what protective boundary stops it becoming control/unchecked harm?
3. **PT-01** — Replay mechanics: what Planet Three reproduces vs. what is new; George/Aiden movements; older leaders' locations and cross-world reach; who authorizes assignments.
4. **OR-01** — Corporate ascension promise: exact promised transformation, and the independent visible result that would convince followers it failed (without touching legitimate regeneration/medicine).
5. **OR-02** — Governance: firms' original rights, leaders' lawful authority, insider-access revocation, differentiated offender entry.
6. **SPR-01** — Character history: when Samuel & Konrad staged their rivalry, and when Samuel first used it to frame Konrad.
7. Downstream gates already in the workshop (incl. EGI layer, superweapon/evidence questions) — worked in workshop order as they become the sole gate.

Per gate, the vault workflow is: session note in `01 Sessions/Daily/` first → promote accepted material to `02 Story/` → update `03 Context/` if the working summary changed → record consequential resolutions in `07 QA/Decisions.md` → update the Dynamic Workshop and CURRENT-PICKUP.

### Phase A2 — Scene-ready check
Book One is scene-ready when the workshop has produced: the exact contest, deception, clock, opening, middle, character choices, evidence order, and sequence map. That is the completion definition from CURRENT-COMPLETION-TODO.

### Phase A3 — Draft
Draft Book One from the sequence map. Default register at this stage: short, readable development prototypes (disposable, assumption-exposing). Archive Thriller / Dark Historical Reconstruction is the later manuscript register, not the drafting default. Author controls final voice.

Already drafted asset: X Article draft "The Process Is Ending" (`05 Public/X Drafts/`) — the Samuel/Sylvan presentation scene, a candidate prototype for the endgame presentation sequence.
