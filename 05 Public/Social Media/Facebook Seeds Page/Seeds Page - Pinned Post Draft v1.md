This page is a development journal for a story about the most dangerous experiment ever run: a civilization that farms evidence.

Seeds of the Throne is a science fiction story in development. Two worlds are settled as one continuing experiment. The participants do not know they are inside it, because the moment they knew, their behavior would stop being evidence. Above them, advanced AI records what people actually do with their beliefs when belief gets the chance to act.

Every faction in the story is running that experiment for its own reasons. The first criminal movement went loud, started a Great War, and was exposed by it. The second learned to stay quiet. Its leader, Samuel Franklin, is not after land or money. His obsession is complete control of every participant's mind and actions, exercised so gently that the controlled believe the thoughts are theirs.

Against that stands a simple discipline the story keeps testing: impressions are not orders, interpretations are not facts, and a record nobody can edit is worth more than a victory everybody applauds.

Here you will find the work as it happens: remote viewing and astral projection research (the declassified files are stranger than the fiction), story problems being solved or failing in public, and the overlap between the two. Drafts, notes, open questions. Nothing here is polished, and the open questions stay open until they are earned.

If you follow one thing, follow this: what happens to a person the moment they realize their own thoughts might have an author?
