## Why this can work: the hinge is already in the bio

The page's Intro already says: "I'm fascinated by what AI is becoming, and how our sleeping minds might eventually train it." Sleeping minds. That single line connects every target topic:

- **Cats are the world's most liminal domestic animal.** Revered as divine (Egypt, Bastet), feared as familiars (medieval Europe), honored as temple guardians (Japan, Thailand), credited with seeing spirits and sensing what humans cannot, in nearly every culture that kept them. "Cats in spiritual context" is not a stretch topic for a cat page. It is the cat page's deepest native subject.
- **Cats are dream creatures.** They sleep 12 to 16 hours and spend much of it in REM. A page about cats and sleep is one honest step from dreams, and dreams are the public doorway to astral projection and out of body experience (Monroe's work begins exactly there).
- **Cats are watchers.** The perception theme (what animals notice that our instruments and habits miss) is the public doorway to remote viewing: disciplined attention to impressions, recorded before checking, scored honestly. That discipline is also the story's epistemology.
- **The story is the destination underneath.** Seeds of the Throne asks what happens when perception, belief, and sacred authority are managed by hidden systems. A serious audience gathered around spiritual cats, dreams, and viewing is an audience pre trained in the novel's exact questions.

That is a role cats already hold in folklore; no rebrand of the characters is needed, only deeper water under the same boat.

## What changes, and what must never change

**Changes:** register (playful to contemplative), caption length and sourcing, color palette for spiritual installments, the questions asked (from "how many lives does your cat have?" to "what has your cat seen that you could not?"), and eventually the About text.

**Never changes:** the page name, the two mascots and their identity markers, factual honesty (legend labeled, sources named, misses published), positivity as a house rule (serious is not the same as dark or combative), and original content only, which is what the monetization rests on.

## Risks, stated plainly

- **Audience mismatch is the main risk.** Some followers came for cozy art and will leave as the page deepens. Mitigation: phased gates, comfort posts retained as seasoning, and unfollow monitoring with a pre agreed brake (two consecutive weeks of net follower loss pauses the deepening, not the page).
- **Platform policy on extraordinary claims.** Facebook restricts content that asserts unverified paranormal claims as fact. Mitigation is the house style itself: history and documents are stated as fact; experiences are reported as experiences; practices are framed as experiments with published misses. This stance is not just compliance, it is the brand.
- **Credibility risk runs both ways.** RV and AP communities include strong believers and strong skeptics. The page's evidence first posture will annoy purists in both camps and is also the only position that survives contact with both. It happens to be the novel's thesis, which is why the fit is real.
- **Time.** This is a 4 to 6 month transition, not a relaunch. Anyone promising faster is selling a cliff.
