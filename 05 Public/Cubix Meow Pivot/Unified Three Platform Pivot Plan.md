*Author direction, 2026-11-06: pivot all three accounts (Facebook Page, Threads, Instagram) into one serious presence centered on the spirit world, astral projection, remote viewing, and Seeds of the Throne, with cats as the overarching theme. This document is the master plan. It builds on the Serious Transition Plan v2 (subject matter and pillars) and the Acquisition Plan v3 (zero base audience strategy, Instagram annex), and supersedes both on questions of identity and coordination. Platform analyses are on file for Facebook (audit), Threads (account analysis), and Instagram (v3 annex).*

## The identity, in one breath

**Cubix Meow is the house of the watcher cats.** Mr. and Mrs. Meow are not mascots pasted onto serious content; they are the frame through which all of it is seen. In nearly every culture, cats are the animals stationed at the boundary: temple guardians, dream travelers, seers of spirits, companions of the dead. The pivot does not bolt spirituality onto a cat brand. It reveals that the cat brand was always standing at that doorway, and walks through it.

- **Umbrella promise (positioning line, draft for Noa's approval):** "Cats, consciousness, and the worlds they watch."
- **The four rooms of the house** (the mature pillars, identical on all three platforms):
  1. **Sacred Cats** — cats in spiritual history: Bastet, temple cats, familiars, folklore, funerary cats.
  2. **The Sleeping Watch** — dreams, sleep, and animal perception: the bridge room.
  3. **The Viewing Room** — remote viewing and astral projection: declassified documents, Monroe, honest skepticism, and the monthly Sealed Target practice.
  4. **The Story World** — Seeds of the Throne: the fiction underneath everything, named openly once the audience has the vocabulary.
- **The cats are the connective tissue in every single post.** A Star Gate document post is framed by a watcher: what would the cat in the room have noticed? A story dispatch opens with the Meows at the edge of the scene. This is the rule that keeps three serious topics and one fiction from reading like four accounts stapled together: the cats are always present, always watching, and the audience learns to trust their vantage point.

## Guardrails that make serious safe

- **Honesty is the brand and the policy shield.** Documents presented as documents, experiences as experiences, legends labeled, Sealed Target misses published in full. Unverifiable claims are never stated as fact, on any platform, which keeps Facebook monetization standing and makes skeptics into a second funnel instead of an enemy.
- **No medical, financial, or guaranteed outcome claims** around AP/RV content, ever. Practice is framed as experiment and community ritual, never as instruction that promises results.
- **No cats added to the story's canon.** The cats frame the telling; they do not enter Seeds of the Throne.
- **The cast stays warm.** Serious register, same affection. The audience is being invited deeper, not lectured.
