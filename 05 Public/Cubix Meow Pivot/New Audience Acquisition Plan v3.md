*Author correction, 2026-11-06: the existing followers on both platforms are passive, non interacting, acquired through AI shorts. This plan overlays the Serious Transition Plan v2 (subject matter and pillars unchanged) and replaces its audience assumptions: v1/v2's "protect the existing audience" logic and unfollow brake are retired. ## The reset, stated plainly

- **Effective audience: zero.** Facebook's 1,573 and Threads' 158 are vanity numbers. Every metric that matters gets measured against engaged reach from non followers, which is currently near nothing on both.
- **The assets that do matter:** Facebook monetization already switched on (protect the Page's policy standing, not its followers), the Cubix Meow name and mascots, the Threads account's June proof that its voice can travel (98K impressions on a text post), and the content system already built (Ancient World №1 and №2 are live and become the first exhibits, not tests for an existing crowd).
- **One job for the next 90 days:** be discovered by strangers who care about cats in spiritual context, dreams, remote viewing, astral projection, and hidden history, and convert discovery into follows with a profile that makes the promise obvious in five seconds.

## Profile conversion (week 1, both platforms)

People follow profiles, not posts. Before scaling output:

- **Bio rewrite (both):** one line that names the territory. Draft: "Cats, consciousness, and the worlds they watch. Ancient cats, dreams, remote viewing, and a story being built in the open." (Noa approves final wording; no em dashes.)
- **Pinned post (both):** a manifesto in miniature: who the Meows are, what this page investigates, the four pillars named, and the ritual promised (a monthly Sealed Target). This is the follow conversion machine; every viral candidate routes here.
- **Facebook About** gains the same text plus the series index (Ancient World installments listed and linked), so a stranger landing from a shared post can binge the back catalog in order.

## Discovery engines, ranked by expected yield

### 1. Facebook Reels (the shorts engine, reclaimed)
The passive followers came from AI shorts; the algorithm lesson is real even if the audience was not. Shorts/Reels are Facebook's main non follower surface. New rule: every Ancient World installment and every document story ships with a 30 to 60 second Reel cut: one hook line spoken or captioned over the illustration (slow zoom), one fact, one question. Original narration and original art keep monetization safe; no recycled clips, no text to speech spam patterns. Target: 3 Reels per week. Reels are discovery; the long caption post is the depth the discovery converts into.

### 2. Groups (borrowed audiences, Facebook's specialty)
RV/AP, Monroe, sacred cats, ancient mysteries, and consciousness groups are where the target strangers already gather. Method: join 8 to 12 groups, contribute value first (answers, documents, the Sealed Target run *inside* a group with admin blessing), page posts cross shared only where rules allow. One good group ritual is worth a month of feed posting. This is also Phase 3's natural home; pull a light version forward: participate now, host later.

### 3. Threads reply and quote engine
Threads grows accounts through conversations, not broadcasts. Daily practice: 5 substantive replies in big spirituality, history, and consciousness threads, written in the proven buried detail voice, never links, never pitches. Plus 4 to 5 original posts per week (document threads, Two Realities, Overlap standalones). The June burst proves a single post can do 98K impressions from a small base; reply grafting is how a small account gets in front of those rooms repeatedly.

### 4. Share engineered formats
Design for the share, not the like:
- **Sealed Target** (participation is a follow magnet: people return for the reveal, and returning is following behavior).
- **Two Realities** (two sourced accounts, reader judges: built for quote posts and arguments in comments).
- **Ancient World** (numbered and collectible: late arrivals binge and follow to not miss installments).
- **Reality checks** (legend vs document, labeled: highly shareable by skeptics, which doubles the funnel).

### 5. Cross platform routing
One research nugget becomes: Facebook Reel + Facebook long post + Threads thread + Instagram Reel/post (the @cubixmeow Instagram is linked and idle; same content, zero extra research). X stays @realiainreid's job with its own strategy; the Cubix system occasionally points to it when the story is named in Phase 4.

## Metrics that count (and targets, labeled as targets)

Measure weekly, per platform: **non follower reach share** (goal: above 70% of reach from non followers, which is the signature of discovery working), **follows gained per post**, **comments per post** (goal: from 0 to a consistent 5+ on pillar posts by day 60), **saves and shares per post** on Facebook, **replies per post** on Threads. Follower totals are reported but never optimized directly.

Ninety day follower targets (ambitious but plausible given the June proof and a zero engaged base; treat as aiming points, not promises): Facebook 1,573 → 2,500 with at least 300 demonstrably active; Threads 158 → 750. If a single Reel or thread breaks out, these numbers become conservative, which is the correct direction for a target to be wrong.
