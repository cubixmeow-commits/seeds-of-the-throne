# Weekly Development Assessment: August 27, 2026

This week, *Seeds of the Throne* moved from broad worldbuilding into structured story completion.

The project now uses a horizontal completion workflow to test every major story problem at the same level before any one branch moves deeper. Nine of twenty-seven active tasks have completed the first Macro Shape pass. The current work is mapping which human-Luminai capabilities, rules, and information skills must be demonstrated before the final conflict can depend on them.

The strongest progress is causal. Character motives, system rules, evidence, and conflicting beliefs are beginning to operate as one story engine. The next phase is less about inventing more lore and more about ordering discoveries, stress-testing permissions, tracking what each character knows, and turning established material into scenes.

Luminai development is also becoming more experiential. The intended pattern is:

`observe -> test -> understand -> counter -> expose`

That lets the interactive world be discovered through anomalies, failed assumptions, bounded experiments, and consequences rather than explained all at once.

Research and experimental ideas remain clearly separated from canon. Five endgame research tracks were completed this week, but their mechanisms remain advisory and every attached creative possibility remains unreviewed until the author makes a decision.

## Next development focus

1. finish the current endgame setup categories at Macro Shape;
2. define exact causal links and bounded permissions without creating unlimited system control;
3. order the evidence and revelations so each answer forces the next question;
4. extract scenes from established facts instead of expanding the world without a structural need.

This public report omits hidden deals, protected evidence, and endgame solutions.
