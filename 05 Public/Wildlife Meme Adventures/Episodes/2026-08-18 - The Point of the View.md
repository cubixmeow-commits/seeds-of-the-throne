6. Build the joke as a two-step observation: `GOOD NEWS: WE FOUND THE POINT.` followed by `BAD NEWS: IT'S STILL OVER THERE.`
7. Keep the episode title in the open sky and the joke in a low sandstone field-note panel so the real coastline remains uninterrupted.
8. Verify `Devereux` spelling and inspect the 512 × 768 vault preview for feed-size readability.
