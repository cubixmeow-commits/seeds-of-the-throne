## Learning record

- What worked: preserving the source panorama and building the joke as a shallow left-to-right comparison entirely within the open sky.
- What failed: treating the retained portrait reference as a mandatory size, accepting a ribbon too small for feed viewing, and then merely rearranging portrait modules on a landscape canvas.
- Author feedback: every meme image must be determined by its photograph; the same fixed size is wrong, the episode ribbon must remain readable, and a horizontal image needs a genuinely horizontal design rather than a resized portrait design.
- Author selection: version 6 approved as the image on 2026-08-19.
- Caption selection: approved on 2026-08-19 after replacing the em dash with a sentence break.
- Photograph-specific lesson: the mountain-to-ocean depth requires a wide canvas and should not be compressed into a vertical center band.
- Reusable lesson: the retained reference supplies identity and visual language, not output dimensions or reading order. Choose the source-led canvas first, then redesign hierarchy and narrative flow specifically for that canvas.
- Rule lifecycle: active; promoted to the series workflow on 2026-08-19
