# Website Voice and Purpose Update

## What changed

The two public sites now speak to visitors instead of describing internal maintenance work.

The story site begins by explaining that *Seeds of the Throne* is a science-fiction story about an interactive colonization planet that creates resources, trains people, contains dangerous criminals, and develops human partnerships with advanced AI. It then introduces the characters, encoded information, and near-end opening in the same simple style.

The Project Explorer now explains that years of conversations and thousands of ideas are being organized into characters, a world, a timeline, and a finished series. Its first development view shows the complete movement from conversation to missing-story questions, connected decisions, and eventual prose. Detailed repository, evidence, progress, and workshop views remain available underneath that introduction.

The workshop now explains its operation directly: it takes one missing story problem, asks one clear question, offers different possible answers, shows what each answer would change, and adds the author's accepted answer to the connected story notes.

## Corrected claims

- Removed “inspect the corrected story” and similar internal operator language from the primary Project Explorer invitation.
- Removed the stale claim that final fiction must be manually human-written.
- Replaced it with the current authority model: AI may assist through finished prose, while the author controls meaning, consequential choices, corrections, and final approval.
- Kept sources, uncertainty, spoilers, and development status visible.

## Public positioning

- `docs/` is the story.
- Project Explorer shows how the story is being built and demonstrates the authoring system.
- Both use the clear public-development style established for X without compressing major ideas into tiny posts.
