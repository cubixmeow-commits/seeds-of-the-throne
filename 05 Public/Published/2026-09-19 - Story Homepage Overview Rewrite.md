# Story Homepage Overview Rewrite

## What changed

The Story homepage now gives a first-time reader a direct overview of the main story instead of relying on short, interpretive statements.

The page explains five connected parts in ordinary language:

1. the colonization planet and its purposes;
2. the new human–Luminai experiment centered on Sylvan;
3. Samuel's hidden takeover of Konrad's defeated hierarchy;
4. the independent Resistance and its distributed evidence;
5. the endgame in which Konrad commits his groups to Sylvan's exposure plan while Sylvan remains separate from Konrad's hierarchy.

The central-conflict section also introduces Samuel Jr. as Samuel's son with Konrad's sister and explains why that relationship matters inside Konrad's bloodline-centered system without resolving Samuel Jr.'s still-open agency.

## Public writing direction

The rewrite uses the established X style at homepage length: familiar words, short paragraphs, visible cause and effect, defined terminology, and concrete consequences. The prose system's anti-AI and tracks passes were used to remove vague slogans, repeated conclusions, development language, and unsupported grandeur.

Exact final processing remains behind a clearly labeled spoiler control. Workshop status and unresolved mechanics remain separate from the story overview.
