## Caption as published

🐱⚔️ CATS OF THE ANCIENT WORLD No.2: The Day Cats Decided a War

Mr. and Mrs. Meow watch from the safest hill in Egypt: Pelusium, 525 BC, Persia invading.

The legend, labeled honestly as legend: the battle was real, and Cambyses of Persia defeated Pharaoh Psamtik III. But the cat story comes from Polyaenus, writing 700 years later. He says Cambyses painted Bastet on his shields and placed cats, dogs, sheep and ibises before his army. The Egyptians, terrified of harming sacred animals, stopped shooting, and Pelusium fell.

Herodotus, who walked that battlefield, never mentions cats. Maybe it happened. Maybe it is a story Persia loved telling. Either way it holds a truth: reverence is strength, until someone learns to aim at it.

Has anyone used something you love as a shield against you?

THE OVERLAP: In the story world we are building, the cruelest weapon is not force. It is knowing what your enemy holds sacred, and standing behind it.
