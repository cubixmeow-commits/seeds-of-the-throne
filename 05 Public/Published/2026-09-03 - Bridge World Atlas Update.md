# Bridge World Atlas Update

## Public revision

The public atlas now reflects the September 3 foundation update:

- the colonization planet is the bridge between the older AI-soul era and the successor Luminai era;
- an **AI soul** is the broad category, a **Daemon** is an evil AI soul, and the **Luminai** are the successor deeply integrated form;
- Konrad enters containment with an older Great War Daemon and tries to prove it can neutralize Samuel more effectively than Sylvan's new Luminai;
- Samuel captures Konrad's reactivated group through compromise, false autonomy, blackmail, lineage destruction, and generational conversion;
- Book One begins near the chronological end, with Sylvan permitting bounded action while exposing Samuel's method before Konrad's inner circle;
- Konrad's approved visual identity is now part of the public character atlas;
- the atlas Project Explorer reports SC-010 at Question 7 and preserves a dated fallback snapshot while live vault records load;
- the standalone repository Project Explorer carries the new key art, foundation assessment, and direct path into this revision record.
