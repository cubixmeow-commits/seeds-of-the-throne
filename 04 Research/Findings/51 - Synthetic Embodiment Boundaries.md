# Synthetic Embodiment Boundaries

Source: [[04 Research/Full Reports/51 - Synthetic Bodies and Brain Transfer Plausibility]].

- **Evidence:** synthetic cell-like compartments, small prototissues, redirected living-cell assemblies, and limited bidirectional neural interfaces exist in research. None demonstrates an engineered human-scale artificial-cell body or whole-brain transfer.
- **Plausible fictional extension:** nanoscale fabrication supports membrane-bound units assembled into specialized perfused tissue; biological development, local signals, vascular support and repair organize a body.
- **Core bottleneck:** manufacturing the body and coupling a biological brain to its complete sensorimotor, autonomic and chemical life are distinct problems. A wireless grid cannot supply oxygen or identity continuity by itself.
- **Canon boundary:** the existing biological synthetics have processors and histories, so a leader's desired replacement body cannot be presumed empty. Information access is staggered among different people and eras; it is no longer categorically absent on Planet Three.
- **False villain premise:** human racial categories supply no biological template for human–synthetic integration. Their program is abuse driven by a false analogy, not a near-discovery.
- **Proposed dramatic use:** their creator's desire to own a new body can collide with the created being's independent agency and the risk that transfer would alter the original mind.
