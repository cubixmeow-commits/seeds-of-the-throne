# Research Request — Canon Authority, Conflict, and Supersession

## Question

How should the graph choose among established, working, proposed, unresolved, rejected, obsolete, and visually approved claims when sources disagree?

## Required output

- Authority matrix by source class and claim type
- Conflict, supersession, deprecation, and non-combinability edges
- Claim-level status rather than file-level assumptions
- Rules preventing old transcripts, research, or generated imagery from overriding story decisions
- Conflict reports and author-resolution workflow

## Development gate

No automatic “latest wins” behavior. Conflicting load-bearing claims must block compilation until precedence is explicit.
