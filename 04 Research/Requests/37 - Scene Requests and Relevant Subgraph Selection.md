# Research Request — Scene Requests and Relevant Subgraph Selection

## Question

How does a short natural-language request select the smallest sufficient vault subgraph?

## Required output

- Scene-intent representation for subjects, action, time, place, purpose, manifestation, and viewpoint
- Deterministic lookup before semantic retrieval
- Metadata filters and bounded semantic-retrieval rules
- Relevance scoring and context budget
- Retrieval receipt showing considered, selected, and excluded sources

## Development gate

Retrieval may propose nodes but cannot change their authority or resolve ambiguity. Selected source results must be inspectable.
