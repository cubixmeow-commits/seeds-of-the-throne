# Research Request — Technology, Capability, and Visibility Graphs

## Question

How should technology be represented by capability, access, social layer, physical consequence, concealment, and observer knowledge before appearance is chosen?

## Required output

- Technology capability and dependency nodes
- Access, controller, user, affected-population, and failure-state edges
- Hidden, mediated, disguised, controlled, and explicit visibility rules
- Function-to-visible-evidence projection
- Protection against generic devices, holograms, robots, and magical shorthand

## Development gate

Undefined appearance must remain undefined even when function is established; the compiler may show consequence without inventing hardware.
