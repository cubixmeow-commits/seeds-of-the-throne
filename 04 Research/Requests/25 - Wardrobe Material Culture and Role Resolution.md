# Research Request — Wardrobe, Material Culture, and Role Resolution

## Story question

How should the compiler resolve clothing from era, place, age, role, class, activity, weather, and personal habits without turning reference cultures into costume stereotypes?

## Scope

Research dress history, textile behavior, climate, occupational clothing, class signals, subculture, grooming, and brand-free translation. Begin with Sylvan's middle-class Los Angeles/United States 1985–present reference and Samuel's Great War role range.

## Required output

- Wardrobe resolver field hierarchy and conflict rules
- Material, fit, wear, layering, footwear, accessory, and grooming vocabulary
- Decade drift and costume-stereotype failure taxonomy
- Character-specific range tests across ordinary, professional, formal, and emergency scenes
- Boundary between Earth reference and invented Seeds material culture

## Development gate

Add wardrobe packets only as working visual definitions; visible culture remains unresolved where the author has not selected a translation.
