# Research Request — Topic

## Story question

What decision or plausibility question should this research inform?

## Scope

State what to investigate and what not to invent.

## Required output

- Current reality
- Plausible extrapolation
- Constraints and failure modes
- Useful terminology
- Sources
- Clear separation of established science, contested claims, and speculation

## Boundary

Research only. Do not propose canon, characters, factions, names, scenes, or plot.
