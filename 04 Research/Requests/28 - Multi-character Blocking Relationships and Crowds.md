# Research Request — Multi-character Blocking, Relationships, and Crowds

## Story question

How can images preserve distinct identities and communicate power, intimacy, concealment, conflict, or coordination through physical relationships rather than poster-like lineup poses?

## Scope

Research proxemics, gaze, eyelines, blocking, conversational grouping, crowd staging, occlusion, and multi-reference identity collision. Use relationship scenes and group images; do not infer canon from staging experiments.

## Required output

- Relationship-edge and blocking packet schemas
- Character-reference assignment and collision-prevention rules
- Gaze, touch, distance, orientation, and power-height vocabulary
- Crowd complexity thresholds and graceful simplification policy
- Failure taxonomy for merged identities, duplicate people, false intimacy, lineup posing, and broken eyelines

## Development gate

Add relationship graph compilation only after each visible body can be mapped to one identity and one intended action.
