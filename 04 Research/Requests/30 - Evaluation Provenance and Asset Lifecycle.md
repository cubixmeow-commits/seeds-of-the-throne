# Research Request — Evaluation, Provenance, and Asset Lifecycle

## Story question

How should the project measure image quality, learn from failures, preserve reproducibility, and promote only reviewed candidates into reusable visual authority?

## Scope

Research multimodal evaluation, human review reliability, benchmark design, pairwise comparison, error taxonomies, metadata and checksums, content provenance, rights, storage, versioning, and candidate-to-publication workflows.

## Required output

- Independent scorecards for identity, action/anatomy, environment, wardrobe, manifestation, composition, and accidental canon
- Benchmark manifest and result-record schema
- Human approval and disagreement protocol
- Candidate, continuity-pass, approved-reference, and published lifecycle rules
- Reproducibility, checksum, source-reference, revised-prompt, and renderer-version fields

## Development gate

Automated scores may triage but may not approve canon. Promotion requires human review and a complete provenance record.
