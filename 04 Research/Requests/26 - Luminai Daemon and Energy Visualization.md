# Research Request — Luminai, Daemon, and Energy Visualization

## Story question

How can cognition energy radiating from the head/brain and chest communicate integration, communication, or corruption without becoming a second person, magic fire, possession, or generic holography?

## Scope

Research scientific visualization, neural and cardiac imagery, volumetric light, atmospheric scattering, bioelectric visual metaphors, and visual-effects readability. Treat current gold, blue, and crimson rules as project definitions; whether observers literally see the energy remains unresolved.

## Required output

- Energy behavior grammar for intensity, coherence, direction, density, rhythm, and interaction with body and environment
- Luminai versus Daemon contrast rules beyond color alone
- Visibility-point-of-view modes: literal, subjective, diagnostic, and audience-only
- Motion and exposure guidance for still images
- Failure taxonomy and benchmark suite, including blue Luminai during natural running

## Development gate

Expand manifestation packets without inventing perception canon or allowing a separate humanoid manifestation.
