# Research Request — Environment Masters, Era, and Social Reality

## Story question

What minimum evidence makes a Seeds location feel inhabited, era-correct, socially specific, and distinct from generic fantasy or science fiction?

## Scope

Research architecture, streets, interiors, infrastructure, ecology, geography, class signals, and ordinary public life by decade and location type. Preserve the surface-versus-hidden civilization rule. Do not invent named Seeds locations or declare decorative generations canonical.

## Required output

- Environment-master schema and evidence hierarchy
- Era-resolution requirements beyond decade labels
- Social-layer, time-of-day, weather, season, maintenance, and population fields
- Rules for hidden, disguised, controlled, and explicit advanced infrastructure
- Priority list of Seeds-specific environment definitions and benchmark establishing shots

## Development gate

Replace generic `surface-civilization` only with author-grounded location masters; undefined load-bearing places must continue to block generation.
