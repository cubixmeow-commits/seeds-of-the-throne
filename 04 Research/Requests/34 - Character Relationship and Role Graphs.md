# Research Request — Character, Relationship, and Role Graphs

## Question

How can family, political, institutional, romantic, adversarial, and cognition-partner relationships become scene-specific blocking and behavior without flattening people into labels?

## Required output

- Relationship nodes or qualified edges with time, direction, status, and privacy
- Distinction between enduring relationship and scene role
- Multi-character identity-reference assignment
- Relationship-to-blocking projection rules
- Protection against unresolved relationships becoming visible canon

## Development gate

Only scene-relevant relationship properties may reach the renderer; hidden motives and nonvisual history remain outside unless they change observable behavior.
