# Research Request — Composition, Cinematography, and Image Purpose

## Story question

How should image purpose control blocking, camera, lens, light, negative space, subject prominence, and visual information density?

## Scope

Research cinematography and visual-narrative practice for key art, narrative cinema, observation, ordinary life, documentary reconstruction, relationship scenes, and environment studies. Do not collapse `image_type`, `composition_mode`, and `render_style` into one field.

## Required output

- Compatibility matrix among image types, composition modes, render styles, and aspect ratios
- Camera packet fields and motivated-light rules
- Composition constraints that are testable rather than decorative adjectives
- Variant strategy for materially different photographs
- Benchmarks for scene readability, visual hierarchy, and unwanted heroic privilege

## Development gate

Implement compatibility warnings and camera presets only after the report shows where defaults help and where they erase story intent.
