# Sylvan–Throne Confrontation

## Working premise

Sylvan possesses extensive recorded evidence of the Throne figure's conduct. If exposed to the right people, the evidence could destroy the Throne figure's legitimacy and end his faction's effective participation in the colonization process.

## Constraint

The Throne figure cannot simply eliminate Sylvan or force the desired outcome. The environment's influence and authority mechanisms are sufficiently stabilized that neither participant can completely impose his will.

## Dramatic pressure

The conflict becomes a contest over truth, legitimacy, evidence, loyalty, and control. The Throne figure is desperate because exposure threatens both his authority and the false victory narrative that has sustained his faction for decades.

## Evidence

The recordings are a working story element. Their exact origin, contents, authenticity safeguards, scope, and route to public exposure remain unresolved.

## Potential Logan connection

Logan may eventually become the human cost of the deception. His possible awakening could transform the confrontation by revealing the depth of the Throne figure's manipulation.

## Questions

- Why can't the Throne figure destroy or discredit the evidence immediately?
- Why can't Sylvan simply publish it?
- Who controls the audience that matters?
- What does each side know about the other's actual authority?
- What would make the Throne figure risk exposing himself?
- What does Sylvan stand to lose even if he wins?
