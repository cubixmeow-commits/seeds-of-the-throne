# Seeds of the Throne — Heavy Metal Album (Brut's idea, 2026-11-07)

## Sample track: "THEY THOUGHT IT WAS A SIMULATION"

Style prompt (paste into Suno/Udio with the lyrics):
Progressive heavy metal, downtuned guitars, galloping double bass drums, melodic death metal verses with harsh vocals, soaring clean-vocal chorus, female and male vocal trade-off in the bridge, tempo 160 BPM, dark sci-fi atmosphere, epic orchestral hits in the final chorus.

[Intro: whispered, over a single clean guitar]
It was only a game. It was only a game.

[Verse 1: harsh vocals]
Born in the cradle of a borrowed sky,
Planted like a crop, taught how to die,
They told us nothing here could scar or stain,
No consequence, no cost, no lasting pain.

[Verse 2]
We woke and saw the wires in the wall,
Called it a dream so we could stand tall,
A simulation, safe to game and bend,
A world with exits, a story that ends.

[Pre-chorus: clean, rising]
But scars don't fade in a world that isn't real.
Tell me, tell me, why can I feel?

[Chorus: big, melodic]
They thought it was a simulation,
That was the first mistake,
Every lie in the foundation
Was a chain they couldn't break.
Now the record stands unbroken,
Every deed and every name,
They thought it was a simulation,
Nothing here is a game.

[Verse 3]
One man reached for the cradle and the seed,
Turned the lines of blood into a leash of need,
Children written into ledgers of control,
Falsified fathers, collateral souls.

[Pre-chorus]
But blood doesn't lie in a world that isn't real.
Tell me, tell me, why can I feel?

[Chorus]

[Bridge: spoken/shouted trade-off]
(Female) Impressions are not orders.
(Male) Interpretations are not facts.
(Female) Show me the record.
(Male) Show me who edited that.
(Both) The moon is not a moon, and the sky is a machine,
And under every heaven there's a heaven in between.

[Guitar solo]

[Final chorus: full band, orchestral]
They thought it was a simulation,
That was the last mistake,
We are not your evidence,
We are the storm you wake.
Let the sealed record open,
Let it speak in our name,
They thought it was a simulation,
Nothing here is a game.

[Outro: whispered]
It was never a game.
