# Draft

- `Scenes/` holds exploratory and revised scenes.
- `Manuscript/` holds ordered manuscript material.

Drafts may draw only from safe story memory and approved research findings. Working story notes remain revisable; a scene does not automatically establish canon.

Use [[08 Story Loop/Templates/scene-packet]] before scene development and [[08 Story Loop/Templates/draft-pipeline-run]] for admission, review, author approval, manuscript promotion, and downstream state propagation.
